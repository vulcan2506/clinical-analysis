# Clinical Care Fundamentals

This protocol ensures accurate electrolyte testing by standardizing specimen collection, processing, and quality control to prevent analytical interference from hemolysis, lipemia, or cellular diffusion.

**Key Features:**
- Specimen Type: Serum or lithium heparin plasma only
- Collection Method: Standard venipuncture technique
- Quality Requirement: Must be free of hemolysis and gross lipemia
- Processing Constraint: Serum must not remain in contact with cells post-centrifugation

**Notable Details:**
- Prevents potassium diffusion and false elevations
- Lithium heparin plasma is the only acceptable alternative to serum

**Test Method:** specimen handling protocol for electrolyte testing

**Performance Characteristics:**
- Use serum free of hemolysis and gross lipemia, collected by standard venipuncture technique; only lithium heparin plasma may be used.
- Do not allow serum to remain on cells after centrifugation to prevent potassium diffusion and falsely elevated results.
- Specimens are allowed to reach room temperature during preparation, with unused portions returned to the freezer.
- Frozen samples are stored at -70°C; sodium and potassium are stable for 2 weeks at 15-25°C or 2-8°C.
- Ensure patient samples, calibrators, and QC are at ambient temperature (20-25°C) before measurement.
- Specimens containing particulate matter must be centrifuged and the material removed before analysis.
- Hemolysis caused by the release of potassium from as few as 0.5% of erythrocytes can increase serum potassium by 0.5 mmol/L and should only be reported at the discretion of the laboratory director.

**Pre-analytical Requirements:**
- Specimens must be collected without preservatives for urine and stored refrigerated during collection.
- Centrifuge samples containing precipitate before assay; clear grossly lipemic specimens by ultracentrifugation.
- A 500-μL sample is required for repeat analyses; 150 μL is required for pipetting into the sample cup.
- All samples, calibrators, and QC must be measured within 2 hours to avoid evaporation effects.
- Specimens thawed and refrozen less than six times are not compromised.
- If a specimen contains more than one analyte of interest and needs to be divided, the appropriate amount of blood, serum, or plasma must be transferred into a sterile Nalge cryovial labeled with the participant's ID.
- Refrigerated samples may be used provided they are kept cold and brought promptly (within 2 hours) from the site of collection.
