# Clinical Care Fundamentals (12)

This topic addresses the specialized inventory management of Roche Diagnostics' reagents and consumables used exclusively for electrolyte testing, ensuring accurate and efficient workflows through standardized components and calibration controls.

**Key Features:**
- Sample trays: Standardized platforms for holding and organizing test samples during processing
- Sample cups: Containers for holding patient samples, available in standard and micro sizes to accommodate different volumes
- Sodium cartridge: Reagent cartridge designed for measuring sodium levels in patient samples
- Potassium cartridge: Reagent cartridge designed for measuring potassium levels in patient samples
- Chloride cartridge: Reagent cartridge designed for measuring chloride levels in patient samples
- Reference cartridge: Calibration or control cartridge used to ensure accuracy and consistency in test results

**Notable Details:**
- Manufacturer: All reagents and consumables are provided by Roche Diagnostics
- Purpose: Specifically used for electrolyte testing, including sodium, potassium, and chloride measurements
- Compatibility: Cartridges are designed to integrate with Roche Diagnostics' electrolyte testing systems
- Inventory tracking: Requires systematic management to prevent shortages or expiration of reagents and consumables
