# Clinical Care Fundamentals (3)

This topic addresses the critical safety and regulatory protocols for the proper disposal of used sharps in healthcare settings to mitigate biological hazards, ensure container integrity, and standardize transfer procedures.

**Key Features:**
- Bio-safe disposal: Use puncture-resistant containers to prevent biological contamination and injury during sharps disposal.
- Container specifications: Only use containers designed for sharps disposal, such as cardboard or glass, that meet regulatory standards for strength and durability.

**Notable Details:**
- Sealing and labeling: Containers must be sealed and clearly labeled before transfer to a healthcare facility to ensure traceability and safety.
- Transfer protocol: Hand over sealed sharps containers to the nearest healthcare facility for proper processing and disposal.
- Container integrity: Containers must withstand puncture and leakage to prevent exposure during handling and transport.
- Regulatory compliance: Adhere to local, state, and federal regulations governing sharps disposal to avoid legal and safety risks.
