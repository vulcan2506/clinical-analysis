# Clinical Care Fundamentals (10)

This topic emphasizes systematic patient feedback collection to drive continuous quality improvement in clinical care while fostering staff recognition through accessible digital platforms.

**Key Features:**
- QR code-based feedback submission enabling quick, mobile-friendly patient input
- Dedicated website for structured, detailed feedback collection with customizable forms

**Notable Details:**
- Feedback data is analyzed to identify care quality gaps and prioritize service improvements
- Staff recognition is automated based on positive feedback trends and volume
- Digital channels (QR codes/website) are the primary feedback collection methods, reducing reliance on paper forms
- Feedback submissions are timestamped and linked to specific care episodes for traceability
- Patient feedback is integrated into performance dashboards for leadership review
- Anonymous feedback options are available to encourage candid responses
- Feedback response rates are tracked to assess engagement and accessibility
- Collected data is stored securely in compliance with healthcare privacy regulations
