# Clinical Care Fundamentals (24)

This topic examines the clinical and socioeconomic determinants of contrast agent administration, emphasizing patient safety in renal impairment and the impact of resource constraints on agent selection.

**Key Features:**
- Renal safety considerations: Management of contrast agent administration in patients with Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD)
- Socioeconomic accessibility: Selection of agents influenced by availability and cost in low- and middle-income countries

**Notable Details:**
- CKD status is a critical factor in agent selection
- Cost-effectiveness is a primary driver in resource-limited settings
- Regional availability dictates clinical preference in lower-income regions
- Contrast agent choice must balance nephrotoxicity risk with diagnostic necessity
- Low-osmolar or iso-osmolar agents are preferred in CKD patients
- Generic formulations may be prioritized in low-resource settings
- Local formulary restrictions may limit agent options
- Patient out-of-pocket costs influence adherence to recommended imaging
