### Source 1: American Thyroid Association.pdf

**Clinical Recommendation:** Clinical Trial Enrollment

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Counsel patients to consider enrolling in prospective clinical trials based on eligibility requirements and potential benefits.
- Clinicians should review available treatment options and eligibility criteria, preferably via discussions with trial center personnel and www.clinicaltrials.gov.
- Referral recommendations are informed through direct contact with institutions conducting studies to ensure trial availability and patient eligibility.
- Consider clinical trial participation when no effective standard of care exists or when comparing standard care with a promising new approach.
- Adjuvant therapy trials may be appropriate for high-risk recurrence patients post-primary treatment.
- Clinicians should review available treatment options and eligibility criteria preferably through discussions with personnel at the trial center and review of materials at the website www.clinicaltrials.gov.
- Referral recommendations are informed through direct contact with the institutions conducting studies of particular interest to assure trial availability and patient eligibility.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patient eligibility must align with specific trial criteria.
- Disease progression must meet RECIST criteria for RAIR DTC patients to be considered for trials.
- Progression must occur after use of an approved therapy for RAIR DTC patients.
- No effective standard of care must exist or a promising new approach must be under investigation.
