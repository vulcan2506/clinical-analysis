# Clinical Care Fundamentals (19)

This topic highlights the clinical utility of B-scan ultrasonography as a preoperative diagnostic tool for surgical planning in cases where direct ocular visualization is obstructed by media opacities, ensuring accurate assessment of ocular structures.

**Key Features:**
- B-Scan Ultrasonography: Recommended for retinal imaging when direct visualization is impaired
- Preoperative Planning: Utilized as a diagnostic tool to assess ocular structures prior to surgery
- Media Opacity Management: Effective in cases involving dense cataracts or vitreous hemorrhage

**Notable Details:**
- Indicated for patients with hazy media
- Essential for T1DM patients with impaired visualization
- Bypasses limitations of direct ophthalmoscopy in dense media
- Provides real-time imaging of posterior segment structures
- Non-invasive and does not require pupillary dilation
- Critical for diagnosing retinal detachment in obscured media
- Supports surgical planning for intraocular lens placement
- Useful in trauma cases with suspected globe rupture
