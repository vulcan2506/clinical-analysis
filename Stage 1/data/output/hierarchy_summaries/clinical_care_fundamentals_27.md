# Clinical Care Fundamentals (27)

Standardized metadata for academic journals ensures persistent digital access, cross-referencing, and centralized information dissemination across print and online formats through unique identifiers and structured bibliographic data.

**Key Features:**
- ISSN identifiers for print and online versions — unique serial numbers distinguishing journal formats
- DOI hyperlink for direct article access — persistent digital object identifier enabling stable links
- Journal website as the primary digital hub — central platform for submissions, updates, and metadata
- Volume, issue, and page numbers — structured bibliographic data for citation and retrieval

**Notable Details:**
- Dual ISSN entries required for print and online formats to maintain format-specific identification
- DOI provides persistent digital access independent of URL changes or journal migrations
- Journal website URL typically includes ISSN or journal title for direct navigation
- Metadata standards (e.g., Crossref, NISO) enforce consistency in volume/issue/page formatting
- ISSN is assigned by the ISSN International Centre and is globally recognized
- DOIs are registered through agencies like Crossref or DataCite for interoperability
- Volume and issue numbers follow a sequential numbering system for traceability
- Page numbers adhere to standard pagination formats (e.g., 123-145) for citation accuracy
