# Clinical Governance

These governance features establish a structured framework to identify, categorize, and mitigate potential conflicts of interest (COIs) within clinical committees, ensuring transparency and scientific integrity through mandatory disclosures, financial thresholds, and leadership oversight.

**Key Features:**
- Mandatory Industry Relationship Disclosure: Committee members must report all financial ties, including ownership, consulting, research funding, and institutional benefits.
- COI Relevance Categorization: Disclosures are classified as 'RELEVANT' or 'NOT RELEVANT' based on predefined financial thresholds.
- Financial Relationship Reporting: Includes honoraria, royalties, expert testimony, speaking engagements, and advisory board positions.
- Leadership Oversight and Adjudication: The CGC Chair and GDP Chairs review and determine the relevance of disclosed conflicts.
- Chair Veto Power: The CGC Chair maintains final authority and veto power over conflict of interest determinations.
- Annual Disclosure and Attestation: Mandatory annual submission of all actual or perceived conflicts of interest by all Work Group members.
- Comprehensive Industry Relationship Disclosure: Requirement to disclose all committee members' industry relationships, including those deemed non-relevant.

**Notable Details:**
- Ownership threshold for relevance is ≥5%
- Financial value threshold for relevance is ≥$5000
- Includes disclosure of leadership positions with relevant entities
- Categorization process is based on specific financial thresholds
- Significant financial relationship threshold: Ownership ≥5% of voting stock
- Significant financial relationship threshold: Fair market value ≥$5,000
- Significant financial relationship threshold: Funds exceeding 5% of gross income
- Chair Veto Power applies to all conflict of interest determinations

### Conflict of Interest Management

Disclosure of all financial relationships (e.g., honoraria, royalties, expert testimony, speaking engagements, advisory boards) and leadership positions with relevant entities.. Review and determination of COI relevance by CGC Chair and GDP Chairs, with CGC Chair having veto power.

### Source 1: Endocrine_Society_Guideline_Methodology_links.pdf

**Guideline Development Component:** Conflict of Interest Management

**Process Requirements:**
- Disclosure of all financial relationships (e.g., honoraria, royalties, expert testimony, speaking engagements, advisory boards) and leadership positions with relevant entities.
- Review and determination of COI relevance by CGC Chair and GDP Chairs, with CGC Chair having veto power.
- Recusal of GDP members with relevant COI from discussions, votes, drafting recommendations, and clinical questions related to their conflict.
- Divestment of direct financial investments and abstention from marketing activities or advisory boards of entities with potential financial interest in the guideline.
- Transparency through detailed COI documentation in published guideline appendices and supplemental materials.
- Members are obliged to report un-declared COIs observed in others to the GDP Chair.
- GDP members are prohibited from determining the strength and direction of a recommendation directly related to their COI.

**Mandatory Standards:**
- Chair and Co-Chair of the Guideline Development Panel must be free of any relevant COI.
- A majority (>50%) of GDP members must be free of relevant COI.
- GDP members must declare new industry relationships to GDP chairs and CGC Chair for assessment before acceptance.
- GDP members with relevant COI must recuse themselves from all related activities.
- Disclosure of COI must occur at every in-person meeting, most conference calls, and via annual COI questionnaires.
- Immediate family members of GDP members must also divest direct financial investments with entities having potential financial interest in the guideline.

### KDIGO conflict of interest policy

KDIGO requires all Work Group members to complete, sign, and submit an annual disclosure and attestation form for all relationships that might be perceived as or are actual conflicts of interest.

### Source 1: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf

**Clinical Recommendation:** KDIGO conflict of interest policy

**Clinical Actions:**
- KDIGO requires all Work Group members to complete, sign, and submit an annual disclosure and attestation form for all relationships that might be perceived as or are actual conflicts of interest.
- All reported conflict of interest information is published in full in the Work Group members' Disclosure section and maintained on file at KDIGO.
- KDIGO makes every effort to avoid actual or reasonably perceived conflicts of interest arising from outside relationships or personal, professional, or business interests.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Work Group members must annually update and submit disclosure forms to KDIGO.
- Disclosure information must be published in its entirety in the Work Group members' Disclosure section.
