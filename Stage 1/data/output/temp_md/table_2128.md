|  Characteristic | T1DM | Slowly evolving immune-mediated diabetes of adults  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  Age at diagnosis | Childhood to adolescence, rarely in adulthood | > 35 years  |
|  Body mass index | Underweight to normal | Normal to overweight  |
|  Onset | Acute | Rarely acute  |
|  Autoimmunity | Severely increased | Increased  |
|  Ketosis | Frequent | Rare  |
|  Insulin dependence | At onset | > 6 months after onset  |
|  Insulin resistance | No change | Increased or no change  |
