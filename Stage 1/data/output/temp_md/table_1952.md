|   | Type 1 diabetes | Type 2 diabetes  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  Age at diagnosis | Usually childhood and adolescence, but can occur in adults as well | Usually postpubertal; most common in middle to later age groups  |
|  Diabetes in 1^{st} degree relative | Unusual | Common  |
|  Severe osmotic symptoms/ Ketosis at diagnosis | Can occur | Rare  |
|  Markers of insulin resistance | Absent | Present  |
|  C-peptide assay | Absence of beta-cell reserve | Preserved beta-cell reserve  |
|  Pancreatic autoantibodies | Present | Absent  |
