# reagent and consumable inventory

**Test Method:** reagent and consumable inventory

**Performance Characteristics:**
- Inventory includes manufacturer-provided materials from Roche Diagnostics for electrolyte testing.

**Pre-analytical Requirements:**
- All cartridges (Sodium, Potassium, Chloride, Reference) must be available and within expiration dates for testing.
- All cartridges must be stored at controlled temperature (2-8°C) and protected from light until use
