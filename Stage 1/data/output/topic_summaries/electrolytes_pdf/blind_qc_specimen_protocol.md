# blind QC specimen protocol
*Source: electrolytes.pdf*

**Test Method:** blind QC specimen protocol

**Performance Characteristics:**
- Blind QC specimens can be inserted into the mix of patient specimens with identical labels.
- One blind QC specimen is randomly selected for concentration and included at a randomly selected location in every 20 specimens analyzed.
- Open label blind QC specimens can be used if at least 6 different pools are available and analyte concentrations are similar to patient samples.

**Pre-analytical Requirements:**
- Blind QC specimens must be prepared at two levels encountered in patient samples.
- Open label blind QC specimens require selection from at least 6 different pools with similar analyte concentrations.
