# data reporting protocol
*Source: electrolytes.pdf*

**Test Method:** data reporting protocol for electrolyte testing

**Performance Characteristics:**
- Test results that are not abnormal are reported to the collaborating agency at a frequency and by a method determined by the study coordinator
- Data from this analysis are compiled with results from other analyses and sent to the responsible person at the collaborating agency as an ASCII text file or Excel file
- Data are generally sent through electronic mail or via ftp site
- For NHANES 1999+, all data are reported electronically weekly to Westat who then transfer the results to NCHS
- For some smaller studies, hard copies of a data report are sent, as well as the results in electronic format
