# electrolyte analyte clinical applications
*Source: electrolytes.pdf*

**Test Method:** electrolyte analyte clinical applications

**Performance Characteristics:**
- Sodium measurements are used in the diagnosis and treatment of aldosteronism, diabetes insipidus, adrenal hypertension, Addison's disease, dehydration, and inappropriate antidiuretic hormone secretion.
- Potassium measurements are used to monitor electrolyte balance in conditions characterized by low or high blood potassium levels.
- Chloride measurements are used in the diagnosis and treatment of electrolyte and metabolic disorders such as cystic fibrosis and diabetic acidosis.
- Urine electrolytes (sodium, potassium, chloride) are primarily used as nutritional indicators in healthy persons and infrequently measured in clinical settings.
- Urine electrolyte data is more important for public health studies than for direct disease diagnosis.
- Urine sodium is specifically noted as an important biomarker of dietary sodium intake, highlighting its role in public health assessments rather than individual clinical diagnosis.
