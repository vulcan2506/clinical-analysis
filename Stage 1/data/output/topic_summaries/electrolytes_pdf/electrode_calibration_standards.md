# electrode calibration standards
*Source: electrolytes.pdf*

**Test Method:** electrode calibration standards and reagent handling

**Performance Characteristics:**
- ISE Reference Electrolyte, ISE Diluent Gen2, and ISE Internal Standard Gen2 are ready-to-use liquid reagents from Roche Diagnostics.
- Reagents are stable up to expiration at 15–25°C; electrode stability varies (Na+/K+/Cl-: 2 months or 9000 tests; Reference: 6 months).
- Slope ranges for newly installed electrodes must be in the upper half of recommended ranges (excluding chloride).
- Calibration requires low and high ISE standards (STD 1: low, STD 2/3: high) and full calibration every 24 hours or after specific events (e.g., electrode replacement, reagent change).
- Calibration verification uses NIST SRM 2201 (NaCl) and NIST SRM 2202 (KCl) at least twice yearly.
- Electrodes should be replaced when slope readings indicate it is needed, even if the fixed time period (2 months for Na+/K+/Cl-, 6 months for Reference) has not yet expired

**Pre-analytical Requirements:**
- Reagent line caps must not be tightened; ensure filter reaches bottle bottom without obstruction.
- Electrodes must be replaced after on-board stability limits (2 months for Na+/K+/Cl-, 6 months for Reference).
- Full calibration is mandatory after ISE cleaning, reagent bottle change, electrode replacement, or major maintenance.
- Calibration verification requires international reference materials (NIST SRMs).
- Assay must adhere strictly to manufacturer specifications; no parameter alterations allowed.
- Calibration verification must be conducted using NIST SRM 2201 (Sodium Chloride) and NIST SRM 2202 (Potassium Chloride) at least twice yearly
