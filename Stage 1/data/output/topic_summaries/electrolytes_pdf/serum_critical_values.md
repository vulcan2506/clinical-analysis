# serum critical values
*Source: electrolytes.pdf*

**Test Method:** serum critical values reporting and follow-up

**Performance Characteristics:**
- Serum critical limits are defined for Sodium (≤120 and ≥158 mmol/L), Potassium (≤2.8 and ≥6.2 mmol/L), and Chloride (≤75 and ≥126 mmol/L).
- Abnormal reports are automatically forwarded to the NCHS survey physician for NHANES studies.
- For non-NHANES studies, abnormal values are reported to the study principal investigator.
- Emails concerning abnormal results are maintained by the supervisor for the duration of the study.
