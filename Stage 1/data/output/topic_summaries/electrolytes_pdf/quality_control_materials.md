# quality control materials
*Source: electrolytes.pdf*

**Test Method:** quality control materials for electrolyte testing

**Performance Characteristics:**
- Quality control materials for serum Na+, K+, and Cl- are lyophilized and require reconstitution with 3.0 mL of diluent.
- Reconstituted serum controls are stable for 5 days at 2-8°C or 4 weeks at -15 to -25°C when frozen once.
- Unopened serum controls are stable at 2-8°C until the expiration date.
- Urine Na+, K+, and Cl- controls are commercially prepared, liquid, and ready for use.
- Urine controls have a stability of 36 months at 2-8°C or until the expiration date, whichever comes first.

**Pre-analytical Requirements:**
- Carefully open one bottle of lyophilized serum control to avoid loss of lyophilizate.
- Dissolve reconstituted serum controls by gentle swirling within 30 minutes, avoiding foam formation.
- Use commercially prepared liquid urine controls directly without reconstitution.
- Pipette exactly 3.0 mL of diluent into the lyophilized serum control bottle.
- Dissolve lyophilized serum control contents completely by occasional gentle swirling within 30 minutes.
