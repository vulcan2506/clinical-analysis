# specimen data management
*Source: electrolytes.pdf*

**Test Method:** specimen data management for NHANES and non-NHANES studies

**Performance Characteristics:**
- LIMS database is used to keep records and track specimens for NHANES 1999+ studies
- Records for non-NHANES studies may be kept in Excel files on the DLS network
- All personal identifiers are restricted to the medical supervisor or project coordinator
- Residual serum from non-NHANES studies may be discarded, transferred to CDC CASPIR, or autoclaved after NHANES analyses
- Analyst maintains a notebook for specimens with preparation errors, labeling issues, or abnormal results
- Copies of study protocols or interagency agreements for specimen tracking procedures are kept by the supervisor

**Pre-analytical Requirements:**
- Specimen ID number must be entered into a database upon receipt
- Specimens stored at -70°C
- Specimen ID read via barcode reader for electronic specimen table preparation
- Result file loaded into the database and linked to specimen ID after analysis
- Study-specific tracking procedures defined in study protocol or interagency agreement
- Specimens must be stored at -70°C in a freezer
