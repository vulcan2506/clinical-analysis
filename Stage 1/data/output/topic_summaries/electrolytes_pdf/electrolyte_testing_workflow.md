# electrolyte testing workflow
*Source: electrolytes.pdf*

**Test Method:** electrolyte testing workflow including calibration, QC, and patient sample handling

**Performance Characteristics:**
- One run is defined as 100 patient samples or less, with controls run at the beginning and end of each run.
- For small or confirmation runs, all levels of QC pools must be run in duplicate.
- Backup all previous test results and clear the 'data review' screen before starting a new run.
- Visually inspect samples for unusual volume, color, or debris/precipitate before pipetting.
- Break wood applicator pieces to pop air bubbles in sample cups if present.
- Pipette 20-25 patient samples at a time and immediately place the racks on the input buffer tray to minimize evaporation

**Pre-analytical Requirements:**
- Calibration and QC checks must be completed before pipetting patient samples.
- Calibrators, QC, and patient samples must be thoroughly mixed before pipetting.
- Sample racks must be measured within 2 hours of loading due to evaporation risks.
- Do not load samples if the green light on the input tray is flashing.
- Use specific racks: black for calibrators, white for QC, and gray for patient samples.
