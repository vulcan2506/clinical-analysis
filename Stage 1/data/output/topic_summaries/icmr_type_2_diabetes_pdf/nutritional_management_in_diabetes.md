# nutritional management in diabetes
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Nutritional management during illness in diabetes

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Modify diabetic diet by adjusting food consistency and texture during fever or illness
- Encourage semi-solid foods and fluids to maintain adequate calorie intake
- Recommend thin soups, milk, buttermilk, or fresh lime juice as suitable options

**Prerequisites:**
- Patient must have diabetes and be experiencing fever or other acute illness
- Patient must be experiencing acute illness (e.g., fever) with associated reduced oral intake or gastrointestinal symptoms
