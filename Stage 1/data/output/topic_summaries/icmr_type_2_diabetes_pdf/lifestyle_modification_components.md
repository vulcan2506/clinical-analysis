# lifestyle modification components
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** lifestyle modification components

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Improve health through optimum nutrition
- Maintain glycemic control and optimum blood lipid levels
- Achieve optimal physical activity and individualized meal plans
- Advise behavioral changes for smoking, tobacco, and alcohol cessation
- Advocate stress management and individualized diet based on complications
- Encourage consumption of high-fiber carbohydrates (e.g., whole grains, legumes, oats, barley) and low-GI fruits to manage glycemic response

**Prerequisites:**
- Nutritional assessment including metabolic, nutritional, and lifestyle parameters
- Setting practical, achievable, and individualized goals
- Nutritional intervention with education and individualized meal plans
- Evaluation of goal achievement and necessary adjustments
- Diet planning based on age, sex, physical activity, height, weight, BMI, and cultural factors
- Consider increased caloric needs during pregnancy, lactation, and recovery from illness for individualized meal planning
