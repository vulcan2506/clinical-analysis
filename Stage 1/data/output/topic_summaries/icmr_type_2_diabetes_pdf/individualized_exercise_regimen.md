# individualized exercise regimen
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** individualized exercise regimen

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Regular physical activity and regulated exercise are essential components of type 2 diabetes management
- Exercise program must be individualized based on patient's ability and capacity
- Complete evaluation of patients with diabetes is required before recommending an exercise program
- Consume extra quick-acting carbohydrate snack before and during prolonged exercise sessions
- Follow 150 minutes of aerobic activity or equivalent per week with resistance training at least twice weekly and flexibility exercises
- Exercise program should include flexibility exercises to maintain agility and prevent musculoskeletal complications

**Prerequisites:**
- Complete evaluation of patients with diabetes before exercise program initiation
- Individualization of exercise program based on patient's ability and capacity
- Complete evaluation of patients with diabetes must assess cardiovascular risk, musculoskeletal status, and presence of complications (e.g., neuropathy, retinopathy) before exercise program initiation
