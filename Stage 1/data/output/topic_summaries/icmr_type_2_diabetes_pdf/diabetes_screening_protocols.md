# diabetes screening protocols
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Diabetes screening protocols in high-risk populations

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Screening should be performed in all individuals >30 years of age.
- Screening should be carried out at an earlier age in adults with risk factors (e.g., family history, overweight/obese, hypertension, dyslipidaemia).
- Screening can be done via fasting plasma glucose, OGTT, random plasma glucose, or HbA1c (with limitations in India).
- Retesting should be done after 3 years for normal glucose tolerance and annually for prediabetes.
- Diagnosis requires confirmation by a repeat test on a different day (unless unequivocal hyperglycemia).
- Screening should include assessment of cardiovascular disease (CVD) risk factors alongside diabetes screening.

**Prerequisites:**
- Age >30 years for general screening.
- Risk factors such as family history, overweight/obese, hypertension, or dyslipidaemia for earlier screening.
- Symptoms present for random plasma glucose testing.
- Confirmation of diagnosis via repeat testing unless unequivocal hyperglycemia.
- HbA1c limitations in India due to local constraints.
- History of gestational diabetes or macrosomia (birth weight > 3.5 kg) as a risk factor for earlier screening.
- History of polycystic ovarian syndrome and/or acanthosis nigricans as a risk factor for earlier screening.
