# injectable therapy categories
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** injectable therapy categories for type 2 diabetes

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Use insulin therapy as a key injectable option for type 2 diabetes management
- Consider non-insulin injectable therapy (GLP-1 receptor agonists) for patients requiring injectable therapy
- Select injectable therapy based on patient-specific factors such as glycemic control, comorbidities, and risk of hypoglycemia
- Consider initiating injectable therapy (insulin or GLP-1 receptor agonists) in patients with marked hyperglycemia (e.g., HbA1c ≥9% or fasting plasma glucose ≥250 mg/dL) despite optimal oral therapy
- Use GLP-1 receptor agonists as first-line injectable therapy in patients with type 2 diabetes and established cardiovascular disease or high cardiovascular risk, unless contraindicated

**Prerequisites:**
- Prioritize oral anti-hyperglycemic drugs before considering injectable therapy unless marked hyperglycemia or contraindications exist
- Assess patient eligibility for GLP-1 receptor agonists based on cardiovascular risk and renal function
- Assess patient’s renal function (eGFR) before initiating GLP-1 receptor agonists, as some agents require dose adjustments or are contraindicated in severe renal impairment
- Evaluate patient’s body weight and hypoglycemia risk when selecting between insulin and GLP-1 receptor agonists
