# dietary fiber and protein recommendations
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** dietary fiber and protein recommendations

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Fiber intake of 40 g/day (2000 Kcals) is recommended for the general population
- Traditional Indian diets with whole grains, whole pulses, green leafy vegetables, and fruits (e.g., papaya, guava, apples) are recommended
- All fruit juices should be avoided
- Proteins should provide 12-15% of total energy intake for people with diabetes
- Protein sources should include vegetable proteins (pulses, soy, grams, peas), low-fat dairy, fish, and lean meats

**Prerequisites:**
- Fruit intake should be in moderation
- Saturated fats (SFA) should be ≤10% of energy intake, or 7% in those with raised blood lipid levels
- Polyunsaturated fats (PUFA) should be 10% of energy intake with n6: 3-7% and n3: >1% energy
- Monounsaturated fatty acids (MUFA) should be 10-15% of energy intake
- Trans fats should be <1% of energy intake and preferably avoided
