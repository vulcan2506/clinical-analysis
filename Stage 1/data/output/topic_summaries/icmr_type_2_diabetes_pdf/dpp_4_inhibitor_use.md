# DPP-4 inhibitor use
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** DPP-4 inhibitor use

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Inhibit DPP-4 enzyme to increase incretin hormones (GLP-1 and GIP), stimulating glucose-dependent insulin secretion and suppressing glucagon release
- Do not cause hypoglycemia as monotherapy and are weight-neutral
- Used as monotherapy, second-line with metformin, or in combination with other oral antihyperglycemic agents or insulin
- Most administered as a single daily dose except vildagliptin (twice daily)
- Dosage reduction required in renal insufficiency except for linagliptin, teneligliptin, and gemigliptin (with caution in advanced renal disease)
- Gliptins have a duration of action ranging from 3 hours (vildagliptin) to 24 hours (sitagliptin), influencing dosing frequency and patient adherence

**Prerequisites:**
- Presence of type 2 diabetes mellitus requiring glycemic control
- Renal function assessment prior to initiation (except for linagliptin, teneligliptin, gemigliptin)
- Consideration of combination therapy with metformin as preferred second-line option
- Monitoring for renal function during therapy (adjust dose as needed)
- Assessment of hypoglycemia risk when combined with sulfonylureas or insulin
- Assessment of predominant mode of excretion (renal vs. hepatic) for drug selection in patients with mixed hepatic-renal dysfunction
