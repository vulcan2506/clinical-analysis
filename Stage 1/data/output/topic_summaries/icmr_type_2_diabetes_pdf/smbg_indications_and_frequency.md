# SMBG indications and frequency
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** SMBG indications and frequency

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Perform SMBG in all people with diabetes on insulin
- Perform SMBG in patients with brittle diabetes
- Perform SMBG in patients prone to ketosis or recurrent hypoglycemia
- Perform SMBG in patients with hypoglycemia unawareness
- Perform SMBG whenever tight control is indicated (e.g., pregnancy, acute illness, complications)

**Prerequisites:**
- Individualize SMBG frequency based on clinical need
- Frequency of SMBG should be individualised based on clinical need, with greater frequency specifically recommended during pregnancy
