# ICMR type 2 diabetes guidelines
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** ICMR type 2 diabetes guidelines 2018

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Emphasize individualized glycemic targets based on patient profile
- Prioritize lifestyle modifications (diet and physical activity) as first-line therapy
- Recommend metformin as the first-line oral hypoglycemic agent unless contraindicated
- Encourage early insulin initiation in patients with marked hyperglycemia or severe symptoms
- Advocate for regular monitoring of HbA1c, lipid profile, and renal function

**Prerequisites:**
- Baseline HbA1c and fasting plasma glucose for diagnosis confirmation
- Assessment of cardiovascular risk factors and comorbidities
- Renal function evaluation before initiating metformin
- Patient education on self-monitoring of blood glucose
- Regular follow-up at 3–6 monthly intervals
