# albuminuria investigation algorithm
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** albuminuria investigation algorithm

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Follow a stepwise investigation protocol for albuminuria/proteinuria in diabetes
- Assess albuminuria using spot urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio (ACR) as the preferred method
- Confirm persistent albuminuria with at least two of three positive tests over 3–6 months
- Evaluate for other causes of proteinuria if non-diabetic kidney disease is suspected
- Integrate albuminuria assessment into routine diabetes care for risk stratification
- Perform first-morning void urine sample collection for spot UACR to minimize variability from postural changes or exercise

**Prerequisites:**
- Person with diabetes as the primary population for screening
- Use spot urine ACR for initial assessment
- Repeat testing over 3–6 months to confirm persistent albuminuria
- Assess kidney function (e.g., eGFR) alongside albuminuria
- Consider referral to nephrology if significant proteinuria or rapid eGFR decline
- Ensure urine sample is tested within 2 hours of collection or stored refrigerated if delay is unavoidable to prevent sample degradation
