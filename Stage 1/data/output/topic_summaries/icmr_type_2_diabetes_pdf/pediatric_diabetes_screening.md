# pediatric diabetes screening
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Pediatric diabetes screening criteria

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Screen children and adolescents aged 18 years and below who are overweight (weight >120% of ideal body weight).
- Screen if any of the following risk factors are present: family history of type 2 diabetes in first-degree relatives, signs of insulin resistance (acanthosis nigricans), hypertension, dyslipidemia, or polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS).
- Screen children and adolescents with a history of maternal gestational diabetes during pregnancy.

**Prerequisites:**
- Patient must be aged 18 years or younger.
- Patient must be overweight (weight >120% of ideal body weight).
- Presence of at least one risk factor: family history of type 2 diabetes, acanthosis nigricans, hypertension, dyslipidemia, or PCOS.
- Screening should be performed using fasting plasma glucose, HbA1c, or oral glucose tolerance test (OGTT) as per standard diagnostic criteria.
