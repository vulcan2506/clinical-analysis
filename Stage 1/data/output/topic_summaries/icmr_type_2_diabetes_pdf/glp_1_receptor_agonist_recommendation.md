# GLP-1 receptor agonist recommendation
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** GLP-1 receptor agonist recommendation

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- GLP-1 receptor agonists reduce hyperglycemia with minimal risk of hypoglycemia
- GLP-1 receptor agonists promote weight loss
- GLP-1 receptor agonists can be combined with all anti-hyperglycemic agents except DPP-4 inhibitors
- GLP-1 receptor agonists improve cardiovascular outcomes beyond glycemic effects
- GLP-1 receptor agonists should be taken at the same time every day

**Prerequisites:**
- GLP-1 receptor agonists are resistant to DPP-4 degradation, requiring longer half-life consideration
- Monitor for nausea, vomiting, and rare cases of acute pancreatitis
- GLP-1 receptor agonists can be taken at any time of day, but preferably at the same time every day
