# alternative antihyperglycaemic agents
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** alternative antihyperglycaemic agents in India

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Hydroxychloroquine and bromocriptine are approved as antihyperglycaemic agents in India
- Role of hydroxychloroquine and bromocriptine in type 2 diabetes therapeutic algorithm requires further study
- Saroglitazar is licensed only for diabetic dyslipidaemia in India, not as an antihyperglycaemic agent
- Hydroxychloroquine and bromocriptine are approved for antihyperglycaemic use in India despite primary indications for other conditions (rheumatoid arthritis for hydroxychloroquine, Parkinson's disease for bromocriptine)
