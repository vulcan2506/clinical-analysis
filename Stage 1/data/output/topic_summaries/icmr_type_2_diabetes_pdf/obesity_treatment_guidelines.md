# obesity treatment guidelines
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Obesity treatment approach

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Weight reduction should be attempted in obese individuals
- Lifestyle modification is the mainstay of treatment
- Drugs may be used judiciously as an adjuvant therapy
- Bariatric surgery may be an option in morbid obesity
- Weight loss goals should be individualized based on patient characteristics, comorbidities, and risk-benefit assessment

**Prerequisites:**
- Obesity diagnosis (BMI ≥30 kg/m² or higher)
- Patient readiness for lifestyle modification
- Assessment of comorbidities and risk factors
- Prior trial of non-pharmacological interventions (if applicable)
- Documentation of failed attempts at lifestyle modification prior to considering pharmacotherapy (unless contraindicated)
