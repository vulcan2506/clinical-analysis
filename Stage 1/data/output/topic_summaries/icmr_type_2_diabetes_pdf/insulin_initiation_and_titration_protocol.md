# insulin initiation and titration protocol
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** insulin initiation and titration protocol

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Initiate insulin on outpatient basis without hospitalization
- Start with small doses and titrate every three days based on blood glucose profile
- Begin with 0.2 units/kg/day insulin dose, individualized to patient needs
- Use two or more insulin injections when single injection plus oral agents fail to maintain control
- Prefer premixed NPH and regular insulin regimens (e.g., 25:75, 30:70, 50:50) before breakfast and dinner
- Modify insulin dose every three days based on blood glucose profile

**Prerequisites:**
- Combination of oral antihyperglycaemic agents no longer maintains desired glycemic control
- Blood glucose profile monitoring for dose adjustment
- Careful monitoring in older persons to avoid hypoglycemia
- Individualize insulin dose based on blood glucose profile and clinical setting
