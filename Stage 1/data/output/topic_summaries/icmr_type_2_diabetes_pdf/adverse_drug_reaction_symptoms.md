# adverse drug reaction symptoms
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** adverse drug reaction symptoms

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Monitor for gastrointestinal problems including nausea, diarrhoea, and stomach pain
- Assess for flu-like symptoms such as headache, runny nose, and sore throat
- Evaluate for skin reactions characterized by painful skin followed by a red or purple rash
- Check for joint pains as a potential adverse reaction
