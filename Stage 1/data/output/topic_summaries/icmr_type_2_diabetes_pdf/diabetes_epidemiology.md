# diabetes epidemiology
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** diabetes epidemiology and risk profile in India

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Diabetes is no longer confined to older, affluent, or urban populations in India
- Rural prevalence of diabetes is increasing rapidly
- Type 2 diabetes is now affecting children and adolescents
- The 'Asian Indian phenotype' shows higher insulin resistance and diabetes prevalence for any given BMI compared to white Caucasians
- Type 2 diabetes accounts for 90-95% of all diabetes cases in India

**Prerequisites:**
- Use ICMR-INDIAB study data for state-wise and urban-rural prevalence assessment
- Classify diabetes into Type 1, Type 2, gestational, and other types for accurate diagnosis
- Differentiate Type 1 and Type 2 diabetes based on clinical features (e.g., age at diagnosis, family history, insulin dependence)
