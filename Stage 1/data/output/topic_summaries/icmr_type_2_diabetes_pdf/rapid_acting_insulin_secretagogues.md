# rapid-acting insulin secretagogues
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** rapid-acting insulin secretagogues (repaglinide, nateglinide)

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Administer with each meal due to rapid absorption (0.5-1 hr) and short half-life (<1 hr)
- Enhance insulin secretion via non-sulphonylurea receptor binding sites on β-cells
- Useful for managing postprandial hyperglycemia
- Produce fewer and milder hypoglycemic episodes and side effects compared to sulfonylureas

**Prerequisites:**
- Must be taken with meals to align with rapid insulin release
- Use in mild renal insufficiency requires supervision
- Elderly patients require supervision when using these agents
- Must be used with caution in patients with hepatic impairment due to potential altered drug metabolism
