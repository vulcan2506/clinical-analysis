# ICMR NCD leadership
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** ICMR NCD leadership profile

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Leads development and dissemination of national non-communicable disease (NCD) guidelines
- Oversees implementation of evidence-based clinical practice guidelines for type 2 diabetes management
- Coordinates with Ministry of Health and Family Welfare for policy integration
- Drives research prioritization in NCD prevention and control
- Ensures alignment of ICMR guidelines with global health standards
- Publishes periodic updates to national NCD guidelines based on emerging evidence and epidemiological trends
- Facilitates multi-stakeholder consultations (e.g., clinicians, patient groups, NGOs) during guideline development

**Prerequisites:**
- Scientific expertise in NCD management
- Regulatory authority under Department of Health Research
- Collaboration with state health departments
- Evidence-based guideline development process
- National health policy integration framework
- Mandatory inclusion of cost-effectiveness analyses for all recommended interventions in guideline development
- Requirement for state-level adaptation of ICMR guidelines to local epidemiology and healthcare infrastructure
