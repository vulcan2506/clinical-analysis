# pregnancy diabetes management
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Pre-gestational diabetes management in pregnancy

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Achieve tight glycemic control (preferably HbA1C < 6.5%) before planned pregnancy
- Provide folic acid supplementation
- Change antihypertensive agents to calcium channel blockers or alpha methyldopa
- Discontinue ACEIs, ARBs, diuretics, and statins
- Conduct pre-pregnancy baseline evaluation of eyes and kidney function

**Prerequisites:**
- Planned pregnancy
- Preconception checkup completed
- Patient has pre-existing type 1 or type 2 diabetes
