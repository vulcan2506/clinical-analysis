# bedtime insulin addition strategy
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** bedtime insulin addition strategy

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Add intermediate, long, or short-acting insulin at bedtime or morning based on blood glucose profile
- Continue oral drugs at optimal doses when adding insulin
- Reduce or stop secretagogues when premixed or multiple-dose insulin is used
- Consider reducing or stopping sulfonylureas when adding bedtime insulin to minimize hypoglycemia risk

**Prerequisites:**
- Glycemic control not achieved with maximum tolerable dose of single or combination oral agents
- Blood glucose profile must guide timing (bedtime vs. morning) of insulin addition
