# diabetic retinopathy management
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Diabetic retinopathy screening and management

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Record visual acuity and perform retinal examination via direct ophthalmoscopy in all diabetic patients
- Obtain retinal photography whenever possible
- Refer patients to a retina specialist for evaluation, treatment, and follow-up when services are available
- Educate patients on the risk of blindness from diabetes and the need for regular eye examinations and blood glucose control
- Compare retinal findings with standard DR images or grading charts if specialist services are unavailable
- Screen patients for other causes of visual loss (e.g., cataract, glaucoma, macular degeneration) alongside diabetic retinopathy assessment

**Prerequisites:**
- All diabetic patients must undergo baseline eye examination at diagnosis (Type 2) or within 5 years of diagnosis (Type 1)
- Pregnant women with diabetes require retinal examination prior to conception and during pregnancy, with early referral to a retina specialist
- Patients with long diabetes duration, poor glycemic control, hypertension, or nephropathy are at higher risk and require closer monitoring
- Visual acuity testing and intraocular pressure measurement should precede retinal examination
- Patients with impaired vision (<6/60) should undergo low vision evaluation and rehabilitation
- Vision testing and intraocular pressure measurement must precede retinal examination in all patients
