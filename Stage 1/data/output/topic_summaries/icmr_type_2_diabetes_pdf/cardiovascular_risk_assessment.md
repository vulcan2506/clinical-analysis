# cardiovascular risk assessment
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** cardiovascular risk assessment in T2DM

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Identify and treat CVD risk factors such as hypertension, dyslipidemia, tobacco use, and sedentary lifestyle in patients with type 2 diabetes
- Screen for albuminuria as part of cardiovascular risk stratification in patients with type 2 diabetes

**Prerequisites:**
- Use the Framingham Risk Score or ASCVD risk calculator adapted for Indian population for formal cardiovascular risk assessment
