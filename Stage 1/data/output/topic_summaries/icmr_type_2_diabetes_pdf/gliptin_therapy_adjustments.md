# gliptin therapy adjustments
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** gliptin therapy adjustments

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Dose of sitagliptin, vildagliptin, and saxagliptin should be reduced in renal impairment
- Teneligliptin and gemigliptin should be used with caution in advanced renal disease
- Linagliptin can be continued at unchanged doses in renal impairment
- Saxagliptin should be avoided in cardiac failure
- Monitor for acute pancreatitis and allergic reactions to gliptins

**Prerequisites:**
- Renal function assessment required before and during gliptin therapy
- Cardiac evaluation for patients considered for saxagliptin
