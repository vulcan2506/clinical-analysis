# monofilament testing protocol
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** monofilament testing protocol for diabetic foot assessment

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Apply the monofilament perpendicular to the skin's surface
- Apply sufficient force to cause the filament to bend or buckle
- Maintain total duration of approach, contact, and departure at approximately 1-2 seconds
- Avoid sliding the filament across the skin or making repetitive contact at the test site
- Do not test on ulcer sites, calluses, scars, or necrotic tissue

**Prerequisites:**
- Use a 10 gram force monofilament
- Test along the perimeter of the foot, not directly on lesions
- Test selected sites must include plantar surface of the great toe, first, third, and fifth metatarsal heads, and the plantar surface of the heel
