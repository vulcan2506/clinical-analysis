# sulphonylurea therapy protocol
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** sulphonylurea therapy protocol

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Initiate therapy with the lowest effective dose and titrate upwards every two weeks until desired control or maximal dosage is reached
- Administer sulphonylureas preferably 15 to 30 minutes before meals
- Combine sulphonylureas with all other anti-hyperglycaemic agents except glinides and other sulphonylureas
- Monitor for hypoglycaemia, especially with older and long-acting sulphonylureas like glibenclamide in the elderly
- Consider switching sulphonylureas in individual cases if benefit is observed, though it may not be long-lasting
- Monitor for rare but serious skin reactions (e.g., Stevens-Johnson syndrome, photosensitivity reactions, or granulomatous hepatitis) during sulphonylurea therapy

**Prerequisites:**
- Evaluate non-responsiveness factors such as weight gain, non-compliance to diet/exercise/drugs, inadequate dosage, impaired absorption, co-existing endocrine disorders, concomitant medications, infections, or stress
- Avoid sulphonylureas in patients with type 1 diabetes, renal insufficiency, hepatic insufficiency, ketoacidosis, acute myocardial infarction, extensive infections, or history of adverse reactions
- Assess for co-existing endocrine disorders (e.g., thyroid disease) when evaluating non-responsiveness to sulphonylureas
