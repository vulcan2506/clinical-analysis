# pioglitazone safety profile
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** pioglitazone safety profile

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Weight gain may be significant and dose-related
- Edema and cardiac failure are reported, especially when combined with insulin
- Other adverse events include anemia and hemodilution
- Higher risk of fractures, especially in post-menopausal women
- Bladder cancer has been reported with prolonged use
- Macular edema has been reported, particularly in patients with pre-existing retinopathy

**Prerequisites:**
- Type 1 diabetes exclusion
- History of cardiac failure exclusion
- Pregnancy and lactation exclusion
- Moderate to severe anemia exclusion
- History of bladder cancer or unexplained hematuria exclusion; known risk factors for bladder cancer must be assessed before starting pioglitazone
- Assess liver function tests (ALT, AST) before and periodically during treatment due to potential hepatotoxicity
