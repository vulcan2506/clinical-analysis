# TB-diabetes comorbidity screening
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** TB-diabetes comorbidity screening

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Maintain a high index of suspicion for TB in all patients with diabetes
- Include chest X-ray in initial evaluation of diabetes patients where clinically indicated
- Implement bidirectional screening for TB and diabetes in routine healthcare settings
- Adopt an integrated approach for prevention, early detection, and management of TB-diabetes comorbidity

**Prerequisites:**
- Clinically indicated chest X-ray for diabetes patients during initial evaluation
- Routine bidirectional screening protocols for TB and diabetes in healthcare settings
