# postpartum diabetes screening protocol
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Postpartum diabetes screening protocol for GDM

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Perform a 2-hour OGTT with 75g glucose 6-8 weeks postpartum for reclassification of diabetes status
- Repeat annual screening if initial postpartum OGTT is normal

**Prerequisites:**
- History of gestational diabetes mellitus (GDM)
- Postpartum period (6-8 weeks after delivery)
