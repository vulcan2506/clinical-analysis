# repaglinide and nateglinide dosing
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** repaglinide and nateglinide dosing

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Administer repaglinide 0.5–6 mg/day in 3 divided doses with meals
- Administer nateglinide 60–360 mg/day in 3 divided doses with meals
- Both drugs have a short duration of action (~1–1.5 hours)
- Repaglinide is predominantly excreted via feces (90%)
- Nateglinide is predominantly excreted via urine (83%)

**Prerequisites:**
- Must be taken with meals to optimize postprandial glucose control
- Dose adjustments may be required based on renal function (especially for nateglinide)
- Dose adjustments for repaglinide may be required in severe hepatic impairment (not just renal function)
