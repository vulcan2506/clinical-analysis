# metformin dosing and safety
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** metformin dosing and safety

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Dose adjustment based on estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR)
- Full dose if eGFR > 60 ml/min
- Continue full dose with frequent monitoring if eGFR 45–60 ml/min
- Halve dose if eGFR < 45 ml/min
- Stop metformin if eGFR < 30 ml/min
- Initiate metformin at a lower starting dose (e.g., 500 mg once or twice daily) in patients with eGFR 45–60 ml/min to minimize risk of lactic acidosis

**Prerequisites:**
- Renal function assessment via eGFR before and during metformin therapy
- Monitoring of renal function more frequently if eGFR is 45–60 ml/min
- Assess renal function (eGFR) prior to metformin initiation and at least annually thereafter, or more frequently if eGFR is 45–60 ml/min or in patients at risk for renal decline
