# diabetes comorbidity assessment
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Comorbidity assessment in diabetes

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Assess for inflammatory bowel disease in patients with diabetes and unexplained gastrointestinal symptoms or malabsorption.
- Evaluate liver function and screen for cirrhosis in diabetic patients with abnormal liver enzymes or hepatomegaly.
- Monitor for malabsorption syndromes in diabetic patients with persistent diarrhea, weight loss, or vitamin deficiencies.
- Screen for moderate to end-stage renal disease in diabetic patients with declining eGFR, albuminuria, or electrolyte imbalances.
- Assess for celiac disease in diabetic patients with persistent diarrhea, unexplained weight loss, or iron/B12 deficiency, particularly in type 1 diabetes.

**Prerequisites:**
- Documentation of diabetes diagnosis (type 1 or type 2) with confirmed HbA1c ≥6.5% or fasting glucose ≥126 mg/dL.
- Presence of gastrointestinal, hepatic, or renal symptoms warranting further evaluation.
- Baseline laboratory tests including liver enzymes, renal function (eGFR, creatinine), and vitamin levels if malabsorption is suspected.
- Documentation of family history of celiac disease or autoimmune conditions in patients undergoing celiac screening.
