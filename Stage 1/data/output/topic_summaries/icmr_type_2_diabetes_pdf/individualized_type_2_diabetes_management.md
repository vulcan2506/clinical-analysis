# individualized type 2 diabetes management
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** individualized type 2 diabetes management

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Treatment should be individualized due to the heterogeneous nature of type 2 diabetes
- Encourage healthy lifestyle choices such as increasing physical activity and diet modification
- Lifestyle changes alone may suffice for glycemic target attainment in some cases
- Most patients will require pharmacotherapy to achieve glycemic goals
- Use a suggested algorithm for selecting antihyperglycaemic agents based on class advantages and disadvantages

**Prerequisites:**
- Assess patient-specific factors to tailor treatment approach
- Evaluate patient readiness and ability to adopt lifestyle modifications
- Refer to a table of class-specific advantages and disadvantages of antihyperglycaemic agents (Figure 7.2)
