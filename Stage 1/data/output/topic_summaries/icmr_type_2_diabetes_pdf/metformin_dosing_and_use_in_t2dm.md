# metformin dosing and use in T2DM
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** metformin dosing and use in T2DM

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Start with 500 mg after meals and increase by 500 mg every two weeks to reach maximum 2500 mg
- Use sustained release preparations and administer after meals to minimize gastrointestinal side effects
- Stop metformin at least 24 hours before elective major surgery or use of radiocontrast media
- Withdraw metformin immediately if any contraindications occur
- Monitor for vitamin B12 deficiency with prolonged use
- Administer metformin after meals to minimize gastrointestinal side effects
- Use sustained release preparations to reduce gastrointestinal side effects

**Prerequisites:**
- Absence of renal insufficiency
- Absence of hepatic insufficiency
- Absence of respiratory insufficiency and other hypoxemic conditions
- Absence of acute myocardial infarction or congestive cardiac failure
- Absence of alcohol abuse, ketoacidosis, or severe infections
- Absence of lactic acidosis risk factors (e.g., conditions predisposing to hypoxia or tissue hypoperfusion)
