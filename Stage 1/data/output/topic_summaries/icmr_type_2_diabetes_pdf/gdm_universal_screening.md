# GDM universal screening
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** GDM universal screening and management

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Ideally all pregnant women in India should be screened for GDM via universal screening
- Screening is ideally done at the first antenatal visit to rule out pre-existing diabetes
- GDM screening at second trimester (24-28 weeks) uses OGTT with IADPSG criteria
- Lifestyle modification is the initial management option for women with gestational diabetes
- All women with GDM should undergo an OGTT 6 weeks after delivery to reclassify glycemic status
- Screening at the first trimester uses single-point tests (fasting glucose, random glucose, or HbA1C) specifically to rule out pre-existing diabetes, with thresholds indicating pre-existing diabetes (≥126 mg/dl fasting, ≥200 mg/dl random, or ≥6.5% HbA1C)

**Prerequisites:**
- Use OGTT with 75 gm glucose load using IADPSG criteria (Fasting ≥92, 1hr ≥180, 2hr ≥153 mg/dl)
- If fasting state is not possible, use DIPSI guidelines with non-fasting 2hr post glucose ≥140 mg/dl
- Oral antihyperglycemic agents are best avoided in management
- Most patients will need insulin therapy
- Post-delivery reclassification requires OGTT at 6 weeks
- A fasting plasma glucose between 92-125 mg/dl at the first trimester screening is considered as GDM according to IADPSG criteria
