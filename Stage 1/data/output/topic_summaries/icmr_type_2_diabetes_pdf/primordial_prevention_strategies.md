# primordial prevention strategies
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Primordial Prevention Strategies for Diabetes

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Reduce or prevent obesity to lower future risk of diabetes
- Address rapid epidemiological transition due to urbanization and lifestyle changes
- Identify individuals at risk for targeted preventive measures
- Encourage healthy diet, adequate exercise, and weight control at every healthcare visit
- Consider metformin in high-risk prediabetic individuals (especially Indians with combined IFG and IGT) when lifestyle measures alone are insufficient

**Prerequisites:**
- Regular screening of individuals at risk
- Holistic care beyond glycemic control, including attention to blood pressure and lipids
- Regular screening of individuals with prediabetes at every healthcare visit
