# national guideline development
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** national guideline development process

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Developed by ICMR as a joint effort of academic and research institutions
- Expert contributions allocated based on experience
- Draft reviewed in a workshop (10th January 2018, Chennai)
- Public feedback incorporated via ICMR website viewing period
- Aims to provide uniform care across tertiary, secondary, and primary care levels

**Prerequisites:**
- Consensus guidelines required due to high prevalence of Type 2 Diabetes mellitus
- Expert contributions from multiple institutions
- Workshop review and incorporation of suggestions
- Public feedback integration
- Endorsement by ICMR and allied health professionals
