# dietary protein management in diabetic nephropathy
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** dietary protein management in diabetic nephropathy

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Recommended protein intake is 0.6 g/kg of ideal body weight plus 24-hour urinary protein loss if significant
- Protein intake should not be less than 40 g/day
- Protein restriction advised for patients with increased creatinine in consultation with nephrologist
- Sodium intake varies from 1000 mg to 2000 mg/day based on fluid status and serum sodium levels
- Potassium restriction may be required based on blood potassium values and type of diuretic used

**Prerequisites:**
- Protein intake calculation must account for 24-hour urinary protein loss if significant
- Protein restriction in increased creatinine requires nephrologist consultation
- Sodium restriction must be tailored to individual fluid status and serum sodium levels
- Potassium restriction decisions must consider blood potassium levels and diuretic type
