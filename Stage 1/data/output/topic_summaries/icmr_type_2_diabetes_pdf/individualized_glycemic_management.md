# individualized glycemic management
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Individualized glycemic management

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Establish individualized glycemic targets rather than relying solely on general guidelines.
- Implement tight glycemic control in early diabetes stages, particularly for young patients to prevent complications.
- Revise glycemic targets based on patient condition when complications arise or in elderly populations.
- Apply strict glycemic control during pregnancy with specific fasting and postprandial targets.
- Monitor using either 1-hour or 2-hour postprandial blood glucose values as clinically indicated.

**Prerequisites:**
- Patient age and stage of diabetes (early vs. late/complicated).
- Presence of established cardiovascular disease, smoking, obesity, hypertension, or family history of premature CVD.
- Pregnancy status requiring specific glucose thresholds.
- Patient-specific factors including life expectancy and hypoglycemia risk.
- Availability of clinical data for blood glucose, blood pressure, BMI, waist circumference, and lipid profiles.
