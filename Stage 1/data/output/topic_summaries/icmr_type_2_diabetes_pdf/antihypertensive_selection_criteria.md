# antihypertensive selection criteria
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** antihypertensive selection criteria

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Use ACE inhibitors or ARBs as first-line drugs (never combine both)
- Use calcium channel blockers, diuretics, or selective beta blockers if ACEIs/ARBs are not tolerated
- Administer selective beta blockers in patients with known previous acute coronary syndrome
- Avoid ACEIs/ARBs in patients with serum potassium > 5.5 meq/L
- Avoid ACEIs/ARBs in patients with serum creatinine > 1.5 mg%
- Use diuretics (preferably loop diuretics) for volume control in patients with fluid overload or oliguria

**Prerequisites:**
- Presence of severe/resistant hypertension
- Presence of proteinuria (nephrotic range or otherwise)
- eGFR < 60 ml/min (calculated by MDRD)
- Pregestational diabetes or GDM with proteinuria
- Presence of fluid overload or oliguria
- Presence of nephrotic range proteinuria
- Proteinuria in absence of retinopathy
