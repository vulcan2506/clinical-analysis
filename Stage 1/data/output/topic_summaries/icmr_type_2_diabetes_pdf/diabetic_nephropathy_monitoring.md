# diabetic nephropathy monitoring
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** diabetic nephropathy monitoring

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Perform urine microscopy to assess casts, pus cells, and RBCs
- Measure blood urea and serum creatinine levels
- Calculate estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR)
- Screen for microalbuminuria
- Conduct USG abdomen if feasible
- Perform urine dipstick test for proteinuria as an initial screening step

**Prerequisites:**
- Availability of laboratory facilities for urine microscopy and serum chemistry
- Access to ultrasound equipment for abdominal imaging
- Patient eligibility for feasible imaging studies
- Ensure standardized laboratory protocols for urine sample collection (e.g., first-morning void for microalbuminuria)
