# pioglitazone therapy regimen
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** pioglitazone therapy regimen

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Pioglitazone is an insulin sensitizer acting at adipose tissue and skeletal muscle via PPAR-γ binding
- Dose range: 7.5 to 30 mg once daily
- Onset of action: 2–4 weeks; maximum effect: 8–12 weeks
- Can be combined with all antihyperglycemic agents except insulin (use cautiously)
- May induce ovulation in women with anovulatory cycles; provide contraceptive advice
- Inhibits hepatic glucose output in addition to improving insulin sensitivity at adipose tissue and skeletal muscle

**Prerequisites:**
- No specific clinical prerequisites mentioned beyond standard antihyperglycemic therapy considerations
- Provide contraceptive advice to women with anovulatory cycles due to potential ovulation induction
