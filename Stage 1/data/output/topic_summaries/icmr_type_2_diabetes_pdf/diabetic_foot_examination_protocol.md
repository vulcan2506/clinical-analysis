# diabetic foot examination protocol
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Diabetic foot examination protocol

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Perform detailed foot exam including palpation of dorsalis pedis and posterior tibial pulses, monofilament and vibration perception testing (VPT)
- Provide foot care education to all patients with diabetes (e.g., daily foot inspection, avoiding barefoot walking, proper foot hygiene)
- Refer patients with foot deformity, absent pedal pulses, or ulceration to specialist assessment within 24 hours
- Commence antibiotics immediately for clinical signs of foot infection; admit urgently for severe/limb-threatening infection
- Use Continuous Glucose Monitoring (CGM) or intermittently scanned CGM (iSCGM) for advanced monitoring in cases of recurrent hypoglycemia or severe glucose fluctuations
- Perform retinal check-up (fundus examination for retinopathy) as part of basic clinical exam
- Perform blood urea/serum creatinine tests as part of basic clinical exam

**Prerequisites:**
- Patient with diabetes mellitus
- Presence of foot deformity, absent pedal pulses, or ulceration for specialist referral
- Clinical signs of infection for antibiotic initiation
- Recurrent hypoglycemia or severe glucose fluctuations for CGM use
- Patient-specific factors (e.g., life expectancy, cognitive function) for individualized care
- Presence of recurrent hypoglycemia, severe glucose fluctuations, disparity in measured glucose values and HbA1c, severe anemia, hemoglobinopathy, or hypoglycemia unawareness for advanced monitoring with CGM or iSCGM
- Presence of clinical signs of severe/limb-threatening infection for urgent admission and intravenous antibiotic therapy
