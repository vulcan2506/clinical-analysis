# diabetic ketoacidosis management
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** diabetic ketoacidosis management

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Monitor glucose levels
- Check for urine ketones
- Start intravenous saline infusion at 1 litre in half hour immediately
- Refer to hospital
- Administer intravenous insulin after initial fluid resuscitation
- Monitor serum potassium levels and correct hypokalemia before starting insulin therapy
- Check arterial blood gases (pH, bicarbonate) to confirm DKA diagnosis

**Prerequisites:**
- Ensure continuous cardiac monitoring during initial fluid resuscitation
- Confirm absence of contraindications to insulin therapy (e.g., severe hypokalemia)
