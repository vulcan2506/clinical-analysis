# foot disease risk stratification
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** foot disease risk stratification

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Categorize feet into Low risk, At Risk, or Active Foot Disease based on clinical features
- Perform annual foot examination for low-risk patients
- Increase frequency of foot assessment to every 6 months or more for high-risk patients
- Examine footwear and inspect skin, nails, and structural deformities
- Record presence of toe deformities, bunions, high arch foot, or Charcot foot
- Record visual impairment status as part of basic foot screening

**Prerequisites:**
- Assess peripheral sensation using 10g monofilament
- Evaluate arterial supply via pedal pulses
- Review history of diabetes-related foot complications
- Include medical history in risk categorization
- Examine for foot deformities as part of basic screening
- Assess vibration sensation in addition to 10g monofilament for peripheral sensation evaluation
