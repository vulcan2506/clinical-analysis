# pioglitazone dosage and pharmacokinetics
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** pioglitazone dosage and pharmacokinetics

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Administer pioglitazone once daily
- Maintain daily dosage within 7.5–30 mg range
- Expect duration of action between 16–24 hours

**Prerequisites:**
- Monitor renal function due to partial urinary excretion
- Monitor hepatic function due to hepatobiliary excretion pathway
