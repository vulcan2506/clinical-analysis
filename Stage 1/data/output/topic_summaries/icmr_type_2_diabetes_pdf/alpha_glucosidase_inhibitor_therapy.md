# alpha-glucosidase inhibitor therapy
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** alpha-glucosidase inhibitor therapy

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Ingest drug with the first bite of food to ensure presence in the small bowel
- Start with minimum dose at any one meal and increase to maximum tolerable dose
- Use oral glucose rather than sucrose to treat hypoglycaemia in combination therapy
- Utilize agents to decrease post-prandial glucose levels
- Combine with all other oral and injectable anti-hyperglycaemic agents

**Prerequisites:**
- Patient must be able to take medication with the first bite of every meal
- Dose titration must begin at the minimum recommended dose (e.g., 25mg for acarbose/miglitol, 0.2mg for voglibose)
- Monitoring for gastrointestinal side effects including bloating, abdominal discomfort, diarrhoea, and flatulence
- Assessment of renal function if using miglitol due to predominant renal excretion
- Evaluation of potential for hypoglycaemia when used in combination with other agents
