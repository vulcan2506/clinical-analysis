# insulin storage guidelines
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** insulin storage guidelines

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Store insulin not in use in the lower compartment of the refrigerator door at 2-8° C
- Avoid freezing insulin or exposing it to direct sunlight/heat
- Prevent excess agitation to maintain insulin potency
- Use earthenware pot of water (in a plastic bag) for storage if refrigeration is unavailable
- Discard regular insulin if hazy or if cloudy insulin cannot be re-suspended
- Insulin should not be kept in the freezer compartment of the refrigerator

**Prerequisites:**
- Refrigeration required for insulin not in use
- Storage temperature must be maintained between 2-8° C
- Insulin not in use must be refrigerated in the lower compartment of the refrigerator door
