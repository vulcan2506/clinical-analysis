# ICMR guideline revision
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** ICMR guideline revision process

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Guidelines revised based on latest developments in type 2 diabetes management over the past decade
- Expert group constituted by ICMR to review and modify old guidelines
- Document circulated for feedback from extended group of researchers and practitioners
- Final guidelines published after multiple rounds of iteration and expert workshop
- Feedback solicited from readers for future guideline improvements

**Prerequisites:**
- Expert group formation by ICMR for guideline revision
- Multiple rounds of iteration and expert review
- Workshop held to finalize changes (ICMR Guidelines for Management of Type 2 Diabetes 2018)
- Document placement on ICMR website for public feedback
- Ratification by extended group of researchers and practitioners
- Ratification of final guidelines by circulation to an extended group of researchers and practitioners drawn from all over India
