# complication screening protocols
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** complication screening protocols

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Monitor blood glucose (FPG and 2 hr PPPG) at least once a month, more frequently if targets are not met
- Measure HbA1c at least every 6-12 months, every 3 months if targets are not met or tight control is attempted
- Perform clinical examination during every visit, minimum every 3 months
- Screen for long-term complications (retinopathy, nephropathy, neuropathy, PVD, CAD) at least annually, more often if needed
- Optimize weight, waist circumference, blood pressure, and lipids

**Prerequisites:**
- Dilated retinal examination by trained ophthalmologist at diagnosis and annually thereafter
- Kidney function assessment at diagnosis and annually using urine albumin-creatinine ratio, serum creatinine, and eGFR
- Comprehensive foot examination at diagnosis and annually focusing on neuropathy and peripheral arterial disease
- Urine examination for ketones if blood glucose exceeds 400 mg/dl
- Urine examination for estimation of ketones if blood glucose exceeds 400 mg/dl
