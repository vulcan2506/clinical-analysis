# insulin mixing protocol
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** insulin mixing protocol

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Administer mixtures of short and intermediate insulin for better glycemic control in some patients
- Inject mixed insulin immediately after preparation
- Clean hands and injection site before each injection
- Wipe vial tops with spirit before drawing insulin
- Roll cloudy insulin vials gently to re-suspend insulin (avoid shaking)
- Use palm rolling (not shaking) to re-suspend cloudy insulin vials before mixing

**Prerequisites:**
- Draw air equal to the dose into the vial before drawing insulin to avoid vacuum
- Inject sufficient air into both vials before drawing mixed doses
- Draw clear rapid or short-acting insulin first when mixing in the same syringe with NPH
- Inject air equal to the dose into the vial before drawing insulin to avoid vacuum formation
