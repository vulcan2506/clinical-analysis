# GDM management protocols
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** GDM management protocols

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Exercise and medical nutritional therapy is the first step and initiated in all women with GDM
- Insulin is the preferred drug of choice in diabetes and pregnancy
- Monitor fetal growth and maternal blood pressure besides glycemic control
- Use fasting, 1-hour, and 2-hour postprandial glucose targets for monitoring

**Prerequisites:**
- Target fasting plasma glucose <90 mg/dl
- Target 1-hour postprandial glucose <140 mg/dl
- Target 2-hour postprandial glucose <120 mg/dl
