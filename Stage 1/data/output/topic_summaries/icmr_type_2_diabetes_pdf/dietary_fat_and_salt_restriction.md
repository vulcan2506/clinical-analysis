# dietary fat and salt restriction
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** dietary fat and salt restriction in T2DM

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Restrict salt intake and maintain optimal body weight
- Encourage fruits and vegetables consumption with good-quality fats in moderation
- Restrict saturated and trans fats (e.g., vanaspati, butter, ghee, margarine, coconut oil, red meats, egg yolk, whole milk products)
- Use healthy oils and fiber-rich foods
- Limit alcohol to reduce triglycerides

**Prerequisites:**
- Dietary management must be accompanied by regular physical activity and exercise
