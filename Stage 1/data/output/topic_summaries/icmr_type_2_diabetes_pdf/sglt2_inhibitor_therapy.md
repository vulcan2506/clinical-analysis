# SGLT2 inhibitor therapy
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** SGLT2 inhibitor therapy

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Inhibit SGLT2 in the proximal convoluted tubule to induce glycosuria
- Reduce blood glucose, weight, and blood pressure
- Provide cardiovascular benefits beyond glucose lowering
- Monitor for genital mycotic infections, urinary tract infections, polyuria, and volume depletion in elderly
- Assess risk of euglycemic ketoacidosis and lower extremity amputations (especially with canagliflozin)

**Prerequisites:**
- Presence of type 2 diabetes mellitus
- Adequate renal function (eGFR criteria as per guideline)
- No history of recurrent genitourinary infections
- No active diabetic ketoacidosis
- No hypersensitivity to SGLT2 inhibitors
