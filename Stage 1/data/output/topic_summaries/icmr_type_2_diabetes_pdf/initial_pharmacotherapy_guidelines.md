# initial pharmacotherapy guidelines
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** initial pharmacotherapy and lifestyle management

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Provide education regarding the disease and its management to all patients
- Lifestyle modification, including healthy dietary practices and sufficient physical activity, should be the first line of management
- Metformin is the initial pharmacotherapy of choice for most patients unless contraindicated or patient has symptomatic hyperglycemia
- Aim to keep HbA1c below 7% in most patients, with individualized treatment targets

**Prerequisites:**
- Specific contraindications or tolerance issues must be considered before initiating metformin
- Symptomatic hyperglycemia may warrant initial pharmacotherapy other than metformin
- Specific contraindications for metformin include renal impairment (eGFR <30 mL/min/1.73 m²), severe hepatic disease, or conditions predisposing to lactic acidosis
