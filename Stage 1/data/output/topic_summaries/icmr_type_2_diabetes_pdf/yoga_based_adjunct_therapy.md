# yoga-based adjunct therapy
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** yoga-based adjunct therapy for type 2 diabetes

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Incorporate yoga as a complementary therapy in holistic diabetes management
- Use asanas (postures), pranayama (breath control), and dhyana (meditation) as core components
- Aim to improve glycemic control, lipid profiles, and body fat content
- Target reduction in oxidative stress, blood pressure, and medication doses
- Enhance pulmonary and autonomic function, mood, sleep, and quality of life

**Prerequisites:**
- Integrate yoga as an adjunct to standard diabetes care, not as a replacement
- Ensure patient suitability and safety before initiating yoga-based interventions
- Clinicians should assess patient suitability and safety before initiating yoga-based interventions, considering individual health status and comorbidities
