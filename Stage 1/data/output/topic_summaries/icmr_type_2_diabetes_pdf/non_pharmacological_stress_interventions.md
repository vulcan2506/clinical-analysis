# non-pharmacological stress interventions
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** non-pharmacological stress interventions

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Practice stress management techniques such as meditation, yoga, outdoor walks, exercise, and hobbies like reading, gardening, or painting
- Engage in yoga under expert guidance for therapeutic benefits in physical and mental health

**Prerequisites:**
- Stress management should be tailored to individual preferences and capabilities
- Expert guidance is required for yoga practice
- Stress management should be culturally appropriate and contextually relevant to the Indian setting
