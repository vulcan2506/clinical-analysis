# glycemic control targets
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Glycemic control targets

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Glycemic targets should be individualized based on patient-specific factors such as age, comorbidities, and hypoglycemia risk.
- A1C targets are typically <7% for most adults with diabetes, but may be adjusted for specific populations.
- Glycemic targets during pregnancy aim for tighter control to reduce maternal and fetal risks.

**Prerequisites:**
- Patient-specific factors (e.g., life expectancy, cognitive function, hypoglycemia risk) must be assessed before setting targets.
- Pregnancy requires preconception counseling and stricter glycemic targets to minimize complications.
