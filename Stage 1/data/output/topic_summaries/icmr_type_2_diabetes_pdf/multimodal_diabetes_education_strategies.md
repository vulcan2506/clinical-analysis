# multimodal diabetes education strategies
*Source: ICMR Type 2 Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** multimodal diabetes education strategies

**Therapeutic Actions:**
- Empower people with diabetes with knowledge and tools to become active partners in their management
- Provide in-depth information about diabetes, its complications, and treatment
- Teach appropriate self-care skills and resources
- Foster a positive attitude toward diabetes management
- Train in self-monitoring skills
- Incorporate culturally tailored educational content to address diverse regional and community contexts in India

**Prerequisites:**
- Compliance of people with diabetes is essential for effective management
- Education programmes must be delivered by trained healthcare professionals or certified diabetes educators
