# Radioiodine Therapy Efficacy Studies
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Radioiodine Therapy Efficacy Studies + Tg-positive, RAI-negative metastases management

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Empiric high-dose 131I therapy lacks efficacy in Tg-positive, RAI-negative patients with follicular cell-derived thyroid cancer.
- Heterogeneous tumor response to radioactive iodine therapy observed in bone metastases from differentiated thyroid cancer.
- Improved survival reported with multimodal approaches including 131I treatment in patients with bone metastases secondary to differentiated thyroid cancer.
- Serum thyroglobulin concentrations often decline for years after total thyroidectomy and radioactive remnant ablation without additional therapy.
- Long-term outcome studies show benefits and limits of radioiodine therapy in patients with distant metastases from papillary and follicular thyroid carcinoma.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Documented elevated serum thyroglobulin levels.
- Negative diagnostic whole-body 131I scan.
- Structurally identifiable metastatic follicular cell-derived thyroid carcinoma.
- Post-therapy 131I whole-body scan positivity.
- Patient suitability for high-dose radioiodine therapy based on risk stratification.
