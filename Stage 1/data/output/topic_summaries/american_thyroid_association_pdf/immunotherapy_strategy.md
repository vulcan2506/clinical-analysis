# Immunotherapy Strategy
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Immunotherapy Strategy in RAIR DTC

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Consider pembrolizumab for RAIR DTC patients with tumor mutational burden-high disease that has progressed on standard treatments (e.g., kinase inhibitors).
- Couple immune checkpoint inhibitors (ICIs) with VEGFR multi-kinase inhibitors (MKIs) to potentially enhance efficacy based on preclinical and early clinical data.
- Evaluate lenvatinib plus pembrolizumab in treatment-naïve progressive RAIR DTC and in patients progressing on lenvatinib, noting mixed efficacy results.
- Explore novel immunotherapy combinations, including chimeric antigen receptor T cell therapy, in ongoing clinical trials for RAIR DTC.
- Consider pembrolizumab for RAIR DTC patients with tumor mutational burden-high disease in the presence of a measurable lesion, provided the TMB-high status is confirmed by an FDA-approved test.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Tumor mutational burden-high status must be confirmed by an FDA-approved test.
- Disease progression on standard treatments (e.g., first- and second-line kinase inhibitors) must be documented.
- Patient eligibility for combination therapy (e.g., VEGFR MKI + ICI) should be assessed based on clinical trial criteria.
- Tumor mutational burden-high status must be determined by an FDA-approved test before considering pembrolizumab.
