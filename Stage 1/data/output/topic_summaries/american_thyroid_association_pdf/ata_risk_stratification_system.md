# ATA Risk Stratification System
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** ATA Risk Stratification System for DTC

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Uses histopathologic features, cervical lymph node count, AJCC staging, postoperative imaging, and serum Tg/TgAb testing to determine risk of structural disease persistence/recurrence or survival in DTC patients.
- Incorporates molecular profiling data if available, but does not recommend routine postoperative molecular profiling.
- Evaluates specific DTC features (e.g., tumor focality, vascular invasion) to refine individual risk stratification.
- Combines 2025 ATA Risk of Recurrence categories with AJCC/UICC 8th edition staging and lymph node ratio (LNR) for accurate risk prediction.
- Replaces the 2015 three-tiered system with a more nuanced approach incorporating additional high-risk features.
- Uses lymph node ratio (LNR) in combination with ATA Risk of Recurrence categories and AJCC staging for improved predictive accuracy of structural disease persistence/recurrence.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Histopathologic evaluation of tumor and cervical lymph nodes must be performed postoperatively.
- Postoperative imaging and serum Tg/TgAb testing (if appropriate) are required for risk assessment.
- AJCC/UICC 8th edition staging system must be used in conjunction with ATA Risk Stratification.
- Risk stratification should occur within ~3 months of surgery.
- Molecular profiling data, if obtained, must be integrated into the 2025 ATA Risk Stratification System.
- Risk stratification must occur within ~3 months of surgery for accurate integration with postoperative imaging and serum Tg/TgAb testing results.
