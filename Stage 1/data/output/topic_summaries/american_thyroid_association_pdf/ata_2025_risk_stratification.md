# ATA 2025 Risk Stratification
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** ATA 2025 Risk Stratification for PTC, FTC, and OTC

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Risk stratification is based on tumor type (PTC, FTC/IEFVPTC, OTC) and completeness of surgical resection (R0, R1, R2).
- Lateral neck dissection is performed only for clinical evidence of metastatic involvement in Levels IIA, III, IV, and VB.
- Prophylactic lateral neck dissection is not recommended in cN0 patients.
- Thyrotropin suppression therapy is tailored to risk of recurrence and response to therapy.
- 131I (RAI) administration is categorized as remnant ablation, adjuvant therapy, or therapeutic treatment based on residual disease status.
- Lymph node metastases are uncommon in OTC and FTC/IEFVPTC compared to PTC, influencing the need for prophylactic or therapeutic dissection.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Preoperative or intraoperative detection of metastatic nodes is required for lateral neck dissection.
- R0 resection (microscopically negative margins) is the goal of surgery for completeness of resection.
- Total or near-total thyroidectomy is required for RAI administration (remnant ablation, adjuvant therapy, or therapeutic treatment).
- Clinical evidence of metastatic disease in specific lymph node compartments (Levels I, IIB, VA) may justify extended dissection.
- Risk stratification must account for tumor type (PTC, FTC/IEFVPTC, OTC) and WHO 2022 definitions.
- Dissection of Levels I, IIB, and VA may be considered based on findings suggestive of metastatic disease in these compartments, even if not routinely performed.
