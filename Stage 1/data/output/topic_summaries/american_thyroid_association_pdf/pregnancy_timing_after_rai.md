# Pregnancy Timing After RAI
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Pregnancy timing after RAI therapy

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Wait at least 6 months after RAI therapy before becoming pregnant to avoid increased congenital malformations (OR 1.74 [CI 1.01–2.97])
- Avoid RAI therapy in recently lactating patients
- Defer thyroid cancer surgery during pregnancy unless clinically urgent, as most tumors show no significant progression
- If surgery is required during pregnancy, perform in the second trimester
- Monitor thyroxine dose requirements closely in pregnant women with hypothyroidism or athyreosis

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Pregnancy should be delayed ≥6 months after RAI therapy to minimize obstetrical complications
- TSH levels should be maintained in the reference range during pregnancy to avoid preterm delivery risks
- Serial neck ultrasounds are recommended for pregnant women with thyroid cancer to monitor tumor progression
- TSH levels should be maintained in the reference range during pregnancy to avoid increased preterm delivery risk (OR 2.14)
