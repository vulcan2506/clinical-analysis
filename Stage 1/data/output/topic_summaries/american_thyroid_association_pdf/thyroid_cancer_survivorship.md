# Thyroid Cancer Survivorship
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Thyroid Cancer Survivorship Concerns

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Cancer survivorship begins at diagnosis and continues throughout life, encompassing patients free of disease, those with treatment sequelae, and those living with active tumor.
- Quality of life (QoL) in thyroid cancer survivors is influenced by multiple aspects beyond treatment side effects or uncontrolled cancer.
- Long-term survivorship concerns are specifically related to the thyroid cancer diagnosis itself, excluding management of hypothyroidism.
- Survivorship concerns include fertility and reproductive health impacts, particularly in premenopausal women undergoing radioactive iodine (RAI) therapy, which may require counseling and monitoring.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Survivorship care plans should be individualized based on the patient's specific treatment history (e.g., extent of thyroidectomy, RAI dose, TSH suppression duration) and risk of recurrence.
