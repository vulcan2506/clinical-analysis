# Contrast Imaging and Risk Stratification
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Contrast Imaging and Risk Stratification in DTC

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Determine imaging needs on an individual patient basis considering risks of intravenous contrast administration.
- Accurate staging is critical for prognosis and tailored treatment in DTC, even with distant metastatic disease.
- Preoperative molecular testing may guide extent of surgery in select cT2N0 DTC patients when clinical/radiographic features are unclear.
- Molecular risk categories (low, intermediate, high) correlate with recurrence risk and may influence surgical decisions.
- BRAF-like tumors are associated with extrathyroidal extension, lymph node metastases, and multifocality.
- Consideration of 18F-fluorodeoxyglucose-PET imaging for select high-risk DTC patients as part of preoperative risk stratification.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patient-specific assessment of imaging needs due to contrast risks.
- Presence of distant metastatic disease does not negate the need for thyroidectomy.
- Molecular testing should be performed in selected patients where extent of surgery is unclear.
- Tumor size and molecular risk profile must be considered for recurrence prediction.
- Clinical trials are preferred for molecular testing in very large invasive DTCs.
- Exclusion of T3a tumors from molecular risk-stratified analysis due to potential limitations in validation for minimal extrathyroidal extension.
