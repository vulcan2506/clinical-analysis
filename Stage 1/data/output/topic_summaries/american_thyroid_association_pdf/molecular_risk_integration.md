# Molecular Risk Integration
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Molecular Risk Integration + Sex + BMI + ATA Response Criteria

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Molecular risk of recurrence should be considered alongside ATA Risk of Recurrence categories, postoperative Tg levels, and imaging.
- Sex is not an independent prognostic factor for DTC recurrence and is excluded from ATA Risk of Recurrence prediction modeling.
- Overweight and obesity may influence DTC outcomes but are not currently part of ATA Risk of Recurrence predictors.
- ATA Response Criteria should be used to categorize response to surgery prior to determining intensity of additional therapy or monitoring.
- Response to therapy should be assessed within 3 months after resection to inform RAI use and dosing.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Molecular risk of recurrence must be obtained and integrated with ATA Risk of Recurrence categories, postoperative Tg levels, and imaging.
- Postoperative serum Tg level should be measured 6–12 weeks after total thyroidectomy while on thyroid hormone therapy.
- Response to therapy assessment requires clinical, biochemical, and structural evaluation (e.g., Tg, TgAb, neck ultrasound, imaging).
- Excellent response requires no clinical, biochemical, or structural evidence of disease.
- Biochemical incomplete response requires abnormal Tg or rising TgAb levels in the absence of localizable disease.
- Proportion of variance explained values associated with risk stratification systems that incorporate response to therapy variables into revised risk estimates should be significantly higher (62–84%) than those seen with initial classification systems.
