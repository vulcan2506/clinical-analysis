# Third-Line Treatment Framework
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Third-Line Treatment Framework for RAIR Metastatic DTC

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Biopsy of progressive disease is advised when acquired resistance emerges to evaluate for actionable resistant mechanisms.
- Cabozantinib or another multikinase inhibitor (MKI) may be considered in patients previously treated with lenvatinib as first-line therapy.
- Dabrafenib plus trametinib is FDA-approved for solid tumors with BRAFV600E mutation who have progressed on prior treatment, though dabrafenib monotherapy may be considered due to lack of superiority.
- Enrollment in a treatment clinical trial is encouraged when available.
- Other third-line options include lenvatinib, cabozantinib, or sorafenib if not already received.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Progression on or intolerance to first-line therapy for RAIR metastatic DTC.
- Availability of biopsy to evaluate resistant mechanisms.
- Presence of actionable resistant mechanisms or specific mutations (e.g., BRAFV600E) for targeted therapy.
- Prior treatment with lenvatinib for consideration of cabozantinib or other MKIs.
- Clinical trial availability for enrollment consideration.
