# Environmental Exposure and Risk
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Environmental Exposure and Risk + Familial Non-Medullary Thyroid Cancer (FNMTC)

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Obesity is positively associated with papillary thyroid cancer (PTC) and follicular thyroid cancer (FTC).
- Flame retardants (FR), including polybrominated diphenyl ethers (PBDEs), alter thyroid hormone homeostasis and may influence thyroid cancer risk.
- High Ki-67 proliferation index correlates with poor prognosis in well-differentiated thyroid carcinomas, though further validation is needed.
- Familial non-medullary thyroid cancer (FNMTC) is classified as syndromic (e.g., PTEN hamartoma tumor syndrome, familial adenomatous polyposis) or non-syndromic.
- DICER1 mutations are linked to pediatric and adolescent-onset differentiated thyroid carcinoma.
- Cribriform-morular variant of papillary thyroid carcinoma (CMV-PTC) is associated with familial adenomatous polyposis (FAP) and may present distinct clinicopathological features compared to sporadic cases.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patient-specific assessment for obesity-related risk in thyroid cancer.
- Evaluation of environmental exposure history (e.g., flame retardants) in thyroid cancer risk stratification.
- Ki-67 proliferation index assessment may be considered for prognostic stratification in well-differentiated thyroid carcinomas.
- Genetic testing for syndromic FNMTC (e.g., PTEN, APC, DICER1) in patients with familial thyroid cancer.
- Surveillance strategies for individuals with germline mutations (e.g., PTEN, DICER1) as per guideline recommendations.
