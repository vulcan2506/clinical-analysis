# RET-Targeted Therapy
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** RET-Targeted Therapy for RAIR DTC with RET fusions

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- RET-targeted therapy is recommended as first-line treatment for progressive RAIR DTC harboring oncogenic RET fusions.
- Use NGS assays optimized for identifying oncogenic RET gene rearrangements with all possible 5′ fusion partners for biomarker testing.
- Selpercatinib is a highly specific and potent RET kinase inhibitor with limited off-target activity, designed to overcome RET V804M/L resistance mutations and penetrate the CNS.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Progressive radioactive iodine-refractory differentiated thyroid cancer (RAIR DTC) with confirmed oncogenic RET fusion.
- NGS assay must be optimized to detect RET fusions with all possible 5′ partners for accurate treatment decision-making.
- The patient must have progressive disease (radiographical tumor progression) to warrant systemic therapy, though this was not an explicit inclusion criterion in the LIBRETTO-001 trial.
