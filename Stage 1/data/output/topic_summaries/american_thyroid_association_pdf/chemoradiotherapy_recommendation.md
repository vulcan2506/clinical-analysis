# Chemoradiotherapy Recommendation
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Chemoradiotherapy for unresectable or gross residual DTC

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Chemoradiotherapy shows superior locoregional control but cannot be recommended for all patients with gross residual or unresectable DTC due to increased toxicity and no overall survival benefit.
- Randomized clinical trials are needed to investigate the clinical benefits of IMRT with or without concurrent chemotherapy in unresectable locoregional disease or gross residual disease post-thyroidectomy.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patients must have unresectable locoregional disease or gross residual disease post-thyroidectomy.
