# Lenvatinib Therapy Recommendation
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Lenvatinib Therapy Recommendation

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- MKI therapy with lenvatinib or sorafenib is recommended for progressive RAIR DTC without actionable biomarker-linked first-line therapy
- Lenvatinib is the preferred first-line MKI in most cases
- Systemic treatment selection considers gene-specific therapy for RET or NTRK fusion-positive disease before general MKI use
- Second-line therapy is considered only after disease progression if treatment was discontinued due to TRAE
- Imaging monitoring includes initial staging with CT/MRI/PET/CT and consideration of brain MRI to rule out metastases
- Initial staging may involve CT, MRI, and/or PET/CT with consideration of brain MRI to rule out brain metastases
- First imaging and Tg monitoring after 2–6 months; timing thereafter is based on rate of progression and/or development of symptoms

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patient must have progressive RAIR DTC
- No actionable biomarker-linked FDA-approved first-line therapy available
- Disease progression must be defined per RECIST criteria within the past 14 months
- Disease must be measurable by RECIST v1.1
- Prior MKI use is allowed (up to one prior MKI) for lenvatinib trials
- Disease progression must be defined per RECIST criteria within the past 12 months (SELECT trial criteria)
