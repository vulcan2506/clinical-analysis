# Management of Nonresectable Disease
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Management of Nonresectable Disease

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Follow patients with indeterminate Tg response (stimulated <10 ng/mL or rhTSH <5 ng/mL) using thyroid hormone therapy alone.
- Consider empiric or dosimetrically determined RAI therapy (3.7–7.4 GBq) when imaging fails to reveal tumor amenable to directed therapy.
- Administer repeated RAI therapy if persistent nonresectable disease shows objective evidence of significant tumor reduction after initial empiric RAI.
- Use empirical RAI therapy to aid in disease localization or treat unidentified disease when diagnostic WBS and structural imaging are negative.
- Reserve additional treatment for emergence of rising serum Tg levels over time or other evidence of structural disease progression.
- Consider empirical or dosimetrically determined RAI therapy (3.7–7.4 GBq) for two purposes: (i) to aid in disease localization and/or (ii) as therapy for unidentified disease when diagnostic WBS and structural or 18FDG-PET/CT imaging are negative.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Absence of structurally demonstrable disease on initial imaging.
- Stimulated serum Tg <10 ng/mL after thyroid hormone withdrawal or <5 ng/mL with rhTSH for observation.
- Negative diagnostic RAI WBS and structural or 18FDG-PET/CT imaging revealing no disease amenable to directed therapy.
- Objective evidence of significant tumor reduction following initial empiric RAI administration.
- Rapidly rising serum Tg levels indicating clinically apparent disease.
- Serum Tg levels suggesting persistent or recurrent disease but with negative diagnostic RAI WBS and no disease amenable to directed therapy on structural or 18FDG-PET/CT imaging.
