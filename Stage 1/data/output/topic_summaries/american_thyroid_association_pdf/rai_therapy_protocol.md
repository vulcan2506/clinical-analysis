# RAI Therapy Protocol
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** RAI Therapy Protocol

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Radioiodine (RAI) administration should be considered for OTC treatment based on patient-specific risk stratification.
- Patients must be prepared for RAI administration with appropriate thyroid hormone withdrawal or recombinant human TSH (rhTSH) stimulation.
- A low-iodine diet should be prescribed prior to RAI administration to enhance iodine uptake by thyroid tissue.
- RAI administration should be tailored to the specific type of differentiated thyroid cancer (e.g., papillary vs. follicular) and its histological subtype, as efficacy may vary.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patient must meet criteria for RAI therapy based on differentiated thyroid cancer risk assessment.
- Thyroid hormone withdrawal or rhTSH stimulation must be completed prior to RAI administration.
- Low-iodine diet adherence must be confirmed before RAI administration.
- Serum TSH levels must be confirmed to be ≥30 mIU/L (or equivalent rhTSH stimulation achieved) prior to RAI administration to ensure optimal thyroid tissue uptake.
