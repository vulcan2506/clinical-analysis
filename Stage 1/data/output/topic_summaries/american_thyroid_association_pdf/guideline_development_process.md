# Guideline Development Process
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Guideline Development Process for DTC

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluation (GRADE) system used for critical appraisal of evidence and determining data quality
- Guidelines panel maintained editorial independence from the ATA
- Competing interests of task force members pre-vetted, regularly updated, and assessed/managed by ATA leadership
- Patient representative included as a full committee member in all consensus discussions
- Guidelines reviewed and approved by ATA Clinical Practice Guidelines and Statements Committee and ATA Board of Directors (BOD)
- Feedback and suggestions from ATA membership were actively solicited and incorporated into revisions prior to journal submission

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Evidence appraisal using GRADE system
- Pre-vetting and management of competing interests by ATA leadership
- Inclusion of patient representative in consensus discussions
- Review and approval by ATA committees and BOD
- Public review and feedback incorporation prior to journal submission
- Guidelines must be reviewed and approved by the ATA Clinical Practice Guidelines and Statements Committee and the ATA Board of Directors (BOD) before publication
