# Biopsy vs ctDNA Preference
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Biopsy vs ctDNA Preference

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Surgical or core biopsy is preferred over ctDNA analysis for diagnostic confirmation.
- ctDNA analysis may be considered only for patients in whom tumor biopsy is not possible.
- ctDNA analysis is used in gene-specific treatment settings to identify acquired resistance mechanisms when disease progression occurs.
- ctDNA testing is performed via NGS to search for alterations explaining resistance to targeted therapies.
- ctDNA analysis may be used to detect polyclonal resistance mechanisms in progressive disease, where multiple acquired resistance alterations emerge due to tumor heterogeneity.
- ctDNA testing is performed to identify both on-target and off-target resistance mechanisms in gene-specific treatment settings (e.g., NTRK or RET fusions).

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Tumor biopsy is not feasible or contraindicated for ctDNA analysis consideration.
- Patients must have undergone biomarker testing by NGS prior to treatment initiation.
- Disease progression must occur during or after initial response to targeted therapy for ctDNA analysis to be relevant.
- Patients must have documented disease progression during or after an initial response to targeted therapy to justify ctDNA analysis for resistance mechanism detection.
