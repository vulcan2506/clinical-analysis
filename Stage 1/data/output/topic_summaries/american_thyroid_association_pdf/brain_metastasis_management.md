# Brain Metastasis Management
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Brain Metastasis Management

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Resection and/or SBRT are the mainstays of therapy for central nervous system metastases.
- RAI can be considered if central nervous system metastases concentrate RAI.
- SBRT and concomitant glucocorticoid therapy are recommended prior to RAI therapy to minimize TSH-induced tumor size increase and RAI-induced inflammatory response.
- Stereotactic radiation therapy is preferred to whole brain irradiation due to lower toxicity and potential for prolonged life expectancy.
- SBRT is as effective as surgery for patients with few (one to three) brain metastases and can be repeated for new lesions.
- Brain necrosis occurs in less than 10% of cases treated with SBRT and is usually limited and without clinical consequences.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Central nervous system metastases must concentrate RAI for RAI therapy consideration.
- Patients must be assessed for high-risk features and advanced disease stage before treatment planning.
- Brain metastases typically occur in older patients with more advanced disease.
