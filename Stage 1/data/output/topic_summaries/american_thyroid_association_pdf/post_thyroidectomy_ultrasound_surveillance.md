# Post-Thyroidectomy Ultrasound Surveillance
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Post-Thyroidectomy Ultrasound Surveillance

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Ultrasound monitoring is indicated in patients with rising thyroglobulin (Tg), new development of anti-thyroglobulin antibodies (TgAb), concerning physical exam, or symptoms.
- Type and location of imaging depend on the histological type of thyroid cancer and other pathology features.
- Use of neck ultrasound in patients with follicular thyroid cancer (FTC) and oncocytic thyroid cancer (OTC) requires further study.
- Intense ultrasound imaging and surveillance in low-risk patients are associated with increased intervention but no impact on patient survival.
- Cost to detect recurrence in low-risk patients is up to seven times higher than in intermediate- and high-risk patients.
- False-positive findings on ultrasound in low-risk patients with excellent biochemical response are common (e.g., 114 false positives in 171 patients), and most do not correlate with clinically significant disease.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Total thyroidectomy with excellent biochemical response and no suspicious features on imaging for ultrasound monitoring in low-risk patients.
- Rising thyroglobulin (Tg), new development of anti-thyroglobulin antibodies (TgAb), concerning physical exam, or symptoms for imaging indication.
- Excellent biochemical response (e.g., undetectable thyroglobulin) must be confirmed before considering selective ultrasound surveillance in low-risk patients.
