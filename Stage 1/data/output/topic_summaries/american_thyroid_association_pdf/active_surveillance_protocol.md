# Active Surveillance Protocol
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Active Surveillance Protocol for low-risk DTC

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Ongoing observation or active monitoring of low-risk DTC with serial imaging as an alternative to upfront surgery
- Use of neck ultrasound for monitoring disease progression every 6 months for 1–2 years, then annually
- Shared clinical decision-making between patient and clinical team regarding risks and benefits
- FNA not routinely recommended for thyroid nodules ≤1 cm with low-risk features
- Rates of tumor growth during active surveillance are low
- Measurement of serum Tg and TgAb levels is not routinely recommended during active surveillance for low-risk DTC

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Only appropriate for a subset of low-risk DTCs (e.g., cT1aN0M0 PTCs)
- Patients must have experience and confidence in neck ultrasound use by the medical team
- Diagnosis of thyroid cancer in a tumor ≤1 cm by FNA (if performed)
- No evidence of persistent or recurrent disease (active surveillance does not pertain to these cases)
- Low-risk features of the tumor
- Active surveillance is only appropriate for intrathyroidal, low-risk DTC (e.g., cT1aN0M0 PTCs) and does not pertain to persistent or recurrent thyroid cancer
