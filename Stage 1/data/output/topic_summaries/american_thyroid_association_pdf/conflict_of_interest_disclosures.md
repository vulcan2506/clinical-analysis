# conflict of interest disclosures
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Conflict of interest disclosures

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Disclosure of financial relationships with pharmaceutical companies or research funding sources is mandatory for guideline contributors.
- Consultant roles and committee memberships must be transparently reported.
- Mitigation measures are required if conflicts are identified to ensure unbiased guideline development.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- All contributors must disclose any financial conflicts of interest (e.g., research support, consulting fees).
- Disclosures must be reviewed and addressed to prevent bias in guideline recommendations.
