# RAI Therapy Recommendation for OTC
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** RAI Therapy Recommendation for OTC

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Outcome data are limited in OTC; specific recommendations regarding RAI use are uncertain.
- If RAI is not administered empirically, evaluation of iodine avidity with a diagnostic whole-body scan (WBS) may be considered.
- Improved ability to employ Tg levels may serve as a rationale for RAI administration in select patients.
- Current data preclude a definitive recommendation with respect to RAI; further research is needed.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Evaluation of iodine avidity with a diagnostic whole-body scan (WBS) if RAI is not administered empirically.
- Consideration of patient-specific factors (e.g., Tg levels) for RAI administration.
