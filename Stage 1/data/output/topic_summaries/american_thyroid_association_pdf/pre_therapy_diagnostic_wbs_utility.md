# Pre-Therapy Diagnostic WBS Utility
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Pre-Therapy Diagnostic WBS Utility

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Pre-therapy diagnostic WBS provides information on retained thyroid bed activity and regional metastatic disease to inform RAI treatment dose.
- Pre-therapy WBS may alter management in a significant proportion of patients (e.g., 25-49% in studies).
- Pre-therapy imaging is strongly recommended for higher RAI activities (e.g., 7.4 GBq/200 mCi) to assess safe maximum tolerated activity (MTA), particularly in elderly or renally impaired patients.
- Pre-therapy WBS can detect large thyroid remnants (>15% uptake), insignificant remnants (with Tg <1 ng/mL), or unsuspected nodal/distant metastases, influencing RAI treatment decisions.
- Post-therapy WBS should be performed after RAI treatment to identify residual/metastatic disease and assess iodine sensitivity, even in patients with negative Tg.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Pre-therapy WBS is particularly important when higher administered activities (e.g., 7.4 GBq/200 mCi) are considered.
- Blood-based dosimetry should be combined with pre-therapy imaging for higher RAI doses to determine safe MTA.
- Post-therapy WBS should be performed 2-10 days after RAI treatment to capture prognostic information.
