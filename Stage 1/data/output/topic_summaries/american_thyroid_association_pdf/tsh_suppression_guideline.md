# TSH Suppression Guideline
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** TSH suppression initiation

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Individualization of decisions to initiate TSH suppression to below the reference range is recommended based on patient-specific factors.
- TSH suppression should be titrated to maintain target levels based on dynamic risk stratification and response to therapy over time

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patient-specific factors (e.g., age, comorbidities, disease risk) must guide TSH suppression initiation decisions.
- Baseline TSH, free T4, and thyroglobulin levels must be documented prior to initiating TSH suppression
