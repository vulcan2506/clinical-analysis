# Immunotherapy for DTC
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Immunotherapy for DTC (ICIs in RAIR DTC)

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Immune checkpoint inhibitors (ICIs) may be offered in selected DTC cases with high tumor mutational burden or mismatch repair deficiency (dMMR).
- PD-1/PD-L1 and CTLA4 axes are primary immunotherapy targets in solid tumors, including DTC.
- PD-L1 expression, MSI-H, dMMR, and high tumor mutational burden are predictive biomarkers for ICI response.
- DTCs typically have low tumor mutational burden and rarely exhibit MSI-H, though dMMR may occur uncommonly.
- Tumor microenvironment with high CD8+ T cell infiltration (
- tumors) is more likely to respond to ICIs.
- PD-L1 positivity in DTC ranges from 25% to 67% and correlates with more aggressive disease

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Tumors must harbor high tumor mutational burden or be mismatch repair deficient (dMMR) for ICI consideration.
- PD-L1 positivity may guide selection but is not a strict requirement.
- Patient must have RAIR (radioactive iodine-refractory) DTC for ICI eligibility.
- Prior progression on standard therapies is typically required before ICI consideration.
- Patient must have PD-L1-positive tumors for eligibility in KEYNOTE-028 trial context (though not a strict requirement per guideline)
