# Lenvatinib Toxicity Profile
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Lenvatinib Toxicity Profile

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Treatment-related adverse events resemble those of sorafenib with additional side effects such as hypertension, stomatitis, proteinuria, arthralgia, and dysgeusia.
- Serious adverse events occurred in 49.8% of patients, with six attributed to lenvatinib.
- Dose interruption, reduction, and discontinuation occurred in 82.4%, 67.8%, and 14.2% of patients, respectively.
- Increases in TSH were common, requiring common dose increases in LT4.
- Rare events include QT prolongation, arterial and venous thrombotic events, renal and hepatic toxicity, gastrointestinal fistula, gallbladder toxicity, and reversible posterior leukoencephalopathy syndrome (RPLS).
- Six fatal serious adverse events were attributed to lenvatinib
- Higher rates of hypertension, proteinuria, and dose reduction were observed in Chinese participants compared to the SELECT trial

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Lenvatinib should be initiated without delay in patients with symptomatic RAIR DTC for whom local therapy is not appropriate (Strong recommendation, Moderate certainty evidence)
- For asymptomatic progressive RAIR DTC with QoL as a major priority, delaying lenvatinib initiation and continuing disease monitoring may be appropriate (Good Practice Statement)
