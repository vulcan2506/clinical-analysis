# Preoperative Thyroglobulin Utility
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Preoperative Thyroglobulin Utility

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Routine preoperative measurement of serum Tg or TgAb levels is not recommended.
- Preoperative serum Tg may predict higher sensitivity for postoperative surveillance with serum Tg.
- Preoperative serum Tg was not a significant predictor of malignancy in a prospective observational study.
- Preoperative Tg levels may correlate with primary tumor size and lymph node metastasis in some retrospective reviews.
- Preoperative TgAb status is not an independent predictor of disease stage or survival.
- Preoperative serum Tg may improve sensitivity of malignancy prediction when combined with validated risk stratification tools (e.g., McGill Thyroid Nodule Score).

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Preoperative diagnosis of thyroid cancer for evaluating extent of disease.
- Availability of postoperative surveillance protocols for serum Tg monitoring.
- Preoperative diagnosis of thyroid cancer must be established prior to considering Tg utility for extent of disease evaluation.
