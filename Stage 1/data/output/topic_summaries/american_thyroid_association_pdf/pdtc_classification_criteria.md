# PDTC Classification Criteria
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** PDTC Classification Criteria

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Follicular cell-derived tumors with minor DTC component (papillary, follicular, oncocytic)
- Solid and/or insular growth pattern with presence of either necrosis or ≥3 mitoses per 2 mm²
- Lacks histological characteristics and aggressiveness of anaplastic thyroid cancer (ATC)
- Clinical behavior intermediate between differentiated thyroid cancer (DTC) and ATC

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Presence of necrosis or ≥3 mitoses per 2 mm² in solid/insular growth pattern
- Exclusion of usual histological features of ATC
- Absence of papillae in NIFTP definition (revised criterion)
