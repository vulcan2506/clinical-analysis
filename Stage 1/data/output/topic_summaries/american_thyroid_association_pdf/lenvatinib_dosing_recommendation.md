# Lenvatinib Dosing Recommendation
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Lenvatinib Dosing Recommendation + Adverse Event Management

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- 24 mg once daily is the recommended starting dose for most patients with progressive RAI-refractory DTC; lower starting doses may be indicated in selected patients.
- Dose holds and dose reductions are critical strategies for managing adverse events related to lenvatinib.
- Starting dose should be individualized, particularly in patients with comorbidities, severe renal impairment (creatinine clearance <30 mL/min), or liver dysfunction (Child-Pugh C), where 14 mg once daily is recommended.
- Hypertension must be well controlled prior to initiating lenvatinib; a lower starting dose may be advisable for patients with challenging-to-manage hypertension.
- Minimize dose interruptions to less than 10% of total treatment time to optimize efficacy.
- Planned drug holiday schedules have been explored with some evidence for continued treatment efficacy, though not studied in comparative trials.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patients must have progressive RAI-refractory differentiated thyroid cancer (DTC) to initiate lenvatinib.
- Hypertension must be well controlled before starting lenvatinib.
- Severe renal impairment (creatinine clearance <30 mL/min) or liver dysfunction (Child-Pugh C) requires a reduced starting dose of 14 mg once daily.
- Hypertension must be well controlled prior to initiating lenvatinib (explicitly stated as a prerequisite).
