# ALK-Targeted Therapy
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** ALK-targeted therapy for RAIR DTC with oncogenic ALK fusion

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- ALK-targeted therapy is recommended as first-line treatment in patients with progressive RAIR DTC harboring an oncogenic ALK fusion.
- ALK-targeted therapy has demonstrated efficacy in case reports for ALK fusion-positive RAIR DTC, including with agents such as crizotinib, alectinib, and lorlatinib.
- Oncogenic kinase fusions involving ALK have been reported rarely in papillary thyroid cancers (PTCs), poorly differentiated thyroid cancers (PDTCs), and anaplastic thyroid cancers (ATCs), with higher rates occurring in patients previously exposed to radiation.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Presence of progressive RAIR DTC (radioactive iodine-refractory differentiated thyroid cancer).
- Confirmation of oncogenic ALK fusion in the tumor.
