# Surgical Treatment Goals for DTC
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Surgical treatment goals and high-volume surgeon referral for DTC

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Shared decision-making between patients and clinicians is paramount in determining initial therapy goals for DTC.
- Primary surgical goal is complete resection of the tumor, extrathyroidal disease, and clinically significant lymph node metastases.
- Consider multimodal treatment options to reduce disease persistence/recurrence and metastatic spread while minimizing treatment-related morbidity.
- Determine staging and risk stratification to estimate prognosis and guide treatment intensity.
- Refer patients to high-volume thyroid surgeons (>25–50 thyroidectomies/year) for improved outcomes, particularly for extensive surgeries.
- Preoperative imaging (CT/MRI with contrast) is required to assess local invasion of laryngeal, tracheal, esophageal structures, and bulky lymphadenopathy with clinical extranodal extension to guide surgical planning and potential need for sternotomy or tracheal/laryngeal resection/reconstruction.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patient selection for thyroid surgery must prioritize completeness of resection to reduce recurrence risk.
- Multimodal treatment options (e.g., postoperative RAI, TSH suppression) should be balanced against treatment-related morbidity.
- Risk stratification requires accurate staging to estimate disease-specific mortality and recurrence risks.
- High-volume surgeon referral is recommended for tumors requiring extensive surgery or in older patients with higher complication risks.
- Preoperative imaging (e.g., CT/MRI) is required to assess local invasion and plan surgical approach.
- Serum thyroglobulin (Tg) measurement prior to surgery is recommended to establish a baseline for postoperative monitoring and risk stratification.
