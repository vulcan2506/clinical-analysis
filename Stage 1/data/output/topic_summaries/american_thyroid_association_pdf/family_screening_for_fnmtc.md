# Family Screening for FNMTC
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Family Screening for FNMTC

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Consider ultrasound screening in first-degree family members of individuals with ≥3 related individuals diagnosed with NMTC.
- Consider ultrasound screening in families with only two affected individuals if they show concerning features (e.g., young age at diagnosis) or limited family structure.
- Initiate screening cautiously, weighing benefits against risks of overtreatment; optimal age for initiation requires further study.
- Screening programs should be individualized based on family history and risk stratification.
- Yearly ultrasound screening may be considered in kindreds with ≥3 affected family members, starting from age 20 or 10 years before the earliest diagnosis in the family.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Family history of FNMTC (defined as ≥3 related individuals with NMTC).
- Presence of concerning features in families with only two affected individuals (e.g., young age at diagnosis).
- First-degree family members of affected individuals.
- Consideration of epidemiological risk (5–10% familial occurrence of NMTC).
- Risk stratification based on age of diagnosis in families with two affected members.
- Presence of three or more (first or second degree) related individuals with diagnoses of NMTC to qualify for standard screening consideration.
- Stratification of families with two affected members based on age of diagnosis (≤45 years vs. >45 years) to identify higher-risk subsets.
