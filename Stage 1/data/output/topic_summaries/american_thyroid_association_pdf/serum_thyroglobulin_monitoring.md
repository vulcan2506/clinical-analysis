# Serum Thyroglobulin Monitoring
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Serum Thyroglobulin Monitoring + Anti-Tg Antibody Assessment

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Serum Tg must be measured using assays calibrated against the BCR457 standard.
- Quantitatively assess Tg antibodies with every serum Tg measurement.
- Measure serum Tg on thyroid hormone therapy after total thyroidectomy (with or without RAI) to monitor response and recurrence.
- Perform serum Tg measurements every 6–12 months during initial follow-up; more frequent measurements for ATA intermediate-high or high-risk patients.
- Avoid routine serum Tg measurements in patients after lobectomy during initial follow-up.
- Measure serum Tg 6–12 weeks after total or near-total thyroidectomy in patients with DTC.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Serum Tg assays must be calibrated against the BCR457 standard to ensure consistency.
- TgAb must be quantitatively assessed alongside every serum Tg measurement due to interference risks.
- Total thyroidectomy (with or without RAI) is required for reliable serum Tg monitoring of recurrence.
- Modern highly sensitive Tg assays (functional sensitivity ≤0.1 ng/mL) are required for accurate basal Tg measurements.
- Longitudinal Tg measurements should ideally be performed using the same assay and laboratory to minimize variability.
- Serum Tg assays must have a functional sensitivity of ≤0.1 ng/mL for adequate clinical sensitivity in basal measurements.
