# Preoperative Diagnosis Criteria
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Preoperative Diagnosis Criteria

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Begin with certain or near-certain diagnosis of thyroid cancer on preoperative FNA testing (Bethesda VI cytology and/or molecular results with high certainty of malignancy)
- Include diagnosis after initial surgery based on surgical histopathology analysis
- Discuss NIFTP and FUMP due to their malignant potential
- Apply updated histological criteria, subtypes of thyroid cancer, and staging

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Preoperative FNA testing showing Bethesda VI cytology
- Molecular results with high certainty of malignancy
- Surgical histopathology analysis following initial surgery
