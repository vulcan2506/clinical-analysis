# Germline Testing Recommendation
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Germline Testing Recommendation

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Focused germline testing for up to 40 genes if somatic mutations are identified in any tumor type
- Prioritize testing for seven genes deemed 'most actionable': BRCA1, BRCA2, MLH1, MSH2, MSH6, PALB2, and RET
- Recommendation based on >5–10% tumor-to-germline conversion rate for variants with variant allele fractions >30%
- Thyroid cancers included in the cohort with approximately 2% representation
- Recommendation applies to somatic mutations identified in any tumor type, not limited to thyroid cancer

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Somatic mutations identified in any tumor type
- Variant allele fraction >30% for targeted variants
- Paired sequencing data must be available for tumor-to-germline conversion rate assessment
