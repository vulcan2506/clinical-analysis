# NIFTP Management Guidelines
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** NIFTP management approach

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- NIFTP and other tumors of uncertain malignant potential (e.g., Follicular Tumor of Uncertain Malignant Potential, Hyalinizing Trabecular Tumor) are diagnosed pathologically and have very low malignant potential.
- Further treatment with completion thyroidectomy, lymphadenectomy, and/or radioactive iodine (RAI) is not advised routinely for NIFTP.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Pathological diagnosis of NIFTP or other tumors of uncertain malignant potential.
- Pathological diagnosis must exclude invasive features or papillary thyroid carcinoma (PTC) nuclear features to classify as NIFTP
