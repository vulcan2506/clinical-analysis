*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Postoperative RAI Imaging Guidance

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Postoperative diagnostic ¹²³I or low-dose ¹³¹I whole-body scan (WBS) may be considered for patients undergoing RAI treatment prior to therapeutic administration to guide treatment planning.
- RAI is a theranostic agent providing both imaging and therapy in differentiated thyroid cancer (DTC).
- Imaging with RAI is performed using either ¹²³I or low-dose ¹³¹I WBS; ¹²⁴I PET is investigational.
- ¹²³I may be preferred for scanning due to lower radiation dose and less concern for 'stunning,' though this is controversial.
- Both ¹²³I and ¹³¹I WBS are considered reasonable in practice, with ¹²³I preferred if available.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patient preparation for diagnostic WBS should follow similar protocols to RAI therapy.
- Diagnostic WBS may be performed as surveillance or as part of RAI treatment protocols.
- Diagnostic WBS should follow patient preparation protocols similar to those used for RAI therapy.
