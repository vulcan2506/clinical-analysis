# MAPK Inhibition for RAI Refractory DTC
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** MAPK Inhibition for RAI Refractory DTC

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Redifferentiation by MAPK pathway blockade may be considered in selected patients with progressive RAI-refractory DTC harboring targetable mutations.
- Clinical trial participation is encouraged for MAPK inhibition approaches.
- Redifferentiation approaches in adjuvant RAI treatment for high-risk, non-gene selected DTC are not recommended.
- MAPK pathway blockade (e.g., MEK or BRAF inhibition) should be administered for 4 weeks prior to reassessment of RAI uptake eligibility via 124I PET or 123I SPECT/CT.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Progressive RAI-refractory DTC with targetable mutations.
- Disease progression within the previous 12 months for redifferentiation trials.
- Evidence of sufficient RAI uptake (e.g., 124I PET or 123I SPECT/CT threshold) before RAI treatment.
- Disease progression within the previous 12 months is required for eligibility in redifferentiation trials involving selumetinib.
