# Financial Toxicity Management
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Financial Toxicity Management + Psychosocial Support

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Clinicians should inform patients about available resources for financial burden due to thyroid cancer diagnosis and treatment.
- Clinicians should discuss financial concerns with patients and families, employing cost-effective approaches to testing, treatment, and monitoring.
- Clinicians should address psychosocial needs, including distress, cancer-related worry, and quality of life concerns.
- Clinicians should provide high-quality information resources, such as online support groups, counseling, and local social work/psychology services.
- Clinicians should employ cost-effective approaches to testing, treatment, and monitoring to reduce financial barriers to care.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patients diagnosed with thyroid cancer are at risk for financial hardship and psychosocial distress.
- Financial burden is influenced by factors such as age, socioeconomic status, race/ethnicity, and comorbidities.
- Financial hardship is influenced by factors such as age, socioeconomic status, race/ethnicity, and comorbidities.
- Black and Hispanic patients with thyroid cancer are at elevated risk for food insecurity.
- Younger patient age is commonly associated with more cancer-related financial hardship due to lack of Medicare access.
