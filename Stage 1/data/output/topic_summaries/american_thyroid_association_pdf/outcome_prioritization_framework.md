# Outcome Prioritization Framework
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Outcome Prioritization Framework + Evidence Review Process

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Survival or mortality outcomes (all-cause and/or cancer-specific) prioritized most highly
- Oncologic outcomes (e.g., metastasis, progression, recurrence) and clinical outcomes (e.g., QoL, function, adverse events) prioritized secondarily
- Intermediate outcomes (e.g., radiological or laboratory) assigned lower priority
- Systematic reviews conducted using PICO framework with prespecified inclusion criteria
- Evidence quality assessed using GRADE methodology with ratings of high, moderate, low, or very low certainty
- Evidence quality assessed using study-design specific criteria adapted from the U.S. Preventive Services Task Force Procedure Manual

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Population restricted to people with differentiated thyroid cancer (DTC)
- English-language studies only
- Exclusion of conference abstracts-only studies
- Two independent investigators for study eligibility review
- Prespecified inclusion criteria based on PICO framework
