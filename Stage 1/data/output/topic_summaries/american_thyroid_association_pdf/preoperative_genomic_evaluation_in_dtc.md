# Preoperative Genomic Evaluation in DTC
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Preoperative Genomic Evaluation in DTC

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Genomic evaluation of confirmed DTC prior to surgery is not recommended routinely.
- If genomic profile is known or performed, specific combinations of abnormalities may inform extent of surgery in context of clinical, radiographical, and cytopathologic data.
- Combination of TERT promoter and BRAFV600E mutations correlates with worse prognosis.
- Presence of multiple mutations (e.g., BRAFV600E with TERT promoter, TP53, or AKT1) is associated with more aggressive disease.
- Molecular tests developed to improve diagnostic accuracy of thyroid nodule FNA cytology may predict tumor behavior to guide preoperative surgical planning.
- Expression of specific miRNAs may be associated with increased risk of locoregional disease, though robust data are lacking.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Confirmed diagnosis of DTC prior to considering genomic evaluation.
- Clinical, radiographical, and cytopathologic data must be integrated with genomic findings to inform surgical planning.
- High-risk molecular profiles may warrant more extensive initial surgery.
- Integration of molecular test results with clinical, radiographical, and cytopathologic data is required to inform surgical planning.
