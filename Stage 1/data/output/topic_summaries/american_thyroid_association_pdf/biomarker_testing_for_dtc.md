# Biomarker Testing for DTC
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Biomarker Testing for RAIR DTC

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Tissue-based biomarker testing to identify actionable oncogenic driver alterations in RAIR DTC should be performed prior to initiating systemic therapy for progressive disease.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- RAIR DTC (radioactive iodine-refractory differentiated thyroid cancer)
- Progressive disease requiring systemic therapy
