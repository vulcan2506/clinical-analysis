# Vocal Fold Impairment Incidence
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Vocal Fold Impairment Incidence

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Routine pre- and immediate postoperative fiberoptic laryngoscopy is recommended to assess vocal fold motion impairment after primary thyroid and parathyroid surgery.
- Vocal fold motion impairment is a recognized complication following thyroid and parathyroid surgery, necessitating early detection and management.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Preoperative baseline laryngoscopy should be performed to document vocal fold function prior to surgery.
- Immediate postoperative laryngoscopy is required to identify acute vocal fold impairment.
