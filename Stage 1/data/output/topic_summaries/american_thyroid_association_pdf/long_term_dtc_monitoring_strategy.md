# Long-Term DTC Monitoring Strategy
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Long-Term DTC Monitoring Strategy

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Monitoring for clinical recurrence in patients thought to be free of disease and identifying progression in patients with residual thyroid cancer are primary goals.
- Use tests with high specificity to identify patients unlikely to experience recurrence for safer and more cost-effective management.
- De-escalate monitoring for low-risk patients with a persistent excellent response many years after initial therapy.
- Monitor high-risk patients more closely for early detection of recurrent disease.
- Pursue shared decision-making with patients, considering side effects and financial implications.
- Use the DATA framework to guide test selection, prioritizing high specificity to minimize false positives and safely reduce monitoring intensity

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patients must have a persistent excellent response to consider de-escalation of monitoring.
- High-risk patients require closer monitoring for early detection of recurrence.
- Monitoring de-escalation requires a persistent excellent response in low-risk patients many years after initial therapy
