# RAI Preparation Strategy
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** RAI preparation strategy

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- rhTSH stimulation is preferred over thyroid hormone withdrawal for RAI remnant ablation or adjuvant therapy.
- LT4 should be withdrawn for 3–4 weeks if thyroid hormone withdrawal is planned.
- LT3 substitution should be considered if LT4 is withdrawn for ≥4 weeks, with LT3 withdrawn for at least 2 weeks.
- Serum TSH should be measured prior to radioisotope administration to evaluate TSH elevation.
- A goal of TSH >30 mIU/L should be employed in preparation for RAI therapy or diagnostic testing.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patients with significant comorbidity that may preclude thyroid hormone withdrawal should consider rhTSH preparation.
- Exclusion of secretory metastatic DTC or large thyroid remnants causing suboptimal TSH response to hormone withdrawal.
- Patients with limited pituitary gland function due to previous head trauma/surgery/radiation or obesity may require rhTSH.
- Exclusion of secretory metastatic DTC or large thyroid remnants causing suboptimal TSH response to hormone withdrawal is required before considering rhTSH preparation.
