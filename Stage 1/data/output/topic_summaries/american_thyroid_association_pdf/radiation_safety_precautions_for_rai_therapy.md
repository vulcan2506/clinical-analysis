# Radiation Safety Precautions for RAI Therapy
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Radiation Safety Precautions for RAI Therapy

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Use shielding and occupancy factors (e.g., <0.25 for time spent <1 m from the patient) to estimate radiation exposure.
- Determine length of time precautions using appropriate models rather than traditional overestimates.
- Cease breastfeeding and lactation prior to treatment.
- Provide written instructions to patients and document them in the medical record.
- Prefer home release over hotel release for patients undergoing RAI therapy.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Breastfeeding and lactation must cease prior to treatment.
- Instructions must be provided in writing and documented in the medical record.
