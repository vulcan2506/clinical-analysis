# RAI Dose Safety Thresholds
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** RAI Dose Safety Thresholds for high-risk patients

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Empirically administered ¹³¹I doses >5.5 GBq (150 mCi) should be avoided in patients >70 years or with renal failure due to high toxicity risk.
- Dosimetry evaluation is required to confirm safety prior to RAI administration if doses >5.5 GBq (150 mCi) are being considered.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patients >70 years or with renal failure must undergo dosimetry to assess safety before receiving >5.5 GBq (150 mCi) of ¹³¹I.
- Dosimetry evaluation must account for individual renal function and age-related physiologic changes to determine safe RAI dosing thresholds.
