# TgAb Prognostic Impact in DTC
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** TgAb Prognostic Impact in DTC

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- TgAb positivity alone is not associated with disease progression or response to therapy in low-risk DTC patients.
- A rising TgAb level, more than an absolute level, is associated with increased risk of structurally incomplete response in intermediate- and high-risk DTC patients.
- Persistent or increasing TgAb levels correlate with higher risk of cancer persistence and mortality compared to decreasing levels.
- De novo TgAb detection in initially TgAb-negative patients does not predict structural recurrence.
- In patients with undetectable serum Tg but elevated TgAb, progression-free survival is not lower than historical controls.
- Initial TgAb positivity alone does not worsen baseline risk but is associated with higher rates of lymph node metastases and cancer persistence at diagnosis, per meta-analysis data.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Total thyroidectomy with or without ablative RAI for DTC patients.
- Intermediate- or high-risk DTC for rising TgAb levels to indicate prognostic significance.
- Preoperative TgAb positivity to assess risk of nodal metastases and extranodal extension.
- Postoperative follow-up with TgAb monitoring in patients with detectable TgAb.
- Structural disease evaluation in patients with negative serum Tg but positive TgAb.
- Postoperative follow-up with TgAb monitoring in patients with detectable TgAb for at least 15.8 months (median time for TgAb levels to become negative post-RAI).
