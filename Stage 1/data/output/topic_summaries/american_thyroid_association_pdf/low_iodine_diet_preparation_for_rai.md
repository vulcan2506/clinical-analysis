# Low-Iodine Diet Preparation for RAI
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Low-Iodine Diet Preparation for RAI

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- A low-iodine diet (restriction to <50 mcg/day) should be used for approximately 1–2 weeks prior to RAI remnant ablation or treatment.
- Confirm adherence to the low-iodine diet and exclude high-dose iodine sources (e.g., IV contrast within 3 months, amiodarone) before RAI administration.
- Urinary iodine can be used to assess recent iodine intake compliance or clearance of iodine-containing agents but not long-term iodine status.
- Avoid restricting non-iodized salt during the diet to prevent hyponatremia, especially in patients undergoing thyroid hormone withdrawal.
- Serious side effects from low-iodine diets are relatively infrequent, but case reports highlight potentially life-threatening hyponatremia in high-risk patients (elderly, metastatic disease, concurrent thiazide diuretics, or diet duration >1 week).

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patients must adhere to a low-iodine diet (<50 mcg/day) for 1–2 weeks prior to RAI therapy.
- Exclusion of high-dose iodine sources (e.g., IV contrast within 3 months, amiodarone) is mandatory before RAI administration.
- Perform diagnostic radioiodine whole-body scan (WBS) to assess iodine uptake and ablation success, particularly in patients with high urinary iodine or excessive iodine uptake linked to failed remnant ablation.
