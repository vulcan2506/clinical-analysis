# Cabozantinib Second-Line Therapy
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Cabozantinib Second-Line Therapy

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Cabozantinib is recommended as second-line therapy for patients with RAIR DTC without actionable oncogenic driver alterations who progressed on or did not tolerate prior MKI therapy.
- Cabozantinib targets VEGFR2, c-MET, RET, and other kinases.
- Common adverse events include hand-foot syndrome, diarrhea, and fatigue.
- Dose reductions for toxicity were required in 56% of patients, and 5% discontinued due to intolerable adverse events.
- Cabozantinib (Cabometyx formulation) differs from the cabozantinib formulation (Cometriq) studied in medullary thyroid carcinoma, and the Cabometyx formulation was specifically studied in COSMIC-311 for RAIR DTC.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patients must have RAIR DTC without an actionable oncogenic driver alteration.
- Patients must have progressed on or not tolerated prior MKI therapy.
- Patients must desire ongoing treatment and have no contraindication to therapy.
- Patients must have received up to two prior VEGFR MKIs (not just one) to be eligible for cabozantinib second-line therapy, as per COSMIC-311 trial criteria.
