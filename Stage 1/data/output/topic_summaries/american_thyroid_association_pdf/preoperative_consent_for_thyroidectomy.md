# Preoperative Consent for Thyroidectomy
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Preoperative Consent for Thyroidectomy + Voice/Laryngeal Assessment + RLN/EBSLN Management + Parathyroid Gland Management + Drainage

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Explicit preoperative consent must include risks of temporary/permanent nerve injury (voice change, swallowing disability, aspiration risk, tracheostomy), hypoparathyroidism, anesthesia risks, bleeding, scarring, recurrence, reoperation, thyroid hormone supplementation, and surveillance.
- All patients undergoing thyroid surgery should undergo voice assessment (patient description + physician evaluation) as part of preoperative physical examination.
- Preoperative laryngeal exam is mandatory for patients with dysphonia, prior cervical/upper chest surgery, or thyroid cancer with posterior extrathyroidal extension/extensive nodal metastases.
- Visual identification of RLN during thyroidectomy is the gold standard; intraoperative neurophysiological monitoring (NIM) is conditionally recommended for malignancy, total/reoperative thyroidectomy, and to assess RLN integrity before contralateral lobe removal.
- Parathyroid glands must be preserved during thyroidectomy; devascularized/removed glands should be auto-transplanted into muscle after frozen section confirmation of benign tissue.
- The preoperative consent process should include discussion of the operating surgeon's own complication rates to ensure transparency and allow patients to make informed decisions based on real-world data.
- Preoperative voice assessment should include patient-reported vocal changes and physician’s objective evaluation, documented in medical records, and may incorporate validated QoL and auditory perceptual assessment voice instruments.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Preoperative voice assessment must include patient-reported vocal changes and physician’s objective evaluation, documented in medical records.
- Preoperative laryngeal exam is required for patients with preoperative dysphonia, prior neck/chest surgery, or thyroid cancer with posterior extrathyroidal extension/extensive nodal metastases.
- Intraoperative RLN identification via visual or NIM is required during thyroidectomy/paratracheal node dissection.
- Postoperative parathyroid hormone-directed calcium/vitamin D supplementation is recommended after total thyroidectomy, central lymph node dissection, or unilateral operations following prior contralateral resections.
- Formal postoperative voice assessment is required if voice abnormalities are present.
- Preoperative laryngeal exam is required for patients with prior external beam radiation to the neck, even if voice is normal.
- Preoperative laryngeal exam is required for patients with a history of prior neck surgery placing the vagus nerve at risk (e.g., carotid endarterectomy, cervical esophagectomy, anterior cervical spine approach), even if voice is normal.
