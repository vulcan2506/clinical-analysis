# ATA DTC Guidelines Update
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** ATA DTC Guidelines Update 2025

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Guidelines emphasize the patient journey beginning with a thyroid cancer diagnosis.
- Recommendations are based on systematic literature reviews with input from a diverse stakeholder panel.
- Incorporates patient-specific factors (e.g., age, comorbidities) in management decisions.
- Includes a patient advocate in the guideline development process.
- Focuses on contemporary, rigorous, and comprehensive guidelines for adult DTC management.
- Guidelines explicitly state the use of a modified Delphi process for consensus-building among stakeholders to refine recommendations.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patient must be an adult with differentiated thyroid cancer (DTC).
- Literature review eligibility includes English-language articles with final search date of July 1, 2024.
- Panel must include multidisciplinary specialists and a patient advocate.
- Systematic reviews or meta-analyses must inform recommendations.
- Guidelines are updated based on advances in the field since the last revision.
- Panel must include an expert in systematic reviews/meta-analyses/guidelines to educate and support task force members.
