# Post-Therapy Scintigraphy and Risk Stratification
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Post-Therapy Scintigraphy and Risk Stratification

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Perform post-ablative serum thyroglobulin measurement to stratify risk and guide further management decisions.
- Utilize 131I whole-body scintigraphy with SPECT/CT fusion imaging to improve detection of structural disease and refine risk classification.
- Adopt dynamic risk stratification incorporating age, thyroglobulin levels, and ATA risk stratification to predict long-term survival.
- Consider delayed risk stratification that includes response to initial surgery and radioiodine ablation for better outcome predictivity.
- Evaluate the utility of repeat stimulated thyroglobulin testing in patients with previously negative results and undetectable basal levels.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patient must have undergone initial thyroablative therapy (surgery and/or radioiodine) prior to risk stratification assessment.
- Serum thyroglobulin levels must be measured in the context of levothyroxine suppressive treatment or TSH stimulation.
- Risk assessment should integrate individual patient factors including age, comorbidities, and disease-specific risk profiles.
- Thyroglobulin antibody status must be assessed to interpret thyroglobulin levels accurately in low-risk patients.
- Clinical evaluation must include neck ultrasonography to detect lymph node metastases alongside biochemical markers.
- Post-therapy scintigraphy should be performed with 131I SPECT/CT fusion imaging to enhance anatomical localization and improve detection of structural disease in patients with differentiated thyroid cancer.
