# NIFTP Diagnostic Criteria
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** NIFTP Diagnostic Criteria

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- Tumors must be well demarcated from the surrounding thyroid parenchyma and can be thinly or partially encapsulated.
- Absence of papillae in the tumor.
- Absence of psammoma bodies (reminiscent of dead papillae).
- Follicular growth pattern observed histopathologically.
- Requires histopathologic examination of the entire tumor capsule and tumor.
- Tumors must lack true papillae (even focal papillae exclude NIFTP diagnosis)
- Follicular growth pattern must be predominant (>99% of the tumor)

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- NIFTP is classified as a low-risk neoplasm, formerly known as noninvasive encapsulated follicular variant of papillary thyroid carcinoma.
- Exclusion of any capsular or vascular invasion
- Exclusion of tumor necrosis
- Exclusion of high mitotic activity (mitotic count <3/10 HPFs)
- Tumor size typically <4 cm (though not strictly required)
- Requires comprehensive histopathologic examination of the entire tumor to confirm encapsulation and absence of invasion
