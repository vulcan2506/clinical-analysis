# BRAFV600E Targeted Therapy
*Source: American Thyroid Association.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** BRAFV600E Targeted Therapy

**Key Clinical Findings:**
- BRAFV600E-directed therapy may be considered in the first line for patients who are poor candidates for lenvatinib.
- BRAF-directed treatment is recommended in patients with BRAFV600E mutation-positive RAIR DTC who have progressed on or did not tolerate one or more prior MKI therapies.
- Currently approved BRAF-directed therapies are not recommended in DTCs harboring non-V600 BRAF alterations.
- Grade 3-4 adverse events with BRAF-directed therapies include cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma, lymphopenia, and increased gamma glutamyl transferase, warranting close monitoring.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patient must have progressive RAIR DTC harboring an oncogenic BRAFV600E mutation.
- Patient must be a poor candidate for lenvatinib for first-line consideration.
- Patient must have progressed on or not tolerated one or more prior MKI therapies for second-line consideration.
- Patient must have progressive disease by RECIST v1.1 criteria for consideration of BRAF-directed therapy in the second-line setting.
