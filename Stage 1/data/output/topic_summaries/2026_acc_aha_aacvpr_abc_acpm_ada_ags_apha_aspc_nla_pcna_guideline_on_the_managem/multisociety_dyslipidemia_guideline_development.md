# Multisociety Dyslipidemia Guideline Development
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Multisociety Dyslipidemia Guideline Development Process

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Developed collaboratively with 12 professional societies including the American Heart Association, American College of Cardiology, and National Lipid Association
- Writing committee members must recuse themselves from voting on sections where they have conflicts of interest
- Comprehensive literature search conducted from October 2024 to December 2024 across multiple databases (MEDLINE, EMBASE, Cochrane Library, AHRQ)
- Peer review committee includes representatives from all endorsing societies
- Document replaces the 2018 guideline, incorporating updated evidence and methodology
- Writing committee includes patient representative (Association of Black Cardiologists representative)
- Peer review committee includes representatives from AHA/ACC Joint Committee on Clinical Practice Guidelines

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Literature search restricted to human studies published in English
- Evidence synthesis must address evaluation, management, and monitoring of dyslipidemias
- Writing committee members must disclose industry relationships in Appendix 1
- Peer review committee must disclose industry relationships in Appendix 2
- Guideline must be cited as: Blumenthal RS et al. 2026 ACC/AHA/AACVPR/ABC/ACPM/ADA/AGS/APhA/ASPC/NLA/PCNA guideline on the management of dyslipidemia
- Literature search must include studies on human participants published in English
- Evidence synthesis must address evaluation, management, and monitoring of high blood cholesterol, hypertriglyceridemia, and elevated lipoprotein(a)
