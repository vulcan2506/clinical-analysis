# CKM Syndrome Clinical Definition
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** CKM Syndrome Clinical Definition

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- CKM syndrome includes abdominal obesity (high BMI and/or large waist circumference)
- Insulin-resistant glucose metabolism (hyperinsulinemia, impaired fasting glucose, impaired glucose tolerance, type 2 diabetes)
- Dyslipidemia (high serum triglycerides and low serum HDL-C concentrations)
- Increased blood pressure, microalbuminuria, proteinuria, or chronic kidney disease (CKD)
- Lifestyle management includes heart-healthy eating, regular physical activity, avoidance of nicotine products, healthy sleep habits, and stress management

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Children are defined as age 18 years or younger
- ASCVD at very high risk requires ≥2 major ASCVD events or 1 major ASCVD event with ≥2 high-risk features
- Lifestyle management includes maintaining a healthy weight as a specific requirement
