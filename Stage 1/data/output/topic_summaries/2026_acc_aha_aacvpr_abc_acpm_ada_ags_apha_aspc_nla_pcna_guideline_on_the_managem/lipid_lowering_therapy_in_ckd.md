# Lipid-Lowering Therapy in CKD
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Lipid-Lowering Therapy in CKD (Stage 3 or Higher)

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Moderate-intensity statin therapy is recommended for adults aged 40–75 with CKD stage 3+ and LDL-C 70–189 mg/dL.
- Moderate-intensity statin combined with ezetimibe is recommended for the same population to reduce ASCVD risk.
- Statin therapy is prioritized for ASCVD risk reduction in CKD patients, with ezetimibe as an adjunct when needed.
- Bempedoic acid is flagged for cautious implementation in patients with renal impairment due to potential effects on kidney function.
- PCSK9 inhibitors are indicated for CKD patients with ASCVD who fail to achieve LDL-C <55 mg/dL or ≥50% reduction on maximally tolerated statin plus ezetimibe.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- CKD stage 3 or higher must be confirmed before initiating lipid-lowering therapy.
- LDL-C levels must be between 70–189 mg/dL (1.8–4.9 mmol/L) for statin therapy initiation.
- LDL-C levels must be between 70–189 mg/dL (1.8–4.9 mmol/L) for moderate-intensity statin therapy initiation in adults aged 40–75 with CKD stage 3+.
