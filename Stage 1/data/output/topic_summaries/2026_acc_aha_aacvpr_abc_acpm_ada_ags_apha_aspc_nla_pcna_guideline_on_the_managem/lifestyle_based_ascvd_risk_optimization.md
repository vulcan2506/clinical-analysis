# Lifestyle-Based ASCVD Risk Optimization
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Lifestyle-Based ASCVD Risk Optimization

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Implement all components of the AHA Life's Essential 8 for cardiovascular health optimization
- Adopt a healthy diet as per the 2019 ACC/AHA Guideline on the Primary Prevention of Cardiovascular Disease (Class 1A recommendation)
- Assess patient baseline behavioral habits using lifestyle screening tools integrated into EMR, digital health technologies, or team-based care
- Combine lifestyle management with medical therapy for optimal ASCVD risk reduction outcomes
- Incorporate structured physical activity programs tailored to patient functional capacity and comorbidities (e.g., cardiac rehabilitation for high-risk patients)
- Provide smoking cessation interventions as a core component of lifestyle optimization for ASCVD risk reduction

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Baseline behavioral habit assessment must be performed to target residual risk from suboptimal lifestyle habits
- Ensure baseline assessment includes quantification of physical activity levels (e.g., minutes/week of moderate-to-vigorous activity) and dietary patterns (e.g., adherence to DASH or Mediterranean diet scores)
- Document patient readiness and barriers to lifestyle change using validated behavioral change models (e.g., Transtheoretical Model)
