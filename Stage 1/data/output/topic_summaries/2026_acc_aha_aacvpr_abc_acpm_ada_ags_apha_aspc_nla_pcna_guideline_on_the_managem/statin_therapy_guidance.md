# Statin Therapy Guidance
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Statin Therapy Intensity and LDL-C Goals by ASCVD Risk Stratification

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Higher baseline predicted risk correlates with greater net benefit from statin therapy; shared decision-making is essential near 3% 10-year event risk threshold.
- In primary prevention with intermediate risk (5% to <10% by PREVENT-ASCVD), initiate at least moderate-intensity statin for ≥30% LDL-C lowering; high-intensity statins (≥50% LDL-C reduction) are reasonable.
- For borderline (3% to <5%) or intermediate (5% to <10%) risk, treat to LDL-C <100 mg/dL and non-HDL-C <130 mg/dL.
- In high-risk primary prevention (≥10% 10-year risk), high-intensity statins are recommended first-line with goal LDL-C <70 mg/dL and non-HDL-C <100 mg/dL.
- In ASCVD patients not at very high risk, consider LDL-C goal <55 mg/dL and non-HDL-C <85 mg/dL due to incremental event reduction at lower LDL-C levels.
- Assessment of potential drug–drug interactions (DDI) with other cardiovascular medications before initiating statin therapy to minimize adverse events.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Baseline 10-year ASCVD risk estimation using PREVENT-ASCVD equations for treatment decisions.
- Follow-up LDL-C testing to assess adherence and adequacy of statin therapy effect.
- Review of concomitant medications for potential drug–drug interactions before initiating statin therapy.
- Patient-clinician discussion of expected net benefit, cost-effectiveness, and risk profile before statin initiation.
- Assessment of statin intolerance and consideration of nonstatin therapies (e.g., ezetimibe, PCSK9 inhibitors) if LDL-C goals are not met.
- Assessment of potential drug–drug interactions with other cardiovascular medications to minimize adverse events and optimize therapy.
