**Clinical Recommendation:** Writing Committee Members Composition

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Committee includes co-chairs (Anand Rohatgi, MD; Samuel M. Kim, MD) and multidisciplinary experts (cardiologists, endocrinologists, pharmacists, epidemiologists)
- Members hold multiple subspecialty certifications (FAHA, FACC, FNLA, etc.)
- Includes representation from academic, clinical, and research backgrounds
- Features dual leadership structure (Co-Chairs) for collaborative oversight
- Includes representation from preventive cardiology and women’s health (C. Noel Bairey Merz, MD, MACC)
- Features members with dual subspecialty certifications (e.g., FNLA + FACC, FAHA + FPCNA)
- Includes nurse practitioners and clinical pharmacists (Jane A. Linderbaum, MS, APRN, CNP; Janelle Ruisinger, PharmD)
