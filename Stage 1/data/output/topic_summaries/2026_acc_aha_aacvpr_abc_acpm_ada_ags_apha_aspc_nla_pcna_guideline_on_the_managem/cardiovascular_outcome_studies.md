**Clinical Recommendation:** Cardiovascular Outcome Studies in Dyslipidemia Management

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- PCSK9 inhibitors (evolocumab, alirocumab) reduce major adverse cardiovascular events (MACE) in high-risk patients with established cardiovascular disease or recent acute coronary syndrome.
- Bempedoic acid demonstrates cardiovascular risk reduction in statin-intolerant patients by lowering LDL-C and MACE.
- Icosapent ethyl reduces cardiovascular events in patients with hypertriglyceridemia on statin therapy.
- Nonstatin therapies (PCSK9 inhibitors, bempedoic acid, icosapent ethyl) are now integral to LDL-C lowering and ASCVD risk reduction strategies.
- Drug-drug interaction management with statins is emphasized to optimize safety and efficacy in cardiovascular disease patients.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Patients must meet specific LDL-C thresholds or ASCVD risk criteria for nonstatin therapy initiation.
- Statin intolerance must be documented before considering bempedoic acid or other nonstatin alternatives.
- Hypertriglyceridemia management requires persistent levels despite statin therapy for icosapent ethyl consideration.
- High-risk patients (e.g., post-ACS) are prioritized for intensive lipid-lowering therapy.
- Comprehensive cardiovascular risk assessment is required before escalating to nonstatin therapies.
- Lipid-lowering therapy decisions must be guided by the 2019 ACC/AHA primary prevention guidelines and 2021 expert consensus pathways for persistent hypertriglyceridemia.
- Nonstatin therapy selection requires adherence to the 2022 ACC expert consensus decision pathway regarding the role of nonstatin therapies for LDL-C lowering in ASCVD risk management.
