# Future Research Priorities
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Future Research Priorities for Risk Assessment and Disparities

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Elucidate mechanisms of ASCVD risk in reproductive-age populations and develop public health interventions for social determinants of health and psychosocial stressors.
- Address disparities in ASCVD events among racial/ethnic groups through advances in interventions and implementation science.
- Investigate lipid changes and potential CVD risk associated with gender-affirming hormone therapy in transgender individuals.
- Refine risk assessment beyond the PREVENT equations by incorporating genetic data.
- Translate knowledge of disease processes and risk assessment into clinical care through clinician-patient partnership discussions.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Incorporation of genetic data into risk assessment models.
- Assessment of social determinants of health and psychosocial stressors.
- Evaluation of lipid profiles in patients undergoing gender-affirming hormone therapy.
- Integration of clinician-patient partnership discussions into risk assessment refinement processes
