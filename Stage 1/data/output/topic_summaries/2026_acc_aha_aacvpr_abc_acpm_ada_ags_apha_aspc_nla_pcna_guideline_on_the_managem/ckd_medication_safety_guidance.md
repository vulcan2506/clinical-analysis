# CKD medication safety guidance
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** CKD Medication Safety Guidance

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Ezetimibe and PCSK9 inhibitors are generally well tolerated in CKD patients.
- Bempedoic acid requires monitoring for elevated blood urea nitrogen, creatinine, and uric acid levels.
- Bile acid sequestrants are not often used due to poor tolerability in CKD patients.
- TG-lowering medications' safety should be reviewed in CKD patients.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Monitoring of blood urea nitrogen, creatinine, and uric acid levels is required for patients on bempedoic acid.
