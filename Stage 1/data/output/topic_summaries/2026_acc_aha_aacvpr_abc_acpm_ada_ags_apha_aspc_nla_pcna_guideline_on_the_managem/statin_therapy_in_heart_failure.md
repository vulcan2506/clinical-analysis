# Statin Therapy in Heart Failure
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Statin Therapy in HFrEF Without ASCVD

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Initiation of lipid-lowering therapy (LLT) is not recommended in adults with heart failure with reduced ejection fraction (HFrEF) who lack clinical atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease (ASCVD) or another indication for LLT.
- Statins do not reduce clinical events or mortality in HFrEF patients without ASCVD.
- Statin therapy may be considered in HFrEF patients with clinical ASCVD or other guideline-directed indications for LLT, as these patients may derive benefit from plaque stabilization and ischemic event reduction.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Presence of HFrEF must be confirmed.
- Exclusion of clinical ASCVD or other LLT indications is required before withholding statin therapy.
- Confirmation of HFrEF diagnosis via echocardiogram or other imaging modality with documented left ventricular ejection fraction ≤40% is required before applying this recommendation.
