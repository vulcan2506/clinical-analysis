# Sex-Specific Cardiovascular Risk Assessment
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Sex-Specific Cardiovascular Risk Assessment

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Incorporate female-specific risk factors (e.g., hypertensive disorders of pregnancy, gestational diabetes, polycystic ovary syndrome) into cardiovascular risk stratification.
- Use risk enhancers such as premature menopause, systemic autoimmune diseases, and breast cancer therapy-related cardiotoxicity for women.
- Apply the American Heart Association’s Life’s Simple 7 metrics tailored for women to assess modifiable risk factors.
- Consider psychosocial stressors and socioeconomic factors as part of risk assessment in women.
- Integrate coronary artery calcium scoring in intermediate-risk women to refine risk prediction.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Documentation of female-specific risk factors (e.g., pregnancy complications, autoimmune diseases) in medical history.
- Use validated risk calculators (e.g., Pooled Cohort Equations for Women) for primary prevention risk assessment.
- Assess menopausal status and hormone therapy use as part of risk evaluation.
- Screen for depression and anxiety in women as potential risk modifiers.
- Evaluate family history of premature cardiovascular disease separately for maternal and paternal lineages.
