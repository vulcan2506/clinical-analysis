# ASCVD Risk Assessment Tools
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** ASCVD Risk Assessment Tools + PREVENT-ASCVD Equations

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Use PREVENT-ASCVD equations for 10-year ASCVD risk estimation in adults aged 30–79 without ASCVD or subclinical atherosclerosis and LDL-C 70–189 mg/dL
- Categorize risk as low (<3%), borderline (3% to <5%), intermediate (5% to <10%), or high (≥10%)
- Incorporate risk enhancers (e.g., hsCRP ≥2 mg/L, family history, ancestry) to personalize risk assessment in borderline-risk patients
- Conduct clinician–patient benefit-risk discussion for LLT initiation at ≥3% 10-year risk
- Consider CAC measurement for reclassification in borderline or intermediate-risk adults
- Consider LLT initiation in individuals with 30-year ASCVD risk ≥10% or high atherogenic lipid levels (e.g., LDL-C ≥160 mg/dL) even if 10-year risk is <3%
- Offer health behavior counseling (diet, lifestyle) to all adults regardless of ASCVD risk level, including those with LDL-C <160 mg/dL and 10-year risk <3%

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Comprehensive patient evaluation including albuminuria, HbA1c, and social determinants of health (e.g., zip code)
- LDL-C and HDL-C measurements for PREVENT-ASCVD input
- Repeat hsCRP measurement (≥2 mg/L on two occasions) for risk enhancer consideration
- Health behavior counseling (diet, lifestyle) for all risk levels
- Consider 30-year risk estimation for individuals with low 10-year risk but high cumulative exposure
- Include zip code for assessment of neighborhood-level social determinants of health in PREVENT-ASCVD risk estimation
- Measure hsCRP on two successive occasions (≥2 mg/L) to confirm elevated risk before considering high-intensity statin therapy in borderline-risk patients
