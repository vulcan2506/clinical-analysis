# ASCVD Risk Threshold
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** ASCVD Risk Threshold for LLT Initiation

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Use PREVENT-ASCVD equations (40%-50% lower than PCE) to estimate 10-year ASCVD risk for primary prevention LLT initiation.
- Set LLT consideration threshold at PREVENT-ASCVD 10-year risk ≥3% (vs. older PCE ≥5%) based on NNT-benefit vs. NND analysis.
- Apply risk-based statin intensity: moderate-intensity for ≥3% risk, high-intensity for higher risk thresholds.
- Consider 30-year risk estimation and exposure duration for younger individuals (<40 years) with low 10-year risk.
- Use PREVENT-ASCVD in high-risk subgroups (diabetes, CKD, HIV) to guide intensification of LLT.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Baseline 10-year ASCVD risk estimation using PREVENT-ASCVD equations for adults aged 30-79 years.
- Documentation of risk enhancers, reproductive risk markers, and competing comorbidities in risk assessment.
- Age-specific considerations for LLT initiation based on RCT inclusion criteria (e.g., 40-75 years for primary prevention trials).
- Risk assessment must account for the long latency periods between risk factor exposure, atherosclerosis development, and ASCVD endpoints, particularly for younger individuals.
- Clinical decision-making for low 10-year risk individuals must incorporate duration of exposure to atherogenic lipoproteins.
- Risk assessment must integrate reproductive risk markers and competing comorbidities alongside quantitative risk estimates.
