# Statin Pharmacokinetic Profile
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Statin Pharmacokinetic Profile

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Atorvastatin has low bioavailability (~14%) with a Tmax of 1–2 hours
- Fluvastatin has higher bioavailability (~24%) with a Tmax <1 hour
- Lovastatin is a pro-drug with very low bioavailability (<5%) and Tmax of 2–4 hours
- Atorvastatin, lovastatin, and simvastatin are P-glycoprotein substrates
- Most statins are highly protein-bound (≥95%) and lipophilic
- Simvastatin has a pro-drug form with very low bioavailability (<5%) and Tmax of 1–2 hours
- Pravastatin and rosuvastatin are hydrophilic statins with low muscle penetration risk due to OATP1B1-mediated hepatic uptake

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Consider drug–drug interactions for P-glycoprotein substrates (e.g., atorvastatin, lovastatin, simvastatin)
- Assess CYP3A4 interactions for atorvastatin and lovastatin
- Evaluate CYP2C9 interactions for fluvastatin
- Consider hydrophilic statins (pravastatin, rosuvastatin) in patients at high risk for myopathy or with multiple drug interactions
- Evaluate OATP1B1 transporter function in patients with hepatic impairment or co-administration of OATP1B1 inhibitors (e.g., cyclosporine)
