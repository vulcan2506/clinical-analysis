# Alirocumab Efficacy in Statin-Intolerant Patients
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Alirocumab Efficacy in Statin-Intolerant Patients

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Alirocumab reduces LDL-C significantly more than ezetimibe in statin-intolerant patients
- Alirocumab is associated with significantly fewer musculoskeletal side effects than atorvastatin
- PCSK9 monoclonal antibodies are recommended for patients requiring additional LDL-C lowering due to statin-attributed side effects
- Alirocumab is the preferred PCSK9 inhibitor in adults with clinical ASCVD experiencing statin-attributed muscle symptoms
- Alirocumab is used when patients are unable to achieve recommended treatment goals on bempedoic acid with or without ezetimibe
- Inclisiran is reasonable for patients unable to tolerate or obtain evolocumab or alirocumab, or who prefer less frequent dosing

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Patient must be unable to tolerate ≥2 statins
- Statin-attributed muscle symptoms must be present (secondary causes excluded)
- Patient must be unable to achieve recommended treatment goals on bempedoic acid with or without ezetimibe
- Patient must have clinical ASCVD
- Patient must be unable to tolerate or obtain evolocumab or alirocumab for inclisiran consideration
- Statin-attributed muscle symptoms must be confirmed during a run-in period with placebo to exclude non-statin causes
- CAC score may be considered to aid decisions about preventive therapy in patients reluctant to reinitiate statins or other lipid-lowering therapy
