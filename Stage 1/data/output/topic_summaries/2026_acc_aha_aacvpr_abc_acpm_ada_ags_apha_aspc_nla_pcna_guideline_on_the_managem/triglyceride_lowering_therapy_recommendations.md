 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on

**Clinical Recommendation:** Triglyceride-lowering therapy recommendations

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Triglyceride lowering with pemafibrate is evaluated for reducing cardiovascular risk in specific patient populations.
- Statins are noted for their effects on triglyceride metabolism, particularly in patients with elevated triglycerides.
- Modest weight loss is emphasized for its beneficial effects on triglyceride levels and overall cardiovascular risk factors.
- Marine n-3 fatty acids (e.g., EPA/DHA) are considered for triglyceride lowering, especially in post-myocardial infarction patients.
- Familial chylomicronemia syndrome (FCS) requires specialized diagnostic and therapeutic approaches, including the use of the NAFCS scoring tool.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Diagnosis of familial chylomicronemia syndrome (FCS) should use the NAFCS scoring tool or expert panel recommendations.
- Patients with clinically significant drug-drug interactions involving statins require careful management to avoid adverse effects.
- Diagnosis of familial chylomicronemia syndrome (FCS) should utilize the North American Familial Chylomicronemia Calculator (NAFCS) or expert panel recommendations.
