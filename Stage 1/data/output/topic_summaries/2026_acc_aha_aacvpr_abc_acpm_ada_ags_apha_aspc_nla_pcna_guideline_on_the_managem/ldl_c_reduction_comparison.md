# LDL-C Reduction Comparison
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** LDL-C Reduction Comparison

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Rosuvastatin showed an LDL-C reduction of 37.9% (95% CI, -42.1 to -33.6), which was superior to supplements or placebo (P<0.001).
- Dietary supplements (in the doses studied) did not demonstrate a significant decrease in LDL-C compared with placebo when using a hierarchical statistical testing approach.
- The guideline specifies that the LDL-C reduction with rosuvastatin was observed in patients with baseline LDL-C levels ≥100 mg/dL (or ≥2.6 mmol/L)

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Lipid measurements for comparison were required to be fasting samples (fasting ≥9 hours)
