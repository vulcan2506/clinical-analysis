# CKD-Specific ASCVD Risk Management
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** CKD-Specific ASCVD Risk Management

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- CKD is a risk factor for cardiovascular events, with moderate CKD conferring similar ASCVD risk as established ASCVD.
- In adults with elevated LDL-C, CKD favors initiation of lipid-lowering therapy (LLT).
- Aggressive LLT (including ezetimibe and PCSK9 mAb) is effective and safe in adults with CKD and ASCVD.
- In adults on dialysis, statin initiation is not beneficial; continuation may be reasonable based on clinical judgment.
- Statin, ezetimibe, and PCSK9 mAb have similar safety profiles in CKD patients compared to non-CKD patients.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Moderate CKD not on dialysis requires consideration of LLT initiation in adults with elevated LDL-C.
- Clinical judgment is required for LLT in dialysis patients, considering longevity and comorbidities.
- Maximally tolerated statin therapy is a prerequisite before adding ezetimibe or PCSK9 mAb in CKD patients with ASCVD.
