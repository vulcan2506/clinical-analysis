# 2026 LDL-C Target Guideline
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** LDL-C Targets + Genetic Testing for FH

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- LDL-C reduction of ≥50% from baseline and a goal LDL-C of ≤130 mg/dL is generally advised
- LDL-C can be lowered to ≤100 mg/dL in patients with additional risk factors
- Genetic testing for FH is recommended to confirm diagnosis, guide treatment, and inform cascade screening
- Genetic testing is prioritized in children/adolescents with unclear phenotype or insufficient response to standard lipid-lowering therapy (LLT)
- Engage patients/families with genetic counselors and address financial/insurance concerns
- Initiate lipid-lowering therapy (LLT) at diagnosis in infants with HoFH, often in early childhood due to risk of coronary obstruction

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Patient and family preference should guide lipid-lowering therapy intensity
- Individualized evaluation by a pediatric cardiologist and lipid specialist for HoFH
- Genetic testing for FH has a Tier 1 indication (CDC, 2012)
- Diagnostic criteria relying solely on phenotype may miss up to half of children with pathogenic FH variants
- Consider genetic testing in adults with borderline LDL-C, unclear family history, or poor response to standard LLT if clinical suspicion of FH is high
