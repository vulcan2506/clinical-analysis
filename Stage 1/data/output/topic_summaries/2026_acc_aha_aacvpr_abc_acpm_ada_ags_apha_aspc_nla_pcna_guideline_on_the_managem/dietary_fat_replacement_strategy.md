# Dietary Fat Replacement Strategy
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Dietary Fat Replacement Strategy

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Replace saturated fats with unsaturated fats (polyunsaturated or monounsaturated) to reduce LDL-C levels
- Prioritize healthy eating patterns that limit saturated fat intake
- Increase intake of sources like fish, nuts, flax/chia seeds, olive oil, avocados, and vegetable oils (corn, sunflower, soybean)
- Avoid excess processed sugar and ultra-processed foods to maximize benefits
