- Included diverse specialists (e.g., cardiologists, primary care physicians, pharmacists, dietitians, nurses) and patient advocates

- Led by a chair and chair-elect with formal leadership roles (e.g., Catherine M. Otto, Sunil V. Rao)
- Included members with subspecialty expertise (e.g., cardiac imaging, interventional cardiology, geriatrics, lipidology)
- Included representatives from 13 professional organizations (e.g., Association of Black Cardiologists, American College of Preventive Medicine, Preventive Cardiovascular Nurses Association)
- Included a cardiothoracic surgeon as a committee member
- Included members with dual leadership roles (e.g., Chair and Chair-Elect with formal titles and affiliations)
