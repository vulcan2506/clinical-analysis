# HeFH Risk Assessment
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** HeFH Risk Assessment

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Standard risk assessment tools (e.g., PCE, PREVENT-ASCVD) developed for the general population should not be used to calculate 10- or 30-year ASCVD risk in individuals with HeFH.
- HeFH is associated with a 2- to 4-fold higher lifetime ASCVD risk compared to the general population, with young adults (<35 years) having up to a 17-fold higher risk.
- FH-specific risk scores (e.g., Montreal-FH-SCORE, SAFEHEART, FH-Risk Score) are recommended for ASCVD risk estimation in HeFH populations.
- Calcium scoring (CAC) is not indicated in individuals with HeFH to defer lipid-lowering therapy (LLT), as CAC=0 should not be used to
- or justify deferral of statin therapy.
- FH-specific risk scores (e.g., Montreal-FH-SCORE, SAFEHEART, FH-Risk Score) are not yet validated for long-term (30-year) ASCVD risk prediction in diverse FH cohorts, including non-European ethnic groups.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Panel-based genetic testing for pathogenic/likely pathogenic FH variants is beneficial in adults with possible, probable, or definite FH to identify high-risk individuals and facilitate cascade screening.
- Panel-based genetic testing is useful in adults with severe hypercholesterolemia (LDL-C ≥190 mg/dL) without secondary causes to identify FH and higher ASCVD risk.
- Panel-based genetic testing for FH requires confirmation of pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants to identify high-risk individuals and facilitate cascade screening.
