**Clinical Recommendation:** Clinical Practice Guideline Framework Updates (2025 AHA/ACC)

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Incorporates cost/value methodology into guideline development (update from 2014 statement).
- Establishes core principles and development processes for guideline creation.
- Integrates FDA processes into clinical practice guideline methodology.
- Explicitly requires multidisciplinary expert panel composition (including clinicians, methodologists, and patient representatives) for guideline development
- Mandates systematic review of evidence using GRADE methodology for all recommendations
- Incorporates patient-centered outcomes (e.g., quality of life, functional status) alongside clinical endpoints in value assessments

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Adherence to updated cost/value assessment frameworks in guideline recommendations.
- Compliance with core principles and development processes for transparency and rigor.
- Guideline recommendations must align with FDA-approved indications for pharmacologic therapies
- Cost-effectiveness analyses must use standardized health economic models (e.g., Markov models) for comparative interventions
- Transparency in panel voting conflicts of interest and recusal processes for guideline authors
