# Cholesterol Lowering Evidence Base
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Cholesterol Lowering Evidence Base

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Statin therapy is supported by long-term randomized trials (e.g., WOSCOPS) for primary prevention in men with LDL-C ≥190 mg/dL.
- Statins demonstrate efficacy in reducing cardiovascular events in familial hypercholesterolemia cohorts.
- Secondary causes of dyslipidemia must be excluded before initiating lipid-lowering therapy.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Exclusion of secondary causes of dyslipidemia (e.g., hypothyroidism, nephrotic syndrome) prior to treatment initiation.
- Long-term follow-up (e.g., 20-year observational data) to assess statin efficacy in primary prevention.
