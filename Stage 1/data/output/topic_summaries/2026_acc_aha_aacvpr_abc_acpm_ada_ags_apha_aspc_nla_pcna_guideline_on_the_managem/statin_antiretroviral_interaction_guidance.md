# Statin-Antiretroviral Interaction Guidance
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Statin-Antiretroviral Interaction Guidance

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Titrate atorvastatin dose carefully when co-administered with atazanavir, using the lowest effective dose while monitoring for toxicities.
- No dose adjustment needed for rosuvastatin when co-administered with all NNRTIs.
- No dose adjustment needed for any statin when co-administered with all NRTIs.
- Avoid lovastatin and simvastatin when co-administered with elvitegravir/cobicistat due to strong CYP3A4 inhibition and elevated risk of myopathy/rhabdomyolysis.
