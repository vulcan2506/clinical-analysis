# Statin Mitigation Strategy
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Statin Mitigation Strategy for Drug-Drug Interactions

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Avoid coadministered interacting medications when possible
- Use an alternative statin without the drug–drug interaction
- Limit statin dose based on the specific statin and interaction severity
