# Individualized Risk Discussion
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Individualized Benefit-Risk Discussion

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Engage in a discussion of the patient's ASCVD risk, healthy lifestyle as the foundation of risk reduction, expected risk reduction benefits from LLT, possible harms and DDI, costs, and patient preferences to make individualized treatment decisions.
- Use simplified, plain language and visual aids to explain the patient's estimated ASCVD risk and available treatment options for risk reduction.
- Provide the patient an opportunity to verbalize their values, attitudes, abilities, concerns, and personal goals for making sustained lifestyle changes and taking medications.
- Use a checklist to facilitate individualized benefit-risk discussions, including ASCVD risk assessment, healthy lifestyle habits, potential net clinical benefit of pharmacotherapy, cost and convenience considerations, and shared decision-making.
- Consider selective use of a CAC scan or other imaging (e.g., non-ECG-gated chest CT, carotid ultrasound) to refine ASCVD risk estimates.
- Reinforce treatment decisions through iterative discussions with multiple clinicians (e.g., pharmacists, nurses) to improve adherence and persistence with lipid-lowering therapy (LLT).
- Address patient questions and concerns in future encounters to reaffirm benefits, review treatment response, and emphasize adherence.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Perform ASCVD risk assessment using tools like the PREVENT-ASCVD Calculator and explain risk in absolute and relative terms.
- Review lifestyle habits (diet, physical activity, weight/BMI, tobacco use) and provide relevant advice, materials, or referrals.
- Recommend statin as first-line therapy and discuss potential risk reduction, adverse effects, and drug-drug interactions.
- Use decision aids (e.g., pamphlets, videos, web-based tools) to clarify evidence-based options and support shared decision-making.
- Consider reproductive age risk markers during ASCVD risk assessment to tailor discussions about lipid-lowering therapy (LLT) and potential teratogenic risks.
