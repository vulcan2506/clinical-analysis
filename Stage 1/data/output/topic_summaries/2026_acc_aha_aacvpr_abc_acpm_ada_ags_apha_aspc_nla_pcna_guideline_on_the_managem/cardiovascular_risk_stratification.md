# Cardiovascular Risk Stratification
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Non-HDL Cholesterol for Risk Stratification + Familial Hypercholesterolemia Screening

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Use non-HDL cholesterol as a primary metric for population-based cardiovascular risk stratification.
- Implement universal or targeted blood cholesterol screening in youth to identify familial hypercholesterolemia early.
- Apply child-parent screening strategies to detect familial hypercholesterolemia in primary care settings.
- Prioritize statin therapy initiation in children with familial hypercholesterolemia based on long-term follow-up evidence.
- Incorporate youth risk factor assessment (e.g., obesity, hypertension, dyslipidemia) into adult cardiovascular risk prediction models to refine long-term ASCVD risk stratification.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Screening must include lipid panels with non-HDL cholesterol calculation for accurate risk assessment.
- Familial hypercholesterolemia screening in youth requires confirmation via genetic testing or clinical criteria.
- Confirm familial hypercholesterolemia diagnosis in youth using Dutch Lipid Clinic Network criteria or Simon Broome criteria prior to genetic testing.
