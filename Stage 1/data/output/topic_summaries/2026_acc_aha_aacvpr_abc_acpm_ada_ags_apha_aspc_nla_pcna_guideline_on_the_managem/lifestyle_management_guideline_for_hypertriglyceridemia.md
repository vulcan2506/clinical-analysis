# Lifestyle Management Guideline for Hypertriglyceridemia
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Lifestyle Management for Hypertriglyceridemia (TG 150–499 mg/dL)

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Diet low in added sugars, refined carbohydrates, and saturated fat
- Minimize alcohol intake
- Shared decision-making intervention for TG ≥150 to 499 mg/dL
- Total fat intake: 30%-35% of calories
- Added sugars: <6% of calories
- Eliminate added sugars entirely in patients with severe hypertriglyceridemia (TG ≥1000 mg/dL)

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Fasting triglyceride (TG) levels of 150 to 499 mg/dL (1.7–5.6 mmol/L)
- Shared decision-making intervention for TG ≥150 to 499 mg/dL
- Shared decision-making intervention for TG ≥500 to 999 mg/dL
- Shared decision-making intervention for TG ≥1000 mg/dL
