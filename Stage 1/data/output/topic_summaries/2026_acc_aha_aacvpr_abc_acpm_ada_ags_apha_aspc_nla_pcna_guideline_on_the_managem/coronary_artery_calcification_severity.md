# Coronary Artery Calcification Severity
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Coronary Artery Calcification Severity Stratification

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- CAC score ≥1000 indicates extensive multivessel and diffuse extra-coronary calcification
- Higher CAC scores correlate with increased likelihood of severe atherosclerotic burden
- CAC severity is used to refine cardiovascular risk stratification beyond traditional risk factors
- CAC score ≥1000 is associated with a higher likelihood of plaque instability and systemic calcification processes due to pro-inflammatory and pro-calcific pathway activation

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- CAC scoring requires non-contrast cardiac CT imaging
- CAC score interpretation should be integrated with clinical risk factors and lipid profiles
- CAC scoring should be performed using standardized non-contrast cardiac CT protocols with Agatston scoring methodology
