# Guideline Retirement and Replacement
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Guideline Retirement and Replacement

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- This guideline retires and replaces the '2018 AHA/ACC/AACVPR/AAPA/ABC/ACPM/ADA/AGS/APhA/ASPC/NLA/PCNA Guideline on the Management of Blood Cholesterol'.
- The scope is expanded to address evaluation, management, and monitoring of patients with dyslipidemias, including high blood cholesterol, hypertriglyceridemia, and elevated Lp(a).
- The guideline is retitled to reflect evolving understanding of ASCVD risk associated with atherogenic lipoproteins beyond LDL particles.
- Includes new recommendations based on high-quality evidence from major randomized, placebo-controlled cardiovascular outcomes trials published since 2018.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Implementation of newer therapeutic agents that reduce atherogenic lipoproteins.
- Use of latest advances in CVD risk assessment relevant to dyslipidemia management in primary and secondary prevention settings.
- Clinicians should review Table 2 for a list of previously published ACC/AHA guidelines and other relevant clinical publications deemed pertinent to this guideline effort.
