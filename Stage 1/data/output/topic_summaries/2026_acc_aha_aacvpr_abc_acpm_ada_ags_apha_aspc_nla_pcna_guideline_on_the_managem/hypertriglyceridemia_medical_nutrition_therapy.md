# Hypertriglyceridemia Medical Nutrition Therapy
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Hypertriglyceridemia Medical Nutrition Therapy

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Tailored medical nutrition therapy is critical for TG ≥1000 mg/dL, especially with FCS or MCS.
- Eliminate alcohol, added sugars, and limit total fat to 10%-15% of calories until chylomicrons clear and TG improve.
- Medical nutrition therapy by an RDN is a core component for dyslipidemia and CKM syndrome management.
- Dietary advice should be individualized based on TG levels, clinical syndrome, and patient preferences.
- Greater number of RDN sessions correlates with improved risk factor optimization and long-term adherence.
- Very limited total fat intake (10%-15% of calories) should be maintained until chylomicrons are cleared and TG levels significantly improve, not just until initial improvement.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- TG ≥500 to 700 mg/dL saturates lipoprotein lipase, necessitating intervention to prevent chylomicronemia and pancreatitis.
- RDN assessment and counseling are required for designing specific eating plans, including potential use of medium-chain TG oil.
- Assessment of potential secondary causes of hypertriglyceridemia is required before initiating medical nutrition therapy.
