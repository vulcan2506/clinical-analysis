# Primary Prevention Guideline
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Primary Prevention Health Behavior Counseling

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Provide health behavior recommendations to all adults assessed for primary prevention of ASCVD
- Counsel adults aged 30 to 59 with low risk (<3%) and LDL-C <160 mg/dL to reduce LDL-C and ASCVD risk
- Integrate benefit-risk discussions for consideration of lipid-lowering therapy alongside behavioral counseling
- Provide benefit-risk discussion for consideration of lipid-lowering therapy (LLT) alongside health behavior recommendations in all adults assessed for primary prevention of ASCVD

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Patient must be an adult aged 30 to 79 years
- Patient must have LDL-C levels between 70 to 189 mg/dL (1.8-4.9 mmol/L)
- Risk assessment must include 10-year and 30-year estimated ASCVD risk estimates
- Risk assessment must include 30-year estimated ASCVD risk estimate for adults aged 30 to 59 years
