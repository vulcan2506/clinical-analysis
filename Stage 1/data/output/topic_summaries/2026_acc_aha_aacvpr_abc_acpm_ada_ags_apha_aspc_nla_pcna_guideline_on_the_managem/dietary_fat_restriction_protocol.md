# Dietary Fat Restriction Protocol
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Dietary Fat Restriction Protocol for Triglyceride Management

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Referral to an RDN and lipid specialist advised for dietary fat restriction.
- Limitation of total fat to 10% to 15% of total daily intake under RDN guidance for patients with FCS.
- Individually tailored total fat limitation under RDN guidance for TG disorders outside FCS.
- Diet low in added sugar, refined carbohydrates, and saturated fat, with no alcohol for TG levels 500–999 mg/dL.
- Very low total fat and refined carbohydrates, with elimination of alcohol and added sugars for TG levels ≥1000 mg/dL.
- Clinicians may opt to reduce total fat as a percent of calories to 10%–15% in patients with a history of pancreatitis or those at the higher end of this range (not limited to FCS).

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Referral to an RDN is necessary for dietary fat restriction.
- TG levels of 500 to 999 mg/dL or ≥1000 mg/dL for specific dietary fat restriction protocols.
- Guideline-directed physical activity (≥150 min/wk) for TG levels ≥150 mg/dL.
