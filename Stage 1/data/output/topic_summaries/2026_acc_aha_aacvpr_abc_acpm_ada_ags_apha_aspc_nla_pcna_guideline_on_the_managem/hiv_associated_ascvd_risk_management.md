# HIV-Associated ASCVD Risk Management
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** HIV-Associated ASCVD Risk Management with Pitavastatin

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- PLHIV have increased ASCVD risk due to insulin resistance, atherogenic dyslipidemia, lipodystrophy, and immune dysfunction.
- Pitavastatin 4 mg daily reduces first MACE risk by 35% in PLHIV (REPRIEVE trial).
- Pitavastatin was selected for its lack of CYP3A4 metabolism, minimizing drug interactions.
- Pitavastatin’s lack of metabolism through P450 CYP3A4 reduces potential drug interactions with antiretroviral therapies (ART) compared with other statins.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Patients must be living with HIV (PLHIV).
- ASCVD risk should be low to moderate for pitavastatin eligibility.
- Patients must have low to moderate ASCVD risk for pitavastatin eligibility, as demonstrated in the REPRIEVE trial.
