# Alirocumab Clinical Evidence Integration
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Alirocumab Clinical Evidence Integration

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Alirocumab is recommended for patients with acute coronary syndrome (ACS) to reduce cardiovascular outcomes.
- Achieved LDL-C levels are associated with long-term cardiovascular and safety outcomes in patients treated with alirocumab.
- Cost-effectiveness of alirocumab has been evaluated in patients with acute coronary syndromes.
- Alirocumab is considered in high-risk patients with hypercholesterolemia and maximally tolerated statin therapy.
- Achieved apolipoprotein B (apoB) levels are now considered alongside LDL-C for long-term cardiovascular risk assessment in alirocumab-treated patients.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Patients must have acute coronary syndrome (ACS) for alirocumab initiation.
- Maximally tolerated statin therapy must be attempted before considering alirocumab.
- Alirocumab should be initiated within 4–12 weeks after an acute coronary syndrome event for optimal benefit.
