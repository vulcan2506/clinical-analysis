# Statin Therapy in HFrEF
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Statin Therapy in HFrEF

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Two large RCTs (CORONA and GISSI HF) showed no significant reduction in hard ASCVD events or primary outcomes with rosuvastatin in HFrEF patients.
- A third trial (PEARL) found no benefit of pitavastatin on death or hospitalization for worsening HF.
- Meta-analyses demonstrated no significant reduction in sudden cardiac death, total mortality, or HF hospitalization with statins in HFrEF.
- A prior meta-analysis combining CORONA and GISSI-HF found a significant reduction in MI with rosuvastatin in ischemic HFrEF patients.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Patients with chronic HFrEF (NYHA class II to IV) and LVEF <40% (or <45% in PEARL) were included in trials.
- Ischemic etiology was a subgroup in some analyses.
- LVEF <45% without consideration of etiology (PEARL trial)
