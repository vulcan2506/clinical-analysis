# ASCVD Risk Reduction Evidence
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** ASCVD Risk Reduction Evidence

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Greater reductions in LDL-C are accompanied by greater reductions in the risk of ASCVD
- Robust randomized controlled trials (CVOTs) support LDL lowering for both primary and secondary prevention
- Increasing evidence supports the efficacy and safety of nonstatin therapies alongside statins
- Guidelines emphasize addressing therapeutic inertia and improving adherence to lipid-lowering therapy (LLT)
- Shared decision-making models are encouraged for personalized patient-clinician discussions
- Early institution of lipid-lowering therapy in low- or borderline-risk individuals, such as younger patients with subclinical atherosclerosis or elevated atherogenic lipoproteins

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Implementation of evidence-based lipid-lowering therapies (LLTs) guided by randomized controlled trials (CVOTs)
- Consideration of patient engagement strategies to improve adherence to therapy
- Use of apoB alongside or instead of LDL-C to guide future treatment recommendations
