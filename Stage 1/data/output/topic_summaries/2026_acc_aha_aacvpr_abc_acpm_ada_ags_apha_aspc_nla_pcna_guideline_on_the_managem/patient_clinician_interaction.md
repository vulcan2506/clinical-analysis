# Patient-Clinician Interaction
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Patient-Clinician Interaction & Shared Decision-Making

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Clinicians and patients navigate treatment choices together using decision aids to communicate trade-offs between options.
- Individualized communication is refined to meet diverse patient needs, literacy levels, and cultural backgrounds.
- Benefit-risk discussions are conducted as a series of ongoing interactions rather than one-time events.
- Digital health tools are utilized to augment clinician–patient discussions and improve workflow efficiency.
- Long-term treatment adherence is fostered by building trust, buy-in, and engagement regarding lifetime therapy.
- Clinicians are encouraged to engage in ongoing studies and quality improvement initiatives to refine patient-clinician interaction strategies for dyslipidemia management.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Use of decision aids to help communicate trade-offs between treatment options.
- Assessment of individual patient literacy levels and cultural backgrounds for tailored communication.
- Ongoing series of discussions for individualized benefit-risk analysis.
- Clinicians must consider the expanded therapeutic armamentarium (including nonstatin therapies) when tailoring individualized benefit-risk discussions.
