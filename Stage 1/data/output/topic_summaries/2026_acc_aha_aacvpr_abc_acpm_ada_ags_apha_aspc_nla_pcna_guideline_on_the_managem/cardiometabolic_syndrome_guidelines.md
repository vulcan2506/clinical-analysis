# Cardiometabolic Syndrome Guidelines
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Cardiometabolic Syndrome Risk Stratification

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Integrate Life's Essential 8 metrics (diet, physical activity, nicotine exposure, sleep health, BMI, blood lipids, blood glucose, and blood pressure) for cardiovascular health assessment.
- Use subclinical atherosclerosis imaging (e.g., coronary artery calcium scoring) to refine risk stratification beyond traditional risk factors.
- Apply updated AHA/ACC cholesterol guidelines for statin therapy initiation based on LDL-C thresholds and ASCVD risk.
- Consider elevated Lp(a) ≥50 mg/dL as a risk enhancer for intensifying lipid-lowering therapy.
- Evaluate metabolic syndrome components (central obesity, hypertension, dysglycemia, and atherogenic dyslipidemia) as part of cardiometabolic risk profiling.
- Incorporate non-HDL-C and apolipoprotein B (ApoB) as preferred lipid measures for assessing atherogenic risk in patients with cardiometabolic syndrome (CMS), particularly when triglycerides are elevated.
- Use high-sensitivity C-reactive protein (hs-CRP) ≥2.0 mg/L as a risk-enhancing factor to guide intensification of statin therapy in intermediate-risk CMS patients.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Comprehensive cardiovascular risk assessment using validated tools (e.g., Pooled Cohort Equations) before statin initiation.
- Documentation of Life's Essential 8 metrics in patient records for longitudinal health tracking.
- Baseline lipid panel (including LDL-C, HDL-C, triglycerides, and non-HDL-C) prior to therapeutic decisions.
- Coronary artery calcium scoring for intermediate-risk patients to guide intensification of preventive therapies.
- Fasting glucose or HbA1c measurement to assess dysglycemia as part of metabolic syndrome evaluation.
- Documentation of waist circumference (or waist-to-height ratio) as part of metabolic syndrome evaluation to assess central obesity.
- Perform baseline evaluation of non-HDL-C and ApoB in patients with CMS or suspected insulin resistance, even if LDL-C is not markedly elevated.
