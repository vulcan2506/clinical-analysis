**Clinical Recommendation:** Weight Loss and Lipid Outcomes Meta-Analysis

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Weight reduction is associated with improvements in blood lipids and lipoproteins, including reductions in LDL-C and triglycerides, and increases in HDL-C.
- Systematic review and meta-analysis demonstrate that weight loss in overweight and obese adults improves serum lipid profiles.
- Exercise is linked to reductions in metabolic abnormalities, including improvements in lipid metabolism and cardiovascular risk factors.
- Weight loss interventions should be considered in overweight or obese patients with dyslipidemia to improve lipid outcomes.
- Meta-analytic evidence supports weight loss as a primary strategy for lipid management in overweight and obese adults.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Weight loss interventions should be considered in overweight or obese patients with dyslipidemia to improve lipid outcomes.
