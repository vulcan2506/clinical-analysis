# Statin Interaction Management Guidelines
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Statin Interaction Management Guidelines

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Prioritize statin dose reduction or temporary discontinuation for patients with significant drug-drug interactions (e.g., strong CYP3A4 inhibitors like clarithromycin or protease inhibitors).
- Use pravastatin or rosuvastatin as preferred alternatives for patients requiring concurrent therapy with interacting drugs due to their minimal CYP3A4 metabolism.
- Monitor liver enzymes (ALT) and creatine kinase (CK) in patients with suspected myopathy or rhabdomyolysis risk from statin interactions.
- Adjust statin therapy based on the severity of interaction and patient-specific ASCVD risk stratification.
- Consult clinical decision pathways for persistent hypertriglyceridemia in patients on interacting medications.
- Consider temporary discontinuation of statin therapy during acute illness or surgery in patients with high interaction risk (e.g., those on strong CYP3A4 inhibitors) to reduce myopathy risk.
- Evaluate for drug-disease interactions (e.g., severe hepatic impairment) that may exacerbate statin-associated adverse effects in interaction scenarios.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Baseline liver enzyme (ALT) and creatine kinase (CK) levels before initiating statin therapy in high-risk patients.
- Assessment of concurrent medications for potential CYP3A4 or other metabolic pathway interactions.
- 10-year ASCVD risk calculation for primary prevention decisions in statin interaction scenarios.
- Documentation of interaction severity and rationale for statin choice or dose adjustment.
- Follow ACC/AHA decision pathways for managing ASCVD risk in patients with persistent hypertriglyceridemia.
- Assess renal function (eGFR) in patients with potential statin interactions, particularly when using hydrophilic statins (e.g., pravastatin, rosuvastatin) due to altered clearance.
- Document patient adherence to interacting medications and statin therapy to optimize interaction management decisions.
