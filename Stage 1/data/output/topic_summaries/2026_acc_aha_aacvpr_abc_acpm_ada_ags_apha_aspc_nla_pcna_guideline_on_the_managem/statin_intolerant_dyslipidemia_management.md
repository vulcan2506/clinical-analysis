# Statin-Intolerant Dyslipidemia Management
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Statin-Intolerant Dyslipidemia Management

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Use ezetimibe therapy in patients with statin-attributed side effects due to its efficacy and generic availability.
- Consider PCSK9 monoclonal antibody (mAb) therapy for hypercholesterolemia in patients intolerant to statins, supported by RCTs.
- Evaluate patients with statin-attributed muscle symptoms for alternative lipid-lowering therapies.
- Monitor LDL-C reduction and cardiovascular outcomes in statin-intolerant patients receiving alternative therapies.
- Consider bempedoic acid as an alternative lipid-lowering therapy in patients with statin-intolerant dyslipidemia, particularly when muscle symptoms are the primary concern

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Documentation of statin-attributed side effects as the reason for discontinuing statin therapy.
- Assessment of patient risk stratification (e.g., ASCVD risk) to guide alternative therapy selection.
- Documentation of the specific type and severity of statin-attributed muscle symptoms (e.g., myalgia, myopathy, or rhabdomyolysis) to guide selection of alternative therapies
