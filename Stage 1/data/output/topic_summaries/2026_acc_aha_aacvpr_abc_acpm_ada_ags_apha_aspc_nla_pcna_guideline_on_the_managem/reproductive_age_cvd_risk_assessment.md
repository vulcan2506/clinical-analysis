# Reproductive-Age CVD Risk Assessment
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Reproductive-Age CVD Risk Assessment

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- CVD mortality rates among younger persons, including those of childbearing age, continue to rise.
- Lifestyle management with diet and exercise is paramount for reproductive-age individuals.
- Optimization of pharmacotherapy can modify cardiovascular risk in individuals considering pregnancy.
- Clinicians must balance cardiovascular risk against potential in utero exposure to pharmacotherapy.
- FDA now recommends individualizing risks and benefits regarding statin use during pregnancy.
- Maternal age is increasing in the United States, contributing to higher baseline cardiovascular risk in reproductive-age individuals.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Preconception cardiovascular risk assessment and CVD risk factor modification.
- Identification of childbearing individuals at increased risk for CVD or adverse pregnancy outcomes.
- Recognition of physiological increases in TC, TG, and LDL-C during pregnancy.
- Assessment of plaque burden in addition to traditional risk factors (e.g., hypertension, dyslipidemia) for reproductive-age individuals.
