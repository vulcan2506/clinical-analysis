# Cardiovascular Risk in Diabetes
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Cardiovascular Risk Stratification in Diabetes

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Type 2 diabetes is more hazardous and lethal than type 1 diabetes for long-term cardiovascular complications
- Diabetes and prior coronary heart disease are not necessarily risk equivalent for future coronary heart disease events
- Albuminuria and renal function are predictors of cardiovascular events and mortality in type 2 diabetes
- Peripheral arterial disease is an independent predictor of coronary heart disease and stroke in type 2 diabetes
- Diabetic retinopathy is prospectively associated with increased cardiovascular disease risk

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Assess albuminuria and renal function for cardiovascular risk stratification in type 2 diabetes
- Evaluate presence of peripheral arterial disease for risk prediction in type 2 diabetes
- Screen for diabetic retinopathy as a marker of elevated cardiovascular risk
- Assess LDL-C levels to determine eligibility for intensive lowering therapy in high-risk diabetes patients
- Evaluate statin intolerance status before considering non-statin therapies such as bempedoic acid, PCSK9 inhibitors, or inclisiran
