# Nutrition Therapy in Dyslipidemia Management
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on

**Clinical Recommendation:** Nutrition Therapy in Dyslipidemia Management

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Medical nutrition therapy (MNT) is the essential cornerstone for treating severe hypertriglyceridemia, even with pharmaceutical interventions.
- Registered dietitian nutritionists (RDNs) should be integrated into team-based care for dyslipidemia management.
- MNT by RDNs is effective in reducing LDL-C and improving lipid profiles in adults with dyslipidemia.
- MNT provided by RDNs is cost-effective and reduces healthcare costs in dyslipidemia management.
- Dietary interventions are recommended as a primary or adjunctive strategy to lower LDL-C and manage ASCVD risk.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Referral to a registered dietitian nutritionist (RDN) for medical nutrition therapy.
- Integration of RDNs into primary care or lipid management teams.
- Evidence-based dietary interventions tailored to individual lipid profiles and cardiovascular risk.
