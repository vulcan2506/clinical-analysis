# Secondary ASCVD Prevention Guideline
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Secondary ASCVD Prevention in Very High-Risk Adults

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Goal LDL-C lowered to <55 mg/dL (1.4 mmol/L) for very high-risk ASCVD patients.
- Use ezetimibe and PCSK9 monoclonal antibodies (evolocumab, alirocumab) to achieve aggressive LDL-C targets.
- Consider bempedoic acid (Class 2a) for patients with statin intolerance or as adjunct therapy.
- Monitor for MACE reduction with inclisiran (50% LDL-C reduction) pending CV outcome trials.
- Prioritize high-intensity statin therapy with add-on therapies for recurrent events.
- Prioritize lower LDL-C targets for younger ASCVD patients regardless of very high-risk status due to longer cumulative exposure.
- Use annual cost-effectiveness analysis specifically for PCSK9 inhibitors in patients with LDL-C >100 mg/dL.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Patients must meet criteria for very high risk: multiple major ASCVD events or 1 major event with multiple high-risk conditions.
- Maximally tolerated statin therapy must be initiated before adding non-statin agents.
- Baseline LDL-C and apoB levels should be measured to guide therapy.
- Annual cost-effectiveness analysis recommended for PCSK9 inhibitors in LDL-C >100 mg/dL.
- Consider patient preferences for administration route (e.g., injectable vs oral) and cost barriers.
- Patients must be on maximally tolerated statin therapy before initiating inclisiran.
- Stratify risk using ≥3 high-risk indicators to determine benefit from ezetimibe intensification.
