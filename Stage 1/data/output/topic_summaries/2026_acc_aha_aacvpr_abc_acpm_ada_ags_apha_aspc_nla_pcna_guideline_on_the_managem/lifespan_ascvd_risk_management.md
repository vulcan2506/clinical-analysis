# Lifespan ASCVD Risk Management
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Lifespan ASCVD Risk Management

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Primary prevention of ASCVD requires early attention to risk factors, with cumulative lifetime exposure to atherogenic lipoproteins closely linked to ASCVD events.
- In younger adults, prioritize lifetime risk estimation and health behavior modification.
- Statin therapy is the recommended initial drug choice for primary prevention due to safety, tolerability, efficacy, cost-effectiveness, and strong evidence for ASCVD risk reduction.
- Additional therapies may be used to achieve optimal LDL-C lowering as an adjunct to statin therapy.
- Consider non-statin therapies (e.g., ezetimibe, PCSK9 inhibitors) for patients with severe hypercholesterolemia (LDL-C ≥190 mg/dL) who do not achieve optimal LDL-C lowering with maximally tolerated statin therapy.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Estimation of lifetime ASCVD risk in younger adults.
- Management of severe hypercholesterolemia (LDL-C ≥190 mg/dL) as a major risk category in primary prevention.
- Documentation of family history of premature ASCVD (e.g., first-degree relatives with ASCVD events before age 55 in men or 65 in women) as part of risk stratification in younger adults.
