**Clinical Recommendation:** Weight Management in Diabetes Care for Dyslipidemia

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Counseling and treatment to achieve weight reduction are recommended for individuals with overweight or obesity and dyslipidemia.
- Maintenance of a healthy weight is emphasized to improve dyslipidemia outcomes.
- Intensive lifestyle interventions (e.g., structured diet, physical activity, and behavioral therapy) should be employed to achieve weight reduction in individuals with overweight or obesity and dyslipidemia.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Individuals must have overweight or obesity with concurrent dyslipidemia.
- Weight management strategies should be tailored to the individual's diabetes type (Type 1 or Type 2), with consideration of glycemic control and hypoglycemia risk.
