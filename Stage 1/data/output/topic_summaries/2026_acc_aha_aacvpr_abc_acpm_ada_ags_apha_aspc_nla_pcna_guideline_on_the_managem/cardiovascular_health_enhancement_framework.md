# Cardiovascular Health Enhancement Framework
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Life's Essential 8 + Cardiovascular Health Enhancement Framework

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Promote adherence to Life's Essential 8 metrics (diet, physical activity, nicotine exposure, sleep health, BMI, blood lipids, blood glucose, and blood pressure) as foundational cardiovascular health behaviors.
- Integrate genetic risk assessment (e.g., APOB/PCSK9 variants) with lifestyle interventions to personalize dyslipidemia management.
- Encourage Mediterranean, DASH, or plant-based dietary patterns to optimize lipid profiles and reduce ASCVD risk.
- Advocate for routine physical activity assessment and promotion in clinical settings, including aerobic and resistance training.
- Address socioeconomic and environmental determinants (e.g., food insecurity, neighborhood walkability) affecting cardiovascular health.
- Incorporate structured sleep health interventions (e.g., cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia) as part of dyslipidemia management, particularly in patients with sleep disorders or poor sleep quality.
- Address shift work and circadian disruption as modifiable risk factors for dyslipidemia, given evidence linking irregular work schedules to adverse lipid profiles.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Baseline lipid panel (fasting or non-fasting) with LDL-C, HDL-C, triglycerides, and non-HDL-C for risk stratification.
- 10-year ASCVD risk calculation using pooled cohort equations for primary prevention decisions.
- Documentation of Life's Essential 8 metrics in electronic health records for longitudinal tracking.
- Genetic testing for FH (e.g., APOB/PCSK9) in patients with severe hypercholesterolemia or family history of premature ASCVD.
- Registered dietitian nutritionist involvement for medical nutrition therapy in dyslipidemia management.
- Mandate documentation of physical activity intensity and duration (e.g., MET-minutes/week) in electronic health records to enable precise tracking of adherence to exercise prescriptions.
- Require assessment of food security status (e.g., via validated screening tools) in all patients with dyslipidemia to tailor dietary interventions and address socioeconomic barriers.
