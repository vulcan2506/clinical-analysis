# Dietary Intervention Evidence
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Dietary Intervention Evidence for Dyslipidemia Management

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Vegetarian or vegan diets are associated with improved blood lipid profiles in randomized trials.
- Mediterranean-style diets reduce cardiovascular risk factors in randomized controlled trials.
- Weight loss in overweight/obese adults improves serum lipid levels.
- Exercise training positively influences blood lipid profiles.
- Dietary interventions are recommended as foundational therapy for lipid management.
- Dietary sodium reduction is recommended as part of dietary interventions for patients with dyslipidemia and hypertension to further reduce cardiovascular risk.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Randomized trials and meta-analyses must be used to evaluate dietary intervention efficacy.
- Lipid profiles should be assessed before and after dietary interventions to monitor response.
- Dietary interventions should be tailored to patient preferences, cultural practices, and socioeconomic status to ensure adherence and long-term sustainability.
