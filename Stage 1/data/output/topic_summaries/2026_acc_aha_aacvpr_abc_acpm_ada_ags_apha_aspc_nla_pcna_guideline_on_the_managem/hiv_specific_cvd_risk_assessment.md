# HIV-Specific CVD Risk Assessment
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** HIV-Specific CVD Risk Assessment

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Incorporate HIV status as a risk-enhancing factor in ASCVD risk stratification due to elevated cardiovascular risk in people living with HIV.
- Consider antiretroviral therapy (ART)-related dyslipidemia and metabolic changes when assessing lipid profiles and cardiovascular risk.
- Evaluate inflammatory biomarkers (e.g., hs-CRP) and immune activation markers in HIV-positive patients for additional risk stratification.
- Use coronary artery calcium (CAC) scoring as a risk modifier in HIV-infected individuals to guide statin therapy decisions.
- Account for sex-specific and reproductive risk markers (e.g., preterm delivery, preeclampsia) in women with HIV for tailored risk assessment.
- Incorporate polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS) as a female-specific risk-enhancing factor in HIV-positive women for tailored cardiovascular risk assessment.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Baseline lipid panel and hs-CRP measurement in all adults with HIV prior to cardiovascular risk assessment.
- Documentation of current ART regimen and duration to assess potential lipid-altering effects.
- Coronary artery calcium scoring in HIV-positive patients with intermediate ASCVD risk to refine risk stratification.
- Consideration of inflammatory biomarkers (e.g., IL-6, D-dimer) in HIV-infected individuals with persistently elevated risk.
- Integration of reproductive history (e.g., gestational diabetes, preeclampsia) in women with HIV for comprehensive risk evaluation.
- Document menstrual cycle irregularity history in women with HIV as a potential risk-enhancing factor for ASCVD risk stratification.
