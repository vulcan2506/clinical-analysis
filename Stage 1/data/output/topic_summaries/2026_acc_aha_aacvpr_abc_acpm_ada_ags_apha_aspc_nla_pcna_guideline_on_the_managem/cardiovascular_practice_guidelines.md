# Cardiovascular Practice Guidelines
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** ACC/AHA Guideline Development and Economic Value Integration

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Translate scientific evidence into clinical practice guidelines to improve cardiovascular health
- Use systematic methods to evaluate and classify evidence
- Provide a foundation for delivering high-quality cardiovascular care
- Include economic value statements with cost-effectiveness analyses when applicable

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Methodology for economic value statements must follow the AHA/ACC Statement on Cost Value Methodology
