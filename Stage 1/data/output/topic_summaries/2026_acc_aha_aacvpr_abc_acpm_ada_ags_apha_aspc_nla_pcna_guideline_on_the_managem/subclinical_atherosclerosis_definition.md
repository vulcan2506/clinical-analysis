# Subclinical Atherosclerosis Definition
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Subclinical Atherosclerosis Definition and CAC Scoring

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Subclinical atherosclerosis is identified by the presence of atheromatous disease in ≥1 arterial territories before clinical symptoms or events occur.
- CAC score ≥1000 AU is associated with substantially increased risk of CVD, CHD, and all-cause mortality.
- CAC score ≥300 has similar estimated 10-year ASCVD risk as patients treated for secondary prevention.
- Subclinical atherosclerosis is identified by the presence of atheromatous disease in ≥1 arterial territories before any signs, symptoms, or events attributable to clinically manifest atherosclerotic disease in those territories.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Treatment with LDL-C-lowering therapies to achieve ≥50% reduction in LDL-C and goal LDL-C <55 mg/dL (1.4 mmol/L) and non-HDL-C <85 mg/dL is advised for adults with CAC score ≥1000 AU.
- CAC score ≥300 AU requires intensive LDL-C lowering therapy to achieve ≥50% reduction in LDL-C and LDL-C <70 mg/dL (not as aggressive as the <55 mg/dL target for CAC ≥1000 AU).
