# LDL Reduction Therapy
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** LDL Reduction Therapy & Risk Stratification

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Consider high-intensity statins or statin combined with ezetimibe to achieve optimal LDL-C reduction.
- Reasonable to consider adding ezetimibe if needed to achieve LDL-C treatment goals for primary prevention in individuals at high ASCVD risk.
- Consider bempedoic acid as an adjunct in high-risk primary prevention populations unable to tolerate recommended dose statins.
- Discontinue statin therapy in patients with a life expectancy of <1 year to avoid unnecessary medication use.
- No clear net benefit to initiation of LLT for individuals with untreated LDL-C levels <70 mg/dL and non-HDL-C levels <100 mg/dL in primary prevention.
- Consider early combination therapy (statin + ezetimibe) in secondary prevention to improve LDL-C lowering and cardiovascular outcomes compared with monotherapy, with similar safety and tolerability.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Patient must be at high ASCVD risk for consideration of ezetimibe addition in primary prevention.
- Patient must have limited life expectancy (<1 year) for consideration of statin discontinuation.
- Patient must have LDL-C <70 mg/dL and non-HDL-C <100 mg/dL to determine lack of net benefit in primary prevention.
- Patient must be at high risk for ASCVD (e.g., advanced subclinical atherosclerosis, diabetes, or statin intolerance) for consideration of bempedoic acid in primary prevention.
