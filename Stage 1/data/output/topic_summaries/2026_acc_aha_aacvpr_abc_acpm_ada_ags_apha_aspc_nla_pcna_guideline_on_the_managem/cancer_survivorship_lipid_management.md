# Cancer Survivorship Lipid Management
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Cancer Survivorship Lipid Management

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Adult cancer survivors with life expectancy ≥2 years and qualifying for lipid-lowering therapy (LLT) should be treated similarly to those without cancer history to reduce ASCVD risk.
- Continue statin therapy in adults with active cancer unless drug interaction concerns arise or life expectancy is <1 year.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Life expectancy ≥2 years for initiating LLT in cancer survivors.
- Life expectancy <1 year as a threshold to discontinue statin therapy in active cancer.
