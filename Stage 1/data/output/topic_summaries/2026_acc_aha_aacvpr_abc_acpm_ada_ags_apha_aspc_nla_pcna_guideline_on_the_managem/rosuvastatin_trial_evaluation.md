# Rosuvastatin Trial Evaluation
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Rosuvastatin Trial Evaluation (JUPITER) and LDL-C Targets

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- The JUPITER trial evaluated rosuvastatin in primary prevention for individuals with LDL-C <130 mg/dL but elevated hs-CRP.
- Evidence supports aggressive LDL-C lowering with statins, including rosuvastatin, to reduce cardiovascular events.
- Non-HDL-C and apoB are emphasized as secondary targets when LDL-C goals are not met.
- Meta-analyses demonstrate apoB and non-HDL-C as superior predictors of cardiovascular risk compared to LDL-C alone.
- JUPITER trial demonstrated a 44% relative risk reduction in major adverse cardiovascular events (MACE) with rosuvastatin 20 mg daily in primary prevention patients with LDL-C <130 mg/dL and hs-CRP ≥2 mg/L.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- LDL-C <130 mg/dL with elevated hs-CRP (high-sensitivity C-reactive protein) for JUPITER trial eligibility.
- Baseline lipid panel including LDL-C, non-HDL-C, and apoB for risk stratification.
- Baseline hs-CRP ≥2 mg/L required for JUPITER trial eligibility in primary prevention.
