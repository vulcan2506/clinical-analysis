# Diabetes-Specific Lipid Management Guideline
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Diabetes-Specific Lipid Management

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- In adults with diabetes and without ASCVD, lipid-lowering therapy (LLT) is recommended to lower LDL-C and reduce ASCVD risk.
- For adults with diabetes who have statin-attributed side effects, initiation of ezetimibe and/or bempedoic acid and/or a PCSK9 monoclonal antibody (mAb) is recommended to lower LDL-C and reduce ASCVD risk.
- Consideration of apoB levels alongside LDL-C for risk stratification in adults with diabetes.
- Consideration of triglycerides ≥150 mg/dL as a diabetes-specific risk enhancer for ASCVD risk stratification in adults with diabetes.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Adults with diabetes without ASCVD require risk stratification using diabetes-specific risk enhancers (refer to Table 17).
- Lipid profile assessment (including LDL-C, non-HDL-C, apoB, and triglycerides) should be performed at baseline and periodically in adults with diabetes to guide lipid-lowering therapy decisions.
