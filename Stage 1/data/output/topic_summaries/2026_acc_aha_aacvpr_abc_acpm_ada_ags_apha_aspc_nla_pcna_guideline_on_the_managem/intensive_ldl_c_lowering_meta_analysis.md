# Intensive LDL-C Lowering Meta-Analysis
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Intensive LDL-C Lowering Meta-Analysis

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Higher LDL-C lowering intensity reduces major vascular events proportionally to absolute LDL-C reduction
- Each 1 mmol/L (39 mg/dL) reduction in LDL-C lowers major vascular events by ~22%
- Benefits of intensive LDL-C lowering are consistent across patient subgroups
- The proportional reduction in major vascular events per 1 mmol/L LDL-C lowering is consistent across different LDL-C lowering strategies (e.g., statins alone, statin+ezetimibe, or PCSK9 inhibitors)
- The benefit of intensive LDL-C lowering is observed within the first year of treatment and continues to accrue over time
- The relationship between LDL-C reduction and event reduction is linear across the range of LDL-C levels studied (down to ~1.3 mmol/L)

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Randomized trial data from 170,000 participants across 26 trials
- Meta-analysis methodology for pooled effect estimation
- Trials must have a minimum follow-up duration of at least 2 years to be included in the meta-analysis
- Randomized trials must have a placebo or standard-care control group for comparison
