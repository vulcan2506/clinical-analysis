# Physical Activity Recommendation
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Physical Activity for Dyslipidemia Management

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Recommend moderate-to-vigorous intensity aerobic exercise for ≥150 minutes per week
- Include upper and lower body resistance exercise 2 days per week
- Prescribe as part of a program to improve blood lipids and cardiovascular health
- Include flexibility or balance exercises (e.g., stretching, yoga) as adjunctive activities for overall musculoskeletal health, particularly in older adults or those with mobility limitations

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Individuals with dyslipidemia
- Prescribe physical activity in a structured, supervised manner for individuals with significant cardiovascular risk or comorbidities (e.g., established ASCVD, heart failure, or severe dyslipidemia)
