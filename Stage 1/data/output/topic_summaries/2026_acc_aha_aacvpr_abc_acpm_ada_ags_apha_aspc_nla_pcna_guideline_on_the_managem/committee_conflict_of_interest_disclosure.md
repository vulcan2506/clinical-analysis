 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on

**Clinical Recommendation:** Conflict of Interest Disclosure and Transparency

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Committee members must disclose all industry relationships, including ownership, consulting, research funding, and institutional benefits.
- Disclosures are categorized as 'RELEVANT' or 'NOT RELEVANT' based on financial thresholds (e.g., ≥5% ownership or ≥$5000 in value).
- Significant relationships are marked with an asterisk (*) and non-financial benefits with a dagger (†).
- Relationships are reviewed at the time of guideline development and may not reflect current industry ties.
- Transparency is prioritized to ensure guideline integrity and mitigate bias in recommendations.
- Committee members with 'NOT RELEVANT' relationships are still listed in the table to ensure full transparency of all reported ties, not just significant ones.
- The table explicitly states it reflects relationships 'at the time this document was under development' and does not necessarily reflect current industry ties at the time of publication.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- All committee members must report financial and non-financial relationships with industry entities.
- Disclosures must include ownership stakes, consulting fees, research funding, and institutional benefits.
- Relationships exceeding 5% ownership or $5000 in value are deemed significant and require additional scrutiny.
- Non-financial benefits (e.g., unpaid roles) must also be disclosed for transparency.
- Disclosures are reviewed against ACC/AHA policy definitions to ensure consistency.
