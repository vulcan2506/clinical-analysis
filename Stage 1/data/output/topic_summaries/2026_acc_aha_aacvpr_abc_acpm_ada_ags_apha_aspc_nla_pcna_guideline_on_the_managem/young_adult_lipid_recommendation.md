# Young Adult Lipid Recommendation
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Young Adult Lipid Recommendation

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- In young adults (>18–39 y of age), dietary optimization recommendations should be provided to reduce cumulative atherogenic lipid exposure and lifetime ASCVD risk.
- In young adults (>18–39 y of age), physical activity recommendations should be provided to reduce cumulative atherogenic lipid exposure and lifetime ASCVD risk.
- In young adults (>18–39 y of age), weight optimization recommendations should be provided to reduce cumulative atherogenic lipid exposure and lifetime ASCVD risk.
