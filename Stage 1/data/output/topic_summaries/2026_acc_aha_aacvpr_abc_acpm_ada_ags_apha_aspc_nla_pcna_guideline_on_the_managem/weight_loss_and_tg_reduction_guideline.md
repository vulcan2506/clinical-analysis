# Weight Loss and TG Reduction Guideline
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Weight Loss and TG Reduction

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Per 1 kg of weight lost, triglycerides (TG) reduced by 4.0 mg/dL (95% CI, -5.24 to -2.77 mg/dL) with lifestyle changes (diet, exercise, or both).
- Adequate physical activity (at least 150 minutes/week moderate-intensity or 75 minutes/week vigorous-intensity aerobic activity, plus 2 days/week resistance exercise) is beneficial for TG lowering.
- Physical activity improves insulin sensitivity, aids in maintaining healthy weight after 5%-10% weight loss, and directly enhances TG clearance.
