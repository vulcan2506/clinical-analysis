# Guideline Peer Review Process
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Guideline Peer Review Process

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- A peer review committee was appointed by the Joint Committee to review the guideline.
- The peer review committee included individuals nominated by the ACC, AHA, and collaborating organizations.
- Reviewers' comprehensive and relevant RWI (Relationships with Industry) are listed in Appendix 2 of the guidelines.
- The peer review committee included external content experts and methodologists in addition to representatives from ACC, AHA, and collaborating organizations.
- The peer review process involved multiple rounds of feedback and revisions based on reviewer comments.
- The guideline document explicitly states that the peer review committee operated under a conflict-of-interest policy to manage RWI.
- The final guideline incorporated revisions in response to peer review feedback before publication.
