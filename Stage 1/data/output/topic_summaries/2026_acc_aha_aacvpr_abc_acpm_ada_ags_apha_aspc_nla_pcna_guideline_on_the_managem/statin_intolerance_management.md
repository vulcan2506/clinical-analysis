# statin intolerance management
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Statin Intolerance Management

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Acknowledge patient concerns about muscle symptoms while reinforcing ASCVD risk reduction benefits
- Exclude excessive muscular activity, disease states, and drug-drug interactions before attributing symptoms to statins
- Perform physical examination for muscle weakness and measure CK levels if symptoms are severe
- Maximize lifestyle management and explore less-than-daily statin dosing or nonstatin therapies for LDL-C lowering
- Reinitiate cardioprotective therapy with alternate statins, nonstatins, or modified dosing to align with ASCVD risk
- Perform neurologic consultation for adults with severe myalgia or muscle weakness and CK levels ≥10 times the upper limit of normal

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Exclude excessive muscular activity, primary muscle/metabolic disorders, and drug-drug interactions before diagnosing statin intolerance
- Perform physical examination for muscle weakness in symptomatic patients
- Measure CK levels in patients with severe myalgia or muscle weakness
- Ensure LDL-C ≥70 mg/dL in patients with established ASCVD before considering nonstatin add-on therapy
- Use maximum tolerated statin therapy or less-than-daily dosing before adding nonstatin therapies
- Exclude primary muscle or metabolic disorders associated with muscle pain or weakness before attributing symptoms to statins
