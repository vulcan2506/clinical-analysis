# Pitavastatin ASCVD Risk Reduction in PLHIV
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Pitavastatin ASCVD Risk Reduction in PLHIV

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Pitavastatin 4 mg daily reduces first MACE by 35% in PLHIV aged 40-75 over 5.1 years (REPRIEVE trial).
- Pitavastatin slows noncalcified plaque progression by 4.3 mm³ overall and 8.8 mm³ in those with baseline noncalcified plaque.
- Pitavastatin is preferred due to minimal CYP3A4 metabolism, reducing DDI with antiretroviral therapies.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- PLHIV aged 40-75 years eligible for primary prevention regardless of baseline LDL-C or 10-year ASCVD risk (median PCE risk 4.5%, LDL-C 108 mg/dL in REPRIEVE).
- CCTA may be used to assess noncalcified plaque progression in select patients.
- Eligibility for REPRIEVE did not require a lower bound on 10-year ASCVD risk or LDL-C level, allowing inclusion of patients with low baseline risk.
