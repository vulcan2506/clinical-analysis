# Cardiovascular-Kidney-Metabolic Risk Prediction
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Cardiovascular-Kidney-Metabolic Risk Prediction Integration

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Incorporates cardiovascular-kidney-metabolic health into absolute risk prediction equations for total cardiovascular disease.
- Uses novel prediction models that integrate multi-system risk factors (e.g., kidney function, metabolic syndrome, and cardiovascular markers).
- Emphasizes subgroup-specific risk stratification (e.g., Asian Americans, Native Hawaiian/Pacific Islander, Hispanic/Latino populations).
- Adjusts screening thresholds for diabetes and dyslipidemia based on racial/ethnic and acculturation factors.
- Prioritizes abdominal obesity indices (e.g., waist circumference) over BMI for cardiovascular risk discrimination.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Mandatory inclusion of kidney function (e.g., eGFR) in cardiovascular risk assessment models.
- Standardized measurement of lipoprotein(a) in high-risk ethnic subgroups for risk refinement.
- Screening for prediabetes/diabetes in adults with BMI ≥23 kg/m² in Asian populations or ≥25 kg/m² in others.
- Use of acculturation-adjusted risk thresholds for cardiovascular disease in immigrant populations.
- Integration of metabolic syndrome components (e.g., triglycerides, HDL-C, blood pressure) into risk calculators.
