# Statin Therapy Intensification in Diabetes
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Statin Therapy Intensification in Diabetes

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- In adults 40 to 75 years with diabetes without ASCVD, moderate-intensity statin therapy is indicated to achieve ≥30% to 49% LDL-C reduction and LDL-C <100 mg/dL (non-HDL-C <130 mg/dL).
- In adults with diabetes and statin-attributed side effects, ezetimibe and/or bempedoic acid or PCSK9 mAb are recommended to lower LDL-C and reduce ASCVD risk.
- In adults 40 to 75 years with diabetes and multiple ASCVD risk factors, high-intensity statin therapy is reasonable to achieve ≥50% LDL-C reduction and LDL-C <70 mg/dL (non-HDL-C <100 mg/dL).
- Icosapent ethyl (IPE) may be considered in patients with diabetes and additional cardiovascular risk factors with persistently elevated triglycerides despite optimized statin therapy.
- For patients >75 years with diabetes, moderate- or high-intensity statin therapy may be beneficial after clinician-patient benefit-risk discussion.
- In adults with diabetes and ASCVD not at very high risk, high-intensity statin therapy should be initiated to achieve ≥50% LDL-C reduction and LDL-C <70 mg/dL (non-HDL-C <100 mg/dL).
- In adults with diabetes and ASCVD not at very high risk on maximally tolerated statin therapy, it is reasonable to add ezetimibe, a PCSK9 mAb, or bempedoic acid to achieve LDL-C <70 mg/dL (non-HDL-C <100 mg/dL).

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Diabetes diagnosis confirmation.
- Age stratification (40–75 years for primary prevention intensification).
- Risk enhancer assessment (e.g., long diabetes duration, albuminuria, eGFR <60 mL/min/1.73m²).
- Use of PREVENT-ASCVD equations for risk-based treatment decisions in patients ≥30 years.
- Maximally tolerated statin therapy before adding non-statin agents in statin-intolerant patients.
- Use of PREVENT-ASCVD equations to guide treatment decisions in adults ≥30 years with diabetes, including those with borderline or intermediate-risk estimates.
- Consideration of diabetes duration (≥10 years for type 2 diabetes or ≥20 years for type 1 diabetes) as a risk enhancer for statin therapy intensification.
