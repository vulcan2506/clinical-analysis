 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on

**Clinical Recommendation:** Conflict of Interest Disclosures in Guideline Development

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Peer reviewers disclose financial relationships with industry entities (e.g., pharmaceutical companies, universities, or research institutions).
- Disclosures include roles such as consultant, institutional research funding, or ownership/partnership.
- Reviewers with no conflicts report 'None' in relevant categories (e.g., speakers bureau, expert witness).
- Financial benefits are categorized by type (e.g., personal research, institutional support, ownership).
- Disclosures are explicitly listed for each reviewer in a standardized format.
- Reviewers disclose specific monetary amounts or ranges for financial relationships (e.g., * for <$10,000, † for $10,000–$50,000, ‡ for >$50,000)

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Mandatory disclosure of all financial relationships with industry or other entities for peer reviewers.
- Transparency in reporting roles (e.g., consultant, speaker, researcher) and monetary sources.
- Reviewers must declare conflicts in categories such as ownership, research funding, or institutional benefits.
- Guideline development process requires at least one reviewer with no industry ties to serve as an independent arbiter for conflict resolution
