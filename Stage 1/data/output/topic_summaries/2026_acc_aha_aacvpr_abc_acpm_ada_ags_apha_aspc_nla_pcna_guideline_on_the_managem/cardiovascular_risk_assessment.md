**Clinical Recommendation:** Lp(a) Integration in Cardiovascular Risk Assessment

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Elevated Lp(a) ≥50 mg/dL is now a threshold for intensifying statin therapy or considering PCSK9 inhibitor therapy in high-risk patients.
- Lp(a) measurement is recommended for patients with familial hypercholesterolemia or recurrent atherosclerotic cardiovascular events.
- Repeat Lp(a) measurements are advised to refine risk stratification due to variability in molar concentrations.
- Genetic characterization of LPA may be considered for primary prevention in select high-risk populations.
- Standardized Lp(a) assays (IFCC-endorsed mass spectrometry-based reference) are preferred for clinical use.
- Lp(a) molar concentration should be used for risk stratification rather than mass-based thresholds due to isoform-dependent variability in mass measurements.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Lp(a) should be measured using standardized, isoform-independent assays for accurate risk assessment.
- Repeat Lp(a) testing is recommended to account for biological variability and improve risk prediction.
- Lp(a) assessment is particularly valuable in patients with familial hypercholesterolemia or prior cardiovascular events.
- Lp(a) testing should be performed in fasting state to minimize postprandial variability in measurements.
