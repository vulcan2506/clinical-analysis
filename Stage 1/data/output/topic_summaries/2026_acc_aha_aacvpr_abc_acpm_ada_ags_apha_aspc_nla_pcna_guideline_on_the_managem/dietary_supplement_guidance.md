# Dietary Supplement Guidance
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Dietary Supplement Guidance for Dyslipidemia

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Dietary supplements are not recommended to lower LDL-C or triglycerides in individuals with dyslipidemia due to limited and inconsistent data.
- Clinicians should educate patients on the inconsistent and limited evidence for dietary supplements in reducing ASCVD risk.
- Supplements evaluated (e.g., fish oil, cinnamon, garlic, turmeric, plant sterols, red yeast rice) showed no significant LDL-C reduction compared to placebo in RCTs.
- Clinicians should address patient concerns about supplement use to prevent misplaced reliance on unproven alternatives for ASCVD prevention.
