# Dietary LDL-C Management Guideline
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Dietary LDL-C Management Guideline

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Emphasize intake of fruits, vegetables, nuts, legumes, whole grains, and fiber
- Replace saturated and trans fats with dietary monounsaturated and polyunsaturated fats
- Aim to decrease LDL-C levels and reduce ASCVD risk
- Limit added sugar intake, particularly from sugar-sweetened beverages, to reduce triglycerides and improve overall cardiometabolic health

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Applies to adults and children with or without ASCVD
- Consider dietary counseling by a registered dietitian or qualified nutrition professional for personalized guidance
