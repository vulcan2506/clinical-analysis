**Clinical Recommendation:** Guideline Development Contributors

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Contributors include leadership (e.g., President, CEO, Chief Medical Adviser) and operational staff (e.g., Divisional Vice President, Director, Project Manager).
- Involves multidisciplinary roles such as medical advisors, science and methodology experts, and medical librarians.
- Organizational structure includes both cardiovascular-specific (e.g., FAHA) and general health leadership (e.g., Chief Science and Medical Officer).
- Guideline development is supported by specialized teams (e.g., Science and Medicine Advisor, Statements and Guidelines Director).
- Collaboration spans clinical content, scientific methodology, and operational execution.
- Includes a 'Reference Consultant, Medical Librarian' role specifically for literature retrieval and evidence synthesis support
- Features a 'Science and Medicine Advisor' with a nursing background (PhD, RN) for translational insights
