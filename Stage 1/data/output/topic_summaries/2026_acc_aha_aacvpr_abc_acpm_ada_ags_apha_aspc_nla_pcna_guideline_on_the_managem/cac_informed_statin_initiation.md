# CAC-Informed Statin Initiation
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** CAC-Informed Statin Initiation

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Initiate high-intensity statin therapy in adults with incidental moderate or severe coronary atherosclerosis on noncardiac CT scans
- Initiate moderate-intensity statin therapy in adults with incidental mild coronary artery calcification on noncardiac CT scans
- Notify primary care clinicians and patients of incidental CAC findings to increase appropriate statin prescriptions
- Use opportunistic screening of nongated chest CT scans for CAC detection
- Consider statin therapy for patients with detected carotid plaque during vascular imaging
- Initiate high-intensity statin therapy based on visual estimation of incidental CAC findings
- Initiate high-intensity statin therapy based on validated artificial intelligence-based algorithms for CAC detection

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Detection of coronary atherosclerosis or carotid plaque via noncardiac CT or vascular imaging
