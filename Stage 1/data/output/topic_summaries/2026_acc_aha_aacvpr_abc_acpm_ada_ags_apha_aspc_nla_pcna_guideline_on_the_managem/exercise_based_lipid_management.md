# Exercise-Based Lipid Management
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Exercise-Based Lipid Management

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Physical activity is the cornerstone of cardiovascular health and dyslipidemia management, reducing CVD risk and mortality.
- Aerobic and resistance training have modest dose-dependent effects on lipids (↑HDL-C, ↓LDL-C, ↓TG).
- Adults should aim for ≥150 min/week moderate or 75–150 min/week vigorous aerobic activity + ≥2 days/week resistance training.
- Assess physical activity as a 'vital sign' at each encounter with tailored counseling and referrals.
- Combine physical activity with diet for comprehensive lipid profile improvement.
- Physical activity reduces cardiovascular and all-cause mortality in individuals with dyslipidemia, with benefits spanning from primordial to secondary prevention of CVD.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Use reliable physical activity assessment tools accounting for patient preferences and social determinants.
- Baseline lipid levels, fitness, and exercise duration influence lipid response to exercise.
- Clinicians must account for social/structural barriers and facilitators when assessing physical activity and prescribing interventions.
