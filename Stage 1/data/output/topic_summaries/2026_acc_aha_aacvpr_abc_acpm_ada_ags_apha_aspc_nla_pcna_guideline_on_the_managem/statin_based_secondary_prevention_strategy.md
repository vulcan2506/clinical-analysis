# Statin-Based Secondary Prevention Strategy
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Statin-Based Secondary Prevention Strategy

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Statin therapy is the foundation of lipid-lowering treatment for secondary prevention due to efficacy, safety, tolerability, ease, and cost-effectiveness.
- There is a graded association between LDL-C lowering and ASCVD risk reduction, with evidence supporting lower LDL-C targets.
- Combination therapy (ezetimibe or PCSK9 mAb) with statins further reduces ASCVD risk in very high-risk patients compared to statin monotherapy.
- Long-term safety and efficacy of achieving LDL-C levels as low as 30 mg/dL over 8 years have been demonstrated without de-escalation signals.
- Extended safety data for PCSK9 mAbs have been reported since the 2018 guideline, supporting their use in very high-risk patients.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Patients must meet criteria for very high risk (as defined in Figure 10) for consideration of combination therapy beyond statin monotherapy.
