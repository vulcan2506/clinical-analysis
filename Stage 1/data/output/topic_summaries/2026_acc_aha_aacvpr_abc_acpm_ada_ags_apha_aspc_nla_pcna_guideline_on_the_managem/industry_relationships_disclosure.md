# Industry Relationships Disclosure
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Industry Relationships Disclosure Transparency

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Disclosure of all committee members' industry relationships, including those deemed non-relevant to the document.
- Financial relationships defined as significant if ownership ≥5% of voting stock, ≥$5000 fair market value, or funds exceeding 5% of gross income.
- Relationships with no financial benefit included for transparency.
- Disclosure categories and definitions aligned with ACC/AHA policy.
- Disclosure table updated at document development but may not reflect current industry relationships.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Disclosure of all financial and non-financial relationships with industry.
- Significant financial relationships (>$5000) must be explicitly marked.
- Relationships with no financial benefit included for full transparency.
- Compliance with ACC/AHA Disclosure Policy for Writing Committees.
