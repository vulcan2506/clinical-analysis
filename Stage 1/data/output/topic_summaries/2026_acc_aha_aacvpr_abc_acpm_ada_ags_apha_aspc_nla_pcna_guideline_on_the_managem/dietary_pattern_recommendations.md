# Dietary Pattern Recommendations
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Dietary Pattern Recommendations for Dyslipidemia Management

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Adopt Mediterranean, DASH, or vegetarian diets for improved cardiovascular outcomes.
- Increase intake of fruits, vegetables, nuts, seeds, fiber-rich foods (legumes, whole grains), and monounsaturated/polyunsaturated fats to modestly lower LDL-C.
- Avoid saturated fats (found in red meat, butter, high-fat milk, coconut oil, palm oil) to reduce LDL-C.
- Limit simple carbohydrates (processed carbs, added sugars) to lower triglycerides (TG).
- Engage a registered dietitian nutritionist (RDN) for tailored dietary counseling, especially in severe hypertriglyceridemia.
- Limit simple carbohydrates (particularly processed carbohydrates and all added sugars) to substantially lower triglycerides (TG).

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Dietary strategies should be complemented by physical activity and weight loss for hypertriglyceridemia management.
