# Nutrition Therapy Recommendation
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Nutrition Therapy for Dyslipidemia Management

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Referral to an RDN is recommended for individuals with fasting TG ≥1000 mg/dL to create an individualized treatment plan aimed at reducing TG and pancreatitis risk.
- RDN counseling is beneficial for individuals with fasting TG ≥150 to 999 mg/dL and features of CKM syndrome to improve lipoprotein levels and reduce pancreatitis risk.
- Medical nutrition therapy provided by an RDN is helpful in lipid lowering and can reduce the burden of pharmacotherapy.
- RDN counseling sessions are generally covered by Medicare for patients with diabetes or CKD and selectively for dyslipidemia, depending on insurance plans.
- Medical nutrition therapy provided by an RDN is recommended to target specific lipid abnormalities (e.g., low saturated fat for LDL-C reduction, carbohydrate restriction for hypertriglyceridemia) to optimize lipid profiles and reduce medication requirements.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Fasting triglyceride (TG) levels must be measured to determine eligibility for RDN referral.
- Presence of CKM syndrome features is required for RDN counseling in individuals with TG ≥150 to 999 mg/dL.
- Fasting triglyceride (TG) levels must be measured to determine eligibility for RDN referral and counseling.
