**Clinical Recommendation:** Polygenic Risk Score Integration for CVD Risk Stratification

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Integrates ancestry-specific polygenic risk scores (PRS) to enhance clinical cardiovascular disease risk assessments beyond traditional metrics.
- Identifies subgroups with higher atherosclerosis burden and greater relative benefit from statin therapy or PCSK9 inhibitors based on genetic risk.
- Evaluates interactions between enhanced polygenic risk scores and lifestyle factors to predict cardiovascular disease and lipid levels.
- Assesses the dynamic importance of genomic and clinical risk for coronary artery disease over the life course.
- Mitigates instability in high polygenic risk classification through integrative scoring approaches.
- Evaluates the impact of PRS disclosure on patient-provider discussions and shared decision-making for lipid-lowering therapy initiation.
- Assesses the role of PRS in stratifying patients for coronary artery calcium (CAC) scoring or other imaging-based risk enhancements.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Use of ancestrally diverse genome-wide association studies and population-specific optimization for PRS calculation.
- Validation of PRS performance and agreement in specific clinical settings before implementation.
- Consideration of large uncertainty in individual PRS estimation when stratifying risk.
- Integration of PRS alongside family history and rare variants for comprehensive risk assessment.
- Clinical trial data supporting the predictive utility of PRS in primary prevention settings.
- Requires standardized reporting of PRS performance metrics (e.g., AUC, net reclassification improvement) in validation studies before clinical integration.
- Mandates consideration of PRS calibration across diverse ancestries to ensure equitable risk prediction in clinical settings.
