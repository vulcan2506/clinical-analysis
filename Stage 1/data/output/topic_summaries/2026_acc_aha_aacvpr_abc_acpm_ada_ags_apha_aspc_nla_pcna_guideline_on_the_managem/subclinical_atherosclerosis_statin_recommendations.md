# Subclinical Atherosclerosis Statin Recommendations
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Subclinical Atherosclerosis Statin Recommendations

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Statin therapy is recommended as first-line LDL-C-lowering therapy for adults with CAC scores ≥100 AU or ≥75th percentile, with ≥50% LDL-C reduction and LDL-C goal <70 mg/dL (1.8 mmol/L).
- High-intensity statin therapy is reasonable for moderate-to-severe incidental coronary atherosclerosis on noncardiac CT, aiming for ≥50% LDL-C reduction and LDL-C <70 mg/dL (1.8 mmol/L).
- Moderate-intensity statin therapy is reasonable for mild incidental CAC, aiming for ≥30% to 49% LDL-C reduction and LDL-C <100 mg/dL (2.6 mmol/L).
- Intensification of therapy (e.g., increasing statin intensity, adding ezetimibe, PCSK9 mAb, or bempedoic acid) is reasonable for CAC scores ≥300 AU to achieve LDL-C <55 mg/dL (1.4 mmol/L).
- CAC scoring is considered for intermediate-risk (5% to <10%) or select borderline-risk (3% to <5%) individuals aged ≥40 (men) or ≥45 (women) when LLT initiation is uncertain.
- For adults with CAC ≥1000 AU, LDL-C-lowering therapies are considered first-line with a goal of LDL-C <55 mg/dL (1.4 mmol/L) and non-HDL-C <85 mg/dL (2.2 mmol/L).
- For adults with CAC ≥300 to 999 AU, LDL-C-lowering therapies are considered first-line with a goal of LDL-C <70 mg/dL (1.8 mmol/L) and non-HDL-C <100 mg/dL (2.6 mmol/L).

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- CAC score measurement in AU for risk stratification.
- Baseline LDL-C assessment before statin initiation.
- Achievement of ≥30% LDL-C reduction in untreated individuals with baseline LDL-C below recommended targets.
- Use of PREVENT-ASCVD equations for risk stratification in borderline/intermediate-risk patients.
- Consideration of noncardiac CT findings (e.g., incidental coronary atherosclerosis) for statin therapy decisions.
- CAC scoring is incremental to traditional risk factors in predicting ASCVD risk, regardless of dyslipidemia burden or lipid-lowering therapy use.
- For individuals not on LLT with baseline LDL-C below recommended targets for their CAC level, treatment is still recommended to achieve at least ≥30% reduction in baseline LDL-C.
