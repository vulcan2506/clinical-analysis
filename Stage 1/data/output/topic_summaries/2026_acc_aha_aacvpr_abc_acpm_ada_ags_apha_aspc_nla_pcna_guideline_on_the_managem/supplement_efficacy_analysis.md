**Clinical Recommendation:** Supplement Efficacy Analysis

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Curcumin/turmeric supplementation shows potential lipid profile improvements but requires GRADE-assessed evidence synthesis.
- Garlic consumption may reduce dyslipidemia risk based on meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials.
- Omega-3 fatty acid supplements are associated with increased risk of atrial fibrillation in secondary analysis from the OMEMI trial.
- Red yeast rice extract is evaluated for hypercholesterolemia reduction with systematic review and meta-analysis.
- Berberine supplementation is assessed for cardiovascular risk factor modification in adults.
- Cinnamon supplementation is evaluated for lipid profile modification via systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials.
- Comparative analysis of low-dose rosuvastatin versus dietary supplements assesses effects on lipids and inflammatory biomarkers.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Evidence synthesis must use GRADE assessment for supplement efficacy evaluation.
- Randomized controlled trials are required for meta-analysis inclusion.
- Regulatory guidelines (e.g., FDA) must be followed for dietary supplement research in the U.S.
- 2023 AHA/ACC/ACCP/ASPC/NLA/PCNA guidelines for chronic coronary disease management must be consulted for comprehensive risk reduction strategies.
- 2021 ACC expert consensus decision pathway is required for managing ASCVD risk reduction in patients with persistent hypertriglyceridemia.
