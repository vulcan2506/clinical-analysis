**Clinical Recommendation:** Primordial Prevention Strategies for Dyslipidemia

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Promote healthy dietary patterns (e.g., Mediterranean, vegan, or plant-based diets) to reduce LDL-C and ASCVD risk.
- Encourage regular physical activity and maintenance of a healthy weight.
- Advocate for healthy sleep, stress management, and avoidance of tobacco products.
- Implement early-life interventions to establish lifelong healthy habits.
- Individualize dietary recommendations based on triglyceride (TG) levels and clinical syndromes.
- Supplementation with fat-soluble vitamins, minerals, and medium-chain triglyceride oil for patients with familial chylomicronemia syndrome (FCS) to meet nutritional requirements.
- At least 2 servings of fatty fish per week for individuals with moderately elevated triglycerides.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Longitudinal sustainability of healthy behaviors to optimize lipid-related ASCVD risk reduction.
- Consideration of environmental and social determinants of health influencing dyslipidemia risk.
- Supervision by a clinical lipid specialist and a registered dietitian nutritionist (RDN) for patients with rare conditions like familial chylomicronemia syndrome (FCS).
