# Statin-Associated Muscle Symptom Management
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Statin-Associated Muscle Symptom Management

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Assess for secondary causes of muscle symptoms in adults with statin-attributed muscle symptoms
- Perform objective clinical measures of muscle strength and CK measurement in severe myalgias or weakness
- Conduct clinician-patient discussion acknowledging side effect concerns and ASCVD risk implications
- Use reduced statin dose (if tolerable) with add-on therapies (bempedoic acid, ezetimibe, PCSK9 mAb) for LDL-C lowering in clinical ASCVD patients unable to tolerate recommended statin intensity
- Consider coronary calcium scoring for ASCVD risk stratification in borderline/intermediate-risk patients without clinical ASCVD
- Evaluate patients with statin-attributed muscle symptoms for risk factors associated with increased risk (e.g., higher statin doses within approved ranges) as outlined in Table 24

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Secondary causes of muscle symptoms must be excluded before attributing symptoms to statins
- Patients with clinical ASCVD unable to achieve treatment goals require evaluation for alternative therapies
- PREVENT-ASCVD equations must be used for ASCVD risk stratification in patients without clinical ASCVD
- CK measurement is required for patients with severe myalgias or weakness
