# Primordial Dyslipidemia Prevention
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Primordial Dyslipidemia Prevention in Young Adulthood

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Prioritize primordial prevention of dyslipidemia in early life to reduce ASCVD risk across the life course.
- Minimize atherogenic lipid exposure during young adulthood through dietary, physical activity, and weight optimization approaches.
- Counsel on health behaviors to reduce atherogenic lipid exposure even at lipid levels considered normal for middle-aged adults.
- Monitor lipid levels in young adults, noting average annual increases of 1-2 mg/dL for LDL-C/non-HDL-C and 0.5 mg/dL for apoB.
- Consider lipid-lowering pharmacotherapy in young adults with high atherogenic lipid burden and risk enhancers, based on clinical judgment and patient preference.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Accurate assessment of lipid-associated risk in young adults.
- High-impact opportunity for ASCVD prevention during young adulthood.
