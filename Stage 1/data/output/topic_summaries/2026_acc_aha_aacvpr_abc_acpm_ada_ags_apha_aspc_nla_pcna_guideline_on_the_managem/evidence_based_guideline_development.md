**Clinical Recommendation:** Evidence-Based Guideline Development Process

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Conducts initial extensive evidence review from October 2024 to December 2024
- Includes literature from human subjects research published in English
- Searches MEDLINE (PubMed), EMBASE, Cochrane Library, and AHRQ databases
- Adds select key studies published through April 2025 as appropriate
- Uses final evidence tables to summarize evidence for recommendation formulation
- References selected and published in the present guideline are not all-inclusive
