# Topic Selection Process
*Source: Endocrine_Society_Guideline_Methodology_links.pdf*

**Guideline Development Component:** Topic Selection Process + Panel Composition

**Process Requirements:**
- CGC analyzes predefined criteria (environmental scanning, scientific relevance, strategic importance) to recommend new guideline topics to the BOD
- Potential topics are identified via Endocrine Society member surveys, CGC suggestions, and annual guideline surveillance for updates
- Final topic candidates undergo formal prioritization by the CGC before BOD approval
- GDP nominees are vetted for COI via Endocrine Society's policy aligned with national standards
- GDP includes a patient representative, multidisciplinary experts, and a trained Endocrine Society Guideline Methodologist (GRADE framework)
- GDP chairs select and appoint GDP members based on specific clinical or other expertise needed for the guideline
- GDP chairs identify potential co-sponsoring organizations based on clinical topic, desired expertise, and intended audience

**Mandatory Standards:**
- CGC must adhere to national/international quality standards and rigorous methodology
- Scientific criteria for topic selection include evidence state, clinical importance, controversies, and practice variations
- COI disclosure required for 12 months prior to guideline development
- GDP chairs selected based on COI relevance, clinical expertise, and skills
- GRADE methodology must be applied with Evidence-to-Decision (EtD) frameworks
- GDP must include a patient representative to ensure patient perspective is represented at all stages
- Mayo Evidence-based Practice Center leads comparative effectiveness researchers for systematic reviews and meta-analyses
