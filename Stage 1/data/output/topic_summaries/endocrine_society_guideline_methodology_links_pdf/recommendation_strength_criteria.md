# Recommendation Strength Criteria
*Source: Endocrine_Society_Guideline_Methodology_links.pdf*

**Guideline Development Component:** Recommendation Strength Criteria (Strong vs Conditional)

**Process Requirements:**
- Strong recommendation (1): Desirable consequences clearly outweigh undesirable consequences in most settings.
- Conditional recommendation (2): Desirable consequences probably outweigh undesirable consequences in most settings.
- Strong recommendation: Most individuals would want the recommended course of action; only a small proportion would not.
- Conditional recommendation: Majority of individuals would want the suggested course of action, but many would not.
- Strong recommendation: Adherence can be used as a quality criterion or performance indicator.

**Mandatory Standards:**
- Desirable consequences must clearly (strong) or probably (conditional) outweigh undesirable consequences.
- For strong recommendations, formal decision aids are unlikely to be needed.
