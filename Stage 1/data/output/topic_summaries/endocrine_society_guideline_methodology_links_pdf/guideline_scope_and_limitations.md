# Guideline Scope and Limitations
*Source: Endocrine_Society_Guideline_Methodology_links.pdf*

**Guideline Development Component:** Guideline Scope and Limitations

**Process Requirements:**
- Guidelines are developed to assist endocrinologists but are not all-encompassing or exclusive of other approaches.
- Guidelines do not guarantee specific outcomes or establish a standard of care.
- Guidelines are not intended to dictate treatment; decisions must be made based on independent clinical judgment and patient-specific circumstances.
- Guidelines are provided 'as is' without warranties of merchantability, fitness for a particular use, or non-infringement.
- The Endocrine Society disclaims liability for any damages related to the use or reliance on guideline information.

**Mandatory Standards:**
- Guidelines must be applied with consideration of individual patient circumstances and healthcare provider judgment.
