# Methodology Standards
*Source: Endocrine_Society_Guideline_Methodology_links.pdf*

**Guideline Development Component:** GRADE Methodology + Evidence-to-Decision (EtD) Frameworks

**Process Requirements:**
- Guidelines must use the GRADE system to rate evidence certainty (high, moderate, low, very low) and strength of recommendations.
- EtD frameworks must be used to ensure systematic, transparent, and consistent decision-making in guideline development.
- Risk of bias in nonrandomized studies must be assessed using tools like ROBINS-I to rate certainty of evidence.
- Standardized wording should be applied to improve clarity and efficiency in GRADE EtD frameworks.
- Guideline panels should avoid informal recommendations and use actionable statements based on a taxonomy framework.
- Guideline panels must use standardized wording for recommendations to improve clarity and efficiency in GRADE EtD frameworks (e.g., 'We recommend...' vs. 'We suggest...').
- Guideline development must include explicit consideration of equity in EtD frameworks to address potential disparities in care.

**Mandatory Standards:**
- Guideline panels must include multidisciplinary experts and, where applicable, patient representatives.
- Evidence appraisal must follow GRADE criteria, including downgrading for risk of bias, inconsistency, indirectness, imprecision, and publication bias.
- EtD frameworks must address key factors such as benefits, harms, values, resource use, equity, and feasibility.
- Certainty of evidence must be explicitly rated for each outcome using GRADEpro or equivalent tools.
- Recommendations must be linked to the underlying evidence and certainty ratings.
- Evidence appraisal must explicitly address indirectness by evaluating the applicability of evidence to the target population and setting.
- Guideline panels must document the rationale for any deviations from standard GRADE methodology in the evidence-to-decision process.
