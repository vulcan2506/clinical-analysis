# Conflict of Interest Management
*Source: Endocrine_Society_Guideline_Methodology_links.pdf*

**Guideline Development Component:** Conflict of Interest Management

**Process Requirements:**
- Disclosure of all financial relationships (e.g., honoraria, royalties, expert testimony, speaking engagements, advisory boards) and leadership positions with relevant entities.
- Review and determination of COI relevance by CGC Chair and GDP Chairs, with CGC Chair having veto power.
- Recusal of GDP members with relevant COI from discussions, votes, drafting recommendations, and clinical questions related to their conflict.
- Divestment of direct financial investments and abstention from marketing activities or advisory boards of entities with potential financial interest in the guideline.
- Transparency through detailed COI documentation in published guideline appendices and supplemental materials.
- Members are obliged to report un-declared COIs observed in others to the GDP Chair.
- GDP members are prohibited from determining the strength and direction of a recommendation directly related to their COI.

**Mandatory Standards:**
- Chair and Co-Chair of the Guideline Development Panel must be free of any relevant COI.
- A majority (>50%) of GDP members must be free of relevant COI.
- GDP members must declare new industry relationships to GDP chairs and CGC Chair for assessment before acceptance.
- GDP members with relevant COI must recuse themselves from all related activities.
- Disclosure of COI must occur at every in-person meeting, most conference calls, and via annual COI questionnaires.
- Immediate family members of GDP members must also divest direct financial investments with entities having potential financial interest in the guideline.
