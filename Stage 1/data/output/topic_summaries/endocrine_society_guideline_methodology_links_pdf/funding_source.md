# Funding Source
*Source: Endocrine_Society_Guideline_Methodology_links.pdf*

**Guideline Development Component:** Funding Source Transparency

**Process Requirements:**
- Funding is exclusively provided by the Endocrine Society for guideline development
- No external entities provide financial support for guidelines

**Mandatory Standards:**
- All guideline development must be funded solely by the Endocrine Society
- No financial contributions from industry, government, or other organizations are permitted
