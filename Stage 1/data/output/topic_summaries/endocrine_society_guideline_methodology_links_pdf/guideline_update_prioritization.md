# Guideline Update Prioritization
*Source: Endocrine_Society_Guideline_Methodology_links.pdf*

**Guideline Development Component:** Guideline Update Prioritization Process

**Process Requirements:**
- The CGC continually surveils published guidelines for potential updates to ensure timely, evidence-based recommendations.
- Guideline chairs/panel members complete surveys assessing new evidence that materially changes benefits/harms, outcomes, or introduces new treatments.
- Panel members provide an overall assessment of whether the guideline requires an update and its prioritization (high, medium, low).
- The CGC analyzes survey results to decide if a guideline enters the update queue.
- Guidelines deemed in need of update but not immediately actionable may be retired temporarily or permanently.
- The CGC continually surveils the portfolio of published clinical practice guidelines for potential updates to ensure timely, evidence-based recommendations.

**Mandatory Standards:**
- Guideline chairs/panel members must complete update assessment surveys.
- New evidence must materially change expected benefits/harms or introduce new treatments.
- CGC must analyze survey results to determine update necessity.
- Prioritization (high, medium, low) must be assigned by panel members.
- Guidelines not immediately updatable may face retirement (temporary or permanent).
- New evidence must materially change expected benefits/harms, outcomes, or introduce new treatments to warrant an update assessment.
