# lipid management in pregnancy
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** lipid management in pregnancy

**Core Management Principles:**
- Statin therapy must be discontinued if the patient is attempting to conceive.
- Use of fibrates and/or niacin is not advisable during pregnancy.
- Bile acid-binding resins are recommended as safe alternatives for treating hypercholesterolemia in pregnancy.

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Assessment of pregnancy status or intent to conceive before initiating lipid-lowering therapy.
- Confirmation of hypercholesterolemia requiring treatment before selecting bile acid-binding resins.
- Confirmation of hypercholesterolemia requiring treatment before selecting bile acid-binding resins as a therapeutic option
