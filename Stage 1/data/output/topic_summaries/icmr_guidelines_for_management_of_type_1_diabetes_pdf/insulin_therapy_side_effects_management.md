# insulin therapy side effects management
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** insulin therapy side effects management

**Core Management Principles:**
- Patient education on hypoglycemia symptoms, management, and risk factors (e.g., exercise, delayed meals) is mandatory.
- Regular examination of injection sites by both physician and patient to detect lipohypertrophy or lipoatrophy.
- Strict adherence to site rotation and periodic needle changes (ideally after each injection) to prevent lipohypertrophy.
- Monitoring for weight gain due to improved glycemic control, recurrent hypoglycemia, or insulin's lipogenic effects.

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Patient must be informed about hypoglycemia symptoms, management, and contributing factors before insulin initiation.
- Injection sites must be inspected periodically to identify lipohypertrophy or lipoatrophy.
- Needle replacement after each injection is strongly recommended to prevent tissue damage.
