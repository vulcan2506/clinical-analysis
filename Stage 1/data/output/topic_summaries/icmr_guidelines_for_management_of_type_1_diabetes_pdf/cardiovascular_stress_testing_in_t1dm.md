# cardiovascular stress testing in T1DM
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** cardiovascular stress testing in T1DM

**Core Management Principles:**
- Exercise treadmill test (TMT) is the first-line test for patients without peripheral neuropathy, foot deformity, previous amputation, or baseline significant ST-T abnormalities due to cost-effectiveness and availability.
- Pharmacological stress testing (dobutamine stress echocardiography or dobutamine stress myocardial perfusion imaging) is used when TMT is contraindicated, though limited by cost and availability.
- Coronary artery calcification (CAC) scoring via electron beam computerized tomography (EBCT) quantifies atherosclerotic plaque burden in T1DM patients.
- CAC scoring is recommended for quantifying atherosclerotic plaque burden in T1DM patients, with specific score ranges (0, 1-10, 11-100, 101-400, >400) corresponding to no, minimal, mild, moderate, and severe plaque burden respectively

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Patients must be evaluated for peripheral neuropathy, foot deformity, previous amputation, or baseline significant ST-T abnormalities before selecting stress testing modality.
- CAC scoring requires electron beam computerized tomography (EBCT) for accurate quantification of plaque burden.
- Patients with peripheral neuropathy, foot deformity, previous amputation, or baseline significant ST-T abnormalities are ineligible for TMT and require pharmacological stress testing or CAC scoring
