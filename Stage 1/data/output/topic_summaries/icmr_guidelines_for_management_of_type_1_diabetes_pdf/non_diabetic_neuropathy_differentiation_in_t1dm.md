# non-diabetic neuropathy differentiation in T1DM
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** non-diabetic neuropathy differentiation in T1DM

**Core Management Principles:**
- Identify non-diabetic neuropathies in T1DM patients by excluding diabetic neuropathies when atypical features are present.
- Use combination of typical symptoms and symmetrical distal sensory loss or signs to suggest diabetic sensorimotor polyneuropathy (DSPN) without additional evaluation.
- Evaluate for non-diabetic neuropathies in patients with atypical features such as onset <5 years from diabetes diagnosis, pure motor neuropathy, asymmetrical neuropathy, rapid progression, or family history of non-diabetic neuropathy.
- Consider pressure palsies and chronic inflammatory demyelinating polyneuropathy (CIDP) as more common non-diabetic neuropathies in T1DM patients, given their higher prevalence in this population.

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Exclude non-diabetic neuropathies before diagnosing diabetic nephropathy (DN) to enable specific treatment measures.
- Avoid extensive laboratory evaluation for non-diabetic neuropathies in all diabetic patients with neuropathy due to cost-effectiveness concerns.
- Diagnose diabetic nephropathy (DN) only after excluding non-diabetic neuropathies to enable targeted treatment interventions.
