# diabetic foot ulcer prevention protocol
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** diabetic foot ulcer prevention protocol

**Core Management Principles:**
- Foot examination should be done at each visit in patients with diabetic neuropathy (DN) and at least annually in those without DN.
- Identification of foot at risk is the most important step in the prevention of diabetic foot ulcer.
- Inspect feet daily yourself (with the help of a mirror to inspect soles) or with the help of someone, especially when vision is compromised.
- Wash feet daily with a non-medicated soap; pat feet dry with a soft absorbent cloth, especially between toes.
- Apply lubricants containing urea or salicylates to the feet, sparing the interdigital spaces.
- Avoid walking barefoot, even indoors, to prevent trauma to the feet.

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Foot examination at each visit for patients with DN.
- Annual foot examination for patients without DN.
- Patients with foot at risk should be educated on foot care practices by a trained healthcare professional during each visit.
