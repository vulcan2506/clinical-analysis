# travel preparation for insulin-dependent diabetes
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** travel preparation for insulin-dependent diabetes

**Core Management Principles:**
- Inform physician 4-6 weeks before travel for education on insulin storage, dose adjustment, hypoglycemia care, and sick day rules
- Carry medications and supplies in hand baggage with reserve supplies for 2-4 weeks
- Use two glucose meters and adequate supplies (strips, batteries, ketone strips, logbooks, glucose tablets, glucagon injection)
- Keep insulin between 4°C-30°C, avoid extreme temperatures, and carry insulin in hand baggage during air travel
- Check blood glucose every 4 hours during travel and adjust insulin doses based on time zone changes
- Inform airline in advance about diabetes status, medications, and materials to facilitate check-in, security checks, and hypoglycemia care
- Arrive at least 3 hours before departure to navigate check-in and security smoothly

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Adequate glycemic control before travel with appropriate insulin type/dose adjustments
- Legible prescription with all medicines and blood testing materials
- Medical identification bracelet with disease, insulin use, and allergies
- Valid travel insurance for international travel
- Carry snacks and supplies to treat hypoglycemia, and inform fellow passengers/crew
- Carry a prescription for common ailments (fever, emesis, diarrhea) and a first aid kit
- Ensure specific vaccines are obtained based on the region of travel
