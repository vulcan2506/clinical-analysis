# high anion gap metabolic acidosis differentials
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** high anion gap metabolic acidosis differentials in T1DM

**Core Management Principles:**
- Diabetic ketoacidosis is a primary consideration in high anion gap metabolic acidosis in T1DM patients.
- Uremia should be evaluated as a potential cause of high anion gap metabolic acidosis.
- Lactic acidosis must be considered in the differential diagnosis.
- Toxic ingestions (methanol, ethylene glycol, ethanol) are critical differentials to rule out.

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Prompt evaluation of anion gap in T1DM patients presenting with metabolic acidosis.
- Systematic assessment of all potential causes listed in the differential diagnosis table.
