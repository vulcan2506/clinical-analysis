# insulin injection technique for pediatric T1DM
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** insulin injection technique for pediatric T1DM

**Core Management Principles:**
- Select an appropriate injection site and always rotate sites
- Clean the skin with soap and water if visibly dirty, then allow the area to dry before injecting
- Pinch 1-2-inch portion of skin and fat between thumb and first finger
- Insert needle at 45° angle for children/adolescents or perpendicularly for most adults, inject steadily, and hold needle in skin for 10-15 seconds
- Press injection site for 5-10 seconds after removal to prevent insulin leakage

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Use appropriate needle length and angle based on pediatric patient's body habitus
- Ensure proper site rotation to prevent lipohypertrophy or lipoatrophy
- Maintain clean injection sites to reduce infection risk
- Hold needle in place for 10-15 seconds to ensure complete insulin delivery
- Dispose of needles safely in a tin or plastic box
- Monitor and adjust insulin requirements based on dynamic changes during puberty, growth hormone levels, and sex hormones
- Account for individual variability in insulin needs related to time of day, meals, and exercise
