# iatrogenic hypoglycemia pathogenesis in T1DM
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** pathogenesis of iatrogenic hypoglycemia in T1DM

**Core Management Principles:**
- Hypoglycemia associated autonomic failure (HAAF) develops as a consequence of recurrent hypoglycemic episodes.
- HAAF leads to the loss of autonomic warning symptoms such as palpitations, tremors, and sweating.
- The absence of warning symptoms increases the risk of severe hypoglycemia and hypoglycemia unawareness.
- Pathogenesis involves the failure of counter-regulatory hormone responses (glucagon and epinephrine) during low glucose states.
- Clinical progression moves from frequent mild hypoglycemia to severe neuroglycopenic events due to autonomic exhaustion.

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Implementation of continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) to detect trends before critical lows.
- Strict avoidance of hypoglycemia through individualized insulin dosing and carbohydrate intake.
- Patient education on recognizing early warning signs before HAAF sets in.
- Regular assessment of hypoglycemia awareness status during clinical visits.
- Adjustment of insulin regimens to prevent nocturnal and fasting hypoglycemia.
