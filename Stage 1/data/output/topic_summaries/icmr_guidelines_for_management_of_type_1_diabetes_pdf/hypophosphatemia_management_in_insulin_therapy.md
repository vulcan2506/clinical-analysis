# hypophosphatemia management in insulin therapy
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** hypophosphatemia management in insulin therapy

**Core Management Principles:**
- Hypophosphatemia usually manifests after treatment initiation due to transcellular shift of phosphate from extracellular to intracellular compartment by insulin.
- Encouraging early oral intake (<24 hours) may help prevent hypophosphatemia.
- Severe hypophosphatemia (serum phosphorus <1 mg/dl) may manifest as muscle weakness and rhabdomyolysis.

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Monitor serum phosphorus levels in patients receiving insulin therapy.
- Assess for symptoms of severe hypophosphatemia (e.g., muscle weakness, rhabdomyolysis).
