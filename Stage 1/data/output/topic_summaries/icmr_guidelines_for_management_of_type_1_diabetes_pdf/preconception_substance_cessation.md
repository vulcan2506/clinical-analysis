# preconception substance cessation
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** preconception substance cessation in T1DM

**Core Management Principles:**
- Women with T1DM should be advised to stop smoking, alcohol, and illicit drug use during the preconception period and thereafter.
- Referral to a de-addiction clinic or expert may be done if required.

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Preconception counseling must include substance cessation advice for women with T1DM.
