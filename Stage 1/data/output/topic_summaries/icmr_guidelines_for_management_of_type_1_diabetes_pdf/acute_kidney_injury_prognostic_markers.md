# acute kidney injury prognostic markers
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** acute kidney injury prognostic markers in T1DM

**Core Management Principles:**
- AKI with urine output < 0.5 ml/kg/hr is identified as a poor prognostic marker
- Poor prognostic markers can predispose patients to cerebral edema
- Poor prognostic markers can predispose patients to acute respiratory distress syndrome (ARDS)
- AKI with serum creatinine rise > 0.3 mg/dL within 48 hours is an additional poor prognostic marker in T1DM

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Monitoring for oliguria requires strict hourly urine output measurement in critically ill T1DM patients with AKI
