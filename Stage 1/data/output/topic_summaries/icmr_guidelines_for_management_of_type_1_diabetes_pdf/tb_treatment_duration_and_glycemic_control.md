# TB treatment duration and glycemic control
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** TB treatment duration and glycemic control in T1DM

**Core Management Principles:**
- Tuberculosis in patients with diabetes is associated with increased relapse rates.
- New cases of PTB with diabetes should be treated for 6 months (as per the standardized WHO regimen).
- Tight glycemic control makes anti-tubercular drugs more effective and leads to better clinical, radiological and bacteriological resolution of the disease.
- Due to worsening of glycemic control with acute infection, insulin doses may need up-titration during the initial phase, requiring close follow-up with the treating physician.

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Standard four-drug regimen for tuberculosis treatment: isoniazid with pyridoxine, rifampicin, ethambutol, and pyrazinamide.
