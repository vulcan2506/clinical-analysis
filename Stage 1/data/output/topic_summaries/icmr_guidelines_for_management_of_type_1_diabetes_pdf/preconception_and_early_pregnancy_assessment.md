# preconception and early pregnancy assessment
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** preconception and early pregnancy assessment in T1DM

**Core Management Principles:**
- Ideal booking appointment by 10 weeks of gestation for comprehensive diabetes and complication review.
- Conduct retinal and renal assessments for women with pre-existing diabetes if not performed in the last 3 months.
- Schedule joint diabetes and antenatal clinic visits every 1–2 weeks throughout pregnancy.
- Perform early viability scan at 7–9 weeks to confirm pregnancy viability and gestational age due to higher miscarriage risk.
- Conduct detailed ultrasound and fetal echocardiogram at 18–20 weeks to screen for neural tube defects, cardiac, and structural abnormalities.

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Blood pressure measurement, per speculum examination, hemogram, kidney function tests, liver function test, TSH, urine routine microscopy and culture.
- HbA1c measurement in every trimester.
- Retinal assessment unless assessed in the last 3 months.
- Renal assessment if not performed in the last 3 months.
- Neural tube/nasal bone scan with dual screen test for chromosomal anomalies at 11–14 weeks.
