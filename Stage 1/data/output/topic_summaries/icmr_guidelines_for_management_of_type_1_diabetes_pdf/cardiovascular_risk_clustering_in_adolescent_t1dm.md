# cardiovascular risk clustering in adolescent T1DM
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** cardiovascular risk clustering in adolescent T1DM

**Core Management Principles:**
- Clustering of cardiovascular risk factors is common in adolescents with T1DM, placing them at high risk for future cardiovascular events.
- 14-45% of children with T1DM have two or more cardiovascular risk factors.
- Overweight/obesity is prevalent in ~40% of children with T1DM and is associated with higher rates of hypertension, metabolic syndrome, and transaminitis.
- Clustering of cardiovascular risk factors in adolescent T1DM includes dyslipidemia (elevated LDL, triglycerides, and low HDL) as a key component, in addition to hypertension and metabolic syndrome.

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Screening for cardiovascular risk factors (e.g., hypertension, metabolic syndrome) in adolescents with T1DM.
- Regular monitoring of body weight and metabolic parameters in this population.
- Mandatory annual screening for dyslipidemia (lipid profile) in adolescents with T1DM, regardless of symptom presence.
