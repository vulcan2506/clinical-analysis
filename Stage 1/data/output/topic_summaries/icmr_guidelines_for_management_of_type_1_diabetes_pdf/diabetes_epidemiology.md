# diabetes epidemiology
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** diabetes epidemiology in young Indians

**Core Management Principles:**
- Increasing burden of diabetes in children and adolescents in India
- Type 1 diabetes is the commonest form in children and adolescents
- Type 2 diabetes is becoming more common in children
- MODY and FCPD may be misdiagnosed as type 1 diabetes in younger patients
- Improved awareness and early diagnosis are contributing to rising type 1 diabetes detection
- Diabetic ketoacidosis remains a significant problem in rural and peripheral healthcare centers despite improvements in urban management

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Accurate differential diagnosis to distinguish type 1 diabetes from MODY, FCPD, and other forms
- Access to precise blood glucose monitoring tools for children with type 1 diabetes
- Early diagnosis and treatment to reduce diabetic ketoacidosis in rural and peripheral centers
- Equitable access to advanced diabetes care tools (e.g., CGM, precise glucometers) across rural and peripheral centers to reduce disparities in type 1 diabetes management
