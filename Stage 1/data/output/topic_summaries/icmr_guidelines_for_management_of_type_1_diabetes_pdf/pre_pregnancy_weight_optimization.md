# pre-pregnancy weight optimization
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** pre-pregnancy weight optimization in T1DM

**Core Management Principles:**
- Optimize weight before pregnancy to reduce adverse outcomes for mother and offspring.
- Women with excess weight should preferably lose weight before pregnancy.
- Strongly advise and encourage weight loss in women with BMI >27 kg/m² before conception.

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- BMI assessment before pregnancy planning.
- Individualized weight loss counseling tailored to pre-pregnancy health status.
- BMI assessment must be conducted as part of pre-pregnancy planning for women with T1DM
