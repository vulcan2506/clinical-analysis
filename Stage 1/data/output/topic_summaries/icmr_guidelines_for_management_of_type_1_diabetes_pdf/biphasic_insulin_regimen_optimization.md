# biphasic insulin regimen optimization
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** biphasic insulin regimen optimization in T1DM

**Core Management Principles:**
- Mimic physiological insulin action relative to meals, snacks, and exercise while avoiding hypoglycemia
- Use premixed insulin (fixed ratio of short/intermediate-acting insulin) twice daily (before breakfast and dinner) with a short-acting insulin before lunch as a practical option in resource-limited settings
- Resuspend premixed insulin by gently shaking or rolling the pen/vial before injection
- Adjust basal insulin based on fasting glucose and bolus doses based on post-meal glucose levels in basal-bolus regimens
- Provide patient/family education and motivation for successful regimen implementation
- Use 'meal bolus' calculators (incorporated into pumps or separate devices) to determine prandial insulin doses based on pre-meal glucose, carbohydrate content, and exercise in basal-bolus regimens
- Adjust basal insulin rates in CSII based on circadian variations (e.g., higher rates after 4 AM due to dawn phenomenon and counter-regulatory hormone release)

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Total daily dose (TDD) should be split approximately 2/3 in the morning and 1/3 at night for biphasic regimens
- Meals and snacks should not vary significantly in timing or quantity to avoid hypoglycemia in fixed-ratio regimens
- Basal-bolus regimens require 40-60% of TDD as basal insulin (preferably long-acting analogs if affordable)
- CSII requires motivated patients/families willing to monitor glucose 4-6 times/day (finger-prick or CGMS) and adjust doses
- CSII candidates must have backup insulin for subcutaneous injection in case of pump malfunction
- For CSII, patients must have access to CGMS for trend-based insulin suspension or hybrid closed-loop functionality to reduce hypoglycemia risk
- Hybrid closed-loop insulin pumps require patient/caregiver willingness to monitor glucose 4-6 times/day and adjust bolus doses manually
