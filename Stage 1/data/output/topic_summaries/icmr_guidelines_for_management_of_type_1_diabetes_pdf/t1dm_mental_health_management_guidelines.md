# T1DM mental health management guidelines
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** T1DM mental health management guidelines

**Core Management Principles:**
- Screen all T1DM patients for depression, anxiety, and diabetes distress at diagnosis and annually using validated tools like PHQ-9, GAD-7, and PAID.
- Integrate mental health professionals into the diabetes care team for collaborative management.
- Provide structured psychological support, including cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) and diabetes self-management education (DSME) to address emotional and behavioral challenges.
- Encourage peer support groups and family involvement to improve coping and adherence to treatment.
- Monitor for burnout and eating disorders, particularly in adolescents and young adults with T1DM.
- Screen for fear of hypoglycemia using validated tools like the Hypoglycemia Fear Survey (HFS) in patients with recurrent hypoglycemia or poor glycemic control.
- Assess sleep disturbances and insomnia in T1DM patients, particularly those with nocturnal hypoglycemia or poor glycemic control.

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Mandatory mental health screening at diagnosis and annual follow-ups for all T1DM patients.
- Training of diabetes care teams in recognizing and managing mental health comorbidities.
- Availability of mental health professionals with expertise in diabetes-related psychological issues.
- Access to culturally appropriate and age-specific mental health interventions.
- Integration of mental health assessments into routine diabetes care visits.
- Mandatory training of mental health professionals in diabetes-specific psychological issues and cultural competencies for Indian patients.
- Establish referral pathways for mental health services within diabetes care centers, including tele-mental health options for remote areas.
