# driving safety and hypoglycemia prevention
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** driving safety and hypoglycemia prevention in T1DM

**Core Management Principles:**
- Ensure blood glucose (BG) is not low or trending low before driving
- Carry non-perishable snacks and simple sugar sources in the vehicle for emergencies
- Monitor BG trends using CGMS and avoid even mild hypoglycemia
- Avoid driving during rapid BG changes that cause vision blurring
- Refrain from consuming alcohol or intoxicants before or while driving

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Minimum age of 16 years for a learner’s license, 18 years for a regular license, and 20 years for a commercial license
- Normal vision before driving
- Frequent BG testing initially
- Normal vision must be maintained for driving
