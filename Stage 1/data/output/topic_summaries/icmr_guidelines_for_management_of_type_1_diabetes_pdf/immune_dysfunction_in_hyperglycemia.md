# immune dysfunction in hyperglycemia
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** immune dysfunction in hyperglycemia

**Core Management Principles:**
- Hyperglycemia impairs chemotaxis, phagocytosis, and bactericidal activity of neutrophils and macrophages.
- Reduced T lymphocyte function is observed in hyperglycemia.
- Defects in humoral innate immunity include reduced complement factor C4 levels.
- Impaired cytokine response to stimulation contributes to increased infection risk.
