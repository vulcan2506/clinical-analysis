# neuropathy grading criteria
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** neuropathy grading criteria

**Core Management Principles:**
- Grade 2a: Abnormal nerve conduction (NC) plus symptoms of diabetic sensorimotor polyneuropathy (DSPN) with or without signs
- Grade 2b: Abnormal NC plus moderate weakness (50%) of ankle dorsiflexion with or without DSPN symptoms
