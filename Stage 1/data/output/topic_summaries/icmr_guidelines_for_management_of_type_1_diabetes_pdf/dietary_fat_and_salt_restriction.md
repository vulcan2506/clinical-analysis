# dietary fat and salt restriction
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** dietary fat and salt restriction in children with diabetes

**Core Management Principles:**
- Ensure RDA of all vitamins and minerals in the diet by appropriate choice of macronutrients
- Limit daily salt intake to 1000 mg (2.5 g salt) in 1-3 years old children
- Limit daily salt intake to 1200 mg (3 g salt) in 4-8 years old children
- Limit daily salt intake to 1500 mg (3.8 g salt) for children and adolescents aged ≥ nine years
- Limit daily salt intake to 2300 mg (6.0 g salt) in adults
