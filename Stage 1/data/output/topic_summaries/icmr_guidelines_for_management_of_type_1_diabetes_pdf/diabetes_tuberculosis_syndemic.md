# diabetes tuberculosis syndemic
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** diabetes-tuberculosis syndemic interaction

**Core Management Principles:**
- Diabetes increases risk of tuberculosis 3.5-5.0-fold, especially in T1DM patients
- Tuberculosis worsens glycemic control, creating a bidirectional vicious cycle
- Diabetes alters tuberculosis clinical features, radiological manifestations, and treatment outcomes
- Uncontrolled hyperglycemia accelerates tuberculosis progression and increases mortality
- Rifampicin in anti-tuberculosis treatment may exacerbate hyperglycemia

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Screening for pulmonary tuberculosis in T1DM patients with sputum culture
- Aggressive glycemic control to reduce tuberculosis progression risk
- Monitoring for drug-resistant tuberculosis in diabetic patients
- Assessment of micronutrient deficiencies and renal insufficiency in diabetic patients with tuberculosis
- Evaluation of insulin resistance and beta-cell function in tuberculosis patients with stress-induced hyperglycemia
