# travel management protocols
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** travel management protocols for T1DM

**Core Management Principles:**
- Increase blood glucose monitoring frequency during travel due to variable eating, activity, and sleep patterns
- Adjust insulin doses based on travel-related changes in routine
- Transport insulin and glucagon at 2-25°C in an insulated carrycase, never in check-in baggage
- Carry all diabetes supplies including insulin, syringes, pen needles, BG strips, meter, sensor, disposables, hypoglycemia prevention items, and concentrated calorie sources
- Carry 50% extra supplies as spares for the duration of travel
- Carry concentrated calorie sources like dry fruits specifically to manage hypoglycemia during travel

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Parents must ensure accompanying teachers can handle routine care and emergencies during school trips
- Emergency contact details must be provided to accompanying teachers
- Discarded sharps must be stored safely for later disposal
- Ensure teachers are trained to handle routine diabetes care and emergencies during school trips
