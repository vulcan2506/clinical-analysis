# telehealth integration in diabetes care
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Telehealth integration in diabetes care for remote and underserved regions

**Core Management Principles:**
- Telehealth enables access to diabetes care experts in remote areas where healthcare providers are scarce.
- Mobile phone penetration and low-cost digital tools facilitate remote patient-provider interactions.
- Government legalization of telemedicine (e.g., in India) supports broader adoption during crises like COVID-19.
- Digital tools improve care delivery in regions with limited access to specialized diabetes management.
- Cold chain breaches for insulin in remote areas lead to variability in glycemic control, requiring urgent attention.

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Availability of mobile phone infrastructure in remote areas.
- Legal and regulatory frameworks supporting telemedicine (e.g., government approval).
- Access to digital tools (e.g., smartphones, internet) for patients and providers.
- Training for healthcare providers in telehealth-based diabetes management.
- Ensuring insulin cold chain integrity in remote storage and transport.
