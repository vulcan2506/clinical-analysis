# T1DM diagnosis and management framework
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** holistic T1DM diagnosis and management framework

**Core Management Principles:**
- Start a long-term, close relationship between patient, family, and diabetes care team for effective management.
- Provide age-appropriate, culturally sensitive education tailored to socio-economic status, motivation, and medical status.
- Emphasize lifelong self-care with insulin, pathophysiology education, and differentiation from T2DM.
- Focus on comprehensive self-management skills including blood glucose/ketone testing, insulin handling, hypoglycemia management, diet, and exercise.
- Address psychological responses, family dynamics, and coping mechanisms to prevent burnout.
- Incorporate cognitive behavior therapy (CBT) explicitly into routine psychological support for patients and families to address burnout and coping mechanisms.

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Lifelong insulin therapy for T1DM management.
- Regular blood glucose and ketone monitoring with pattern discernment.
- Standardized education on insulin types, action profiles, storage, and injection techniques.
- Access to diabetes technology and peer support groups.
- Psychological support and cognitive behavior therapy for patients and families.
- Mandatory inclusion of ketone testing in blood glucose monitoring protocols to detect and prevent diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA).
