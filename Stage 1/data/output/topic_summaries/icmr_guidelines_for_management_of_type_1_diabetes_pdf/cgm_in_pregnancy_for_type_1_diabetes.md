# CGM in pregnancy for type 1 diabetes
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** CGM in pregnancy for type 1 diabetes

**Core Management Principles:**
- CGM provides detailed glucose pattern data to optimize diet, lifestyle, and insulin doses
- CGM alerts users to impending hypoglycemia or hyperglycemia for earlier corrective action
- Sensor-integrated insulin delivery improves time in range compared to stand-alone pump therapy
- CGM reduces neonatal intensive care admissions, large-for-gestational-age births, and neonatal hypoglycemia in T1DM pregnancies
- CGM alerts healthcare providers to glycemic trends for remote monitoring and shared decision-making in pregnancy

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Women with type 1 diabetes mellitus (T1DM) at <14 weeks gestation
- Continuous CGM use from randomization until delivery
- Preconception initiation of insulin therapy for women with recurrent hypoglycemia or hypoglycemia unawareness
- Women with T1DM and a history of recurrent troublesome hypoglycemia or hypoglycemia unawareness should initiate insulin therapy preconception
