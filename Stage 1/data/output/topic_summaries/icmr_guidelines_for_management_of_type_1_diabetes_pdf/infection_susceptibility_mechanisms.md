# infection susceptibility mechanisms
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** infection susceptibility mechanisms in T1DM

**Core Management Principles:**
- Impaired immune function increases susceptibility to infections in patients with diabetes.
- Micro and macroangiopathy contribute to increased infection risk.
- Glucosuria acts as a mechanism for increased infection susceptibility.
- Diabetic sensory neuropathy leads to loss of protective sensation.
- Diabetic autonomic neuropathy contributes to infection susceptibility.
- Impaired neutrophil function (e.g., chemotaxis, phagocytosis) contributes to higher infection risk in T1DM
- Chronic hyperglycemia directly impairs immune cell function and cytokine production

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Assessment of immune function status in diabetic patients.
- Evaluation of micro and macroangiopathy presence.
- Monitoring of glucosuria levels.
- Screening for diabetic sensory neuropathy.
- Evaluation of diabetic autonomic neuropathy.
- Assessment of glycemic control (HbA1c) as a proxy for infection risk stratification
- Evaluation of chronic hyperglycemia severity to identify high-risk patients
