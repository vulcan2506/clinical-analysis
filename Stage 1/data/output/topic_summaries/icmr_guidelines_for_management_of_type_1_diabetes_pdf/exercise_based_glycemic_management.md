# exercise-based glycemic management
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** exercise-based glycemic management in diabetes

**Core Management Principles:**
- Reduce pre-meal bolus insulin dose if exercise is anticipated within 1 hour of rapid-acting insulin or within 3 hours of regular insulin
- Prefer reduced insulin dosage over carbohydrate supplementation for regular physical activities in children with obesity
- Use carbohydrate consumption before activity to maintain blood glucose in cases of spontaneous exercise or postprandial hyperglycemia
- Set a temporary lower basal rate (50-80% reduction) in CSII users at least 90 minutes before exercise, lasting until activity stops
- Monitor blood glucose at regular intervals during unaccustomed activities and supplement carbohydrates adequately
- Consider a 30-50% reduction in basal insulin the previous day and on the day of activity for all-day or prolonged activities
- Prefer administration of foods containing fat or protein with carbohydrates for activities lasting more than 30-60 minutes

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Patients must avoid exercise if hyperglycemia (random BG >250 mg/dl) with any ketonuria or ketosis (blood β-hydroxybutyrate >0.5 mmol/L) is present
- Blood ketone measurement is preferred over urine ketones for rapid detection and monitoring of ketosis
- Patients using CSII should have access to continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) with low glucose suspension for hypoglycemia prevention
- Patients must maintain adequate hydration before, during, and after exercise
- Patients should engage in 150+ minutes of moderate-to-vigorous activity weekly, spread over at least 3 days, with 2-3 resistance sessions weekly
- Adults with T1DM should engage in 150+ minutes of moderate-to-vigorous intensity activity per week spread over at least three days/week
- Adults with T1DM should engage in 2-3 sessions per week of resistance exercise on non-consecutive days
