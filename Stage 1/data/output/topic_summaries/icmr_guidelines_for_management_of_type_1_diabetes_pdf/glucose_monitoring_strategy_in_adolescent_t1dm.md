# glucose monitoring strategy in adolescent T1DM
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** glucose monitoring strategy in adolescent T1DM

**Core Management Principles:**
- Monitor capillary blood glucose prior to meals/snacks, at bedtime, postprandial occasionally, before exercise, when hypoglycemia is suspected, after treating hypoglycemia, and before critical tasks (e.g., driving)
- Check blood glucose intermittently (1-2 weeks) at 1-3 AM for nocturnal hypoglycemia and frequently during illness/travel
- Use SMBG values systematically for insulin dose adjustments, meal timing/content, and treatment decisions
- Review documented SMBG logs during each visit, correlating with HbA1c and insulin doses
- Educate patients/caregivers on interpreting SMBG patterns and making minor insulin adjustments
- Cross-check SMBG values with venous plasma glucose when in doubt, especially outside the 60-160 mg/dl range or in postprandial states
- Document remarks in SMBG logs explaining aberrant glucose values (e.g., hyperglycemia due to eating outside, less insulin dose, or hypoglycemia due to delayed meals, excessive exercise)

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- T1DM diagnosis in adolescent patients
- Access to glucometer and test strips (or urine glucose strips as last resort)
- Patient/caregiver training on SMBG technique and interpretation
- Psychosocial support and parental supervision for consistent SMBG adherence
- Financial resources for frequent testing (alternative strategies for cost-constrained settings)
- Patient/caregiver training on recognizing and responding to SMBG patterns, including minor insulin dose adjustments
- Assessment of psychosocial factors (e.g., stress, self-esteem) and parental support as predictors of SMBG adherence in adolescents
