# neuropathy-associated infection risks
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** neuropathy-associated infection risks

**Core Management Principles:**
- Loss of protective sensation (LOPS) due to neuropathy is associated with increased risk of foot ulceration and diabetic foot infection.
- Impaired bladder emptying due to diabetic autonomic neuropathy may predispose to urinary tract infection.
- Gastrointestinal dysmotility due to diabetic autonomic neuropathy may predispose to gastrointestinal infection.

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Assessment of loss of protective sensation (LOPS) to identify patients at risk for foot ulceration.
- Evaluation of bladder emptying function to detect autonomic neuropathy.
- Monitoring of gastrointestinal motility to identify dysmotility risks.
