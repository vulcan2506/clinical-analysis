# glucometer accuracy optimization
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** glucometer accuracy optimization

**Core Management Principles:**
- Use control solution (CS) to assess glucometer working status, though storage and stability of CS are concerns.
- Address pre-analytical variables like expired test strips, improper storage conditions (temperature/humidity), unwashed hands, and hematocrit/hypotension.
- Ensure adequate blood volume (as low as 0.3 µl) for accurate readings.
- Adjust screen/font size or use talking meters for visually impaired patients.
- Consider dexterity limitations in patients performing SMBG.
- Verify glucometer calibration periodically as part of accuracy optimization

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Control solution (CS) usage for glucometer accuracy assessment.
- Proper storage of test strips (avoid temperature/humidity extremes).
- Adequate blood volume (0.3 µl) for testing.
- Screen/font size adjustments or talking meters for visually impaired patients.
- Assessment of patient dexterity for SMBG feasibility.
- Ensure test strips are stored in original containers to maintain stability
- Use glucometers with ISO 15197:2013 certification for clinical accuracy
