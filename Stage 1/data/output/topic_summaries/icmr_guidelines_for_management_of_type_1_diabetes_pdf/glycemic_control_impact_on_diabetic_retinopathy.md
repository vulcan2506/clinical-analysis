# glycemic control impact on diabetic retinopathy
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** glycemic control impact on diabetic retinopathy

**Core Management Principles:**
- Glycemic control remains paramount in management, as established by DCCT.
- Controlling hyperglycemia is associated with reduction in the incidence as well as progression of diabetic retinopathy (DR).
- Each 10% proportional decline in HbA1c (e.g., from 9.0% to 8.1%) is associated with a 39% reduction in DR progression.
- HbA1c targets should be individualized based on patient factors such as age, duration of diabetes, and risk of hypoglycemia, with stricter targets (e.g., <7.0%) for those at lower hypoglycemic risk and less stringent targets for older adults or those with limited life expectancy.

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- HbA1c monitoring is essential for assessing glycemic control and DR risk.
- Regular retinal screening is mandated at diagnosis for T1DM patients with duration ≥5 years, and annually thereafter if retinopathy is present or progressing.
