# individualized maternal nutrition plan
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** individualized maternal nutrition plan for T1DM

**Core Management Principles:**
- Create culturally sensitive and socio-economically viable nutrition plans with expert dietitian support
- Adopt a less carbohydrate-restricted approach using higher quality carbohydrates, lower fat, and ethnically acceptable foods
- Structure meals as three major meals and three to four adequately spaced snacks to avoid hypoglycemia and prevent glycemic fluctuations
- Ensure adequate caloric intake for fetal and maternal health while achieving glycemic goals without hypoglycemia
- Monitor attention to timings, content, and quality of meals and snacks
- Incorporate ethnically acceptable foods to enhance adherence and cultural relevance in meal planning

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Involvement of an expert dietician for plan formulation
- Adherence to Dietary Reference Intakes (DRI) for macronutrients
- Maintenance of appropriate gestational weight gain
- Prevention of hypoglycemia events
- Achievement of target glycemic goals
- Ensure adequate caloric intake to support fetal growth and maternal health while achieving glycemic goals
