# macrovascular complication prevention
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** macrovascular complication prevention in T1DM

**Core Management Principles:**
- Optimal glycemic control (HbA1c <7%) using multiple subcutaneous insulin injections or insulin pump therapy while minimizing iatrogenic hypoglycemia
- Treat hypertension with ACE inhibitor/ARB in combination with calcium channel blockers or thiazide diuretics, targeting BP <140/90 mm Hg (or <130/80 mm Hg if achievable without additional burden)
- Use ACE inhibitor/ARB in patients with albuminuria regardless of hypertension to prevent progression
- Initiate statin therapy in patients with pre-existing CVD lifelong, regardless of lipid levels, and consider antiplatelet agents (aspirin alone or aspirin plus clopidogrel for 12 months post-acute coronary syndrome or PCI)
- Engage in regular physical activity, consume a diet low in salt, saturated fat, and simple sugars, and rich in fruits, green leafy vegetables, dairy products, and fiber; structured diet plans designed by a qualified dietician should be provided

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Glycemic control should be attempted in all individuals, especially those with short duration of diabetes and without other co-morbidities
- ACE inhibitor/ARB should be used in patients with albuminuria regardless of hypertension
- Statin therapy should be initiated in children aged 11 years or more with LDL-cholesterol >130 mg/dl despite adequate dietary and lifestyle measures
- Weight management and periodic body weight monitoring are required
- Smoking cessation programs should be considered in selected cases
