# gastrointestinal complications in diabetes
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** gastrointestinal complications in diabetes

**Core Management Principles:**
- Gastrointestinal dysmotility and immune dysfunction increase predisposition to enteric infections in diabetes patients.
- Emphysematous cholecystitis is a severe complication of acute cholecystitis with high perforation and gangrene risks.

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Emergent cholecystectomy is required for emphysematous cholecystitis.
