# carbohydrate restriction risks in pediatric T1DM
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** carbohydrate restriction risks in pediatric T1DM

**Core Management Principles:**
- Restrictive carbohydrate diets in children with T1DM may lead to endocrine and metabolic consequences, as highlighted in case series evidence.
- Dietary advice on carbohydrate intake for pregnant women with T1DM should be carefully managed to avoid adverse outcomes.
- Physical activity interventions in children with T1DM require careful consideration of carbohydrate availability to prevent hypoglycemia.
- Pregnant women with T1DM require tailored carbohydrate intake to balance glycemic control and fetal growth, with risks of excessive restriction including preterm birth or macrosomia if not properly managed.

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Carbohydrate intake must be sufficient to support growth, development, and metabolic needs in pediatric T1DM.
- Dietary recommendations should be individualized based on age, activity level, and glycemic control.
- Close monitoring of blood glucose levels is essential when adjusting carbohydrate intake or physical activity.
- Carbohydrate intake should be adjusted based on the child's growth velocity and pubertal status to support optimal development without compromising glycemic stability.
