# pediatric diabetes cardiovascular risk management
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Pediatric diabetes cardiovascular risk management

**Core Management Principles:**
- Screen for hypertension in children and adolescents with type 1 diabetes using standardized pediatric blood pressure tables.
- Assess and manage obesity in pediatric patients with diabetes using WHO or CDC growth charts for BMI classification.
- Evaluate cardiovascular risk in pediatric diabetes using validated risk prediction models adapted for children (e.g., Reynolds Risk Score adaptations).
- Monitor glycemic control closely in pediatric diabetes to mitigate progression of coronary artery calcification.
- Screen for subclinical atherosclerosis in high-risk pediatric diabetes patients using imaging modalities such as MRI or CT angiography.
- Screen for dyslipidemia in pediatric patients with type 1 diabetes using fasting lipid profiles or non-HDL cholesterol, with thresholds adjusted for age and pubertal status.
- Assess endothelial dysfunction in high-risk pediatric diabetes patients using non-invasive methods such as flow-mediated dilation (FMD) or pulse wave velocity (PWV).

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Diagnosis of type 1 diabetes in pediatric patients.
- Age-appropriate blood pressure and BMI percentile data available.
- Access to validated pediatric cardiovascular risk assessment tools.
- Baseline assessment of glycemic control (e.g., HbA1c) and lipid profile.
- Presence of additional risk factors (e.g., nephropathy, family history of CVD).
- Documentation of pubertal status (Tanner staging) for accurate interpretation of BMI percentiles and blood pressure thresholds in pediatric diabetes patients.
- Baseline assessment of albuminuria or estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) to identify nephropathy as a cardiovascular risk multiplier in pediatric diabetes.
