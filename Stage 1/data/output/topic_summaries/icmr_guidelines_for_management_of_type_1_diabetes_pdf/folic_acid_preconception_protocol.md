# folic acid preconception protocol
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** folic acid preconception protocol for T1DM

**Core Management Principles:**
- Folic acid supplementation with a daily dose of 5 mg should be started 3 months before planned conception.

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Planned conception timing must be assessed to initiate folic acid supplementation 3 months prior.
- Assess folate status (if feasible) before initiating supplementation to guide dosing and monitor for deficiency.
