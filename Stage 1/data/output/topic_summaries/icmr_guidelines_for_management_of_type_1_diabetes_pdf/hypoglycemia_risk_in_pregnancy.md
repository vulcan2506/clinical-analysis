# hypoglycemia risk in pregnancy
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** hypoglycemia risk in pregnancy

**Core Management Principles:**
- Severe hypoglycemia is 3 to 5 times more frequent in the first half of pregnancy than before conception.
- Up to 45% of women with T1DM experience severe hypoglycemia during pregnancy.
- A history of severe hypoglycemia in the year preceding pregnancy and impaired hypoglycemia awareness are significant risk factors.
- Impaired hypoglycemia awareness triples the risk of severe hypoglycemia.
- Intensive insulin treatment, lower HbA1c in early pregnancy, and fluctuating plasma glucose increase hypoglycemia risk.
- 60% of severe hypoglycemic episodes are accounted for by only 10% of pregnant women with T1DM
- Severe hypoglycemia incidence decreases after the first trimester

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Assess hypoglycemia awareness using a simple question like 'Do you recognize symptoms when you have hypoglycemia?'
- Evaluate history of severe hypoglycemia in the year preceding pregnancy.
- Monitor for fluctuating plasma glucose values (<70 to >180 mg/dl).
- Consider longer duration of diabetes as a risk factor.
- Individualize glycemic targets to balance near normoglycemia with hypoglycemia risk.
