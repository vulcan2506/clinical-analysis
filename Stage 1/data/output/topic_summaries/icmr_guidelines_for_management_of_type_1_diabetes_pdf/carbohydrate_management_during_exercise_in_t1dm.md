# carbohydrate management during exercise in T1DM
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** carbohydrate management during exercise in T1DM

**Core Management Principles:**
- Adjust carbohydrate intake based on pre-exercise blood glucose (BG) levels: 0.3-0.5 g/kg/h for BG < 90 mg/dl, 0.8-1.0 g/kg/h for BG 90-150 mg/dl, and avoid carbohydrates for BG 150-250 mg/dl until BG < 150 mg/dl.
- For prolonged activities, consume additional carbohydrates (0.5-1.0 g/kg/h) based on BG testing results.
- Children and adolescents with T1DM should engage in 60+ minutes/day of moderate or vigorous-intensity aerobic activity, plus vigorous, muscle-strengthening, and bone-strengthening activities at least three days a week.
- Patients with cardiovascular disease (CVD) signs, diabetes-related complications, or sedentary individuals >30 years with diabetes ≥10 years should undergo CVD evaluation before starting new exercise programs.
- Vigorous exercise is contraindicated in patients with severe non-proliferative diabetic retinopathy (NPDR) or proliferative diabetic retinopathy (PDR) to reduce vitreous hemorrhage risk.
- For prolonged activities (>60 minutes), monitor blood glucose every 30 minutes and adjust carbohydrate intake (0.5-1.0 g/kg/h) based on trends to prevent hypoglycemia or hyperglycemia.

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Pre-exercise BG testing to determine carbohydrate intake adjustments.
- CVD evaluation for high-risk patients before initiating new exercise programs.
- Avoidance of vigorous exercise in patients with severe NPDR or PDR.
- Use quick-acting carbohydrates (e.g., glucose tablets, fruit juice) for hypoglycemia correction during exercise to ensure rapid glucose absorption.
