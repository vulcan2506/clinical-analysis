# physical activity contraindications
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Physical activity contraindications in pregnancy with type 1 diabetes

**Core Management Principles:**
- Identify absolute contraindications to physical activity during pregnancy including hemodynamically significant heart disease, restrictive lung disease, incompetent cervix, multiple gestation at risk for premature labor, persistent second or third trimester bleeding, placenta previa after 26 weeks, premature labor, ruptured membranes, and pre-eclampsia.
- Assess relative contraindications such as severe anemia, unevaluated maternal cardiac arrhythmias, chronic bronchitis, uncontrolled diabetes, extreme morbid obesity, extreme underweight (BMI <12 kg/m²), history of extremely sedentary lifestyle, poorly controlled hypertension, hyperthyroidism, seizures, orthopedic limitations, and heavy smoking.
- Implement hypoglycemia prevention measures in high-risk patients with type 1 diabetes during pregnancy, including frequent blood glucose monitoring between 02:00 and 04:00 am, avoiding pre-bedtime plasma glucose below 108 mg/dl, and prescribing glucagon for home use by a partner.
- Screen for intrauterine growth restriction (IUGR) in the current pregnancy as a relative obstetric contraindication to physical activity

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Pregnancy with type 1 diabetes must be evaluated for absolute and relative contraindications to physical activity before recommending any exercise regimen.
- High-risk patients for hypoglycemia should be identified early, particularly those with impaired hypoglycemia awareness or a history of severe hypoglycemia in the year preceding pregnancy.
- Reduce insulin dose by approximately 10% at 8-16 weeks gestation as a hypoglycemia prevention measure in high-risk patients
