# diabetes complications research
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** infection risk and complications in diabetes

**Core Management Principles:**
- Patients with diabetes are at higher risk of pulmonary infections (e.g., pneumococcus, influenza) leading to increased morbidity and mortality.
- Diabetes increases susceptibility to infections with organisms like Mycobacterium tuberculosis, Staphylococcus aureus, gram-negative bacilli, and fungi (e.g., Cryptococcus, Candida, Mucor).

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Routine screening for infections in diabetic patients, particularly those with poor glycemic control.
- Vaccination against pneumococcus and influenza recommended for diabetic patients.
