# T1DM retinopathy screening guidelines
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** T1DM retinopathy screening guidelines

**Core Management Principles:**
- Annual screening for diabetic retinopathy (DR) is recommended in individuals with T1DM aged >10 years with a disease duration of ≥5 years.
- Screening can be performed using direct ophthalmoscopy, indirect ophthalmoscopy, slit lamp fundus exam, or fundus cameras (mydriatic or non-mydriatic).
- Mydriatic retinal imaging with >2 fields of view provides the highest sensitivity for DR detection.
- Direct ophthalmoscopy sensitivity may be lower, especially when performed by non-eye care professionals.

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Disease must be an important public health problem with a well-understood natural history and long latent phase.
- Acceptable, cost-effective diagnostic tests and available treatment facilities are required.
- Case finding should be a continuous process, not a one-time project.
- Dilated ophthalmoscopy is the standard for assessing DR severity.
- Treatment for DR must be available and proven to improve patient outcomes.
- Facilities for both diagnosis and treatment must be accessible.
- Case finding must be a continuous process rather than a one-time project.
