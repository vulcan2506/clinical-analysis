# DKA resuscitation protocol
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** DKA resuscitation protocol

**Core Management Principles:**
- Follow ABCs (airway, breathing, circulation) as the first principle of resuscitation in DKA
- Administer isotonic saline bolus (10 ml/kg over 1 hour) before insulin therapy to stabilize cardiovascular status
- Initiate intravenous regular insulin infusion (0.05–0.1 U/kg/h) only after confirming serum potassium >3.3 mmol/L and at least 1 hour after starting fluids
- Start potassium supplementation concurrently with insulin therapy if serum potassium is 3.3–5.3 mmol/L, or delay insulin until potassium >3.3 mmol/L if hypokalemic
- Titrate insulin infusion to achieve blood glucose reduction of 50–75 mg/dl/hour and continue until resolution of DKA (BG <200 mg/dl, pH >7.3, bicarbonate >15 mmol/L)
- Administer a bolus dose of isotonic (0.9%) normal saline at 10 ml/kg intravenously over an hour or less before insulin
- Calculate total fluid requirement as the sum of maintenance requirement (1500 ml/m2/day) and fluid deficit (3%, 6%, or 9% of body weight based on severity)

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Confirm normal serum potassium before initiating intravenous insulin infusion
- Assess degree of dehydration and level of consciousness clinically
- Use isotonic saline for initial 5–6 hours, then switch to 0.45% saline if serum sodium is rising
- Add 5% dextrose to fluids when blood glucose falls below 200–250 mg/dl
- Monitor ECG for surrogate assessment of tissue potassium levels if serum potassium is unavailable
- Confirm normal urine output before initiating intravenous potassium supplementation
- Ensure serum potassium is >3.3 mmol/L before starting intravenous insulin therapy
