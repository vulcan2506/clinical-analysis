# pediatric diabetes care guidelines
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Pediatric diabetes care guidelines

**Core Management Principles:**
- Adopt individualized A1C targets for children and adolescents based on age, pubertal status, and hypoglycemia risk.
- Implement structured education programs for pediatric patients and families to enhance self-management skills.
- Monitor growth, development, and nutritional status regularly in pediatric diabetes care.
- Screen for psychological comorbidities (e.g., depression, anxiety) and provide integrated mental health support.
- Ensure continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) use in pediatric patients to improve glycemic control and reduce hypoglycemia.
- Incorporate screening for substance use (e.g., alcohol, recreational drugs) in adolescents with diabetes, particularly those with poor glycemic control or unexplained hypoglycemia.

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Diagnosis of type 1 diabetes confirmed via autoantibody testing and clinical criteria.
- Baseline evaluation including HbA1c, lipid profile, thyroid function, and celiac screening.
- Family engagement and support systems in place for effective diabetes management.
- Access to a multidisciplinary care team (endocrinologist, dietitian, psychologist, nurse educator).
- Regular follow-up visits every 3–6 months for children under 12 years and more frequently for adolescents.
- Ensure insulin storage and handling education for families, including use of thermochromic vial monitors for insulin stability verification.
