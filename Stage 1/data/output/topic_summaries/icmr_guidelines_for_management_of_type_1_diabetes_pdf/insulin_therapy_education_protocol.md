# insulin therapy education protocol
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** insulin therapy education protocol

**Core Management Principles:**
- Educate patients about the action profile of different insulin preparations
- Emphasize the importance of regular meal timings
- Avoid intramuscular administration of insulin
- Prevent syringe-vial mismatch
- Teach symptoms of hypoglycemia, its recognition and appropriate management
- Teach proper insulin storage conditions (e.g., refrigeration for unopened vials, room temperature for in-use vials) and handling to maintain potency
- Demonstrate and practice correct injection site rotation to prevent lipodystrophy and ensure consistent absorption

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Ensure patients understand the necessity of matching insulin type to meal carbohydrate content for prandial dosing
- Mandate verification of insulin type and dose before each administration to prevent errors
- Require documentation of insulin doses, timing, and glucose levels in a logbook or digital app for review during follow-ups
