# lifestyle management in T1DM
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** lifestyle management in T1DM

**Core Management Principles:**
- Maintain glycemia in the normal to near-normal range with minimal/no hypoglycemia
- Ensure adequate nutrition to facilitate healthy growth and development in children and adolescents
- Address individual nutrition needs, incorporating personal, social, and cultural preferences
- Improve overall health through appropriate food choices and individualized diet plans
- Encourage consumption of complex carbohydrates (≥70% of total carbohydrates) and limit sucrose to <10% of total calories
- Maintain optimal blood pressure, weight, and lipid levels
- Prevent the development or progression of diabetes-related microvascular and macrovascular complications

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Individualize diet plans considering socio-cultural environment, food habits, preferences, and work schedule
- Ensure minimum carbohydrate intake of 130 g/day in adults and 175 g/day in pregnancy
- Limit saturated fat to <10% of total calories and dietary cholesterol to <300 mg/day
- Encourage fiber intake (≥14 g per 1000 kcal in children ≥1 year or 'Age in years + 5 g')
- Promote balanced protein intake (15-20% of calories) with preference for low-fat and vegetarian sources
- In patients with raised LDL cholesterol, restrict saturated fat intake to <7% of total calories and dietary cholesterol to <200 mg/day
- In patients with diabetic nephropathy, daily protein intake should not be restricted to <0.8 g/kg body weight
