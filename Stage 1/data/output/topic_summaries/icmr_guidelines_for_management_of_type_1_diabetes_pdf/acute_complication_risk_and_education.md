# acute complication risk and education
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** acute complication risk and education

**Core Management Principles:**
- Sick day education should be imparted to all T1DM patients to mitigate DKA risk
- Education should include management strategies for acute complications like DKA, hypoglycemia, and infections
- Sick day education should emphasize the importance of monitoring blood glucose and ketones (urine or blood) during illness to detect early signs of DKA

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Refer to sick day guidelines in the chapter on 'Education' for structured management
- Healthcare providers must ensure sick day education is tailored to the patient's literacy level and cultural context for effective comprehension and adherence
