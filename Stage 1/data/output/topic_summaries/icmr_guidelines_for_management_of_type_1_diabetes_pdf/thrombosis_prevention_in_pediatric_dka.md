# thrombosis prevention in pediatric DKA
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** thrombosis prevention in pediatric DKA

**Core Management Principles:**
- DKA is considered a prothrombotic state, increasing venous thrombosis risk.
- Central venous access should preferably be avoided to prevent thrombosis.
- Heparin prophylaxis may be considered for children requiring central venous catheters.
- Heparin prophylaxis may be considered for children likely to remain immobile for more than 24–48 hours.

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Children requiring central venous catheters placement.
- Children likely to remain immobile for more than 24–48 hours.
