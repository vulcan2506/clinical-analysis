# ICMR type 2 diabetes guidelines
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** ICMR type 2 diabetes guidelines context and priorities

**Core Management Principles:**
- Address the disproportionate impact of SARS-CoV-2 on people with diabetes in India
- Focus on glycemic control as the gold standard for diabetes management
- Emphasize multidisciplinary team-based care for comprehensive diabetes management
- Prioritize culturally adaptive and context-specific guidelines for low- and middle-income countries
- Commit to periodic updates to reflect advances in scientific knowledge and clinical care
- Acknowledgment of the rising prevalence of type 1 diabetes in children and adolescents in India
- Recognition of the need for intensive management of all aspects of diabetes, especially glycemic control, as the gold standard in type 1 diabetes care

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Standardized laboratories and treatment guidelines for consistent diabetes care
- Accessibility to affordable drugs and services for diabetes management
- Availability of diabetes educators and specialists for comprehensive care
- Use of newer insulin analogues, pumps, and automated insulin delivery systems where feasible
- Multidisciplinary provider teams for holistic diabetes management
- Need for context-specific, culturally adaptive guidelines tailored for low- and middle-income countries like India
- Requirement for standardized laboratories and treatment guidelines to ensure consistent diabetes care across regions
