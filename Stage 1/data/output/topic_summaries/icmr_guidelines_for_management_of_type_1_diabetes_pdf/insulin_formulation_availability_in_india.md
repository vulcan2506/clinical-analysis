# insulin formulation availability in India
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** insulin formulation availability in India

**Core Management Principles:**
- Human regular and NPH insulin are available in strengths of 40 units/ml and 100 units/ml in vials.
- All insulin (regular, NPH, analogs) in pen cartridges have a strength of 100 units/ml.
