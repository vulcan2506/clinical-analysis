# exercise management in pediatric T1DM
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** exercise management and hypoglycemia treatment in pediatric T1DM

**Core Management Principles:**
- Exercise must always be encouraged for the child and family members to improve diabetes control and quality of life.
- Blood glucose (BG) must be checked frequently before, after, and during prolonged sports or unexpected activity.
- Insulin doses should be decreased in anticipation of planned physical activity.
- For prolonged sports, consume slowly absorbed snacks like nuts, seeds, peanut chikki, coconut barfi, or chocolate before starting.
- If hypoglycemia occurs during activity, administer simple sugars to correct it immediately.

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Confirm hypoglycemia with a glucometer whenever possible before initiating treatment.
- Ensure the patient is conscious and able to take oral glucose for standard treatment.
- For unconscious patients or those having fits, place in recovery position and do not force food/drink into the mouth.
- Administer Inj Glucagon (0.5 mg for children <12 years; 1 mg or 10-30 µg/kg for children >12 years) if unconscious.
- If glucagon is unavailable, shift to a nearby medical facility to administer IV glucose.
