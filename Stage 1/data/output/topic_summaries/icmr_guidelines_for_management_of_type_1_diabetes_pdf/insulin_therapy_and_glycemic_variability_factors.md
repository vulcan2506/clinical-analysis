# insulin therapy and glycemic variability factors
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** insulin therapy and glycemic variability factors

**Core Management Principles:**
- Incorrect insulin therapy due to syringe-vial mismatch, incorrect technique, incorrect timing, insulin stacking, or higher insulin dose
- Decreased exogenous glucose delivery into blood from missed meals, delayed absorption, or malabsorption
- Increased glucose utilization during or after exercise
- Impaired endogenous glucose production due to alcohol ingestion, liver disease, or renal disease
- Improved insulin sensitivity late after exercise, in the middle of the night, or following improved fitness
- Concomitant adrenal insufficiency leading to unpredictable glycemic variability due to altered counterregulatory hormone responses

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Accurate insulin administration technique and timing
- Regular self-monitoring of blood glucose (SMBG)
- Monitoring for conditions affecting glucose metabolism (e.g., liver/renal disease, hypothyroidism)
- Adjustment of insulin doses during exercise or illness
- Avoidance of alcohol ingestion to prevent impaired glucose production
- Monitoring for adrenal insufficiency in patients with unexplained glycemic variability or recurrent hypoglycemia
