# preconception hypertension management
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** preconception hypertension management in T1DM

**Core Management Principles:**
- Hypertension risk is 2–4 times higher in women with diabetes.
- BP <130/80 mm Hg should be achieved before conception.
- ACE inhibitors or angiotensin-receptor blockers should be discontinued prior to conception.

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Women with T1DM should be screened for hypertension preconception.
