# ultrasonography for surgical planning
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** ultrasonography for surgical planning in T1DM with hazy media

**Core Management Principles:**
- B-Scan Ultrasonography is recommended for retinal imaging as a preoperative tool in patients with hazy media
- Useful in cases of dense cataract or vitreous hemorrhage where direct visualization is impaired

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Presence of hazy media (e.g., dense cataract or vitreous hemorrhage) that obstructs retinal visualization
