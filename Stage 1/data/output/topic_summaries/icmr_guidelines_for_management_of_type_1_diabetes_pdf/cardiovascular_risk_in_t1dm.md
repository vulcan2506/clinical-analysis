# cardiovascular risk in T1DM
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** cardiovascular risk in T1DM

**Core Management Principles:**
- Patients with T1DM have increased risk of CVD (CAD, stroke, PAD) compared to non-diabetic population, with CAD being most common.
- CV events occur earlier in T1DM (third and fourth decades of life) and are more severe (extensive disease, distal vessel involvement, severe stenosis).
- Preconception optimization of blood glucose, blood pressure, weight control (for overweight/obese), and smoking cessation is critical.
- Screening for CAD is recommended in older patients with longer disease duration and multiple CV risk factors.
- Avoid hypoglycemia due to catecholamine-induced tachycardia increasing myocardial demand; aggressive labor pain management with early epidural analgesia is suggested.

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Glycemic control is mandatory in patients with CAD.
- Continuous cardiac monitoring during labor is recommended.
- Instrumental delivery may be preferred to avoid Valsalva maneuver in patients with CAD.
- Preconception risk factor optimization (blood glucose, blood pressure, weight, smoking cessation).
