# pediatric insulin dosing strategy
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** pediatric insulin dosing strategy

**Core Management Principles:**
- An optimal insulin dose achieves good glycemic control without frequent hypoglycemic episodes.
- Insulin dose may be high initially due to counterregulatory hormones and glucotoxicity.
- Dose typically decreases during the 'honeymoon phase' to < 0.5 IU/kg/day.
- Dose adjustments should support appropriate growth and development in children.

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Initial dose must account for high counterregulatory hormone levels and glucotoxicity.
- Dose titration should be individualized to avoid hypoglycemia.
- Monitoring for the 'honeymoon phase' to adjust dose downward as needed.
