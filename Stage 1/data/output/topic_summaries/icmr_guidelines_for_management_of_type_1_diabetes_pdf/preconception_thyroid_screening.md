# preconception thyroid screening
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** preconception thyroid screening in T1DM

**Core Management Principles:**
- Screen all pregnant women with T1DM for thyroid disorders in the preconception period using TSH, T4, and TPO antibodies.

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Preconception screening must include TSH, T4, and TPO antibody tests.
- Preconception thyroid screening must be performed in the preconception period, not during pregnancy.
