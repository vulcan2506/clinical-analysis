# hypoglycemia awareness restoration
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** hypoglycemia awareness restoration

**Core Management Principles:**
- Temporary relaxation of glycemic control to restore awareness
- Scrupulous avoidance of hypoglycemia for 2-3 weeks
- Breaking the vicious cycle of recurrent hypoglycemia due to blunted sympathoadrenal flow
- Use of continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) during the avoidance period to detect asymptomatic hypoglycemia

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Patient must be under supervision during temporary glycemic relaxation
- Duration of avoidance period must be 2-3 weeks
- Monitoring of sympathoadrenal flow response is implied
- Patient education on recognizing early autonomic symptoms of hypoglycemia before the avoidance period
- Regular follow-up with healthcare provider during the 2-3 week avoidance period
