# severe non-proliferative retinopathy criteria
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** severe non-proliferative retinopathy criteria

**Core Management Principles:**
- Presence of at least one microaneurysm
- Hemorrhages/microaneurysms ≥ standard photograph 2A
- Cotton-wool spots, venous beading, or IRMA definitely present
- Criteria not met for more severe retinopathy
- Total number of prepubertal years contributing to DR risk

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Duration of diabetes
- Poor glycemic control
- Suboptimal blood pressure control
- Microalbuminuria
- Increased waist circumference
