# intraoperative glucose management
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** intraoperative glucose management in T1DM

**Core Management Principles:**
- Use insulin infusion and intravenous dextrose (5%) running separately during surgery.
- Monitor glucose hourly and maintain levels between 72 and 126 mg/dl.
- Reduce blood glucose monitoring to every 30 minutes during cesarean section until delivery and maternal consciousness.
- Conduct anesthetic assessment in the third trimester for women with diabetes and comorbidities (e.g., obesity, autonomic neuropathy).

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Separate administration of insulin infusion and intravenous dextrose (5%).
- Hourly glucose monitoring during surgery.
- Third-trimester anesthetic assessment for high-risk patients.
