# DKA prevention in pediatric T1DM
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** DKA prevention and cerebral edema management in pediatric T1DM

**Core Management Principles:**
- Early contact with healthcare provider during illness to emphasize insulin importance and review blood glucose goals.
- Initiation of easily digestible liquid diet containing carbohydrates and salt when nauseated, with plenty of fluids.
- Education of family members on sick day management, including monitoring temperature, blood glucose, ketones, and insulin administration.
- Use of home glucose-ketone meters to enable early recognition of impending ketoacidosis and guide home insulin therapy.
- Counseling families that insulin is the only effective treatment and discouraging the use of non-injectable alternative medications.

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Access to medical care and effective communication with healthcare providers during intercurrent illness.
- Availability of medications to suppress fever and treat infections.
- Ability to perform home glucose and ketone testing.
- Financial stability to prevent insulin withholding due to economic reasons.
- Immediate initiation of treatment for cerebral edema pending neuroimaging results.
