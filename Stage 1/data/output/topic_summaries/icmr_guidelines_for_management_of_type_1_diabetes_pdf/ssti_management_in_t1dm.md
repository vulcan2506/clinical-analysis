# SSTI management in T1DM
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** SSTI management in T1DM

**Core Management Principles:**
- Prompt surgical debridement is required for SSTIs like furuncles and carbuncles.
- Empirical antibiotic therapy should cover Staphylococcus aureus for common SSTIs.
- Necrotising fasciitis (NF) is a surgical emergency requiring aggressive debridement.
- NF management includes parenteral antibiotics with clindamycin/metronidazole plus broad-spectrum penicillin.
- Hyperglycemia must be controlled alongside SSTI treatment in T1DM.
- NF involving scrotal and perineal region is known as Fournier gangrene

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Surgical debridement is mandatory for necrotising fasciitis due to poor antibiotic penetration.
- Empirical antibiotics for NF should include agents targeting polymicrobial infections.
- Prompt identification of SIRS features is critical for NF management.
- Control of hyperglycemia is essential during SSTI treatment in T1DM patients.
- Testicles are spared in Fournier gangrene due to separate blood supply
