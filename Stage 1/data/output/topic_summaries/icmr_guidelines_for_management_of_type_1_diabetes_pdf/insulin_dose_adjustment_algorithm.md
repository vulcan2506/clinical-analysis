# insulin dose adjustment algorithm
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** insulin dose adjustment algorithm for T1DM

**Core Management Principles:**
- Adjust insulin doses based on daily blood glucose patterns, food consumption, and exercise.
- Increase insulin (5-10% of TDD) at 4-6 hourly intervals during acute illness or sick days.
- Calculate insulin-to-carbohydrate ratio (ICR) using 450/TDD (regular insulin) or 500/TDD (rapid-acting analogs).
- Calculate insulin sensitivity factor (ISF) using 1500/TDD (regular insulin) or 1800/TDD (rapid-acting analogs).
- Adjust pre-meal short-acting insulin based on blood glucose values, carbohydrate content, and exercise.

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Daily pattern monitoring of blood glucose levels.
- Frequent blood glucose monitoring during exercise and sick days.
- Urine ketone testing during acute illness.
- Standardized carbohydrate counting for meal-related insulin adjustments.
- Small incremental adjustments for patients with high insulin sensitivity or low insulin doses.
- Increased fluids intake during acute illness
- Frequent monitoring of blood glucose during acute illness
