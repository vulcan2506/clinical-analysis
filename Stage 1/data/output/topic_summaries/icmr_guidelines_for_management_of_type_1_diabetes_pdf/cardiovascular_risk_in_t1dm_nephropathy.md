# cardiovascular risk in T1DM nephropathy
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** cardiovascular risk in T1DM nephropathy

**Core Management Principles:**
- Cardiovascular risk is elevated in T1DM patients with nephropathy, though non-albuminuric phenotypes are less common in T1DM compared to T2DM.
- Finnish study reported 2% prevalence of non-albuminuric phenotype in T1DM with increased cardiovascular mortality risk.
- Long-term follow-up (25 years) from DCCT/EDIC trials shows ESRD prevalence of 1–2% in T1DM patients.
- Non-albuminuric nephropathy in T1DM is associated with increased cardiovascular mortality risk, as demonstrated in the Finnish study, though its prevalence is low (2%) compared to albuminuric forms.

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Monitor for nephropathy progression in T1DM patients to assess cardiovascular risk.
- Further studies needed to quantify exact cardiovascular risk posed by non-albuminuric phenotypes in T1DM.
- Nephropathy screening in T1DM should include both albuminuria assessment and estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) monitoring to detect non-albuminuric phenotypes early.
