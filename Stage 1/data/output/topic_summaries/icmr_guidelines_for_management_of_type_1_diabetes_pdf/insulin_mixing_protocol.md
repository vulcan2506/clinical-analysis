# insulin mixing protocol
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** insulin mixing protocol and preparation

**Core Management Principles:**
- Draw regular insulin into the syringe first, followed by NPH insulin to prevent contamination
- Gently roll NPH vial between palms to mix; do not shake vigorously
- Inject air into NPH vial before drawing insulin, then inject air into regular insulin vial
- Remove air bubbles from the syringe after drawing regular insulin
- Slowly draw NPH insulin after re-inserting needle into NPH vial
- Remove air bubbles from the syringe after drawing NPH insulin (if present) before finalizing the dose

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Allow insulin vials to reach room temperature before use
- Check insulin vials for clumps or particles before administration
- Clean vial tops with an alcohol swab before puncture
- Use appropriate pre-mixed formulations if available (e.g., Huminsulin 30/70, Novomix 30/70)
- Avoid mixing insulin glargine with any other insulin due to acidic pH
- Ensure the total volume of mixed insulin does not exceed the syringe capacity to avoid dosing inaccuracies
