# acute complications of hypoglycemia
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** acute complications of hypoglycemia

**Core Management Principles:**
- Hypoglycemia can lead to severe systemic complications including hypokalemia, cardiac arrhythmias, and neurological emergencies.
- Monitor for pulmonary edema, aspiration pneumonia, and acute gastric dilatation in severe hypoglycemic episodes.
- Assess for acute pancreatitis, upper gastrointestinal bleeding, and rhabdomyolysis as potential complications.
- Neurological complications such as cortical venous thrombosis, basilar artery thrombosis, and subarachnoid hemorrhage require urgent evaluation.

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Immediate correction of hypoglycemia with glucose administration is mandatory.
- Electrolyte and cardiac monitoring are essential in managing hypokalemia and arrhythmias.
- Neurological imaging (e.g., CT/MRI) should be performed if thromboembolic complications are suspected.
