# multimodal diabetes education strategies
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** multimodal diabetes education strategies

**Core Management Principles:**
- Education should be delivered in diverse settings (clinic, schools, camps, social media, virtual platforms)
- Group-based education (physical or virtual) is more effective and cost-efficient than one-on-one sessions
- Use age- and culture-appropriate educational materials (books, apps, websites, games) for repeated self-review
- Messages from the diabetes care team and peer groups must be consistent to avoid confusion
- Digital tools should be monitored by the diabetes care team to ensure accuracy and remove myths
- Messages from the diabetes care team and peer groups must be culturally sensitive and contextually relevant to avoid confusion

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Education should be imparted by trained professionals (endocrinologist, diabetes educator, dietician, psychologist)
- Peer education (parents, older patients in self-help groups) should be integrated where feasible
- Group sessions must be supervised by the diabetes care team to validate information
- Digital tools should be personalized or generalized based on patient needs
- Education should be reinforced at key transition points (diagnosis, sick days, provider changes)
- Digital tools must be validated or endorsed by the diabetes care team before use by patients
