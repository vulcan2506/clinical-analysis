# pain reduction in self-monitoring of blood glucose
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** pain reduction in self-monitoring of blood glucose (SMBG)

**Core Management Principles:**
- Rotate fingers to avoid repeated use of the same site
- Use the side of fingers rather than the central fat pad for lancing
- Use a fresh lancet for each prick to ensure sharpness and reduce pain
- Use lancets with a depth gauge and adjust to the minimal setting for an adequate blood drop
- Use smaller gauge lancets to minimize tissue damage and pain

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Ensure alternate testing sites (palm, forearm, thigh) are used only when blood glucose is stable, not during rapid changes (e.g., after meals, insulin, or exercise)
- Avoid using alternate sites during suspected hypoglycemia due to delayed detection of glucose changes
- Use alternate testing sites (palm, forearm, thigh) only in fasting state or when blood glucose is stable, as accuracy is nearly similar to fingertip in these conditions
