# urinary tract infection risk
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** urinary tract infection risk in T1DM

**Core Management Principles:**
- Glucosuria due to uncontrolled hyperglycemia is associated with increased risk of urinary tract infections.

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Control of hyperglycemia to reduce glucosuria
- Screening for asymptomatic bacteriuria in high-risk T1DM patients (e.g., those with recurrent UTIs or poor glycemic control)
