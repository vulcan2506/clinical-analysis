# pregnancy counseling and risks
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** pregnancy counseling and risks in T1DM

**Core Management Principles:**
- Proactively counsel women of child-bearing age with T1DM on contraception, pregnancy planning, and risks of unplanned pregnancy.
- Achieve HbA1c <7% (preferably close to 6%) without significant hypoglycemia before conception.
- Implement basal-bolus insulin regimens or continuous subcutaneous insulin infusion (CSII) for physiological insulin dosing.
- Start folic acid supplementation (at least 400 µg/day) in the preconceptional period.
- Substitute teratogenic drugs (statins, ACEI, ARB) with safer alternatives (methyldopa, calcium channel blockers, labetalol) before pregnancy.

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Patient must have Type 1 Diabetes Mellitus (T1DM).
- Patient must be of child-bearing age.
- Desired glycemic control must be achieved prior to conception.
- Pre-existing complications (e.g., retinopathy) and lifestyle factors (obesity, smoking) must be addressed.
- Contraception must be practiced until glycemic targets are met.
- Patient must have a past or family history of thromboembolic diseases to consider non-hormonal contraception (barrier methods or IUD) as preferable.
