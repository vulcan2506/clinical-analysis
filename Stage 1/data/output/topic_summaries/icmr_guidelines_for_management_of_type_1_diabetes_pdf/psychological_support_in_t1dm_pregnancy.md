# psychological support in T1DM pregnancy
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** psychological support in T1DM pregnancy

**Core Management Principles:**
- Proactively support women with T1DM in informed decision-making during pregnancy transition
- Facilitate communication within the healthcare team for coordinated care
- Conduct psychological assessment as part of preconception to postpartum care
- Refer to psychiatrist/psychologist if psychological issues (e.g., depression, anxiety) are identified

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Psychological assessment must be integrated into preconception to postpartum care pathways
- Healthcare team coordination is mandatory for managing psychological support in T1DM pregnancy
