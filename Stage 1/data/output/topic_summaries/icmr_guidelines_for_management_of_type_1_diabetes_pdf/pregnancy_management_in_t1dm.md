# pregnancy management in T1DM
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** pregnancy management in T1DM

**Core Management Principles:**
- Achieve near normoglycemia before and during pregnancy with pre-pregnancy HbA1c < 7% (or < 6.5% if safely achievable) to reduce adverse outcomes.
- Monitor blood glucose 6-8 times daily with individualized targets: fasting 80-110 mg/dL, pre-meals 72-126 mg/dL, and 1-hr post-meals 100-155 mg/dL.
- Educate patients on insulin storage/technique, self-adjustment, sick day rules, ketone testing, and hypoglycemia management.
- Conduct monthly HbA1c monitoring (not as a replacement for self-monitoring) and assess fetal growth via scans at 28 weeks and 4-weekly intervals.
- Review teratogenic drugs (e.g., ACE inhibitors, statins), optimize thyroid status, and manage comorbidities (nephropathy, retinopathy, hypertension).
- Review and adjust insulin requirements throughout pregnancy, as insulin needs typically increase in the first trimester and may double or triple by the third trimester due to hormonal changes and insulin resistance.

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Planned pregnancy with 6 months of preconception glycemic optimization to achieve near-normal HbA1c.
- Preconception counseling to inform women about risks and the importance of glycemic control.
- Monthly HbA1c monitoring during preconception and pregnancy.
- Fetal surveillance via non-stress tests and amniotic fluid volume assessment based on institutional protocols.
- Psychological assessment and referral to mental health professionals if needed during preconception to postpartum care.
- Assess and manage thyroid status (TSH levels) as part of preconception care, given the higher prevalence of thyroid dysfunction in women with T1DM.
