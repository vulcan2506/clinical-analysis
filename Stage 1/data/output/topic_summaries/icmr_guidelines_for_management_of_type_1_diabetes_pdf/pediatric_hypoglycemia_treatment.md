# pediatric hypoglycemia treatment
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** pediatric hypoglycemia treatment

**Core Management Principles:**
- Treat both symptomatic and asymptomatic hypoglycemia detected by SMBG or CGMS in children.
- Administer 0.3 g/kg body weight oral glucose (e.g., ~5 g for a 15 kg child) for correction.
- Follow oral glucose with a mixed snack/meal after 15 minutes to sustain glucose levels.
- Recheck plasma glucose 15 minutes post-treatment to confirm normalization.
- Administer glucagon subcutaneously or intramuscularly (0.5 mg ≤20 kg, 1 mg >20 kg) for severe neuroglycopenic symptoms or inadequate oral intake.
- Repeating the oral glucose treatment if the recheck blood glucose remains below 70 mg/dl

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Glucose or sucrose is preferred over fructose for hypoglycemia correction.
- IV dextrose (0.25 g/kg, max 25 g) is required for severe cases with IV access and qualified personnel.
- Glucagon kit must be available at home, school/daycare, and during travel.
- Glucagon prescriptions must be renewed before expiration.
- Patients should wear a recognition band for emergency intervention.
- Presence of qualified medical personnel is required for IV dextrose administration
