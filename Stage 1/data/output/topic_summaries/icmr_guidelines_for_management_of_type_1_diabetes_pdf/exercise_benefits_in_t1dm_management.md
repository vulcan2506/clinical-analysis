# exercise benefits in T1DM management
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** exercise benefits and risks in T1DM management

**Core Management Principles:**
- Regular physical activity increases general well-being and helps prevent obesity and cardiovascular risk in T1DM patients.
- Exercise may modestly improve glycaemic control and reduce microvascular complications in T1DM.
- T1DM should not limit participation in sports or career pursuits in sports.
- Exercise facilitates glucose uptake, improves glucose clearance, and sustains insulin sensitivity.
- Exercise regulates counter-regulatory hormones and decreases hepatic glucose output.
- Prior exposure to aerobic exercise or hypoglycemia blunts glucose counter-regulatory responses (e.g., glucagon and catecholamines) in T1DM individuals.

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Carbohydrate intake (e.g., 20g glucose) before, during, and after physical activity to avoid hypoglycemia if glucose < 90 mg/dl.
- 10g glucose ingestion before aerobic exercise if starting glycemia is 90–124 mg/dl.
- Aerobic exercise can be started without glucose ingestion if starting glycemia is 126–180 mg/dl.
- Consistency in timing and dose of insulin, carbohydrate intake, duration, and intensity of exercise to minimize glucose variability.
- Women without medical or obstetric contraindications should be encouraged to do at least 30 min/day of physical activity.
- Adjustments in insulin or diet are easier once the individual adapts to a predictable and regular exercise schedule.
