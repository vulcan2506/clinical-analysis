# perioperative care phase definitions
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** perioperative care phase definitions in T1DM

**Core Management Principles:**
- The peri-operative period spans from admission to the pre-operative area until discharge from the post-anesthesia care unit (PACU).
- The peri-operative period is divided into pre-operative, intra-operative, and post-operative segments.
- The pre-operative segment includes the time from admission to the pre-operative area until transfer to the operating suite.
- The intra-operative segment ends with transfer to the PACU.
- The post-operative segment ends with discharge from the PACU.

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- A systematic approach to assessing the quality of care throughout the spectrum of elective surgical procedures.
- Metabolic optimization must begin at admission to the pre-operative area, not just on the day of surgery, to prevent hyperglycemia and ketosis risks associated with surgical stress.
