# insulin syringe selection and usage guidelines
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** insulin syringe selection and usage guidelines

**Core Management Principles:**
- Use U-40 syringe (red cap) for U-40 insulin vials and U-100 syringe (orange cap) for U-100 insulin vials
- Avoid reuse of disposable plastic insulin syringes; if necessary, change syringes daily
- Discard used needles and syringes by cutting and placing in a tamper-proof container
- Allow insulin to reach room temperature (~30 minutes) before injection
- Prime insulin pens by removing air bubbles and testing for correct dose delivery
- Clean visibly dirty injection sites with soap and water (not alcohol swabs) before injecting, as alcohol may strip silicone coating from needles

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Ensure correct syringe-insulin vial compatibility (U-40 with U-40, U-100 with U-100)
- Clean visibly dirty injection sites with soap and water before injecting
- Prime insulin pens by dialing 2 units and injecting into air to remove air bubbles
- Mix NPH insulin by inverting the pen 5 times before use
- Use fresh pen needles for each injection to prevent contamination
- Ensure no air bubbles are present in the needle before injection
- Attach a fresh pen needle to the pen before each use
