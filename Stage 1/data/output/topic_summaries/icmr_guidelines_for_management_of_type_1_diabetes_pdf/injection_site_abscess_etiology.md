# injection site abscess etiology
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** injection site abscess etiology in T1DM

**Core Management Principles:**
- Poor hygiene at injection site
- Repeated use of the same needle
- Repeated injections at the same site
- Use of contaminated insulin
- Failure to change catheter at recommended intervals in CSII users

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Incision and drainage of the abscess
- Antimicrobial therapy guided by pus culture results
