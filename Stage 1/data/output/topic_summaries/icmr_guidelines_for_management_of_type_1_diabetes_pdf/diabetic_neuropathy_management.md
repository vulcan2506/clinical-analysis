# diabetic neuropathy management
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Painful diabetic neuropathy management

**Core Management Principles:**
- Treat painful neuropathy with drugs chosen based on efficacy and tolerability for the individual patient
- Continue treatment for at least 2-4 weeks before assessing effectiveness
- Consider a 30-50% reduction in pain as a marker of effectiveness
- Use pregabalin or gabapentin as first-line agents due to strong evidence and FDA approval
- Titrate gabapentinoids and TCAs cautiously in elderly patients due to higher side effect risk
- Consider benfotiamine (a lipid-soluble thiamine derivative) as a potential adjunctive therapy for diabetic neuropathy, particularly in patients with poor glycemic control, based on randomized controlled trials showing improvement in neuropathic symptoms

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Diagnosis of painful diabetic neuropathy confirmed by clinical evaluation
- Assessment of patient-specific factors including age, comorbidities, and renal function
- Prioritize non-opioid agents for neuropathic pain management
- Monitor for adverse effects such as sedation, dizziness, and orthostatic hypotension
- Avoid TCAs in patients with glaucoma, symptomatic prostatic disease, or cardiovascular disease
- Assess and document glycemic control (HbA1c) as a prerequisite for initiating neuropathic pain management, as poor glycemic control may exacerbate neuropathy progression
