# non-nutritive sweetener use in diabetes
*Source: ICMR_Guidelines_for_Management_of_Type_1_Diabetes.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** non-nutritive sweetener use in diabetes

**Core Management Principles:**
- Non-nutritive sweeteners (e.g., saccharin, neotame, aspartame, acesulfame K, stevia, alitame, sucralose) are used to improve sweetness and palatability in low-sugar or 'diet' products.
- They may replace table sugar in home cooking.
- Intake not exceeding acceptable daily intakes (ADI) is considered safe.
- Potential to replace caloric or carbohydrate intake if substituted for caloric sweeteners.

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Patient education on ADI compliance for NNS use is recommended to ensure safe consumption.
