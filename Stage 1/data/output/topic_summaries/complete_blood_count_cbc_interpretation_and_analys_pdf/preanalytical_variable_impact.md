# preanalytical variable impact
*Source: Complete_Blood_Count_CBC_Interpretation_and_Analys.pdf*

**Analytical Parameter or Test Component:** preanalytical variable impact on CBC

**Clinical Findings or Diagnostic Patterns:**
- Delayed sample processing (>4h) increases MCV by 2.1 ± 0.8 fL (p<0.001)
- Inadequate sample volume prevents processing entirely
- Clotted samples cause 15% underestimation of platelet counts (p<0.001)
- Preanalytical errors account for 3.2% of total sample frequency
- Statistical significance thresholds (p<0.001) guide error detection

**Pre-analytical Conditions:**
- Process samples within 4 hours of collection to prevent MCV drift
- Ensure adequate sample volume for complete CBC analysis
- Reject clotted samples to avoid platelet count underestimation
- Monitor preanalytical error rates (target <5% frequency)
- Validate statistical significance (p<0.05) for result interpretation
