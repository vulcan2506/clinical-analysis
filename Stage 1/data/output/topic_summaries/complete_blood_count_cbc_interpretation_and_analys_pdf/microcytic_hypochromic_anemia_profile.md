# microcytic hypochromic anemia profile
*Source: Complete_Blood_Count_CBC_Interpretation_and_Analys.pdf*

**Analytical Parameter or Test Component:** microcytic hypochromic anemia profile

**Clinical Findings or Diagnostic Patterns:**
- Reduced RBC count (3.8 ×10¹²/L, Z-score -1.8, p<0.05)
- Significantly low hemoglobin (85 g/L, Z-score -3.2, p<0.001)
- Low hematocrit (0.26 L/L, Z-score -2.9, p<0.001)
- Microcytic (MCV <80 fL) and hypochromic (MCH <27 pg) red blood cells
- Common causes include iron deficiency, thalassemia, or chronic disease
- Elevated RDW (19.2%) indicating anisocytosis and heterogeneous RBC population
- Low MCHC (290 g/L) confirming hypochromia alongside low MCV (68 fL) and MCH (22 pg)

**Pre-analytical Conditions:**
- Fasting sample collection to avoid lipemia interference
- Venous blood in EDTA anticoagulant for accurate CBC
- Calibration of hematology analyzer using certified reference materials
- Daily quality control checks with low, normal, and high controls
- Review of peripheral blood smear for morphological confirmation
- Review of patient history for dietary iron intake, malabsorption conditions, or chronic blood loss
- Correlation with iron studies (ferritin, TIBC, serum iron) for differential diagnosis
