# platelet disorder assessment framework
*Source: Complete_Blood_Count_CBC_Interpretation_and_Analys.pdf*

**Analytical Parameter or Test Component:** platelet disorder assessment framework

**Clinical Findings or Diagnostic Patterns:**
- Thrombocytopenia evaluation requires assessment of platelet production, consumption, and destruction.
- Decreased platelet production may result from bone marrow disorders, chemotherapy, or nutritional deficiencies.
- Increased platelet consumption occurs in disseminated intravascular coagulation, thrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura, or hypersplenism.
- Thrombocytosis may be reactive to inflammation, malignancy, or iron deficiency.
- Primary hematologic disorders such as essential thrombocythemia require evaluation of platelet morphology and function.

**Pre-analytical Conditions:**
- Accurate patient history to identify potential causes of platelet disorders.
- Proper sample collection and handling to prevent platelet clumping or activation.
- Use of validated reference ranges for platelet counts in different age groups.
- Correlation with clinical findings and other laboratory parameters.
- Consideration of bone marrow examination for unexplained thrombocytopenia or thrombocytosis.
