# quality control data
*Source: Complete_Blood_Count_CBC_Interpretation_and_Analys.pdf*

**Analytical Parameter or Test Component:** quality control data

**Clinical Findings or Diagnostic Patterns:**
- Monitoring quality control performance over a 24-month period to ensure accuracy and reliability of CBC results
- Identifying trends or deviations in control data to trigger corrective actions
- Validating analyzer performance through statistical analysis of control samples
- Ensuring compliance with laboratory accreditation standards for quality control

**Pre-analytical Conditions:**
- Regular calibration and maintenance of hematology analyzers
- Use of certified reference materials for quality control samples
- Documentation of all quality control activities and results
- Adherence to predefined control limits and Westgard rules for result validation
