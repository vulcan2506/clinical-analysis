# premenopausal microcytic anemia diagnosis
*Source: Complete_Blood_Count_CBC_Interpretation_and_Analys.pdf*

**Analytical Parameter or Test Component:** premenopausal microcytic anemia diagnosis

**Clinical Findings or Diagnostic Patterns:**
- High probability of microcytic anemia (MCV <80 fL) with 94% confidence
- Iron deficiency likelihood at 89% based on CBC pattern
- Combined diagnostic score of 8.7/10 for premenopausal microcytic anemia
- Confirmatory testing shows low ferritin (8 μg/L) and elevated TIBC (420 μg/dL)
- Hypochromia (low MCH/MCHC) present as part of CBC pattern
- Elevated red cell distribution width (RDW) often observed in iron deficiency

**Pre-analytical Conditions:**
- Patient must be premenopausal for accurate interpretation of microcytic anemia
- CBC must include MCV, ferritin, and TIBC for diagnostic confirmation
- Symptoms such as fatigue and weakness should be present for at least 8 weeks
- Reference ranges for ferritin (15-150 μg/L) and TIBC (250-400 μg/dL) must be applied
- Patient must not have recent blood transfusion or iron supplementation within 4 weeks
- Sample must be collected in EDTA tube for CBC and serum separator tube for ferritin/TIBC
- Avoid hemolyzed or lipemic samples for accurate ferritin and TIBC measurements
