# anemia subtype prevalence statistics
*Source: Complete_Blood_Count_CBC_Interpretation_and_Analys.pdf*

**Analytical Parameter or Test Component:** anemia subtype prevalence statistics

**Clinical Findings or Diagnostic Patterns:**
- Iron deficiency is the most prevalent anemia subtype (14.2%) with significant gender disparity (female cases 1,404 vs. male 847, P<0.001)
- Chronic disease anemia shows no significant gender difference (P=0.234) with 6.1% prevalence
- Overall anemia prevalence is 23.4% across 15,847 cases with statistically significant gender distribution (P<0.001)
- Age-specific anemia prevalence varies (e.g., 18.4% in 0-1 years, 15.2% in 2-5 years)
- Anemia prevalence is highest in the >70 years age group (35.4%) and lowest in the 2-5 years group (15.2%)
- Chronic disease anemia shows a slight female predominance (511 vs. 456 males) but no statistically significant gender difference (P=0.234)

**Pre-analytical Conditions:**
- Statistical significance threshold (P<0.05) must be applied to interpret gender differences in anemia subtypes
- Age-stratified reference ranges must be used for accurate anemia prevalence assessment
- Sample size of 15,847 cases must be representative for reliable prevalence statistics
- Age-stratified reference ranges for hemoglobin (Hgb) must account for physiological variations across pediatric, adult, and geriatric populations
- Analytical performance must validate CBC instruments for accuracy in low-Hgb ranges to prevent misclassification of anemia
