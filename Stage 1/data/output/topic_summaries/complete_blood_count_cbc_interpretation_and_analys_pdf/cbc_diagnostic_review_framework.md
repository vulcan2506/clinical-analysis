# CBC diagnostic review framework
*Source: Complete_Blood_Count_CBC_Interpretation_and_Analys.pdf*

**Analytical Parameter or Test Component:** CBC diagnostic review framework

**Clinical Findings or Diagnostic Patterns:**
- Integrates automated analyzers with morphological findings and clinical presentation for accurate CBC interpretation
- Emphasizes quality control measures, pre-analytical variables, and post-analytical considerations to ensure reliable results
- Correlates CBC parameters with clinical conditions such as hematologic malignancies, infections, and systemic diseases
- Utilizes advanced detection principles (impedance, optical methods, flow cytometry) for precise cell analysis
- Follows evidence-based practices including reference range establishment and quality assurance procedures
- Incorporates systematic literature review and evidence-based practices from peer-reviewed journals, professional organization standards, and authoritative textbooks for CBC interpretation

**Pre-analytical Conditions:**
- Patient preparation and proper sample handling to minimize pre-analytical errors
- Adherence to standardized quality control procedures for accurate CBC testing
- Integration of automated results with morphological examination for validation
- Use of age, sex, and ethnicity-specific reference ranges where applicable
- Correlation of CBC findings with clinical presentation and additional laboratory tests
- Adherence to Clinical and Laboratory Standards Institute (CLSI) guidelines for hematology testing and quality assurance procedures
