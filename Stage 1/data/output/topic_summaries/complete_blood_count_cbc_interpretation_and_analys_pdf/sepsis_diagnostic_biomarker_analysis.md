# sepsis diagnostic biomarker analysis
*Source: Complete_Blood_Count_CBC_Interpretation_and_Analys.pdf*

**Analytical Parameter or Test Component:** sepsis diagnostic biomarker analysis in CBC

**Clinical Findings or Diagnostic Patterns:**
- Elevated WBC count (18.9 ± 8.4 ×10⁹/L) and neutrophil percentage (87.2 ± 6.8%) in sepsis cases compared to non-sepsis infections and controls
- AI-assisted CBC analysis improves accuracy, sensitivity, and specificity for sepsis detection (e.g., 96.4% accuracy, 94.8% sensitivity, 97.2% specificity for blast cell detection)
- Critical value alerts demonstrate high performance (98.9% accuracy, 97.8% sensitivity, 99.2% specificity) with 78% time reduction
- Automated + AI methods reduce labor hours by 58.6% (45.2 to 18.7 hours/1000 tests) and reagent costs by $0.35 per test
- Total annual savings of $24,647 with 89% ROI through reduced labor, reagent, error correction, and maintenance costs
- Positive predictive value (PPV) of 93.1% for blast cell detection
- Negative predictive value (NPV) of 98.1% for blast cell detection

**Pre-analytical Conditions:**
- Standardized sample collection and handling for accurate CBC and differential leukocyte counting
- Age, sex, and ethnicity-specific reference ranges for pediatric and adult populations
- Quality control measures for AI-assisted analysis to ensure consistent performance
- Validation of AI models against clinical sepsis criteria and laboratory standards
- Regular calibration and maintenance of hematology analyzers for reliable biomarker detection
- Patient preparation and sample handling standards for accurate biomarker detection
- Analytical standards for error correction cost reduction
