# hematology analyzer quality assurance
*Source: Complete_Blood_Count_CBC_Interpretation_and_Analys.pdf*

**Analytical Parameter or Test Component:** hematology analyzer quality assurance

**Clinical Findings or Diagnostic Patterns:**
- Requires regular calibration and quality control monitoring for accurate results
- Daily quality control includes analysis of control materials with known target values
- Uses statistical quality control methods like Levey-Jennings charts and Westgard rules
- Involves regular maintenance procedures (background counting, aperture cleaning, hydraulic system verification)
- Participation in external quality assessment programs for analytical accuracy verification
- Verification of instrument precision and systematic bias monitoring must be performed alongside daily quality control

**Pre-analytical Conditions:**
- Control materials must span the analytical measurement range (normal, abnormal low, abnormal high levels for each CBC parameter)
- Instrument precision verification and systematic bias monitoring
- Adherence to Clinical and Laboratory Standards Institute (CLSI) guidelines (2019)
- Proper sample handling and patient preparation for CBC testing
- Documentation of all quality control and maintenance activities
- Control materials must be traceable to reference methods or materials (e.g., WHO or NIST standards) for each CBC parameter
- Adherence to manufacturer-specific maintenance schedules and procedures for analyzer-specific components
