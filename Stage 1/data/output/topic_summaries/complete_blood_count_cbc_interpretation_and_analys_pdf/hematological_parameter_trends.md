# hematological parameter trends
*Source: Complete_Blood_Count_CBC_Interpretation_and_Analys.pdf*

**Analytical Parameter or Test Component:** hematological parameter trends monitoring

**Clinical Findings or Diagnostic Patterns:**
- Tracks monthly trends in WBC, hemoglobin, and platelet counts with sample size and infection rate correlation
- Uses mean ± standard deviation for parameter representation
- Identifies infection rate fluctuations alongside hematological changes
- Supports longitudinal analysis for clinical decision-making
- Enables detection of seasonal or procedural impacts on blood parameters
- Tracks inter-parameter relationships (e.g., WBC vs. infection rate, hemoglobin vs. platelet trends) to identify potential hematological syndromes or secondary effects

**Pre-analytical Conditions:**
- Consistent sample collection timing and patient preparation
- Standardized laboratory processing and measurement techniques
- Sufficient sample size (n) for statistical significance
- Accurate infection rate documentation for correlation analysis
- Regular data validation and trend review cycles
- Ensures consistent units of measurement (e.g., ×10⁹/L for WBC/platelets, g/L for hemoglobin) across all datasets and reporting periods
- Validates sample representativeness by stratifying data by age, sex, or clinical department to avoid demographic bias in trend analysis
