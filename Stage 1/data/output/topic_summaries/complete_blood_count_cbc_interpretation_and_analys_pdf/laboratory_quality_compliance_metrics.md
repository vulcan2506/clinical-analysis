# laboratory quality compliance metrics
*Source: Complete_Blood_Count_CBC_Interpretation_and_Analys.pdf*

**Analytical Parameter or Test Component:** laboratory quality compliance metrics

**Clinical Findings or Diagnostic Patterns:**
- Monitor and report compliance with ISO 15189:2012 standards (e.g., CV <5% for major parameters)
- Adhere to CAP Guidelines for turnaround time (TAT <60 min for routine tests)
- Meet CLIA Requirements for proficiency testing (minimum 80% pass rate)
- Track and document performance metrics against regulatory standards
- Ensure continuous compliance through regular audits and corrective actions

**Pre-analytical Conditions:**
- Maintain CV <5% for major CBC parameters (e.g., Hb, WBC, PLT)
- Achieve TAT <60 minutes for routine CBC testing
- Achieve ≥80% proficiency testing pass rate per CLIA
- Document all quality control and compliance activities
- Implement corrective actions for any non-compliance findings
