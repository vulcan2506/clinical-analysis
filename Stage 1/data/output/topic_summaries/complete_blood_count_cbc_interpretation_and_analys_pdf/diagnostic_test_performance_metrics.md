# diagnostic test performance metrics
*Source: Complete_Blood_Count_CBC_Interpretation_and_Analys.pdf*

**Analytical Parameter or Test Component:** diagnostic test performance metrics for CBC interpretation

**Clinical Findings or Diagnostic Patterns:**
- Evaluates diagnostic accuracy using sensitivity, specificity, PPV, NPV, and accuracy metrics for conditions like Iron Deficiency Anemia and infections
- Monitors proficiency testing performance across CBC parameters (e.g., WBC, RBC, Hemoglobin) with mean scores, ranges, and Z-scores
- Ensures high diagnostic reliability with sensitivity >89% and specificity >82% for key conditions
- Uses proficiency testing to validate inter-laboratory consistency and analytical precision
- Tracks PPV and NPV to assess clinical utility and false-positive/false-negative rates
- Tracks diagnostic metrics (e.g., sensitivity, specificity) for Bacterial Infection and Viral Infection alongside Iron Deficiency Anemia
- Uses case counts (n) to contextualize diagnostic performance metrics for each condition

**Pre-analytical Conditions:**
- Standardized patient preparation and sample collection protocols for accurate CBC results
- Regular calibration and maintenance of hematology analyzers to ensure measurement precision
- Participation in external proficiency testing programs (e.g., 24 surveys over 24 months)
- Adherence to reference intervals validated for age, sex, and ethnicity where applicable
- Implementation of quality control measures to minimize pre-analytical and analytical errors
- Validation of diagnostic metrics against minimum case thresholds (e.g., ≥600 cases for meaningful PPV/NPV assessment)
- Ensures proficiency testing participation includes ≥24 surveys over 24 months for robust inter-laboratory consistency assessment
