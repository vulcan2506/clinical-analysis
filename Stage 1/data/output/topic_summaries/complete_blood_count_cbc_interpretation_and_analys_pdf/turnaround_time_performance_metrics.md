# turnaround time performance metrics
*Source: Complete_Blood_Count_CBC_Interpretation_and_Analys.pdf*

**Analytical Parameter or Test Component:** turnaround time performance metrics

**Clinical Findings or Diagnostic Patterns:**
- Routine CBC TAT achieved 42 ± 12 minutes with 96.8% compliance against a <60 minute target
- STAT CBC TAT achieved 18 ± 8 minutes with 98.9% compliance against a <30 minute target
- Critical Value Notification achieved 8 ± 4 minutes with 99.2% compliance against a <15 minute target

**Pre-analytical Conditions:**
- Target compliance rates must exceed 95% for routine and STAT categories
- Achieved times must remain within the 95% confidence interval bounds (e.g., 96.2-97.4% for routine)
- Critical value notifications must be processed within 15 minutes of sample receipt
