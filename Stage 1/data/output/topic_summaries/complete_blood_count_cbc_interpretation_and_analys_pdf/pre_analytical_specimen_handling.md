# pre-analytical specimen handling
*Source: Complete_Blood_Count_CBC_Interpretation_and_Analys.pdf*

**Analytical Parameter or Test Component:** pre-analytical specimen handling

**Clinical Findings or Diagnostic Patterns:**
- Specimen collection requires proper anticoagulation with EDTA at a ratio of 1.8 mg per milliliter of blood.
- Inadequate anticoagulation may result in microclot formation, leading to falsely decreased platelet counts and unreliable results.
- Specimens require analysis within 24 hours of collection when stored at room temperature to prevent cell swelling.
- Prolonged storage may cause cell swelling, affecting MCV measurements and cell viability.
- Proper patient preparation and standardized collection procedures help minimize variables from exercise, stress, medications, and circadian rhythms.

**Pre-analytical Conditions:**
- Use EDTA anticoagulant at a specific ratio of 1.8 mg per milliliter of blood.
- Analyze specimens within 24 hours of collection if stored at room temperature.
- Control patient factors including recent exercise, stress, medications, and circadian rhythms.
- Ensure proper patient preparation prior to sample collection.
- Follow standardized collection procedures to ensure result reliability.
