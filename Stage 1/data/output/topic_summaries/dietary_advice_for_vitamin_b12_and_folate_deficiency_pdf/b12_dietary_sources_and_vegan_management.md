# B12 dietary sources and vegan management
*Source: Dietary advice for Vitamin B12 and folate deficiency.pdf*

**Nutrient Deficiency:** B12 dietary sources and vegan management

**Risk Factors and Clinical Manifestations:**
- Consume vitamin B12 rich foods including red meat, fish, shellfish, eggs, poultry, dairy, yeast extract, fortified cereals, tofu, and plant-based milks.
- Eat a varied and balanced diet to maintain adequate B12 levels.
- Vegans should aim to eat foods fortified with vitamin B12 at least 3 times a day.
- If fortified foods are inadequate, take a vitamin B12 supplement of 10 micrograms per day as recommended by the Vegan Society.
