# ESC/EAS dyslipidaemia management guideline
*Source: Lipid Disorders.pdf*

**Recommendation Topic:** 2025 ESC/EAS dyslipidaemia guideline focused update

**Clinical Recommendations:**
- Updates recommendations based on new evidence published up to March 31, 2025
- Assigns Class I/IIa or Class III recommendations only if new or changed by evidence
- Votes on recommendations with conflicted members abstaining from voting
- Additive to 2019 ESC/EAS Guidelines; changed recommendations substitute prior versions
- Considers major RCTs, meta-analyses, and new risk algorithms for guideline updates

**Eligibility Criteria:**
- New evidence must lead to Class I/IIa or Class III recommendation changes
- Evidence cutoff date: March 31, 2025
- Consensus required among Task Force members for inclusion of recommendations
- SCORE2 and SCORE2-OP risk algorithms for 10-year CV risk estimation
- Patient subgroups must meet defined risk categories for updated recommendations
- SCORE2-Diabetes risk algorithm must be used for 10-year CV risk estimation in patients with type 2 diabetes
- Lipoprotein(a) measurement required for risk stratification in primary prevention for patients with intermediate risk per SCORE2/SCORE2-OP
