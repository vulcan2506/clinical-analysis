# LDL-C treatment goals and risk reduction strategies
*Source: Lipid Disorders.pdf*

**Recommendation Topic:** LDL-C treatment goals and risk reduction strategies

**Clinical Recommendations:**
- Set LDL-C treatment goals based on total cardiovascular risk categories
- Use pharmacological therapies with proven cardiovascular benefits to reduce LDL-C levels
- Assess Lp(a) levels to refine lifetime risk of major cardiovascular events
- Initiate or intensify lipid-lowering therapy promptly in patients with very high cardiovascular risk to achieve LDL-C goals within 3–6 months
- Consider non-statin therapies (e.g., ezetimibe, PCSK9 inhibitors) in high-risk patients with persistently elevated LDL-C despite maximally tolerated statin therapy
- Monitor LDL-C levels at 4–12 week intervals after therapy initiation or dose adjustment to assess response and adherence

**Eligibility Criteria:**
- Total cardiovascular risk categorization for LDL-C goal setting
- Use of therapies with proven cardiovascular benefit for LDL-C reduction
- Lp(a) measurement for risk refinement in selected patients
- Document baseline LDL-C levels before initiating therapy
- Ensure adherence to lifestyle modifications (diet, exercise) as adjunct to pharmacological therapy
- Use validated risk calculators (e.g., SCORE2, ASCVD) for total cardiovascular risk stratification
