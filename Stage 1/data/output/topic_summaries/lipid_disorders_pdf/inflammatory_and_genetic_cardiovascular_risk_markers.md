# inflammatory and genetic cardiovascular risk markers
*Source: Lipid Disorders.pdf*

**Recommendation Topic:** inflammatory and genetic cardiovascular risk markers

**Clinical Recommendations:**
- Consider persistently elevated hs-CRP (>2 mg/L) as a risk modifier in cardiovascular risk assessment.
- Measure lipoprotein(a) [Lp(a)] at least once in adults to refine cardiovascular risk, particularly in those with a family history of premature ASCVD.
- Use coronary artery calcium (CAC) score as a risk modifier in individuals at moderate risk or near treatment decision thresholds, though not for broad screening.
- Interpret CAC scores cautiously in statin-treated patients due to potential plaque stabilization effects.
- Avoid using SCORE2/SCORE2-OP risk algorithms in patients with existing ASCVD or those on lipid-lowering therapy.
- Consider subclinical atherosclerosis or increased CAC score as risk modifiers in individuals at moderate risk or near treatment decision thresholds.

**Eligibility Criteria:**
- Persistently elevated hs-CRP (>2 mg/L) for consideration as a risk modifier.
- Elevated Lp(a) (>50 mg/dL or >105 nmol/L) for risk refinement.
- CAC score measurement in individuals at moderate risk or near treatment thresholds.
- Baseline (untreated) LDL-C levels and estimated CV risk for initiating pharmacological LDL-C-lowering therapy in primary prevention.
- Use of SCORE2-Diabetes algorithm for 10-year CVD risk estimation in patients with type 2 diabetes mellitus without ASCVD.
- Use SCORE2-Diabetes algorithm for 10-year CVD risk estimation in patients with type 2 diabetes mellitus without ASCVD.
