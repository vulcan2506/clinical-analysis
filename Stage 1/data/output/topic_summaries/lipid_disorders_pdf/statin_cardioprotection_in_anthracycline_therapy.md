# statin cardioprotection in anthracycline therapy
*Source: Lipid Disorders.pdf*

**Recommendation Topic:** statin cardioprotection in anthracycline therapy

**Clinical Recommendations:**
- Statins should be considered in adult patients at high or very high risk of developing chemotherapy-related cardiovascular toxicity to reduce the risk of anthracycline-induced cardiac dysfunction.
- The STOP-CA trial showed a significant reduction in ≥10% absolute decline in LVEF to <55% at 12 months with atorvastatin 40 mg daily vs placebo (9% vs 22%).
- Evidence is not unequivocal due to mixed results in smaller trials, but net positive results from the largest trial and meta-analyses support the recommendation.
- Statin use in this setting is overall safe without reported increase in hepatotoxicity or myopathy.

**Eligibility Criteria:**
- Adult patients at high or very high risk of chemotherapy-related cardiovascular toxicity.
- Baseline cardiovascular toxicity risk stratification as per 2022 ESC Guidelines on cardio-oncology.
- Anthracycline-based chemotherapy regimens (e.g., for breast cancer or lymphoma)
