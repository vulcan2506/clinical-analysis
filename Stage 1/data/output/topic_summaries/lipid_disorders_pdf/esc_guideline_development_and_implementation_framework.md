# ESC guideline development and implementation framework
*Source: Lipid Disorders.pdf*

**Recommendation Topic:** ESC guideline development and implementation framework

**Clinical Recommendations:**
- Guidelines assist health professionals in proposing the best diagnostic or therapeutic approach for individual patients, emphasizing shared care decisions with patients or caregivers.
- Guideline topics are selected for updating after annual expert review of new evidence by the ESC Clinical Practice Guidelines (CPG) Committee.
- Interim Focused Updates are created when new evidence could influence clinical practice before the next full guideline update.
- Recommendations are based on major randomized trials, systematic reviews, meta-analyses, and other evidence types (e.g., diagnostic accuracy studies, prognostic models).
- Recommendations are scored using predefined criteria for strength (Class I, IIa, IIb, III) and level of evidence (A, B, C).

**Eligibility Criteria:**
- Recommendations require at least 75% agreement among voting Task Force members to be approved.
- Declaration of interest forms must be provided and reviewed for all Task Force members to manage conflicts of interest.
- Funding for guideline development must be derived without healthcare industry involvement.
- Peer review must include multiple rounds by topic experts, ESC National Cardiac Societies, and relevant ESC Subspecialty Communities.
- Off-label medication use requires sufficient evidence, ethical consideration, patient consent, and compliance with country-specific regulations.
- Systematic literature searches are conducted in cases of controversy or uncertainty to ensure all key studies are considered for recommendations.
