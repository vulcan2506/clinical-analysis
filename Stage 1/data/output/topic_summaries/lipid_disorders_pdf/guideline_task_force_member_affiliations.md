# guideline task force member affiliations
*Source: Lipid Disorders.pdf*

**Recommendation Topic:** guideline task force member affiliations

**Clinical Recommendations:**
- Task force members are affiliated with academic institutions, hospitals, and research centers across multiple countries.
- Affiliations span cardiology, clinical biochemistry, public health, and cardiovascular prevention specialties.
- Members hold roles in both clinical practice and academic/research institutions.
- Affiliations include leading universities and specialized cardiovascular centers.
- Collaborations reflect a multidisciplinary and international approach to guideline development.
- Task force members hold dual or multiple affiliations across institutions and countries, enabling cross-institutional collaboration and knowledge exchange.
