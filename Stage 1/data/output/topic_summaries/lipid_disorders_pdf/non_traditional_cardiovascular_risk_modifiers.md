# non-traditional cardiovascular risk modifiers
*Source: Lipid Disorders.pdf*

**Recommendation Topic:** non-traditional cardiovascular risk modifiers

**Clinical Recommendations:**
- Consider non-traditional risk factors beyond traditional lipid parameters for cardiovascular risk assessment.
- Evaluate family history of premature CVD (men: <55 years; women: <60 years).
- Assess high-risk ethnicity (e.g., Southern Asian).
- Screen for psychosocial stressors and stress symptoms.
- Identify social deprivation as a risk modifier.

**Eligibility Criteria:**
- Risk assessment should incorporate these modifiers alongside traditional risk factors.
