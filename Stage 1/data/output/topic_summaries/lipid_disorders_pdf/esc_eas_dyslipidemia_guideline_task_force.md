# ESC/EAS dyslipidemia guideline task force
*Source: Lipid Disorders.pdf*

**Recommendation Topic:** ESC/EAS dyslipidemia guideline task force development and scope

**Clinical Recommendations:**
- Developed by the joint task force of the European Society of Cardiology (ESC) and European Atherosclerosis Society (EAS)
- Published as a focused update in the European Heart Journal (2025)
- Co-published with Atherosclerosis journal with stylistic differences
- Includes 24 task force members and 40 document reviewers from multiple European countries and specialties
- Provides disclaimers on guideline interpretation and professional responsibility
- Associations include ACNAP, ACVC, EACVI, EAPC, EAPCI, EHRA, and HFA
- Working Groups include Atherosclerosis and Vascular Biology, Cardiovascular Pharmacotherapy, and Thrombosis

**Eligibility Criteria:**
- Requires careful consideration of scientific and medical evidence available at the time of publication
- Mandates declaration of interests by all experts involved in guideline development
- Encourages health professionals to integrate guideline recommendations with individual patient assessment
- Requires verification of applicable rules and regulations for drugs and medical devices before clinical decisions
- Emphasizes checking for more recent versions of the document
- Requires verification of applicable rules and regulations for drugs and medical devices prior to making any clinical decision
- Requires checking whether a more recent version of this document exists before clinical decisions
