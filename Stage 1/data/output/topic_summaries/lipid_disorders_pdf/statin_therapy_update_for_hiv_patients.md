# statin therapy update for HIV patients
*Source: Lipid Disorders.pdf*

**Recommendation Topic:** statin therapy update for HIV patients

**Clinical Recommendations:**
- Recommend statin therapy for people with HIV (PWH) aged ≥40 years in primary prevention to reduce cardiovascular events (Class I, Level B).
- Consider pitavastatin 4 mg daily for primary prevention in PWH due to REPRIEVE trial efficacy (35% MACE reduction, HR 0.65).
- Choose statin based on potential drug interactions with antiretroviral therapy (ART).
- Monitor for muscle-related symptoms and new-onset diabetes with statin therapy in PWH.
- Use ezetimibe or PCSK9 inhibitors (evolocumab) for additional LDL-C reduction in PWH if statins are insufficient or contraindicated.
- Consider high-dose icosapent ethyl (2 × 2 g/day) in combination with statin therapy in high-risk or very high-risk PWH with elevated triglyceride levels (135–499 mg/dL) to reduce cardiovascular events (Class IIa, Level B).

**Eligibility Criteria:**
- Age ≥40 years with HIV in primary prevention.
- Receiving antiretroviral therapy (ART).
- Low-to-moderate ASCVD risk (per REPRIEVE trial criteria).
- Consideration of statin choice based on ART drug interactions.
- Monitoring for adverse effects (muscle symptoms, diabetes risk).
- PWH must be receiving antiretroviral therapy (ART) for statin therapy recommendation in primary prevention.
