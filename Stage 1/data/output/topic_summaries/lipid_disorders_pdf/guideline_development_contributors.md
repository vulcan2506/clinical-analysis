# guideline development contributors
*Source: Lipid Disorders.pdf*

**Recommendation Topic:** Guideline Development Contributors

**Clinical Recommendations:**
- Includes Document Reviewers and ESC National Cardiac Societies
- ESC Clinical Practice Guidelines (CPG) Committee oversees development
- EAS Executive Committee participates in guideline formulation
- Contributors represent diverse international cardiology societies
- Document Reviewers include ESC and EAS co-ordinators
- ESC Clinical Practice Guidelines (CPG) Committee includes a Chairperson and Co-Chairperson roles
- EAS Executive Committee includes a President role

**Eligibility Criteria:**
- Active involvement of ESC National Cardiac Societies in review process
- Document Reviewers must be engaged in the review process
- ESC National Cardiac Societies must be officially recognized national cardiology societies
