# hypothyroidism-induced hyponatraemia
*Source: Sodium and Cloride .pdf*

**Electrolyte Parameter:** hypothyroidism-induced hyponatraemia mechanisms

**Pathophysiology and Clinical Associations:**
- Non-osmotic release of AVP (arginine vasopressin) due to reduced cardiac output
- Reduced osmotically active substances in tubular diluting segments
- Excretion of negatively charged ketone bodies in poorly controlled diabetes mellitus
- Formation of edema and hyponatraemia in chronic kidney disease
- AVP secretion despite hypoosmolality in glucocorticoid deficiency

**Clinical Prerequisites:**
- Assessment of cardiac output and renal function
- Evaluation of concurrent conditions like diabetes mellitus or chronic kidney disease
- Measurement of urine sodium concentration
- Assessment of extracellular fluid volume (ECFV)
- Evaluation of glucocorticoid status
