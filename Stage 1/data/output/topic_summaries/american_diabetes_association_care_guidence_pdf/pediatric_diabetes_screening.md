# pediatric diabetes screening
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Pediatric diabetes screening and risk-based screening protocol

**Core Recommendations:**
- Screen children and adolescents with overweight (BMI ≥85th to <95th percentile) or obesity (BMI ≥95th percentile) and one or more additional risk factors for diabetes after the onset of puberty or after 10 years of age, whichever occurs earlier.
- Repeat screening at minimum 2-year intervals if normal, or more frequently if BMI is increasing.
- Use fasting plasma glucose, 2-h plasma glucose during a 75-g oral glucose tolerance test, elevated random glucose with symptoms of hyperglycemia, and/or A1C for testing prediabetes or type 2 diabetes in children and adolescents.
- Test pancreatic autoantibodies in children and adolescents with overweight or obesity and hyperglycemia to exclude autoimmune type 1 diabetes.
- Screen for tobacco/nicotine vaping, substance use, and alcohol use at diagnosis and regularly thereafter, with appropriate referrals to cessation programs as needed.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Children and adolescents must have overweight (BMI ≥85th to <95th percentile) or obesity (BMI ≥95th percentile) and at least one additional risk factor for diabetes.
- Screening should occur after the onset of puberty or after 10 years of age, whichever occurs earlier.
- A1C is not recommended for diagnosis in children with cystic fibrosis or symptoms suggestive of acute type 1 diabetes.
- Only A1C assays without interference should be used in children and adolescents with hemoglobinopathies.
- Comorbidities such as hypertension, dyslipidemia, albuminuria, neuropathy, and retinopathy should be assessed at diagnosis.
- A1C is not recommended for diagnosis in children and adolescents with cystic fibrosis or symptoms suggestive of acute type 1 diabetes.

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Using fasting glucose alone for diabetes diagnosis in children and adolescents, as it may overdiagnose diabetes.
- Relying solely on A1C for diagnosis in certain high-risk pediatric populations due to limited validation in pediatric outcomes.
- Assuming A1C is universally superior to other diagnostic methods in all pediatric ethnic groups without consideration of context.

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Incorporation of risk-based screening protocols tailored to pediatric populations with overweight or obesity and additional risk factors.
- Recommendation to test pancreatic autoantibodies in hyperglycemic children and adolescents to differentiate type 1 from type 2 diabetes.
- Emphasis on screening for tobacco/nicotine vaping, substance use, and alcohol use in pediatric diabetes care.
- Inclusion of comprehensive comorbidity assessment at diagnosis, including blood pressure, lipid panel, albumin-to-creatinine ratio, foot examination, and dilated eye examination.
- Discouragement of cannabis use in adolescents with diabetes due to risks of hyperglycemic ketosis-cannabis hyperemesis syndrome.
- Consideration of hyperglycemic ketosis-cannabis hyperemesis syndrome in adolescents with type 1 diabetes presenting with hyperglycemic emergencies (pH ≥7.4 and bicarbonate >15 mmol/L in the presence of ketosis).
- Routine discouragement of recreational cannabis use in all forms for adolescents with diabetes.
