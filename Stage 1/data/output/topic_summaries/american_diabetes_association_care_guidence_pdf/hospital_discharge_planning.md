# hospital discharge planning
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Hospital discharge planning for diabetes care

**Core Recommendations:**
- Identify healthcare professionals responsible for post-discharge diabetes care
- Assess patient understanding of diabetes diagnosis, glucose monitoring, and home glucose goals
- Provide education on healthy food choices and refer to outpatient dietitian or diabetes educator if needed
- Review medication administration, including insulin and noninsulin injectables
- Review sick-day management and proper use/disposal of diabetes supplies
- Document patient's baseline functional status and any barriers to self-care (e.g., vision impairment, dexterity issues) to tailor discharge education and support needs

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patient must have a confirmed diabetes diagnosis requiring post-discharge care
- Healthcare team must coordinate follow-up care before discharge
- Patient or caregiver must demonstrate ability to perform glucose monitoring and medication administration
- Prescriptions for medications and supplies must be provided at discharge
- Education must be tailored to patient's health literacy and cultural background
- Patient or caregiver must demonstrate proficiency in at least one method of glucose monitoring (fingerstick or CGM) before discharge
- Healthcare team must verify patient's insurance coverage for prescribed diabetes supplies and medications to prevent post-discharge access barriers

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Discharging patients without confirmed follow-up care arrangements
- Providing generic discharge instructions without patient-specific education
- Assuming patients understand medication administration without verification
- Not providing prescriptions for necessary diabetes supplies at discharge
- Ignoring patient's cultural or literacy needs in education
- Relying solely on verbal discharge instructions without written or digital reinforcement of key education points

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Mandatory coordination with outpatient diabetes care team before discharge
- Patient-specific sick-day management plans reviewed at discharge
- Provision of durable medical equipment and supplies at discharge
- Inclusion of CGM sensors or test strips in discharge prescriptions
- Standardized education on proper disposal of sharps and diabetes supplies
- Include a written or digital action plan for hypoglycemia management in discharge materials
- Provide a 30-day supply of diabetes medications and supplies at discharge to ensure immediate continuity of care
