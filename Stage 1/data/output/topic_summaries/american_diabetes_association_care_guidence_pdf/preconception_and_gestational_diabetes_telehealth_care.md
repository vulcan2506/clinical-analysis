# preconception and gestational diabetes telehealth care
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** telehealth care for preconception and gestational diabetes

**Core Recommendations:**
- team-based care is recommended either through a single specialized center or multiple centers with interprofessional team members
- telehealth interventions (e.g., telehealth visits, health apps) combined with in-person visits for gestational diabetes mellitus (GDM)
- telehealth care demonstrated reduced incidences of cesarean delivery, premature rupture of membranes, pregnancy-induced hypertension or preeclampsia, preterm birth, neonatal asphyxia, and polyhydramnios compared with standard in-person care alone
- telehealth interventions should include remote blood glucose monitoring with real-time data sharing for GDM management

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- preconception care should be part of the care plan during pregnancy
- telehealth interventions should be used in combination with in-person visits for GDM
- preconception care must include glycemic control optimization prior to conception for individuals with pre-existing diabetes

**Updated Recommendations:**
- telehealth interventions (telehealth visits, health apps) as part of GDM management
- remote blood glucose monitoring with real-time data sharing as a core component of GDM telehealth care
