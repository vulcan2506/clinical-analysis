# copyright and usage guidelines
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Copyright and usage guidelines for ADA clinical guidelines

**Core Recommendations:**
- Readers may use this work for educational, noncommercial purposes if properly cited and unaltered.
- The version of record may be linked at https://diabetesjournals.org/care.
- ADA permission is required to post this work on any third-party website or platform.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Proper citation must be provided when using the work.
- The work must remain unaltered when used for educational, noncommercial purposes.

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Explicit prohibition on text or data mining, machine learning, or similar technologies without prior written permission.
