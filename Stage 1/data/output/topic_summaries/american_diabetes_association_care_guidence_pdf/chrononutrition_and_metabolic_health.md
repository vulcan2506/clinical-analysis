# chrononutrition and metabolic health
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** chrononutrition and metabolic health

**Core Recommendations:**
- Glucose metabolism follows a circadian rhythm with diurnal variation of glucose tolerance peaking during daylight hours.
- Preliminary studies suggest cardiometabolic benefits when food is consumed earlier in the day.
- Chrononutrition examines how the timing of food ingestion affects metabolic health.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patient-specific assessment of circadian rhythm and meal timing patterns.
- Evaluation of shift work or irregular eating schedules as risk factors for metabolic dysfunction.

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Consideration of earlier meal timing as a potential strategy to improve metabolic health pending further evidence.
