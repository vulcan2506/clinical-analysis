# expert contributor list
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Expert contributor list for diabetes care guidelines

**Core Recommendations:**
- Contributors are multidisciplinary experts in diabetes care, endocrinology, cardiology, nephrology, and public health.
- Includes clinicians, researchers, and epidemiologists with advanced degrees (MD, PhD, MBBS, etc.).
- Represents diverse specialties such as endocrinology, cardiology, nephrology, and behavioral medicine.
- Includes international contributors from multiple countries and healthcare systems.
- Contributors are selected based on expertise, research contributions, and clinical experience in diabetes care.
- Includes specific patient advocacy representatives (e.g., Eva Vivian, CDCES, BC-ADM) alongside clinicians and researchers.
- Features contributors with specialized certifications in diabetes education and management (e.g., CDCES, BC-ADM).
