# hyperglycemia monitoring in cancer therapy
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** hyperglycemia monitoring in cancer therapy

**Core Recommendations:**
- Educate patients starting cancer therapy (ICIs, PI3Kα inhibitors, mTOR inhibitors) about hyperglycemia risks, symptoms, and crises.
- Test fasting or random plasma glucose before initiating ICIs, PI3Kα inhibitors, or mTOR inhibitors.
- Monitor fasting or random plasma glucose at each visit during ICI, PI3Kα inhibitor, or mTOR inhibitor therapy.
- For PI3Kα inhibitors, test random plasma glucose weekly for the first 2 weeks, then every 4 weeks; consider A1C every 3 months.
- Promptly refer patients with ICI-induced autoimmune diabetes to an endocrinologist and provide diabetes self-management education.
- Provide education and counseling on hyperglycemia symptoms and glucose testing tools for patients treated with chemotherapy or glucocorticoids, in addition to ICIs, PI3Kα inhibitors, and mTOR inhibitors.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patients starting ICIs, PI3Kα inhibitors, or mTOR inhibitors must be assessed for hyperglycemia risk.
- Symptoms or signs of hyperglycemia during or after treatment cessation warrant immediate glucose testing.
- ICI-induced autoimmune diabetes requires lifelong insulin therapy and urgent endocrinology referral.
- PI3Kα inhibitor-induced hyperglycemia peaks early (median 13 days), necessitating frequent initial monitoring.
- mTOR inhibitor-induced hyperglycemia lacks a clear temporal pattern, requiring ongoing monitoring.
- Patients treated with chemotherapy or glucocorticoids must be assessed for hyperglycemia risk and educated about symptoms and crises.

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Reliance solely on A1C for early detection of hyperglycemia in PI3Kα inhibitor therapy (due to early peak onset).
- Fixed screening intervals without consideration of symptom-driven testing for hyperglycemia.
- Assuming hyperglycemia risk is uniform across all cancer therapies without tailored monitoring strategies.

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Weekly random plasma glucose testing for the first 2 weeks of PI3Kα inhibitor therapy, then every 4 weeks.
- Consider A1C testing every 3 months during PI3Kα or mTOR inhibitor therapy.
- Mandatory patient education on hyperglycemia symptoms and crises for ICIs, PI3Kα inhibitors, and mTOR inhibitors.
- Immediate glucose testing for any symptoms or signs of hyperglycemia during or after treatment cessation.
- Recognition of ICI-induced autoimmune diabetes as a distinct entity requiring lifelong insulin therapy.
- Consider HLA-based risk prediction scores for targeted screening strategies in ICI-induced autoimmune diabetes.
