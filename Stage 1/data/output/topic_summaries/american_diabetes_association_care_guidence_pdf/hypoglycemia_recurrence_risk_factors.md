# hypoglycemia recurrence risk factors
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Hypoglycemia recurrence risk factors

**Core Recommendations:**
- An episode of severe hypoglycemia increases the risk for subsequent events due to impaired counterregulation.
- Hypoglycemia incidence peaks between midnight and 6:00 A.M. in individuals taking basal insulin.
- 75% of individuals do not have their basal insulin dose adjusted after hypoglycemia recognition.
- A fasting blood glucose of <100 mg/dL predicts next-day hypoglycemia.
- 84% of individuals with severe hypoglycemia (<40 mg/dL [<2.2 mmol/L]) had a preceding hypoglycemic episode (<70 mg/dL [<3.9 mmol/L]) during the same hospitalization

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Documented prior episode of hypoglycemia (<70 mg/dL [<3.9 mmol/L]) increases recurrence risk.
- Severe hypoglycemia defined as <40 mg/dL [<2.2 mmol/L].
- Hypoglycemia defined as <50 mg/dL [<2.8 mmol/L] in relevant studies.
- Hypoglycemia defined as <50 mg/dL [<2.8 mmol/L] in relevant studies for recurrence risk assessment

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Development of algorithms to predict inpatient hypoglycemia episodes.
- Validation of hypoglycemia prediction models for general use.
- Validation of hypoglycemia prediction models for general clinical use (e.g., post-discharge or outpatient settings)
