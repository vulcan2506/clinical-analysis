# discharge communication protocols
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Discharge communication protocols

**Core Recommendations:**
- Accurately and promptly communicate information on medication changes, pending tests, and follow-up needs to outpatient health care professionals.
- Transmit discharge summaries to the primary care health care professional as soon as possible after discharge.
- Schedule follow-up appointments prior to discharge with patient agreement on time and place to increase attendance likelihood.
- Include patient/caregiver education on new medications, self-monitoring instructions, and warning signs requiring immediate attention in the discharge communication.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patient has diabetes and is being discharged from an acute care setting.
- Outpatient health care professional is identified and available to receive the discharge summary.
- Patient agrees to the scheduled time and place for follow-up appointments.
- Patient or caregiver must demonstrate understanding of discharge instructions and follow-up plan before discharge.
- Outpatient healthcare professional must confirm receipt and review of the discharge summary within 48 hours of transmission.
