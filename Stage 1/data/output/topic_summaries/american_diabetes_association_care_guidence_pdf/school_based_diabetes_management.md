# school-based diabetes management
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** school-based diabetes management

**Core Recommendations:**
- Close communication with school personnel is essential for optimizing diabetes management, safety, and access to school-sponsored opportunities.
- Training and cooperation of school staff are required for effective diabetes care in school settings.
- Optimal use of diabetes technologies should be integrated into school-based care plans.
- Behavioral health considerations must be addressed in school-based diabetes management.
- Emergency situation protocols and clinical trial participation guidance should be established for school settings.
- Legal protections and accommodations for children with diabetes must extend to childcare settings (e.g., preschools, camps) for young children aged <5 years.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- School personnel must receive appropriate training and resources for diabetes management.
- A communication system between school staff, parents/guardians, and healthcare professionals must be in place.
- Written policies and procedures for diabetes management in schools must be developed.
- Access to necessary diabetes supplies and equipment must be ensured in school settings.
- Legal protections for children with diabetes in school settings must be upheld.
- Written policies and procedures for diabetes management in schools must address security needs, transfers, and access to medical personnel in detention facilities.

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Integration of advanced diabetes technologies into school-based care plans is now emphasized.
- Behavioral health considerations are explicitly included in school-based diabetes management guidelines.
- Emergency situation protocols for schools are newly detailed in the updated guidelines.
- Guidance for diabetes care during clinical trial participation in school settings is introduced.
- Legal protections and accommodations for children with diabetes in schools are reinforced.
- Guidance for diabetes care in detention facilities is newly introduced, including policies for empowering diabetes self-management and addressing unique security challenges.
