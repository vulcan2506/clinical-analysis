# erectile dysfunction treatments
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Erectile dysfunction treatments in diabetes

**Core Recommendations:**
- Treat hypogonadism if present as part of erectile dysfunction management
- Use phosphodiesterase type 5 inhibitors as first-line therapy
- Consider intracorporeal or intraurethral prostaglandins for non-responders
- Use vacuum devices as a non-invasive alternative
- Consider penile prostheses for severe or refractory cases

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Presence of erectile dysfunction symptoms
- Evaluation for hypogonadism if clinically indicated
- Assessment of contraindications to phosphodiesterase type 5 inhibitors
- Patient preference and willingness to try non-invasive options
- Specialist referral for complex or refractory cases
- Documentation of erectile dysfunction severity and duration as part of baseline assessment
