# weight-beneficial diabetes pharmacotherapy
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Weight-beneficial diabetes pharmacotherapy

**Core Recommendations:**
- Prioritize GLP-1 receptor agonists or dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonists for patients with diabetes and overweight/obesity due to superior weight loss efficacy.
- Continue obesity pharmacotherapy beyond reaching weight loss goals to maintain health benefits and prevent weight regain.
- Individualize dosing and titration approaches to balance effectiveness, health benefits, and tolerability rather than automatically using maximum approved doses.
- Minimize use of weight-promoting medications for other conditions among adults with diabetes and obesity.
- Modify or intensify treatment with lifestyle programs, metabolic surgery, or alternative agents if weight treatment goals are not reached.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patient must have diabetes and overweight or obesity.
- Patient should have stable insurance coverage for preferred agents.
- Treatment should be clinically appropriate and tailored to individual tolerability.
- Weight treatment goals must be defined and monitored.
- Other weight-promoting medications should be reviewed for potential discontinuation.
- Patient must have stable insurance coverage for preferred agents.

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Automatic use of maximum approved doses without individualization.
- Discontinuation of obesity pharmacotherapy once weight loss goals are reached.
- Prioritization of weight-neutral or weight-gain associated agents over weight-loss agents when weight management is a goal.

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Semaglutide and tirzepatide as preferred agents for weight loss in diabetes.
- Structured lifestyle management programs and metabolic surgery as additional intensification strategies.
- Recognition of sodium–glucose cotransporter 2 inhibitors, metformin, and amylin mimetics as agents with smaller but meaningful weight loss effects.
- Categorization of dipeptidyl peptidase 4 inhibitors, bromocriptine, α-glucosidase inhibitors, and colesevelam as weight neutral.
- Identification of sulfonylureas, meglitinides, thiazolidinediones, and insulin as agents often associated with weight gain.
- Agents associated with clinically meaningful weight loss include glucagon-like peptide 1 (GLP-1) receptor agonists and dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonists, which are the preferred agents in individuals with stable insurance coverage.
