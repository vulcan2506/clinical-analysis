# glucagon formulation options
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** glucagon formulation options

**Core Recommendations:**
- Glucagon is available in multiple formulations: injection powder with diluent for reconstitution, nasal powder, and prefilled pen/syringe.
- Dosage varies by formulation: 1 mg (injection), 3 mg (nasal), and 0.5 mg or 1 mg (prefilled pen/syringe).
- Median costs differ by formulation: injection ($303 AWP, $242 NADAC), nasal ($357 AWP, $285 NADAC), and prefilled pen/syringe ($391 AWP, $303 NADAC).
- Median AWP and NADAC prices include minimum and maximum ranges for some formulations (e.g., injection: AWP $180–$337, NADAC $207–$242)
- NADAC for prefilled pen/syringe is $303–$304, indicating slight price variability within this formulation class

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patients with severe hypoglycemia or recurrent hypoglycemic episodes may benefit from glucagon formulations.
- Formulation selection should consider patient-specific factors such as ease of administration and cost.
- Clinicians should consider formulation selection based on patient-specific factors such as age (e.g., pediatric dosing for 0.5 mg pen), caregiver capability, and likelihood of successful administration during emergencies

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Nasal glucagon powder (3 mg) is a newly introduced formulation option.
- Prefilled pen and prefilled syringe formulations (0.5 mg, 1 mg) are newly available options.
