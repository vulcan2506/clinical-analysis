# breastfeeding health benefits
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Breastfeeding health benefits in diabetes care

**Core Recommendations:**
- All mothers, including those with diabetes, should be supported in attempts to breastfeed due to immediate nutritional and immunological benefits for the baby.
- Breastfeeding is associated with reduced infant mortality from infectious diseases at <6 months of age (OR 0.22–0.59).
- Breastfeeding reduces respiratory infections in children aged <2 years.
- Breastfeeding reduces asthma or wheezing in children and adolescents aged 5–18 years (OR 0.91, 0.85–0.98).
- Breastfeeding is associated with reduced risks of breast cancer (OR 0.81), ovarian cancer (OR 0.70), and type 2 diabetes (OR 0.68) in mothers.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Mothers with diabetes should be supported in breastfeeding attempts.
- Monitoring for overnight hypoglycemia and potential insulin dose adjustments may be required during lactation.

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Breastfeeding reduces the risk of developing type 2 diabetes in mothers with previous gestational diabetes mellitus (GDM).
- Breastfeeding may improve metabolic risk factors in offspring, though further studies are needed.
