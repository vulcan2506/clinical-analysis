# prenatal and postpartum exercise guidelines
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** prenatal and postpartum exercise guidelines

**Core Recommendations:**
- At least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic activity per week during pregnancy and postpartum, preferably spread throughout the week
- Consult a healthcare professional before making significant changes to physical activity intensity
- Regular exercise reduces odds of gestational diabetes mellitus (GDM), gestational hypertension, and preeclampsia by 38%, 39%, and 41%, respectively
- Acute and long-term prenatal exercise improves glycemia, with larger effects in people with diabetes
- Avoid supine exercises after the first trimester due to potential compression of the inferior vena cava

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Generally healthy individuals
- Consultation with a healthcare professional for activity adjustments or significant changes
- Avoid exercise in individuals with absolute contraindications such as significant heart or lung disease, incompetent cervix, multiple gestation with risk of preterm labor, persistent second- or third-trimester bleeding, placenta previa after 26 weeks, or ruptured membranes

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Exercise interventions improve glucose outcomes and reduce need to start insulin or insulin dose requirements in GDM
- Insufficient evidence to recommend a specific type of exercise program (aerobic, resistance, or combined) for diabetes-related outcomes in pregnancy
- Consider supervised exercise programs for individuals with GDM or pre-existing diabetes to optimize glycemic control and safety
