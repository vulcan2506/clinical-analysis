# pediatric diabetes cardiovascular risk management
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Pediatric diabetes cardiovascular risk management

**Core Recommendations:**
- Lipid screening in children with type 1 diabetes soon after diagnosis (after glycemia improvement and age ≥2 years), then at 9–11 years and every 3 years if LDL ≤100 mg/dL
- Annual lipid screening in children with type 2 diabetes soon after diagnosis (after glycemia improvement)
- Blood pressure (BP) measurement at every clinic visit; ambulatory BP monitoring for persistent high BP (≥90th percentile for age/sex/height or ≥120/80 mmHg in adolescents ≥13 years)
- Nephropathy screening in type 1 diabetes at puberty or age ≥11 years (after 5 years of diabetes) and annually thereafter; in type 2 diabetes at diagnosis and annually
- Annual foot exams for neuropathy in type 1 diabetes starting at puberty or age ≥11 years (after 5 years of diabetes) and at diagnosis for type 2 diabetes
- Evaluate children and adolescents with type 2 diabetes for MASLD (by measuring AST and ALT) at diagnosis and annually thereafter
- Consider referral to gastroenterology for persistently elevated or worsening transaminases in children with type 2 diabetes

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Glycemia must be optimized before initial lipid screening
- BP measurements must account for age, sex, and height percentiles
- Nephropathy screening requires confirmation of elevated UACR (>30 mg/g creatinine) on two of three samples over 6 months
- Hypertension treatment requires exclusion of secondary causes before initiating ACE inhibitors or ARBs
- Statin therapy requires reproductive counseling for individuals of childbearing potential due to teratogenic risks
- Non-HDL cholesterol level is a significant predictor of atherosclerosis and can be accurately calculated in a nonfasting state
- Children and adolescents with a primary lipid disorder (e.g., familial hyperlipidemia) should be referred to a lipid specialist

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Routine use of total cholesterol as a primary screening marker for atherosclerosis risk
- Fixed numeric A1C targets for cardiovascular risk stratification in pediatric diabetes
- Non-fasting lipid panels without consideration of non-HDL cholesterol as a predictive marker

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Non-HDL cholesterol prioritized as a screening marker for atherosclerosis risk in pediatric diabetes
- Statin initiation for LDL >130 mg/dL after 6 months of medical nutrition therapy (MNT) with goal LDL <100 mg/dL
- Use of ACE inhibitors or ARBs for moderately increased albuminuria (UACR 30–299 mg/g) and strongly recommended for severely increased albuminuria (UACR ≥300 mg/g) or eGFR <60 mL/min/1.73m²
- Annual MASLD evaluation (AST/ALT) in children with type 2 diabetes
- Remote or validated nondilated retinal photography as acceptable screening for diabetic retinopathy
