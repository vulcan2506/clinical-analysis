# type 1 diabetes prediction monitoring
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Type 1 Diabetes Prediction Monitoring

**Core Recommendations:**
- Monitor individuals with islet autoantibody-positive pre-stage 3 type 1 diabetes using consensus guidance.
- Utilize OGTT metrics as they surpass continuous glucose monitoring data for predicting progression in multiple-autoantibody-positive individuals.
- Implement in-home blood spot sampling for islet autoantibody screening throughout the general population.
- Consider ethical, equity, and health system perspectives when planning type 1 diabetes screening programs.
- Evaluate dietary patterns and sugar intake as factors associated with progression from islet autoimmunity to type 1 diabetes.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patient must be islet autoantibody-positive to be considered for pre-stage 3 type 1 diabetes monitoring.
- Screening should target children and young adults, particularly those with family history or in high-risk populations.
- Clinical assessment must include evaluation of multiple islet autoantibodies to assess risk of progression.
- Health systems must have capacity for ethical and equitable implementation of population-wide screening.
- Monitoring protocols should account for patient-specific factors including age and family history.

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Relying solely on continuous glucose monitoring data for T1D prediction in multiple-autoantibody-positive individuals.
- Using lifestyle intervention or metformin for the prevention of type 1 diabetes.
- Screening the general adult population for type 1 diabetes without specific risk stratification.
- Using vitamin D supplementation for the prevention of type 1 diabetes progression.
- Relying on statin therapy for the prevention of type 1 diabetes.

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Consensus guidance for monitoring individuals with islet autoantibody-positive pre-stage 3 type 1 diabetes.
- In-home blood spot sampling for islet autoantibody screening in the general population.
- Use of OGTT metrics for improved prediction of T1D progression in autoantibody-positive individuals.
- Consideration of ethical and equity frameworks in T1D screening program design.
- Integration of dietary and sugar intake monitoring in T1D risk assessment protocols.
