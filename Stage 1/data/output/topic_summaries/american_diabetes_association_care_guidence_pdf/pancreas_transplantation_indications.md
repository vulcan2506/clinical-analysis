# pancreas transplantation indications
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Pancreas and islet transplantation indications

**Core Recommendations:**
- Pancreas transplantation should be reserved for people with type 1 diabetes undergoing simultaneous kidney transplantation, following kidney transplantation, or for those with recurrent ketoacidosis or severe hypoglycemia despite optimized glycemic management.
- Allogenic islet transplantation is indicated for adults with type 1 diabetes who are unable to reach their A1C goals due to repeated episodes of severe hypoglycemia despite intensive diabetes management and education.
- Pancreas transplantation may be considered for individuals with type 1 diabetes and hypoglycemia unawareness despite optimized glycemic management, even in the absence of recurrent ketoacidosis or severe hypoglycemia

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Type 1 diabetes diagnosis
- Undergoing simultaneous kidney transplantation or following kidney transplantation
- Recurrent ketoacidosis or severe hypoglycemia despite optimized glycemic management
- Inability to reach A1C goals due to repeated severe hypoglycemia despite intensive diabetes management and education (for islet transplantation)
- Documented hypoglycemia unawareness (for pancreas transplantation consideration)

**Updated Recommendations:**
- First allogeneic islet cell therapy (donislecel) approved in 2023 for specified type 1 diabetes cases
- Regulation of allogenic islet transplantation as cell therapy in the U.S.
- 2021 ADA/EASD consensus report on β-cell replacement therapy indications (Fig. 9.3) as a simplified reference for clinical decision-making
