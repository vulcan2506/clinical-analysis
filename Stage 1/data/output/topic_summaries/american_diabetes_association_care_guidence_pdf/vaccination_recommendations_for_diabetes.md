# vaccination recommendations for diabetes
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Vaccination recommendations for diabetes

**Core Recommendations:**
- COVID-19 vaccination with updated doses is recommended for everyone aged 6 months and older.
- Hepatitis B vaccine is recommended for adults with diabetes aged <60 years.
- Influenza vaccination is recommended annually for all individuals ≥6 months of age.
- Live attenuated influenza vaccine is contraindicated for people with diabetes; inactive or recombinant vaccines are recommended.
- RSV vaccine is recommended for adults aged ≥75 years and those aged ≥60 years at high risk.
- Pneumococcal vaccination (PCV15 or PCV20) is recommended for adults with diabetes, with PCV15 or PCV20 as the primary option and PPSV23 not indicated after PCV20

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patient must be aged 6 months or older for COVID-19 and Influenza vaccines.
- Hepatitis B vaccine is prioritized for adults with diabetes under 60 years of age.
- Patient must not have contraindications to receive influenza vaccination.
- Patient must not be pregnant to receive live attenuated influenza vaccine (which is contraindicated for diabetics anyway).
- Patient must be aged ≥60 years to be considered for RSV vaccination based on risk stratification.
- Patient must not have contraindications to pneumococcal vaccination (e.g., severe allergic reaction to vaccine components)

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Use of live attenuated influenza vaccine (nasal spray) for people with diabetes.
- Fixed age cutoffs for Hepatitis B vaccination without considering individual risk for adults ≥60 years.

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Updated COVID-19 vaccines for all ages ≥6 months.
- Annual trivalent influenza vaccines (2025–2026 season).
- RSV vaccination for high-risk adults aged ≥60 years.
- Individualized Hepatitis B vaccination discretion for adults ≥60 years based on infection likelihood.
- Explicit recommendation against live attenuated influenza vaccine for all people with chronic conditions like diabetes.
- Trivalent influenza vaccines are specified for the 2025–2026 season as the standard for all individuals ≥6 months of age
