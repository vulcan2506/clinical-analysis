# diabetes hospital readmission
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Diabetes hospital readmission prevention

**Core Recommendations:**
- Monitor insulin adjustments for individuals admitted with A1C >9% or DKA
- Follow a transitional care model to prevent readmissions
- Prescribe glucagon for individuals hospitalized with severe hypoglycemia, impaired awareness, or high risk for hypoglycemia
- Initiate CGM prior to discharge to prevent acute diabetes-related complications and readmission
- Implement collaborative person-centered medical homes for people with diabetes and chronic kidney disease
- Implement scheduled home health visits for high-risk individuals post-discharge
- Ensure timely ambulatory follow-up care within 7-14 days post-discharge for all hospitalized diabetes patients

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Individuals admitted with A1C >9% or DKA require insulin adjustment monitoring
- High-risk individuals for hypoglycemia (e.g., kidney failure, intensive insulin management) require glucagon prescription
- People with diabetes and chronic kidney disease require collaborative person-centered medical homes
- CGM initiation prior to discharge requires follow-up facilitation
- Transitional care model requires proactive care transitions from inpatient to outpatient settings
- High-risk individuals for readmission require identification based on male sex, longer prior hospitalization duration, multiple previous hospitalizations, number/severity of comorbidities, or lower socioeconomic/educational status
- Timely ambulatory follow-up care requires proactive scheduling prior to discharge

**Previous Recommendations:**
- No standardized protocol to prevent readmissions (previously lacked structured approach)

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Initiate CGM prior to discharge to prevent acute diabetes-related complications and readmission
- Collaborative person-centered medical homes for people with diabetes and chronic kidney disease
- Prescribe glucagon for high-risk hypoglycemia patients hospitalized with severe hypoglycemia or impaired awareness
- Initiate CGM prior to discharge to facilitate structured follow-up and prevent acute complications (newly emphasized mechanism beyond general prevention)
