# DPP-4 inhibitor use
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** DPP-4 inhibitor use in diabetes care

**Core Recommendations:**
- DPP-4 inhibitors have few side effects and minimal risk of hypoglycemia
- Relatively weak glucose-lowering agents compared to GLP-1 RAs and SGLT2 inhibitors
- May be useful in older adults with mild hyperglycemia or high hypoglycemia risk
- Alternative to metformin in older adults with low eGFR
- Linagliptin does not require renal dose adjustment
- Cost may be a barrier to some older adults

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Older adults with mild hyperglycemia
- High risk of hypoglycemia
- Intolerance to GLP-1 RAs or dual GIP/GLP-1 RAs
- Low eGFR (for alternative to metformin)
- Patient-specific factors favoring minimal side effects
