# SGLT2i therapeutic recommendation
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** SGLT2i + nonsteroidal MRA (finerenone) combination therapy

**Core Recommendations:**
- Simultaneous initiation of SGLT2 inhibitor and finerenone can be considered in adults with type 2 diabetes and UACR ≥100 mg/g with eGFR 30–90 mL/min/1.73 m² on a renin-angiotensin system inhibitor.
- Combination therapy is associated with beneficial effects on albuminuria and kidney/cardiovascular outcomes.
- Healthcare professionals should use clinical judgment to determine initial therapy (SGLT2i or GLP-1 RA) and whether combination therapy is warranted.
- Addition of a GLP-1 RA on top of SGLT2 inhibitor treatment is associated with a 30% lower risk of major adverse cardiovascular events and 57% lower risk of serious kidney events compared with GLP-1 RA therapy alone.
- Addition of an SGLT2 inhibitor on top of GLP-1 RA is associated with a 29% lower risk of major adverse cardiovascular events compared with SGLT2 inhibitor alone.
- Simultaneous initiation of finerenone and empagliflozin led to a reduction in UACR at 180 days of 52%, which was 32% greater than empagliflozin alone and 29% greater than finerenone alone.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Adults with type 2 diabetes and UACR ≥100 mg/g
- eGFR 30–90 mL/min/1.73 m²
- On a renin-angiotensin system inhibitor (maximally tolerated dose)
- Evidence of CKD (albuminuria UACR >100 and <5,000 mg/g)
- Participants must have CKD with eGFR >30 and <90 mL/min per 1.73 m².
- Participants must have albuminuria with UACR >100 and <5,000 mg/g.
- Participants must be taking either an ACE inhibitor or an ARB, often at maximally tolerated doses.

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Simultaneous initiation of SGLT2 inhibitor and finerenone for albuminuria reduction and kidney protection.
- Combination therapy with SGLT2i and GLP-1 RA may reduce major adverse cardiovascular events and serious kidney events.
- CONFIDENCE trial supports initial combination therapy (finerenone + empagliflozin) for UACR reduction in CKD with type 2 diabetes.
- CONFIDENCE trial (Combination Effect of Finerenone and Empagliflozin in Participants With Chronic Kidney Disease and Type 2 Diabetes Using a Urinary Albumin-to-Creatinine Ratio End Point).
