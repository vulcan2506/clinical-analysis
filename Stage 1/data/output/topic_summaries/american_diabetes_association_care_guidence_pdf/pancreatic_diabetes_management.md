# pancreatic diabetes management
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Pancreatic diabetes management

**Core Recommendations:**
- Screen for diabetes within 3–6 months following acute pancreatitis and annually thereafter; screen annually for chronic pancreatitis
- Avoid incretin-based therapies (GLP-1 RAs, dual GIP/GLP-1 RAs, DPP-4 inhibitors) in pancreatitis-related diabetes due to pancreatitis risk
- Initiate insulin therapy early in pancreatic diabetes to achieve glycemic goals
- Use OGTT for posttransplantation diabetes mellitus (PTDM) diagnosis due to immunosuppressant effects
- Implement interprofessional team-based management for posttransplantation diabetes
- Consider screening individuals with acute and chronic pancreatitis for exocrine pancreatic insufficiency by measuring fecal elastase
- Use insulin pump therapy, including automated insulin delivery (AID) systems, for cystic fibrosis–related diabetes when appropriate

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Documented episode of acute pancreatitis or diagnosis of chronic pancreatitis
- Evidence of pancreatic exocrine insufficiency (e.g., low fecal elastase) or pathological pancreatic imaging
- Absence of type 1 diabetes-associated autoimmunity
- Pretransplant OGTT for diabetes risk assessment in transplant candidates
- eGFR ≥45 mL/min/1.73m² for metformin initiation
- eGFR ≥30 mL/min/1.73m² for metformin continuation (discontinue if eGFR <30 mL/min/1.73m²)
- Stable kidney function for sulfonylurea use in early postoperative periods posttransplantation

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Fixed A1C targets without individualized consideration
- Use of incretin-based therapies in pancreatitis-related diabetes
- Reliance on A1C or fasting plasma glucose alone for PTDM diagnosis
- Passive lifestyle interventions without behavior therapy components
- Sulfonylureas as first-line therapy in early postoperative periods posttransplantation

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Annual diabetes screening post-acute pancreatitis and in chronic pancreatitis
- Early insulin initiation in pancreatic diabetes management
- OGTT as preferred diagnostic tool for PTDM
- Consideration of islet autotransplantation in selected pancreatectomy patients
- Use of GLP-1 RAs and SGLT2 inhibitors in PTDM with caution for UTI risk
- Perform early OGTT at 3 months posttransplantation and late OGTT at 1 year and onward for PTDM diagnosis
- Consider DPP-4 inhibitors in the immediate posttransplant period for mild hyperglycemia or impaired glucose tolerance due to potential to decrease progression to PTDM
