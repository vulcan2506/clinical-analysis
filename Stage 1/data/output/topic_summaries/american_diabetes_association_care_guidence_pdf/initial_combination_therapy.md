# initial combination therapy
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Initial combination therapy in type 2 diabetes

**Core Recommendations:**
- Consider initial combination therapy for patients with A1C levels 1.5–2.0% above individualized goal or those at high risk for cardiovascular disease (CVD) or with established CVD.
- Incorporate high-glycemic-efficacy therapies (e.g., GLP-1 RAs, dual GIP and GLP-1 RAs, SGLT2 inhibitors) to reduce hypoglycemia and weight gain risks.
- Prioritize GLP-1 RAs or dual GIP and GLP-1 RAs over basal insulin for intensive glucose management due to lower hypoglycemia risk and favorable weight/cardiovascular profiles.
- Use fixed-ratio combination products (e.g., IDegLira, iGlarLixi) for patients on GLP-1 RA and basal insulin.
- Avoid combining GLP-1 RAs (or dual GIP and GLP-1 RAs) with DPP-4 inhibitors due to lack of added glucose-lowering benefit.
- Consider switching from evening NPH to a basal analog if hypoglycemia occurs or NPH administration is frequently forgotten, with preference for morning dosing of long-acting basal insulin.
- Consider dosing NPH insulin in the morning for patients with steroid-induced hyperglycemia.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- A1C levels 1.5–2.0% above individualized goal or presence of high CVD risk/established CVD.
- Patient-centered treatment goals aligned with glycemic control, cardiovascular risk reduction, and tolerability.
- Prioritize GLP-1 RAs or dual GIP and GLP-1 RAs in patients requiring intensive glucose management unless contraindicated.
- Consider insulin as first injectable if hyperglycemia symptoms, A1C >10%, or blood glucose ≥300 mg/dL.
- Review treatment plans continuously for efficacy, side effects, and treatment burden.
- Consider individual preference, glycemic lowering, weight-lowering effect, and injection frequency when selecting GLP-1 RAs.
- If CVD is present, select a GLP-1 RA with proven cardiovascular benefit (oral or injectable formulations are appropriate).

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Stepwise addition of medications to metformin as the sole initial strategy for all patients.
- Use of DPP-4 inhibitors in combination with GLP-1 RAs or dual GIP and GLP-1 RAs.
- Routine initiation of basal insulin without first considering GLP-1 RAs or dual GIP and GLP-1 RAs.
- Fixed numeric A1C targets without individualized adjustments for patient-specific factors.

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Initial combination therapy for rapid glycemic goal attainment in high-risk patients (A1C 1.5–2.0% above goal or CVD risk).
- Preferential use of GLP-1 RAs or dual GIP and GLP-1 RAs for intensive glucose management due to lower hypoglycemia risk and weight benefits.
- Fixed-ratio combination products (e.g., IDegLira, iGlarLixi) for patients on GLP-1 RA and basal insulin.
- Avoidance of GLP-1 RA + DPP-4 inhibitor combinations due to lack of added benefit.
- Continuous review of treatment plans for deintensification based on efficacy, side effects, or changes in patient goals.
- Oral semaglutide is a commercially available GLP-1 RA formulation for patients preferring non-injectable therapy.
- Inhaled human insulin is an option for prandial insulin therapy.
