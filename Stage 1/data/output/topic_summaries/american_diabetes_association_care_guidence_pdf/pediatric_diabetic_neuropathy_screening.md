# pediatric diabetic neuropathy screening
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Pediatric diabetic neuropathy screening protocol

**Core Recommendations:**
- Annual comprehensive foot exam including inspection, pulse palpation, proprioception, vibration, and monofilament sensation testing
- Assessment of neuropathic pain symptoms during annual visits
- Foot inspection at every visit to educate youth on foot care and detect concurrent issues like tinea pedis or paronychia

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Type 1 diabetes diagnosis
- Adolescent age group (post-pubertal)
- Diabetes duration of at least 1–2 years
- Presence of CVD risk factors (for neuropathy prevalence association)

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Screening for diabetic neuropathy in prepubertal children
- Screening for diabetic neuropathy after only 1–2 years of diabetes

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Annual comprehensive foot exam including monofilament testing for adolescents with type 1 diabetes
- Integration of foot care education during routine visits
