# RSV vaccination recommendation
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** RSV vaccination for high-risk adults

**Core Recommendations:**
- Recommend a single dose of RSV vaccine for all adults aged ≥75 years
- Recommend a single dose of RSV vaccine for adults aged 60–74 years who are at increased risk for severe RSV
- Identify people with chronic conditions such as diabetes as having a higher risk of severe illness

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patient must be aged ≥60 years for FDA-approved vaccine eligibility
- Patient must be aged ≥75 years for universal recommendation
- Patient aged 60–74 years must have increased risk for severe RSV (e.g., chronic conditions like diabetes)
- Patient must have documented increased risk for severe RSV (e.g., chronic conditions like diabetes) to qualify for vaccination in the 60–74 age group

**Updated Recommendations:**
- FDA approval of first vaccines for prevention of RSV-associated lower respiratory tract disease in adults aged ≥60 years
- ACIP recommendation for single-dose RSV vaccination strategy
- ACIP recommendation for RSV vaccination in adults aged ≥75 years regardless of risk factors
