# sleep duration guidelines
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Sleep duration and quality management in diabetes

**Core Recommendations:**
- Aim for consistent, uninterrupted sleep, even on weekends.
- Avoid long (>8 h) and short (<6 h) sleep durations as they negatively impact A1C.
- Address irregular sleep patterns influenced by insomnia, obstructive sleep apnea, and restless leg syndrome.
- Consider chronotype differences, noting evening chronotypes may face higher risks of inactivity and poorer glycemic levels.
- Prioritize early bedtimes and wake times for evening chronotypes to mitigate glycemic risks.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patient has type 2 diabetes.
- Patient exhibits irregular sleep patterns or specific sleep disorders (insomnia, OSA, RLS).
- Patient is an evening chronotype (night owl) requiring specific behavioral adjustments.

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Consistent sleep schedule enforcement across weekends.
- Specific monitoring of sleep duration thresholds (>8h and <6h) for A1C impact.
- Chronotype-aware glycemic management strategies.
