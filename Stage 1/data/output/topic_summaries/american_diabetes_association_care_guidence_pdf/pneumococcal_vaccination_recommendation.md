# pneumococcal vaccination recommendation
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Pneumococcal vaccination in diabetes

**Core Recommendations:**
- All people with diabetes should receive one of the CDC-recommended pneumococcal vaccines.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- People with diabetes are at increased risk for pneumococcal infection.
- Vaccination should be administered regardless of diabetes type (Type 1 or Type 2)
