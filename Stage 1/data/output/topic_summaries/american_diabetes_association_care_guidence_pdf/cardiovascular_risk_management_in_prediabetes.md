# cardiovascular risk management in prediabetes
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Cardiovascular risk management in prediabetes

**Core Recommendations:**
- Screen for and treat modifiable cardiovascular risk factors in all patients with prediabetes.
- Do not discontinue statin therapy due to concerns about increased diabetes risk.
- Consider pioglitazone for patients with history of stroke and insulin resistance, balancing benefits against weight gain and fracture risks.
- Implement intensive preventive approaches for individuals at particularly high risk of progression to diabetes.
- Prioritize weight loss and maintenance as a core goal to reduce cardiovascular risk factors.
- Evaluate and treat hypertension and dyslipidemia in prediabetes based on individual cardiovascular risk levels.
- Refer patients for tobacco cessation and monitor for diabetes development in the years immediately following smoking cessation.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patient must have a diagnosis of prediabetes.
- Patient must have evidence of insulin resistance for pioglitazone consideration.
- Patient must have a history of stroke or transient ischemic attack for pioglitazone consideration.
- Patient must be at particularly high risk of progression (e.g., BMI ≥35 kg/m², A1C ≥6.0%, or history of gestational diabetes).
- Patient must be an adult (age >70 years diagnosis is less relevant for progression).
- Patient must have evidence of insulin resistance (e.g., HOMA-IR ≥3.0) for pioglitazone consideration in stroke/TIA history.
- Patient must have recent stroke or transient ischemic attack (<6 months) for pioglitazone consideration.

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Avoiding or discontinuing statin therapy solely due to the risk of developing type 2 diabetes.
- Using fixed numeric A1C targets without individualization based on patient-specific factors.
- Ignoring cardiovascular risk factors in the management of prediabetes.

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Individualized risk-to-benefit ratio assessment for screening and intervention.
- Use of pharmacotherapy for weight management and cardiovascular risk reduction to support person-centered goals.
- Consideration of lower doses of pioglitazone to mitigate adverse effects while maintaining efficacy.
- Recognition that regression to normoglycemia or remaining with IGT reduces long-term cardiovascular risk.
- Monitoring for diabetes development in the years immediately following smoking cessation.
- Use of pharmacotherapy for weight management and cardiovascular risk reduction to support person-centered care goals.
- Consideration of lower doses of pioglitazone (e.g., <45 mg daily) to mitigate adverse effects while maintaining efficacy.
