# protein intake guideline for CKD
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Protein intake guideline for CKD

**Core Recommendations:**
- For CKD stage G3 or higher, protein intake should be 0.8 g/kg body weight per day.
- For individuals on dialysis, protein intake of 1.0–1.2 g/kg/day should be considered.
- Higher protein intake (>1.3 g/kg/day or >20% of daily calories) should be avoided due to increased albuminuria, rapid kidney function loss, and CVD mortality.
- Low-protein eating patterns (0.6–0.8 g/kg/day) may be recommended for kidney protection but require guidance from a healthcare professional experienced in CKD nutrition.
- Medical nutrition therapy by a registered dietitian nutritionist is highly successful in achieving protein and sodium intake goals.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- CKD stage G3 or higher diagnosis for standard protein intake recommendation.
- Dialysis dependence for higher protein intake consideration.
- Individualization of protein intake based on comorbid conditions, medication use, and laboratory data.
- Guidance from a healthcare professional experienced in CKD nutrition for low-protein diets.
- Monitoring of serum potassium and blood pressure for sodium and potassium intake adjustments.
- Individualization of sodium and potassium intake based on comorbid conditions, medication use, blood pressure, and laboratory data.

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Protein intake above 1.3 g/kg/day or >20% of daily calories for non-dialysis CKD patients.
- Fixed high-protein diets without consideration of CKD stage or dialysis status.
- Unsupervised low-protein diets without professional guidance.

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Consideration of protein-energy wasting in dialysis patients for higher protein intake (1.0–1.2 g/kg/day).
- Recommendation for medical nutrition therapy by a registered dietitian nutritionist to achieve dietary goals.
- Emphasis on individualized potassium intake management for reduced eGFR patients.
- Avoidance of protein intake below 0.8 g/kg/day in CKD with diabetes due to lack of benefit.
- Integration of sodium restriction (<2,300 mg/day) to manage blood pressure and cardiovascular risk.
- Avoidance of protein intake below 0.8 g/kg/day in CKD with diabetes due to lack of benefit on blood glucose, cardiovascular risk, or GFR decline.
