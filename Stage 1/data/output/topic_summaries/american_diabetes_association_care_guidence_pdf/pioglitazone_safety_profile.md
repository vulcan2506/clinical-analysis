# pioglitazone safety profile
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** pioglitazone safety profile

**Core Recommendations:**
- Low risk for hypoglycemia
- Use cautiously in select individuals due to risks for heart failure, fluid retention, weight gain, osteoporosis, and/or macular edema
- Adverse effect risks reduced with lower doses and combination therapy
- May have beneficial effect of stroke reduction
- May increase risk of bladder cancer with long-term use (>1 year), particularly in males with a history of smoking or hematuria
- Associated with increased risk of fractures in postmenopausal women and elderly men
- May exacerbate or precipitate heart failure in patients with NYHA Class III or IV heart failure

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Use in select individuals only
- Consider lower doses to minimize adverse effects
- Use as part of combination therapy to reduce risks
- Baseline assessment of cardiac function (e.g., echocardiogram or BNP levels) in patients with known heart failure risk factors
- Renal function monitoring (e.g., serum creatinine, eGFR) due to potential fluid retention effects
- Baseline and periodic liver function tests (LFTs) to monitor for hepatotoxicity
- Avoid initiation in patients with active bladder cancer or history of bladder cancer
