# cardiovascular risk assessment
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Cardiovascular Risk Assessment in Special Populations

**Core Recommendations:**
- Candidates for advanced or invasive cardiac testing include those with symptoms or signs of cardiac or vascular disease and an abnormal resting ECG
- Exercise ECG testing (with or without echocardiography) may be used as the initial test
- Coronary artery calcium measurement is reasonable for cardiovascular risk assessment in adults with diabetes aged ≥40 years
- Pharmacologic stress echocardiography or nuclear imaging should be considered in individuals with diabetes and resting ECG abnormalities precluding exercise stress testing
- Pharmacologic stress echocardiography or nuclear imaging is required for individuals who cannot exercise and need stress testing

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Symptoms or signs of cardiac or vascular disease
- Abnormal resting ECG
- Adults with diabetes aged ≥40 years
- Resting ECG abnormalities precluding exercise stress testing (e.g., left bundle branch block or ST-T abnormalities)
- Inability to exercise

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Coronary artery calcium measurement for cardiovascular risk assessment in adults with diabetes aged ≥40 years
- Pharmacologic stress echocardiography or nuclear imaging for individuals with diabetes and ECG abnormalities precluding exercise stress testing
