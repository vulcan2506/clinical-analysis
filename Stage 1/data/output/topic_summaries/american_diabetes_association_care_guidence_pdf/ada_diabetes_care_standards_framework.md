# ADA diabetes care standards framework
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** ADA Diabetes Care Standards Framework

**Core Recommendations:**
- Provides standardized recommendations for diabetes care across hospital and ambulatory settings
- Includes glycemic goals, glucose monitoring, and hypoglycemia management protocols
- Emphasizes individualized care plans for special situations and transitions
- Promotes medical nutrition therapy and self-management education in hospital settings
- Focuses on preventing admissions and readmissions through structured care pathways
- Includes a dedicated section on 'Standards for Special Situations' to address diabetes management in patients with critical illness, pregnancy, or perioperative care
- Promotes the use of continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) in hospitalized patients when clinically appropriate

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Healthcare professionals must adhere to ADA Standards of Care for diabetes management
- Patient-specific factors (e.g., comorbidities, life expectancy) must inform glycemic targets
- Hospitalized patients require continuous glucose monitoring and hypoglycemia risk assessment
- Transition plans must be established for patients moving from hospital to ambulatory care
- Nutrition therapy and self-management education must be integrated into hospital care
- Glycemic targets in hospitalized adults must account for severity of illness, nutritional status, and risk of hypoglycemia
- Hospitalized patients with diabetes must receive a nutrition assessment by a registered dietitian or qualified healthcare professional

**Previous Recommendations:**
- One-size-fits-all glycemic targets in hospitalized patients
- Routine use of sliding-scale insulin without individualized adjustments
- Lack of structured transition protocols from hospital to outpatient care
- Ignoring hypoglycemia risk in diabetes management plans
- Exclusion of medical nutrition therapy in hospital diabetes care
- Routine use of sliding-scale insulin as the sole method for glycemic control in hospitalized patients
- Discontinuation of all oral diabetes medications in hospitalized patients without individualized assessment of their appropriateness

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Standardized glycemic goals tailored to hospitalized adults with diabetes
- Mandatory glucose monitoring protocols in hospital settings
- Integration of self-management education during hospitalization
- Structured transition plans to reduce readmissions
- Emphasis on preventing admissions through proactive diabetes care
- Mandatory use of CGM in ICU settings for patients with diabetes or hyperglycemia when available and clinically feasible
- Standardized protocols for perioperative glycemic management in surgical patients with diabetes
