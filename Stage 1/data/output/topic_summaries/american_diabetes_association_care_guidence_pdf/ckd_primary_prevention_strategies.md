# CKD primary prevention strategies
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** CKD primary prevention in diabetes

**Core Recommendations:**
- Blood glucose management (A1C goal of 7%)
- Blood pressure management (<130/80 mmHg)
- Smoking cessation
- Weight loss
- Physical activity
- Changes in eating patterns (decreased salt intake and/or protein intake)

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- People with diabetes without existing CKD
- No evidence of albuminuria or hypertension for primary prevention
- Metformin monotherapy as baseline therapy in GRADE study

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Routine use of RAS inhibitors (ACE inhibitors or ARBs) for CKD prevention in absence of hypertension or albuminuria
- Fixed numeric A1C targets without individualized consideration

**Updated Recommendations:**
- SGLT2 inhibitors as part of primary prevention strategies (though not included in GRADE study)
- GLP-1 receptor agonists and MRAs as emerging options for kidney protection
- Dietary modifications (decreased salt and/or protein intake) for CKD risk reduction
- Treatment with SGLT2 inhibitors, MRAs, or GLP-1 RAs for albuminuria reduction
