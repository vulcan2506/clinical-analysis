# niacin therapy recommendations
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Niacin therapy recommendations

**Core Recommendations:**
- Combination therapy with a statin and niacin is not recommended
- Monotherapy with niacin is also not recommended
- Large clinical trials failed to demonstrate benefit of adding niacin to individuals on appropriate statin therapy
- Possible increased risk of ischemic stroke observed in combination therapy
- Increased incidence of new-onset diabetes observed in combination therapy
- No benefit on major ASCVD outcomes observed in large clinical trials (AIM-HIGH, HPS2-THRIVE)

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patients must be on appropriate statin therapy before considering any lipid-modifying therapy

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Combination therapy with a statin and niacin
- Monotherapy with niacin
- Adding niacin to individuals on appropriate statin therapy
