# statin cognitive safety evidence
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** statin cognitive safety evidence

**Core Recommendations:**
- No adverse impact of statins on cognitive function observed in three large randomized trials comparing statin vs. placebo
- No change in cognitive function reported with addition of ezetimibe or PCSK9 monoclonal antibodies to statin therapy
- Systematic reviews of RCTs and cohort studies found no evidence of statins causing cognitive dysfunction or dementia
- Concerns about statins causing cognitive dysfunction are not supported by current evidence
- Statin use should not be deterred in individuals with diabetes at high risk for ASCVD due to cognitive safety concerns
- No cognitive function changes observed in studies adding ezetimibe or PCSK9 inhibitors to statin therapy, even at very low LDL cholesterol levels
