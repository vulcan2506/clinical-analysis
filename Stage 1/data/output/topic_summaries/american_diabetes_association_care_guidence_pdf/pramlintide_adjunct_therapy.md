# pramlintide adjunct therapy
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Pramlintide adjunct therapy in type 1 diabetes

**Core Recommendations:**
- Pramlintide is approved for use in adults with type 1 diabetes as an adjunct to insulin therapy.
- Modest reduction in A1C (0.3–0.4%) and modest weight loss (~1–2 kg) observed with pramlintide.
- Pramlintide is based on the naturally occurring β-cell peptide amylin.
- Pramlintide requires administration as a subcutaneous injection immediately prior to major meals (pre-meal dosing)
- Gastrointestinal side effects (e.g., nausea, vomiting) are common with pramlintide and may limit adherence

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Must be used in adults with type 1 diabetes.
- Requires concurrent insulin therapy as an adjunct treatment.
- Pramlintide should be initiated at a low dose (15 mcg) and titrated gradually to reduce gastrointestinal adverse effects
- Pramlintide is contraindicated in patients with confirmed gastroparesis or other significant gastrointestinal motility disorders

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Sotagliflozin is contraindicated in type 1 diabetes due to an eightfold increased risk of DKA compared with placebo
