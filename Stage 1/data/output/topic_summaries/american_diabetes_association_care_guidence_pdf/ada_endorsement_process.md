# ADA endorsement process
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** ADA endorsement process for clinical guidelines

**Core Recommendations:**
- Formal review by the Professional Practice Committee (PPC) for alignment with ADA’s mission, Standards of Care, and evidence-based principles
- Endorsement signifies scientific accuracy, relevance, and value to healthcare professionals caring for people with diabetes
- Endorsement does not extend to every specific statement or phrasing in the endorsed document
- ADA representatives may be involved in document development but endorsement does not reflect the official ADA position
- Type and level of endorsement vary based on document type and ADA involvement in development
- ADA representatives may be involved in document development, but their involvement does not guarantee endorsement of the final document

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Document must align with ADA’s mission and Standards of Care
- Document must adhere to evidence-based principles
- Document must demonstrate scientific accuracy and relevance to diabetes care
- Document must be a scientific document, such as a clinical guideline or consensus report, to be eligible for endorsement
