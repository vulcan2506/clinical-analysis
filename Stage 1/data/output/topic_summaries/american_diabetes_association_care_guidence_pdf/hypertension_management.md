# hypertension management
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Hypertension management in diabetes

**Core Recommendations:**
- Encourage systolic blood pressure goal <120 mmHg for individuals with high cardiovascular or kidney risk
- Titrate blood pressure-lowering pharmacologic therapy to achieve individualized blood pressure goals
- Use ACE inhibitors or ARBs as first-line therapy for hypertension in people with diabetes and albuminuria or coronary artery disease
- Initiate lifestyle behaviors including weight loss, DASH diet, sodium restriction, alcohol limitation, increased physical activity, and smoking cessation for blood pressure >120/80 mmHg
- Monitor for eGFR drop, serum potassium changes, and hypokalemia when using ACE inhibitors, ARBs, MRAs, or diuretics
- Initiate two antihypertensive medications for individuals with confirmed office-based blood pressure ≥150/90 mmHg
- Use objective measures such as pharmacy records, pill counting, or chemical analysis of blood or urine to detect inconsistencies in medication adherence rather than relying solely on subjective methods

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Confirmed office-based blood pressure ≥130/80 mmHg for pharmacologic therapy initiation
- Albuminuria (UACR ≥30 mg/g) or coronary artery disease for ACE inhibitor or ARB first-line therapy
- Severely increased albuminuria (UACR ≥300 mg/g) or eGFR <60 mL/min/1.73 m² for maximally tolerated ACE inhibitor or ARB dose
- Resistant hypertension diagnosis requires blood pressure ≥130/80 mmHg despite lifestyle management plus a diuretic and two other antihypertensive drugs at maximally tolerated doses
- Exclusion of missed doses, white coat hypertension, and secondary causes before diagnosing resistant hypertension
- Diagnosis of resistant hypertension requires blood pressure ≥130/80 mmHg despite lifestyle management plus a diuretic and two other antihypertensive drugs with complementary mechanisms of action at maximally tolerated doses
- Exclusion of missed doses, white coat hypertension, and primary or secondary hypertension before diagnosing resistant hypertension

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Combination of ACE inhibitors and ARBs or ACE inhibitor/ARB with direct renin inhibitors due to lack of ASCVD benefit and increased adverse events
- Addition of fibrate, niacin, or dietary supplements containing n-3 fatty acids to statin therapy for additional cardiovascular risk reduction
- Use of diuretics for routine blood pressure management in pregnancy
- Fixed numeric A1C targets for older adults
- Reliance on subjective methods alone to detect inconsistencies in antihypertensive medication adherence

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Recommendation of GLP-1 RA with demonstrated cardiovascular benefit for people with type 2 diabetes and CKD to reduce cardiovascular events
- Recommendation of GLP-1 RA with heart failure prevention benefit for people with type 2 diabetes and asymptomatic (stage B) heart failure with high cardiovascular risk
- Recommendation of dual GIP and GLP-1 RA or GLP-1 RA with demonstrated benefit for reduction in heart failure events and symptoms in adults with type 2 diabetes, obesity, and symptomatic heart failure with preserved ejection fraction
- Recommendation of nonsteroidal MRA with proven benefit for reducing worsening heart failure events in people with diabetes and symptomatic stage C heart failure with ejection fraction >40%
- Use of internet or mobile-based digital platforms to reinforce healthy behaviors as part of hypertension management
