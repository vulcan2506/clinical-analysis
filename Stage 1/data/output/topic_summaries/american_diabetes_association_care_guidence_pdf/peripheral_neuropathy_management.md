# peripheral neuropathy management
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Peripheral neuropathy management in exercise and foot care

**Core Recommendations:**
- Assess kinesthetic and proprioceptive sensation during physical activity to prevent skin breakdown and Charcot joint destruction
- Use proper footwear during moderate-intensity walking to reduce risk of foot ulcers or reulceration
- Engage in 150 minutes/week of moderate exercise to improve outcomes in prediabetic neuropathy
- Wear proper footwear and examine feet daily to detect lesions early
- Restrict to non-weight-bearing activities for individuals with foot injury or open sores
- Perform a thorough assessment of kinesthetic and proprioceptive sensation during physical activity, particularly in individuals with severe neuropathy

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Peripheral neuropathy diagnosis confirmed
- Assessment of neuropathy severity to determine risk of skin breakdown or joint damage
- Proper footwear must be used during exercise
- Daily foot examination for early lesion detection
- Non-weight-bearing restriction for active foot injuries or open sores
- Assessment of pain sensation and pain threshold in extremities to evaluate neuropathy severity

**Updated Recommendations:**
- 150 minutes/week of moderate exercise recommended for prediabetic neuropathy
- Emphasis on daily foot examination and proper footwear as standard care
