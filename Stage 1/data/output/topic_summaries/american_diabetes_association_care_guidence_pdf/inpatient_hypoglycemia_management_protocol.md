# inpatient hypoglycemia management protocol
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Inpatient hypoglycemia management protocol

**Core Recommendations:**
- Treat hypoglycemia with fast-acting carbohydrates at glucose ≤70 mg/dL (3.9 mmol/L)
- Recheck glucose 15 minutes after carbohydrate ingestion and repeat treatment if unresolved
- Call emergency services for unalert or unable-to-administer patients
- Use 15 g carbohydrates for most individuals; 5–10 g for automated insulin delivery users unless hypoglycemia is exercise-related or bolus overestimation occurred
- Administer pure glucose for initial treatment; avoid sucrose-containing carbohydrates for patients on acarbose
- Administering oral glucose only to alert individuals who can swallow safely

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Hypoglycemia alert value of ≤70 mg/dL (3.9 mmol/L) triggers treatment
- Individualized plans for preventing and treating hypoglycemia must be developed
- Hypoglycemia treatment embedded into all insulin and insulin infusion orders
- First aid kits in public settings must include oral glucose products
- Blood glucose monitoring before and 15 minutes after treatment
- Patient must be alert and able to swallow safely before administering oral glucose

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Using sucrose-containing carbohydrates for hypoglycemia in patients on acarbose
- Relying on dietary protein to treat hypoglycemia
- Omitting glucose from first aid kits in public settings
- Fixed carbohydrate dosing without consideration of automated insulin delivery systems
- Ignoring recurrent hypoglycemia risk due to ongoing insulin activity or secretagogues
- Using dietary protein to treat hypoglycemia

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Standardized nurse-initiated hypoglycemia treatment protocol for blood glucose <70 mg/dL (<3.9 mmol/L)
- Documentation and tracking of inpatient hypoglycemia episodes in EHR for quality improvement
- Inclusion of glucose tablets or gel in first aid kits for public settings
- Review of treatment plans after any blood glucose <70 mg/dL (<3.9 mmol/L) to prevent level 3 hypoglycemia
- Embedding hypoglycemia treatment into insulin and insulin infusion order sets
- Standardized hospital-wide, nurse-initiated hypoglycemia treatment protocol
