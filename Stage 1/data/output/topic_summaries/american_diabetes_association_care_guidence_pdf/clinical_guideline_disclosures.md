# clinical guideline disclosures
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Clinical guideline disclosures

**Core Recommendations:**
- Disclosure statements are required for all authors, reviewers, and contributors involved in diabetes care guidelines.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Disclosures must be submitted annually and updated if any conflicts arise during the guideline development process.
- Disclosures must include financial relationships with industry, intellectual property interests, and any other relevant conflicts of interest.

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Mandatory electronic disclosure submission system implemented for transparency.
- Disclosures must be publicly accessible on the guideline development organization's website within 30 days of submission.
