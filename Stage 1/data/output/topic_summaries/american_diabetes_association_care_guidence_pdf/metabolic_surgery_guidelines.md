# metabolic surgery guidelines
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Metabolic surgery for type 2 diabetes management

**Core Recommendations:**
- Consider metabolic surgery for weight and glycemic management in people with type 2 diabetes with BMI ≥30.0 kg/m² (or ≥27.5 kg/m² in Asian American individuals) who are good surgical candidates.
- Perform metabolic surgery in high-volume centers with interprofessional teams experienced in managing obesity, diabetes, and gastrointestinal surgery.
- Evaluate candidates for comorbid psychological conditions and social circumstances that may interfere with surgery outcomes.
- Monitor individuals post-surgery for insufficient weight loss or weight recurrence at least every 6–12 months and consider additional interventions if needed.
- Use continuous glucose monitoring to manage post-metabolic surgery hypoglycemia and adjust medications as required.
- Assess candidates for substance use disorders, severe depression, suicidal ideation, or other significant behavioral health conditions prior to metabolic surgery and postpone surgery until these are appropriately addressed.
- Provide medical nutrition therapy with a registered dietitian nutritionist experienced in post-metabolic surgery hypoglycemia for individuals with post-metabolic surgery hypoglycemia.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- BMI ≥30.0 kg/m² (or ≥27.5 kg/m² for Asian American individuals) with type 2 diabetes.
- Good surgical candidacy (e.g., absence of severe uncontrolled psychiatric conditions or substance use disorders).
- Evaluation for comorbid psychological conditions and social circumstances prior to surgery.
- Post-surgical monitoring for weight loss outcomes and glycemic control.
- High-volume surgical centers with experienced interprofessional teams.
- Assessment by a behavioral health professional with expertise in obesity management prior to consideration for metabolic surgery.
- Evaluation for substance use disorders, severe depression, suicidal ideation, or other significant behavioral health conditions as part of pre-surgical assessment.

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Routine use of nutrient-stimulated hormone-based therapeutics pre- and post-surgery for chronic obesity without evidence of benefit.
- Fixed numeric A1C targets post-surgery without individualized assessment.
- Reliance solely on medical therapy for type 2 diabetes in eligible surgical candidates.
- Performing metabolic surgery in low-volume centers without specialized teams.
- Ignoring psychological and social factors in surgical candidacy assessment.
- Pre- and post-metabolic surgery use of nutrient-stimulated hormone-based therapeutics for chronic obesity without evidence of benefit (except in individuals with BMI >50 kg/m² where GLP-1 RAs may be considered for significant weight loss prior to surgery).

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Recommendation for metabolic surgery as a weight and glycemic management approach in eligible individuals with type 2 diabetes.
- Post-metabolic surgery hypoglycemia management with continuous glucose monitoring and medical nutrition therapy.
- Consideration of GLP-1 receptor agonists as adjuvants for weight loss post-surgery or to treat weight recurrence.
- Routine assessment of psychological conditions and social circumstances pre-surgery to optimize outcomes.
- Long-term monitoring for vitamin/mineral deficiencies, osteoporosis, and other metabolic complications post-surgery.
- Consider GLP-1 receptor agonists as adjuvants for weight loss shortly after metabolic surgery or when weight loss has plateaued.
- Consider GLP-1 receptor agonists to treat weight recurrence after metabolic surgery as an alternative to revisional surgery.
