# diabetes-cancer comorbidity risk
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Diabetes-cancer comorbidity risk management

**Core Recommendations:**
- Encourage age- and sex-appropriate cancer screenings for people with diabetes, coordinated with primary care
- Promote reduction of modifiable cancer risk factors (obesity, physical inactivity, alcohol use, smoking)
- Monitor for atypical diabetes presentation (lean body habitus, negative family history) in middle-aged or older adults as potential pancreatic cancer precursor
- Avoid routine pancreatic cancer screening in absence of symptoms (e.g., weight loss, abdominal pain)

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Diabetes diagnosis confirmed
- Patient-specific risk assessment for cancer based on age, sex, and comorbidities
- Coordination with primary healthcare professional for screening plans

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Routine pancreatic cancer screening in asymptomatic diabetes patients
- Reliance on fixed cancer risk reduction strategies without addressing modifiable factors

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Consider postpancreatitis diabetes mellitus as a high-risk state for pancreatic cancer (2.4-fold increased risk)
- GLP-1 receptor agonists not associated with increased thyroid, pancreatic, or other cancer incidence in humans
- Metformin and sulfonylureas may have potential anticancer properties (evidence limited)
- Pioglitazone use requires caution due to mixed evidence regarding bladder cancer risk
