# CKD albuminuria management strategy
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** CKD albuminuria management strategy

**Core Recommendations:**
- Aim to reduce urinary albumin by ≥30% in people with CKD and albuminuria ≥300 mg/g to slow CKD progression
- Monitor albuminuria and eGFR annually to enable timely diagnosis, progression monitoring, detection of superimposed kidney diseases, and assessment of CKD complications
- Monitor serum potassium in individuals treated with diuretics, ACE inhibitors, ARBs, or MRAs to assess for hypokalemia or hyperkalemia
- Titrate ACE inhibitors or ARBs to maximum tolerated doses to maximize reduction in UACR
- Evaluate and minimize exposure to nephrotoxins (e.g., NSAIDs, iodinated contrast) in individuals with eGFR <60 mL/min/1.73 m²
- Verify medication dosing in individuals with eGFR <60 mL/min/1.73 m²
- Evaluate for potential CKD complications in individuals with eGFR <60 mL/min/1.73 m²

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- CKD with albuminuria ≥300 mg/g
- eGFR <60 mL/min/1.73 m² for intensified monitoring and medication adjustments
- Prior treatment with ACE inhibitors or ARBs for UACR reduction
- Patient-specific factors such as life expectancy, comorbidities, and medication tolerance for individualized therapy
- Annual quantitative assessment of UACR for diagnosis and treatment monitoring
- Presence of CKD complications such as hypertension, volume overload, or electrolyte abnormalities
- Likelihood of progressing to kidney failure for hepatitis B vaccination consideration

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Submaximal dosing of RAS blockade without titration to maximize UACR reduction
- Routine fixed numeric A1C targets without consideration of albuminuria or CKD stage
- Unmonitored use of nephrotoxic agents in patients with eGFR <60 mL/min/1.73 m²
- Delayed nephrology referral without annual albuminuria and eGFR monitoring
- Assumption that eGFR changes precede albuminuria in CKD progression detection

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Recommendation to reduce albuminuria by ≥30% as a therapeutic target in CKD with albuminuria ≥300 mg/g
- Annual monitoring of albuminuria and eGFR for early detection of CKD progression and complications
- Periodic serum potassium monitoring in patients on diuretics, ACE inhibitors, ARBs, or MRAs
- Titration of ACE inhibitors or ARBs to maximum tolerated doses to achieve UACR reduction
- Early vaccination against hepatitis B virus in individuals likely to progress to kidney failure
