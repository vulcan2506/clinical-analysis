# implanted weight loss devices
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Implanted weight loss devices in diabetes care

**Core Recommendations:**
- Gastric banding devices have fallen out of favor due to limited long-term efficacy and high complication rates.
- Minimally invasive devices such as implanted gastric balloons, vagus nerve stimulators, and gastric aspiration therapy are approved for short-term weight loss.
- High cost, limited insurance coverage, and limited efficacy data in diabetes treatment have created uncertainty for current use.
- Several medical devices have been voluntarily removed from the U.S. market due to safety and efficacy concerns.
- Minimally invasive devices are approved for short-term weight loss but lack long-term efficacy data in diabetes care.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- FDA approval for short-term weight loss indication.
- Assessment of patient-specific factors regarding cost and insurance coverage.
- Evaluation of limited efficacy data specifically for individuals with diabetes.
- Patient-specific evaluation of cost and insurance coverage is required before considering implanted weight loss devices.

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Gastric banding devices
- Devices with high complication rates and limited long-term efficacy

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Implanted gastric balloons
- Vagus nerve stimulator
- Gastric aspiration therapy
