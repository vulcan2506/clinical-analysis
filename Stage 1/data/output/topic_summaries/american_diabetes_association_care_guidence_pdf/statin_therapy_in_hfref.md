# Statin Therapy in HFrEF
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Statin + Ezetimibe Combination Therapy in HFrEF

**Core Recommendations:**
- Combination therapy of statins and ezetimibe is supported by evidence from the IMPROVE-IT trial.
- Addition of ezetimibe to moderate-intensity statin reduces major adverse cardiovascular events by 6.4% relative and 2% absolute.
- Benefit is directly proportional to the reduction in LDL cholesterol.
- Subanalysis shows significant reduction in major adverse cardiovascular events in participants with diabetes.
- Combination therapy is recommended for patients with HFrEF who have not achieved LDL-C targets on moderate-intensity statin monotherapy alone.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Baseline LDL-C levels should be assessed prior to initiating combination therapy to document the need for additional lipid-lowering beyond statin alone.

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Consideration of ezetimibe addition is emphasized in HFrEF patients with persistently elevated LDL-C (>70 mg/dL) despite moderate-intensity statin therapy.
