# CGM indication expansion in pregnancy
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** CGM indication expansion in pregnancy

**Core Recommendations:**
- CGM indication has expanded to include pregnancy for Dexcom G7, FreeStyle Libre 2, and FreeStyle Libre 3
- Enhances care in the pregnant population

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Pregnancy status must be confirmed for CGM use
- CGM use in pregnancy must be discussed in section 15, 'Management of Diabetes in Pregnancy'

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Dexcom G7 CGM indication expanded to pregnancy
- FreeStyle Libre 2 CGM indication expanded to pregnancy
- FreeStyle Libre 3 CGM indication expanded to pregnancy
