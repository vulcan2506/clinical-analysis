# diabetes risk prediction
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Diabetes risk prediction + Weight management interventions

**Core Recommendations:**
- Risk stratification for type 2 diabetes incorporates BMI thresholds, with lower cutoffs for Asian populations (e.g., ≥23 kg/m²) due to higher diabetes risk at lower BMIs.
- Lifestyle interventions (diet, exercise, behavioral therapy) are prioritized for diabetes risk reduction in individuals with prediabetes or obesity.
- Pharmacotherapy (e.g., GLP-1 receptor agonists, SGLT2 inhibitors, orlistat, phentermine/topiramate) is considered for high-risk individuals when lifestyle changes are insufficient.
- Bariatric/metabolic surgery is recommended for patients with severe obesity (BMI ≥35 kg/m²) and type 2 diabetes or prediabetes, with evidence of durable diabetes remission.
- Weight loss maintenance strategies include long-term pharmacotherapy (e.g., semaglutide, tirzepatide) and structured follow-up to prevent relapse.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- BMI ≥23 kg/m² for Asian populations or ≥25 kg/m² for other groups as screening thresholds for diabetes risk.
- Confirmed prediabetes (A1C 5.7–6.4%, fasting glucose 100–125 mg/dL, or 2-hour OGTT 140–199 mg/dL) or obesity (BMI ≥30 kg/m², or ≥27 kg/m² with comorbidities).
- Exclusion of contraindications for pharmacotherapy (e.g., history of pancreatitis for GLP-1 agonists, uncontrolled hypertension for phentermine).
- Multidisciplinary team involvement (e.g., dietitians, endocrinologists, surgeons) for comprehensive risk management.
- Baseline assessment of comorbidities (e.g., cardiovascular disease, sleep apnea) to tailor interventions.

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Fixed A1C targets (e.g., <7%) without individualized adjustments for life expectancy, hypoglycemia risk, or patient preferences.
- Routine use of orlistat as monotherapy for long-term weight management due to modest efficacy and gastrointestinal side effects.
- Unsupervised very-low-calorie diets (<800 kcal/day) without medical monitoring, given risks of micronutrient deficiencies and muscle loss.
- Glucocorticoid use for weight loss in diabetes, as it exacerbates hyperglycemia and insulin resistance.
- Relying solely on BMI for risk stratification without considering visceral adiposity or metabolic syndrome components.

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Tirzepatide (dual GIP/GLP-1 agonist) approved for chronic weight management in adults with obesity or overweight with comorbidities, including diabetes prevention.
- Semaglutide 2.4 mg (Wegovy) for long-term weight management in individuals with obesity or overweight, including those with prediabetes.
- Bariatric surgery outcomes data supporting durable diabetes remission and cardiovascular risk reduction in patients with BMI 30–34.9 kg/m² (if uncontrolled diabetes).
- Remote monitoring and digital health tools (e.g., app-based interventions) to enhance adherence to lifestyle modifications.
