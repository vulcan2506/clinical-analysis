# MASH management with SGLT2 inhibitors
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** MASH management with SGLT2 inhibitors

**Core Recommendations:**
- SGLT2 inhibitors are considered weight-neutral or modestly beneficial for weight loss in diabetes with obesity.
- SGLT2 inhibitors are not prioritized for MASH management due to limited histological evidence compared to GLP-1 RAs or pioglitazone.
- SGLT2 inhibitors may be used as add-on therapy in type 2 diabetes with obesity if more effective weight-loss agents are not tolerated or contraindicated.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Presence of type 2 diabetes or type 1 diabetes with obesity.
- Consideration of MASLD or MASH when selecting glucose-lowering therapies.
- Biopsy-proven MASH or high risk of clinically significant liver fibrosis identified via noninvasive tests for combination therapy with pioglitazone plus GLP-1 RA.

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Insulins, sulfonylureas, and thiazolidinediones for weight management in obesity due to promotion of weight gain.

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Semaglutide approved by the FDA for MASH with moderate to advanced liver fibrosis based on phase 3 histological outcomes.
- Tirzepatide and semaglutide prioritized for glucose lowering and weight loss in type 2 diabetes with obesity.
- Combination therapy with pioglitazone plus GLP-1 RA recommended for hyperglycemia in type 2 diabetes with biopsy-proven MASH or high fibrosis risk.
- Semaglutide is the only agent with FDA approval for MASH with moderate to advanced liver fibrosis based on phase 3 histological outcomes.
