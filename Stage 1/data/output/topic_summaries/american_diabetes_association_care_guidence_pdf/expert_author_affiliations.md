# expert author affiliations
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Expert author affiliations in diabetes care guidelines

**Core Recommendations:**
- Authors are affiliated with academic medical centers, universities, and research institutions globally.
- Authors hold advanced degrees (MD, PhD, MPH, DPhil, etc.) and subspecialty certifications.
- Authors represent diverse clinical and research expertise in diabetes, endocrinology, and related fields.
- Authors include both senior and junior investigators, reflecting a broad spectrum of experience.
- Authors are affiliated with institutions across multiple countries, indicating international collaboration.
- Authors include representation from professional societies and associations (e.g., FACP, FRCPC, FASN, FAHA, FMedSci, FRCPath, FRCP, FRACP, FACMG) as part of their affiliations or credentials, indicating involvement in guideline development bodies or specialty organizations.
