# sleep health screening recommendation
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Sleep health screening in diabetes

**Core Recommendations:**
- Screen for sleep health in people with prediabetes or diabetes
- Screen for sleep disorders and diabetes-related sleep disruptions
- Refer to sleep medicine specialists and/or qualified behavioral health professionals or diabetes care team as indicated
- Screen for sleep disorders commonly associated with diabetes, such as obstructive sleep apnea (OSA), insomnia, restless legs syndrome, and circadian rhythm disorders

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- People with prediabetes or diabetes
- Those at risk for diabetes
- Screening should be performed at initial diabetes diagnosis and periodically thereafter, particularly in patients with poor glycemic control or unexplained daytime symptoms
