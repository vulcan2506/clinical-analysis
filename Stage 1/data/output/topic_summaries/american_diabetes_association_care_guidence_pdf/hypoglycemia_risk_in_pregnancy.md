# hypoglycemia risk in pregnancy
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Hypoglycemia risk in pregnancy + DKA risk and retinopathy management

**Core Recommendations:**
- Educate pregnant individuals with diabetes and family members on hypoglycemia prevention, recognition, and treatment before, during, and after pregnancy.
- Advise pregnant individuals with type 1 diabetes to obtain ketone test strips and receive education on DKA prevention and detection due to increased ketogenic state and lower blood glucose thresholds for DKA.
- Implement dilated eye examinations before pregnancy, each trimester, and for 1 year postpartum in individuals with preexisting diabetes to monitor and manage retinopathy risk.
- Stop potentially harmful medications (e.g., ACE inhibitors, angiotensin receptor blockers, mineralocorticoid receptor antagonists) prior to conception or as soon as possible in the first trimester in individuals of childbearing potential.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Pregnant individuals with type 1 or type 2 diabetes must be assessed for hypoglycemia risk, particularly in the first trimester and postpartum.
- Individuals with preexisting diabetes require baseline dilated eye examination prior to pregnancy.
- Pregnant individuals with diabetes and chronic hypertension require blood pressure monitoring and management with thresholds <140/90 mmHg for therapy initiation.
- Individuals with diabetes and chronic hypertension require blood pressure monitoring throughout gestation to screen for hypertensive disorders of pregnancy.

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Fixed numeric A1C targets during pregnancy without consideration of hypoglycemia risk.
- Use of ACE inhibitors or angiotensin receptor blockers during pregnancy due to fetal risks.
- Lipid-lowering medications (e.g., statins) routinely continued during pregnancy without shared decision-making.
- Use of atenolol for hypertension management during pregnancy due to safety concerns.

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Prescribe low-dose aspirin (100–150 mg/day) starting at 12–16 weeks of gestation in pregnant individuals with type 1 or type 2 diabetes to reduce preeclampsia risk.
- Recommend intensive lifestyle interventions and/or metformin for individuals with a history of GDM, overweight/obesity, and prediabetes to prevent type 2 diabetes.
- Screen individuals with recent GDM at 4–12 weeks postpartum using a 75-g oral glucose tolerance test.
- Encourage breastfeeding for all individuals with diabetes, including those with a history of GDM, for maternal and infant health benefits.
- Stop lipid-lowering medications (e.g., statins, bempedoic acid, PCSK9 therapies, fibrates) prior to pregnancy or at the first pregnancy visit, except in high-risk cases (e.g., familial hypercholesterolemia, severe hypertriglyceridemia, prior atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease) where shared decision-making is required.
