# exercise and muscle preservation
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Exercise and muscle preservation in diabetes/obesity management

**Core Recommendations:**
- Emphasize muscle-strengthening activities alongside physical activity guidelines for people with diabetes and obesity, especially those undergoing obesity pharmacotherapy or metabolic surgery
- Prioritize combined aerobic and resistance training over aerobic exercise alone for greater improvements in weight loss, functional capacity, muscle mass, and strength
- Engage in exercise programs before and after metabolic surgery to support muscle mass, reduce weight recurrence, and improve insulin sensitivity
- Monitor for potential inhibitory effects on cardiac function when combining exercise with GLP-1 RA therapy, though evidence is limited and emerging
- Monitor for potential inhibitory effects on left ventricular diastolic function when combining structured exercise with GLP-1 RA therapy, particularly in people with type 2 diabetes, though evidence is limited and emerging

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patients undergoing obesity pharmacotherapy or metabolic surgery should be assessed for muscle mass and strength prior to initiating exercise programs
- Exercise programs should be tailored to individual functional capacity and comorbidities
- Combination therapy (e.g., GLP-1 RA + exercise) should be individualized based on patient-specific risks and benefits
- Assess cardiac function (e.g., left ventricular diastolic function) in patients with type 2 diabetes prior to initiating combined exercise and GLP-1 RA therapy, especially those with pre-existing cardiovascular conditions

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Reliance solely on aerobic exercise without resistance training for muscle preservation in diabetes/obesity management
- Assuming that weight loss alone (without exercise) preserves muscle mass and strength

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Combined aerobic and resistance training as a preferred strategy for muscle preservation and functional improvement in diabetes/obesity
- Pre- and post-operative exercise programs to mitigate muscle loss in metabolic surgery patients
- Synergistic benefits of structured exercise with GLP-1 RA therapy on body composition and metabolic outcomes
- Need for longer-duration studies on exercise combined with newer-generation obesity pharmacotherapies
- Need for longer-duration studies specifically evaluating the interaction between newer-generation GLP-1 RAs and structured exercise on cardiac function and body composition in diabetes/obesity management
