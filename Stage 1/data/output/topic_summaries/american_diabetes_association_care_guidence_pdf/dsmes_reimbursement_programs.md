# DSMES reimbursement programs
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** DSMES reimbursement and telehealth eligibility

**Core Recommendations:**
- Medicare reimburses DSMES when services align with National Standards and are recognized by ADA or ADCES.
- Medicare Part B initial DSMT is a 'once-in-a-lifetime' benefit covering up to 10 hours of individual and group training.
- Two hours of follow-up DSMT are allowed annually after the initial benefit.
- Programs recognized by ADA and accredited by ADCES are included on the list of telehealth professionals approved by CMS.
- As of October 1, 2025, Medicare recipients must be in a U.S.-based rural office or medical facility for most telehealth services.
- Ongoing support after formal DSMES is instrumental for improving outcomes and is reimbursable as part of follow-up DSMT (2 hours annually).
- Medicare Part B covers Medical Nutrition Therapy (MNT) separately, with 3 hours in the first year and up to 2 hours annually thereafter, with additional hours available for treatment changes.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Services must be provided in accordance with the National Standards for DSMES.
- Programs must be recognized by the American Diabetes Association through the Education Recognition Program.
- Programs must be recognized by the Association of Diabetes Care & Education Specialists (ADCES).
- For telehealth services, the patient must be located in a U.S.-based rural office or medical facility.
- Individual encounters must be billed within the first 10 hours for the initial benefit.
- For Medicare Part B MNT, additional hours beyond the standard benefit are available if a subsequent referral identifies a change in treatment.
- MNT must be delivered and billed by Registered Dietitian Nutritionists (RDNs) only.

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Telehealth professionals approved by CMS via the Consolidated Appropriations Act of 2023.
- Restriction requiring patients to be in a U.S.-based rural office or medical facility for most telehealth services as of October 1, 2025.
- Consolidated Appropriations Act of 2023 inclusion of ADA/ADCES-recognized programs on CMS telehealth professional approval lists.
