# cardiovascular outcomes guidance for diabetes drugs
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Cardiovascular outcomes trials for diabetes drugs

**Core Recommendations:**
- All new medications for type 2 diabetes must undergo cardiovascular outcomes trials as mandated by FDA guidance issued in 2008.
- The 2023 revision updated the cardiovascular outcomes guidance for diabetes drugs.
- Pre-2008 diabetes medications were not subject to cardiovascular outcomes trial requirements.
- Cardiovascular outcomes trials must now evaluate both safety and potential cardiovascular benefits (e.g., reduction in major adverse cardiovascular events) for new diabetes drugs.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- New type 2 diabetes medications must demonstrate cardiovascular safety through outcomes trials.
- Compliance with FDA guidance for cardiovascular risk assessment is mandatory for drug approval.
- Drug developers must include patients with established cardiovascular disease or high cardiovascular risk in cardiovascular outcomes trials to ensure robust assessment of cardiovascular outcomes.

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Diabetes medications approved prior to 2008 without cardiovascular outcomes trial data.

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Revised FDA guidance (May 2023) for cardiovascular outcomes trials in diabetes drugs.
- May 2023 FDA guidance explicitly encourages the use of composite cardiovascular endpoints (e.g., MACE: cardiovascular death, non-fatal myocardial infarction, non-fatal stroke) in trial design.
