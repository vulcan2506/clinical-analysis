# cognitive capacity monitoring guideline
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Cognitive capacity monitoring in diabetes

**Core Recommendations:**
- Monitor cognitive capacity throughout the life span for all individuals with diabetes
- Particular attention to those with documented cognitive disabilities
- Monitor those who experience severe hypoglycemia
- Monitor very young children
- Monitor older adults

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Documented history of severe hypoglycemia episodes as a trigger for cognitive capacity monitoring
