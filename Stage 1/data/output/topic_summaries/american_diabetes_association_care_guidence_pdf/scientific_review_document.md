# scientific review document
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Scientific review document in diabetes care

**Core Recommendations:**
- A balanced systematic review and analysis of literature on a scientific or medical topic related to diabetes
- Produced under the auspices of the ADA by invited experts
- Does not contain clinical practice recommendations
- May provide a scientific rationale for clinical practice recommendations in the Standards of Care
- May include task force and expert committee reports
- May include meta-analyses or pooled analyses of clinical trial data to strengthen evidence synthesis
- Often undergoes external peer review by independent experts before publication
