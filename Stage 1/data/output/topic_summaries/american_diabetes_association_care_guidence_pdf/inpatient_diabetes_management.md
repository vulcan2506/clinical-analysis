# inpatient diabetes management
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Inpatient diabetes management with EHR decision support

**Core Recommendations:**
- Use of decision support tools and practice advisories in the EHR to guide healthcare professionals in following standardized recommendations
- Management of acute illness to mitigate risks of hypoglycemia and hyperglycemia in hospitalized patients with diabetes
- Integration of personal and hospital-owned diabetes devices and dosing algorithms into care protocols
- Implementation of standardized protocols for glycemic targets tailored to specific patient populations (e.g., ICU vs. non-ICU, surgical vs. medical patients)
- Routine screening for diabetes distress and hypoglycemia awareness in hospitalized patients with diabetes

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Acute illness present in patients with diabetes requiring inpatient management
- Access to EHR-based decision support tools and practice advisories
- Availability of diabetes devices and dosing algorithms for clinical use
- Presence of a dedicated inpatient diabetes care team or endocrinology consultation service for complex cases
- Established protocols for insulin pump and continuous glucose monitor (CGM) management during hospitalization

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Routine use of sliding-scale insulin regimens as monotherapy for glycemic control in hospitalized patients

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Enhanced use of EHR decision support tools for inpatient diabetes management
- Adoption of personal and hospital-owned diabetes devices and dosing algorithms
- Focus on transitions from inpatient to outpatient care for diabetes management
- Integration of real-time CGM data into EHR decision support systems for dynamic insulin dosing adjustments
- Mandatory discharge planning protocols for diabetes education and follow-up within 7–14 days post-discharge
