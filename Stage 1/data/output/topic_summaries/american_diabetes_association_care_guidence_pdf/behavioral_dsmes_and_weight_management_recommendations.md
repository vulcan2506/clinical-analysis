# behavioral DSMES and weight management recommendations
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Behavioral DSMES and Weight Management

**Core Recommendations:**
- Use behavioral strategies to support diabetes self-management education and support (DSMES) and engagement in positive health behaviors.
- Provide overweight or obesity treatment plans including nutrition, physical activity, and behavioral health support to aim for at least 5–7% weight loss.
- Counsel and monitor individuals pursuing intentional weight loss for adequate nutrition intake.
- Encourage increases in physical activity levels to meet activity guidelines.
- Screen for anxiety symptoms at least annually and refer for fear of hypoglycemia intervention when clinically appropriate.
- Provide or refer individuals for combination treatment for tobacco and e-cigarette/vaping cessation, including counseling and pharmacologic therapy

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- DSMES participation must be culturally and socially appropriate based on personal preferences and needs.
- DSMES engagement must be communicated with the diabetes care team.
- Screening for anxiety and fear of hypoglycemia should occur at least annually or when clinically appropriate.
- Referral to a qualified behavioral health professional is required if diabetes distress is not adequately addressed during the medical appointment.
- Comprehensive prefasting risk assessment must be used to generate a risk score for the safety of religious fasting.
- Screening for sleep health must be performed in people with diabetes and those at risk for diabetes

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Routine assessment and avoidance of tobacco and e-cigarette/vaping use with referral for combination treatment.
- Screening for sleep health in people with diabetes and those at risk for diabetes.
- Updated comprehensive prefasting risk assessment criteria for people with diabetes seeking to fast during Ramadan.
- Updated narrative discussion on the role of physical activity during obesity treatment.
- Recommendation for screening individuals at high risk for hypoglycemia for fear of hypoglycemia.
- Updated criteria for comprehensive prefasting risk assessment for people with diabetes seeking to fast during Ramadan (Table 5.3)
