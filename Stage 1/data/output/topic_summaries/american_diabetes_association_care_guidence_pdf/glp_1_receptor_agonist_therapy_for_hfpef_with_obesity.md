# GLP-1 receptor agonist therapy for HFpEF with obesity
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** GLP-1 receptor agonist therapy for HFpEF with obesity

**Core Recommendations:**
- GLP-1 receptor agonists (GLP-1 RAs) or dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonists (e.g., tirzepatide) are recommended to improve heart failure symptoms and reduce heart failure events in individuals with HFpEF and obesity.
- Symptom improvement is measured using the Kansas City Cardiomyopathy Questionnaire clinical summary score (KCCQ-CSS) and 6-minute walk distance.
- Weight reduction is a secondary benefit observed with GLP-1 RA therapy in this population.
- Therapy should be considered irrespective of glycemic control status or need for additional glucose lowering.
- Metformin may be used for hyperglycemia management in people with stable HFpEF, provided kidney function remains within recommended ranges.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Individuals must have established HFpEF with obesity (BMI ≥ 30 kg/m²).
- Approximately 45% of individuals with HFpEF have diabetes, but therapy is not restricted to those with diabetes.
- Cardioprotective agents (e.g., SGLT2 inhibitors) should be considered as part of a comprehensive risk reduction plan.
- Close collaboration between primary and specialty care professionals is recommended to facilitate therapy adoption and minimize adverse effects.
- DPP-4 inhibitors are generally considered safe in heart failure, but saxagliptin should be used with caution due to increased risk of heart failure hospitalization.

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Thiazolidinediones should be avoided in people with heart failure due to a strong and consistent relationship with increased risk of heart failure.
- Saxagliptin is associated with increased risk of heart failure hospitalization and should be used with caution in this population.

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Semaglutide (GLP-1 RA) demonstrated significant improvement in KCCQ-CSS and weight reduction in the STEP-HFpEF DM trial.
- Tirzepatide (dual GIP/GLP-1 RA) reduced worsening heart failure events and improved KCCQ-CSS in the SUMMIT trial.
- GLP-1 RA or dual GIP/GLP-1 RA therapy is now recommended for reduction in heart failure events and symptoms in people with HFpEF and obesity.
- GLP-1 RA or dual GIP/GLP-1 RA therapy is recommended for reduction in heart failure events and symptoms in people with HFpEF and obesity, irrespective of glycemic control status or need for additional glucose lowering.
