# physical activity counseling guideline
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Physical activity counseling in diabetes care

**Core Recommendations:**
- Evaluate baseline physical activity and sedentary time for all people with diabetes and those at risk.
- Encourage increase in physical activities above baseline to meet activity guidelines for those not meeting targets.
- Interrupt prolonged sitting at least every 30 minutes for blood glucose and other health benefits.
- Counsel children/adolescents with type 1 or type 2 diabetes to engage in 60+ min/day of moderate/vigorous aerobic activity, with muscle/bone strengthening at least 3 days/week.
- Counsel adults with type 1 or type 2 diabetes to engage in 150+ min/week of moderate-to-vigorous aerobic activity, spread over at least 3 days, with no more than 2 consecutive inactive days.
- Counsel adults with type 1 or type 2 diabetes to include flexibility and balance training 2–3 days/week, particularly for older adults or those with limited mobility.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- People with diabetes or at risk for diabetes must be evaluated for baseline physical activity and sedentary time.
- Children/adolescents with type 1 or type 2 diabetes require counseling for 60+ min/day aerobic activity.
- Adults with type 1 or type 2 diabetes require counseling for 150+ min/week aerobic activity.
- Adults treated with obesity pharmacotherapy or metabolic surgery should be counseled on meeting activity recommendations, particularly muscle-strengthening exercises.
- For adults with type 1 or type 2 diabetes, assess readiness to change and barriers to physical activity before counseling to tailor recommendations effectively.

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Interrupting prolonged sitting at least every 30 minutes for blood glucose and other health benefits.
- Recommendation for 2–3 sessions/week of resistance exercise on nonconsecutive days for adults with type 1 or type 2 diabetes.
- Emphasis on muscle-strengthening exercises for individuals treated with obesity pharmacotherapy or metabolic surgery to maintain lean body mass.
- Recommend structured warm-up and cool-down periods for adults engaging in moderate-to-vigorous aerobic activity to reduce injury risk and improve adherence.
