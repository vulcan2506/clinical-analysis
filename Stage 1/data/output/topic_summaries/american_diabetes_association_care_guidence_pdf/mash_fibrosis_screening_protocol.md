# MASH fibrosis screening protocol
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** MASH fibrosis screening protocol

**Core Recommendations:**
- Screen adults with type 2 diabetes or prediabetes (especially with obesity, cardiometabolic risk factors, or cardiovascular disease) for MASH-related cirrhosis risk using FIB-4 index, even with normal liver enzymes.
- Use FIB-4 ≥1.3 as threshold for additional risk stratification with liver stiffness measurement (LSM) via transient elastography or ELF test if unavailable.
- Refer adults with type 2 diabetes or prediabetes at higher risk for significant liver fibrosis (FIB-4 ≥1.3, LSM ≥8.0 kPa, or ELF ≥9.8) to gastroenterology/hepatology for further evaluation and management.
- Implement lifestyle changes (weight loss ≥10%, structured nutrition, physical activity) and consider GLP-1 RAs or pioglitazone for glycemic management in adults with type 2 diabetes and MASLD.
- Use resmetirom for adults with MASLD and moderate (F2) to advanced (F3) fibrosis, initiated by specialists within an interprofessional team.
- Evaluate adults with diabetes or prediabetes with persistently elevated plasma aminotransferases (>30 units/L for >6 months) and low FIB-4 for other causes of liver disease.
- Follow individuals with prediabetes or type 2 diabetes and LSM <8.0 kPa in nonspecialty clinics with repeat surveillance testing every ≥2 years.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Adults must have type 2 diabetes or prediabetes, particularly with obesity or cardiometabolic risk factors, for initial FIB-4 screening.
- FIB-4 requires age, ALT, AST, and platelet count for calculation.
- LSM via transient elastography or ELF test is required for FIB-4 ≥1.3 to stratify fibrosis risk.
- Resmetirom treatment requires MASLD with moderate (F2) to advanced (F3) fibrosis confirmed by histology or validated imaging/blood-based tests.
- Specialist referral (gastroenterology/hepatology) is required for FIB-4 ≥1.3, LSM ≥8.0 kPa, or ELF ≥9.8.
- FIB-4 calculation requires age, ALT, AST, and platelet count.
- Resmetirom treatment requires MASLD with moderate (F2) to advanced (F3) liver fibrosis confirmed by liver histology or validated imaging/blood-based test, excluding cirrhosis.

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Use of elevated plasma aminotransferases (>40 units/L) as sole screening criterion for MASH-related fibrosis.
- Reliance on nonproprietary diagnostic panels (e.g., MASLD fibrosis score) over FIB-4 for initial risk stratification.
- Vitamin E monotherapy for MASH in adults with type 2 diabetes.
- Metformin for histological improvement of steatohepatitis in MASH.
- Statin therapy in decompensated cirrhosis without close monitoring.

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Replacement of NAFLD terminology with MASLD, emphasizing cardiometabolic risk factors and insulin resistance.
- FDA approval of semaglutide for MASH treatment with moderate to advanced fibrosis.
- FDA approval of resmetirom for MASLD with moderate (F2) to advanced (F3) fibrosis.
- Use of FIB-4 ≥1.3 as threshold for additional fibrosis risk stratification (LSM or ELF).
- Recommendation for GLP-1 RAs (including dual GIP/GLP-1 agonists) or pioglitazone for glycemic management in adults with type 2 diabetes and MASLD.
