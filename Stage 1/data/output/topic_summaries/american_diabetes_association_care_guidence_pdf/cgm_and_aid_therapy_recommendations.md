# CGM and AID therapy recommendations
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** CGM and AID therapy recommendations

**Core Recommendations:**
- CGM use is recommended for adults with diabetes on insulin or noninsulin therapies that can induce hypoglycemia to improve glycemic outcomes and reduce hypoglycemia episodes.
- AID systems should be offered to adults with type 1 or type 2 diabetes on insulin therapy to improve glycemic management and outcomes.
- Technology selection (CGM/AID) should be based on patient/caregiver preference, resource availability for training, and support systems.
- All individuals treated with insulin or at high risk for hypoglycemia should be prescribed glucagon.
- Individuals prescribed glucagon and their close contacts should receive education on glucagon use and administration.
- Clinicians should routinely review individuals' access to glucagon to ensure appropriate prescribing and adherence.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Adults with diabetes on insulin or noninsulin therapies that can induce hypoglycemia for CGM use.
- Adults with type 1 or type 2 diabetes on insulin therapy for AID system consideration.
- Availability of resources for training and education for CGM/AID implementation.
- Prescription of glucagon for individuals treated with insulin or at high hypoglycemia risk.
- Education on glucagon use for individuals prescribed glucagon and their close contacts.
- Individuals prescribed glucagon must have education on use and administration provided to them and their close contacts.

**Updated Recommendations:**
- AID systems recommended for adults with type 1 or type 2 diabetes on insulin therapy.
- Non-reconstitution glucagon products preferred for ease of administration.
- ADA resource (diabetes.org/living-with-diabetes/treatment-care/diabetes-technology-guide) for technology decision support.
