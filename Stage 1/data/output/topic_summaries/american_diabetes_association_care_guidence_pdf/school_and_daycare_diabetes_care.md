# school and daycare diabetes care
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** School and daycare diabetes care training and legal compliance

**Core Recommendations:**
- Training of school or daycare personnel and athletic coaches to provide care in accordance with the individualized diabetes medical management plan
- Ensuring safe access to all school- or daycare-sponsored opportunities for children and adolescents with diabetes
- Compliance with federal and state laws requiring schools and daycare facilities to provide needed diabetes care
- Provision of emergency glucagon administration training for school or daycare personnel

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Children and adolescents who require insulin must have trained personnel available
- Individualized diabetes medical management plan must be followed
- Schools and daycare facilities must have written policies for diabetes care that align with the individualized diabetes medical management plan
