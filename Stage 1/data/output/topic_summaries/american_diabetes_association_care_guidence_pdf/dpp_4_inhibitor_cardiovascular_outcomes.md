# DPP-4 inhibitor cardiovascular outcomes
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** DPP-4 inhibitor cardiovascular outcomes

**Core Recommendations:**
- DPP-4 inhibitors have not shown cardiovascular benefits relative to placebo in cardiovascular outcomes trials.
- CAROLINA trial demonstrated noninferiority between linagliptin and glimepiride on cardiovascular outcomes.
- DPP-4 inhibitors primarily reduce postprandial glucose by inhibiting degradation of incretin hormones (GLP-1 and GIP), but their cardiovascular effects are likely indirect and insufficient to reduce major adverse cardiac events (MACE)

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patients with type 2 diabetes considered for DPP-4 inhibitors should be evaluated for cardiovascular risk.
- Cardiovascular risk assessment should include evaluation of prior cardiovascular events, heart failure history, and renal function before initiating DPP-4 inhibitors

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Linagliptin showed lower rates of hypoglycemia compared to glimepiride in the CAROLINA trial.
- Consider GLP-1 receptor agonists or SGLT2 inhibitors with proven cardiovascular benefits in patients with type 2 diabetes and high cardiovascular risk
