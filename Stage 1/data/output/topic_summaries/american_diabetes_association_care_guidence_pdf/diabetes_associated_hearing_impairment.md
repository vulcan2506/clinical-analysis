# diabetes-associated hearing impairment
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Diabetes-associated hearing impairment

**Core Recommendations:**
- Hearing impairment is more common in people with diabetes than in those without, affecting both high-frequency and low- to midfrequency ranges.
- Hyperglycemia and oxidative stress contribute to cochlear microangiopathy and auditory neuropathy as proposed pathophysiologic mechanisms.
- Low HDL cholesterol, coronary heart disease, peripheral neuropathy, and general poor health are identified risk factors for hearing impairment in individuals with diabetes.
- Impairment in smell is reported in individuals with diabetes, whereas impairment in taste is not consistently observed.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Long-term follow-up (>20 years) is required to observe associations between elevated time-weighted mean A1C and increased risk of hearing impairment.

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Every 10% increase in A1C is associated with a 19% higher risk of high-frequency hearing impairment in long-term follow-up.
