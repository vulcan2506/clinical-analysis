# diabetes treatment guidelines
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Comprehensive diabetes treatment guidelines

**Core Recommendations:**
- Lifestyle behavior change is prioritized for Type 2 diabetes prevention
- Pharmacologic interventions are used to delay Type 2 diabetes progression
- Person-centered care goals are emphasized over rigid numeric targets
- Vascular disease and mortality prevention is a core focus
- Prevention or delay of symptomatic Type 1 diabetes is addressed

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Diagnosis of Type 1 or Type 2 diabetes must be confirmed before treatment initiation
- Patient-specific factors (e.g., life expectancy, comorbidities) must guide care goals
- Baseline cardiovascular risk assessment is required for vascular disease prevention
- Hypoglycemia risk must be evaluated before intensifying pharmacologic therapy
- Shared decision-making between patient and provider is mandatory

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Fixed A1C targets without consideration of patient-specific factors
- Routine use of sulfonylureas as first-line therapy in Type 2 diabetes
- Universal insulin initiation without individualized risk-benefit analysis
- Rigid dietary restrictions without behavioral support integration
- One-size-fits-all treatment algorithms for diabetes management

**Updated Recommendations:**
- GLP-1 receptor agonists and SGLT2 inhibitors recommended for cardiovascular risk reduction
- Continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) integration for real-time glycemic management
- Digital health tools (e.g., telemedicine, mobile apps) for diabetes self-management
- Early combination therapy for high-risk Type 2 diabetes patients
- Focus on social determinants of health in diabetes care planning
