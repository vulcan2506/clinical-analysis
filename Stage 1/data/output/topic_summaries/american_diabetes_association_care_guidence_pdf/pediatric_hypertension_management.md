# pediatric hypertension management
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Pediatric hypertension diagnosis and initial treatment

**Core Recommendations:**
- Blood pressure measurements must use an appropriate size cuff with the arm at heart level and the child seated and relaxed.
- Elevated blood pressure should be confirmed on at least three separate days.
- Ambulatory blood pressure monitoring should be considered for confirmation.
- Evaluation for secondary causes of hypertension should proceed as clinically indicated.
- Initial treatment is generally initiated with an ACE inhibitor, or an angiotensin receptor blocker if ACE inhibitor is not tolerated.
- Blood pressure measurements should be taken with the child or adolescent's feet flat on the floor and back supported.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Appropriate cuff size for accurate measurement.
- Child or adolescent must be seated and relaxed during measurement.
- Confirmation of elevated blood pressure on at least three separate days.
- Consideration of ambulatory blood pressure monitoring for confirmation.
- Clinical indication for evaluation of secondary causes of hypertension.
- Blood pressure should be measured in the right arm unless contraindicated, with the cuff bladder covering 80-100% of the arm circumference.

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Consideration of ambulatory blood pressure monitoring for diagnosis confirmation.
- Consider home blood pressure monitoring (HBPM) as an adjunct to clinic measurements for confirmation of elevated blood pressure.
