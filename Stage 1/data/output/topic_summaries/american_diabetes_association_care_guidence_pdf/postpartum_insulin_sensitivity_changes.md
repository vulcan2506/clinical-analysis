# postpartum insulin sensitivity changes
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** postpartum insulin sensitivity changes

**Core Recommendations:**
- Insulin sensitivity increases dramatically immediately postpartum with placental delivery
- Insulin requirements in the immediate postpartum period are roughly 34% lower than prepregnancy levels
- Insulin sensitivity returns to prepregnancy levels over 1–2 weeks postpartum
- Hypoglycemia prevention is critical for individuals taking insulin during breastfeeding and with erratic sleep/eating schedules
- Individuals with gestational diabetes mellitus (GDM) typically do not require diabetes medications postpartum
- Insulin sensitivity may remain elevated for up to 6 weeks postpartum in some individuals, particularly those with preexisting type 1 or type 2 diabetes

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Individuals requiring insulin after delivery
- Postpartum period (immediate to 1–2 weeks)
- Breastfeeding status
- Gestational diabetes mellitus (GDM) diagnosis
