# GLP-1 receptor agonist cardiovascular benefits
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** GLP-1 receptor agonist cardiovascular benefits

**Core Recommendations:**
- GLP-1 RAs (liraglutide, semaglutide, albiglutide, efpeglenatide, dulaglutide) reduce major atherosclerotic cardiovascular outcomes (MI, stroke, or cardiovascular death).
- Oral semaglutide has also demonstrated cardiovascular benefit.
- Lixisenatide and extended-release exenatide are not recommended for CVD risk reduction due to lack of superiority to placebo.
- GLP-1 RAs and SGLT2 inhibitors reduce risk of atherosclerotic major adverse cardiovascular events comparably in people with type 2 diabetes and established ASCVD.
- Combination therapy with an SGLT2 inhibitor and a GLP-1 RA may be considered for additive cardiovascular and kidney outcomes benefits.
- SGLT2 inhibitors reduce risk of heart failure hospitalization and progression of kidney disease in people with established ASCVD, multiple ASCVD risk factors, or albuminuric kidney disease

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patients must have type 2 diabetes with established ASCVD, multiple ASCVD risk factors, or CKD to qualify for GLP-1 RA cardiovascular benefit recommendations.
- GLP-1 RAs with demonstrated cardiovascular benefit (liraglutide, semaglutide, dulaglutide) are preferred over those without (lixisenatide, extended-release exenatide).
- Combination therapy with SGLT2 inhibitors and GLP-1 RAs is considered for patients with established ASCVD or multiple risk factors.
- Use of GLP-1 RAs in type 1 diabetes is not currently recommended due to increased risk of diabetic ketoacidosis.
- Patients with type 1 diabetes are not currently recommended to receive SGLT2 inhibitors for cardiovascular or kidney outcomes due to increased risk of diabetic ketoacidosis and euglycemic ketoacidosis

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Lixisenatide and extended-release exenatide are no longer recommended for CVD risk reduction.
- Albiglutide is no longer recommended due to market removal (business reasons).
- Fixed A1C targets for cardiovascular risk reduction are no longer emphasized; outcomes-based therapy is prioritized.

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Oral semaglutide is now included among GLP-1 RAs with demonstrated cardiovascular benefits.
- Combination therapy with SGLT2 inhibitors and GLP-1 RAs is newly recommended for additive cardiovascular and kidney outcomes benefits.
- Meta-analyses support comparable cardiovascular risk reduction between GLP-1 RAs and SGLT2 inhibitors in established ASCVD.
- Tirzepatide (GIP/GLP-1 RA) cardiovascular outcome trials are ongoing and not yet recommended.
- Albiglutide is noted as removed from the market for business reasons (not solely due to lack of cardiovascular benefit)
