# SGLT2 inhibitor perioperative management
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** SGLT2 inhibitor perioperative management

**Core Recommendations:**
- SGLT2 inhibitors should be held for 3–4 days before elective surgery.
- Individuals using SGLT2 inhibitors undergoing nonelective surgeries should be closely monitored for DKA after surgery.
- Euglycemic DKA should be considered in perioperative patients on SGLT2 inhibitors.
- Temporary discontinuation of SGLT2 inhibitors at least 3 days prior to surgery is advised by the FDA.
- SGLT2 inhibitors may be restarted in the hospital setting for heart failure indication when nutritional intake is resumed.
- SGLT2 inhibitors should be held for 3–4 days before surgery even if the patient is on a low-dose regimen.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Risk factors for ketoacidosis must be resolved prior to reinitiating SGLT2 inhibitor therapy.
- Nutritional intake must be resumed before restarting SGLT2 inhibitors in the hospital setting.
- Risk factors for ketoacidosis (e.g., prolonged fasting, dehydration, infection, or critical illness) must be assessed and mitigated prior to surgery.

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Consideration of euglycemic DKA in perioperative monitoring of SGLT2 inhibitor users.
- FDA recommendation for temporary discontinuation of SGLT2 inhibitors at least 3 days prior to surgery.
- Consider holding SGLT2 inhibitors for longer than 3–4 days in patients with renal impairment or those undergoing major surgeries with prolonged fasting periods.
