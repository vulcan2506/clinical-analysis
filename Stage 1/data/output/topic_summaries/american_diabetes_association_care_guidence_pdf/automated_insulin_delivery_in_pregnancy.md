# automated insulin delivery in pregnancy
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** automated insulin delivery in pregnancy

**Core Recommendations:**
- AID systems in pregnancy require careful assessment due to non-pregnancy-specific glucose goals and algorithms
- Selected individuals with type 1 diabetes may be evaluated for AID systems under expert guidance
- Use of insulin delivery technology is an individual choice for people with diabetes
- Disposable patch-like devices provide basal or bolus insulin in 2-unit increments for type 2 diabetes

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Expert guidance is required for evaluating candidates for AID systems in pregnancy
- Capability to safely use the device is necessary for insulin delivery technology adoption

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Disposable patch-like insulin delivery devices for type 2 diabetes
