# pregnancy diabetes nutrition counseling
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Pregnancy diabetes nutrition counseling + lifestyle management

**Core Recommendations:**
- Promote an eating pattern including fruits, vegetables, legumes, whole grains, nuts, seeds, fish, and other lean protein to balance macronutrients and healthy n-3 fatty acids
- Lifestyle behavior change is essential for managing GDM and may suffice as treatment for many individuals
- Telehealth visits combined with in-person visits for pregnant people with GDM can improve outcomes
- Insulin is the preferred agent for managing GDM, type 2 diabetes, and type 1 diabetes in pregnancy
- Automated insulin delivery (AID) systems with pregnancy-specific glucose targets are recommended for pregnant individuals with type 1 diabetes
- Physical activity goals should be included in the management plan for pregnancies associated with diabetes.
- Management must support maternal, fetal, and placental needs to reach glycemic goals regardless of diabetes type.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Pregnant individuals with GDM, type 1 diabetes, or type 2 diabetes
- Glycemic goals must be defined and monitored
- Healthcare team with experience in pregnancy and diabetes management
- Access to telehealth and in-person care resources
- Appropriate diagnostic criteria for GDM or pre-existing diabetes
- Use of AID systems without pregnancy-specific glucose targets requires assistive techniques and collaboration with experienced healthcare teams.
- Metformin use for PCOS requires discontinuation by the end of the first trimester.

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Metformin and glyburide as first-line agents for diabetes management in pregnancy
- Use of oral or noninsulin injectable glucose-lowering medications without long-term safety data
- Fixed A1C numeric targets without individualized consideration
- Reliance solely on in-person care without telehealth integration for GDM

**Updated Recommendations:**
- AID systems with pregnancy-specific glucose targets for type 1 diabetes in pregnancy
- Telehealth combined with in-person visits for GDM management
- Discontinuation of metformin for PCOS by end of first trimester
- Emphasis on lifestyle and behavior management as primary treatment for GDM
- Use of multiple daily injections or insulin pump technology for type 1 diabetes in pregnancy
