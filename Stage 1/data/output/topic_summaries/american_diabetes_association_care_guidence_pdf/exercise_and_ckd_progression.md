# exercise and CKD progression
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Exercise and CKD progression

**Core Recommendations:**
- Physical activity can acutely increase urinary albumin excretion
- No evidence that vigorous-intensity exercise accelerates CKD progression
- No need for specific exercise restrictions for people with CKD in general
- Regular moderate-intensity exercise may reduce albuminuria over time in people with CKD

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Exercise recommendations should be individualized based on CKD stage, comorbidities, and functional capacity

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Encourage structured aerobic and resistance exercise programs tailored to CKD severity and patient tolerance
