# device adhesive contact dermatitis management
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** device adhesive contact dermatitis management

**Core Recommendations:**
- Ask CGM users periodically about adhesive reactions as tape formulations may change over time
- Identify and eliminate tape allergens to ensure comfortable device use and promote self-care
- Use patch testing to identify the cause of contact dermatitis when suspected

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Presence of contact dermatitis (irritant or allergic) linked to device adhesives
- Patient using a device that attaches to the skin (e.g., CGM)
- Suspected isobornyl acrylate sensitivity or spreading allergic reaction
- Presence of contact dermatitis (both irritant and allergic) linked to device adhesives

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Routine use of fixed tape formulations without periodic reassessment of reactions

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Consideration of implanted sensors for patients sensitive to tape adhesives
- Utilization of the PANTHER Program resources for adhesive dermatitis management
