# SGLT2 inhibitor and GLP-1 therapy in CKD
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** SGLT2 inhibitor and GLP-1 therapy in CKD

**Core Recommendations:**
- SGLT2 inhibitors can be safely continued in individuals with eGFR <20 mL/min/1.73 m² and not on dialysis to reduce CKD progression and cardiovascular risks.
- GLP-1-based therapy (not dependent on kidney clearance) can be safely initiated or continued in individuals on dialysis to reduce cardiovascular risk and mortality.
- SGLT2 inhibitors should be continued unless kidney failure develops, as they have non-kidney effects including cardiovascular benefits.
- GLP-1-based therapy in dialysis patients has shown benefits in weight reduction, glycemic management, and cardiovascular outcomes.
- Referral to a nephrologist is recommended for rapidly increasing albuminuria, rapidly decreasing eGFR, or eGFR <30 mL/min/1.73 m².
- GLP-1-based therapy in dialysis patients is associated with increased gastrointestinal symptoms due to elevated GLP-1 levels from reduced renal clearance.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- eGFR <20 mL/min/1.73 m² and not on dialysis for SGLT2 inhibitor continuation.
- Dialysis dependence for GLP-1-based therapy initiation or continuation.
- Tolerability of SGLT2 inhibitors for continuation.
- Albuminuria and/or declining eGFR for nephrology referral.
- Uncertainty about kidney disease etiology or difficult management issues for nephrology referral.
- Individuals on peritoneal dialysis may require closer monitoring for GLP-1-based therapy due to observed improvements in left ventricular mass index and ejection fraction.

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Exenatide and lixisenatide should not be used in individuals on dialysis.
- Fixed A1C targets without consideration of patient-specific factors in CKD.

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Post hoc analysis of DAPA-CKD trial supports SGLT2 inhibitor use in stage G4 CKD with albuminuria.
- Ongoing Renal Lifecycle trial investigating SGLT2 inhibitor safety and efficacy in eGFR ≤25 mL/min/1.73 m² or kidney failure.
- GLP-1-based therapy associated with 23% lower mortality and 66% higher transplant waitlisting in dialysis patients.
- Nephrology referral thresholds include continuously rising UACR, continuously declining eGFR, or eGFR <30 mL/min/1.73 m².
- Proactive education on CKD progression, blood pressure/glucose management, and kidney replacement therapy needs.
- Consultation with a nephrologist at stage G4 CKD (eGFR <30 mL/min/1.73 m²) has been shown to reduce cost, improve quality of care, and delay the need for dialysis.
