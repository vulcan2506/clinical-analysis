# Severe Hypercholesterolemia Management
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Bempedoic Acid for Severe Hypercholesterolemia

**Core Recommendations:**
- Bempedoic acid lowers LDL cholesterol by 15% in statin users and 24% in non-statin users
- Combined use with ezetimibe yields an additional 19% LDL cholesterol reduction
- CLEAR Outcomes trial showed a 13% reduction in four-point major adverse cardiovascular events in statin-intolerant patients
- Prespecified subanalyses demonstrated a 17% reduction in four-point major adverse cardiovascular events for individuals with diabetes
- Primary prevention subgroup showed a 30% reduction in primary composite outcome
- Bempedoic acid reduces LDL cholesterol by 24% in non-statin users and 15% in statin users, with no additional benefit observed in patients already on high-intensity statin therapy

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patients must be intolerant to statin therapy or require additional LDL cholesterol lowering despite statin use
- Patients must be intolerant to statin therapy or have a contraindication to statins, or require additional LDL cholesterol lowering despite maximally tolerated statin therapy

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Bempedoic acid as a therapeutic option for severe hypercholesterolemia management
- Use of bempedoic acid in statin-intolerant patients with established ASCVD or high ASCVD risk
- Consideration of bempedoic acid for primary prevention in high-risk patients intolerant to statins
- Consideration of bempedoic acid for patients with familial hypercholesterolemia who are statin-intolerant or require additional LDL lowering
