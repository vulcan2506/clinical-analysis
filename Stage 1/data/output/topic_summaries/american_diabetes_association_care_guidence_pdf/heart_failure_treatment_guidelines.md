# heart failure treatment guidelines
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Heart Failure Prevention and Treatment in Diabetes

**Core Recommendations:**
- Implement early primary prevention strategies including lifestyle intervention, nutrition, physical activity, weight management, and smoking cessation.
- Manage hypertension intensively using ACE inhibitors or ARBs as the preferred strategy, particularly in the presence of albuminuria or coronary artery disease.
- Treat asymptomatic patients with structural heart disease (e.g., LVEF ≤40%) with ACE inhibitors/ARBs plus β-blockers to prevent progression to symptomatic heart failure.
- Prescribe SGLT2 inhibitors for patients with type 2 diabetes and established HFpEF or HFrEF to reduce hospitalization and cardiovascular death.
- Consider adding finerenone in patients with type 2 diabetes and CKD with albuminuria to improve cardiovascular outcomes and reduce heart failure hospitalization risk.
- Assess susceptibility to diabetic ketoacidosis and provide education regarding risks, symptoms, and prevention strategies for patients on SGLT2 inhibitors.
- Prescribe home monitoring supplies for beta-hydroxybutyrate to minimize the risk of diabetic ketoacidosis in patients using SGLT2 inhibitors.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patient must have type 2 diabetes and established cardiovascular disease or increased cardiovascular risk for SGLT2 inhibitor initiation.
- Patient must have eGFR ≥ 20 mL/min/1.73 m² and potassium < 5.0 mEq/L for SGLT2 inhibitor use.
- Patient must have symptomatic stage C or D heart failure or history of MI with reduced LVEF for standard guideline-directed medical therapy.
- Patient must have type 2 diabetes and CKD with albuminuria treated with maximum tolerated doses of ACE inhibitor or ARB for finerenone consideration.
- Patient must have symptomatic HFpEF (ejection fraction ≥40%) for finerenone consideration in heart failure outcomes.
- Patient must have symptomatic HFpEF (ejection fraction ≥40%) for finerenone consideration specifically for heart failure outcomes.
- Patient must have eGFR ≥30 mL/min/1.73 m² for the use of SGLT1/2 inhibitors as an alternative to SGLT2 inhibitors.

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Fixed numeric A1C targets for older adults without individualization based on life expectancy and hypoglycemia risk.
- Use of SGLT2 inhibitors in patients with eGFR < 20 mL/min/1.73 m² without specific alternative protocols.
- Reliance on glucose-lowering activity alone as the primary mechanism for heart failure benefit in SGLT2 inhibitors.
- Exclusion of patients with type 1 diabetes from SGLT2 inhibitor cardiovascular outcome trials without specific risk assessment.
- Use of non-selective MRAs without consideration of nonsteroidal alternatives like finerenone in diabetic kidney disease.

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Recommendation to assess susceptibility, provide education, and prescribe home monitoring supplies for β-hydroxybutyrate to minimize diabetic ketoacidosis risk with SGLT2 inhibitors.
- Use of sotagliflozin (SGLT1/2 inhibitor) initiated during or shortly after hospitalization for heart failure in people with diabetes.
- Extension of SGLT2 inhibitor benefits to patients with heart failure irrespective of the presence or absence of type 2 diabetes.
- Consideration of finerenone addition in patients with symptomatic HFpEF to improve cardiovascular and kidney outcomes.
- Integration of collaborative approaches with cardiovascular specialists for management of symptomatic stage C and D heart failure in people with diabetes.
