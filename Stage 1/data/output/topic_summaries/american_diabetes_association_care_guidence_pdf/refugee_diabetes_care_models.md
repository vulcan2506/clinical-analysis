# refugee diabetes care models
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Refugee/migrant diabetes care models

**Core Recommendations:**
- Refugee status and migrant farmworker status are independent risk factors for cardiovascular disease and type 2 diabetes.
- Nontraditional care delivery models (e.g., mobile integrated health, telehealth) should be leveraged to improve access to diabetes care for migrant populations.
- Healthcare professionals should initiate referrals to social workers and community resources to address barriers to care (e.g., cost, language, transportation, food insecurity).
- Diabetes care must account for unique barriers such as migration frequency, work hours, cultural/linguistic differences, and systemic healthcare complexity.
- Refugee status is an independent risk factor for cardiovascular disease, compounding diabetes management challenges in humanitarian crises.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patient must be identified as a refugee, migrant farmworker, or agricultural worker with diabetes.
- Assessment of barriers to care (e.g., food insecurity, language, transportation) must be conducted.
- Referral pathways to social services and community resources must be established or accessible.
- Healthcare professionals must assess pesticide exposure history in migrant agricultural workers as part of diabetes risk stratification.

**Previous Recommendations:**
- One-size-fits-all diabetes care models without consideration of migrant/refugee-specific barriers.
- Reliance solely on traditional clinic-based care without leveraging mobile or telehealth solutions.

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Integration of mobile integrated health and telehealth to overcome geographic and logistical barriers.
- Targeted screening for diabetes in migrant farmworker populations due to elevated risk.
- Collaboration with community resources to address social determinants of health (e.g., food access, housing).
- Establish partnerships with agricultural worker health centers to facilitate care continuity during seasonal migration.
