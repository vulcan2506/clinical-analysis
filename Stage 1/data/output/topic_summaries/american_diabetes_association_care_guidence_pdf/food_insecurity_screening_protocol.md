# food insecurity screening protocol
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** food insecurity screening protocol

**Core Recommendations:**
- Food insecurity screening should occur at all levels of the healthcare system.
- Any member of the healthcare team can screen using the Hunger Vital Sign.
- Households are considered at risk if they answer either or both Hunger Vital Sign questions as 'often true' or 'sometimes true'.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Screening must include the two Hunger Vital Sign questions.
- Screening should be conducted at all healthcare system levels.
- Screening must be conducted using the exact two Hunger Vital Sign questions as specified in the protocol.

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Use of the Hunger Vital Sign as the standardized screening tool for food insecurity.
- Positive screening results must trigger referral to appropriate food insecurity programs and resources.
