# pediatric diabetes kidney risk management
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Pediatric diabetes kidney risk management

**Core Recommendations:**
- Emphasize meeting glycemic and blood pressure goals to reduce chronic kidney disease risk in individuals <20 years with diabetes
- Conduct routine screening for albuminuria to ensure early diagnosis and timely treatment
- Estimate glomerular filtration rate (GFR) at baseline and repeat based on clinical status, age, diabetes duration, and therapies
- Avoid reliance on estimated GFR for screening early GFR loss when GFR >75 mL/min/1.73 m² due to inaccuracy
- Consider cystatin C-based GFR estimation or direct GFR measurement in high-risk pediatric patients when eGFR is >75 mL/min/1.73 m² to detect early GFR loss
- Prioritize dual optimization of glycemic control and blood pressure management to mitigate pediatric CKD risk, as poor control in either domain independently contributes to kidney damage

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patient age <20 years
- Diabetes duration considered for screening frequency
- Serum creatinine, height, age, and sex available for GFR estimation
- Clinical status and therapies influencing repeat GFR testing
- Diabetes duration must be considered when determining the frequency of albuminuria screening and GFR monitoring
- Serum creatinine, height, age, and sex must be available for accurate GFR estimation using GFR-estimating equations

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Reliance on estimated GFR for detecting early GFR loss when GFR >75 mL/min/1.73 m²

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Consider ACE inhibitor treatment in adolescents with type 1 diabetes as safe, though not shown to change albumin-to-creatinine ratio
