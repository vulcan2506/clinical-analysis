# obesity treatment guidelines
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Obesity treatment guidelines updates

**Core Recommendations:**
- Screening for overweight and obesity should occur annually using BMI with additional body fat measurement (e.g., anthropometric assessment) where feasible.
- Weight loss of 5–7% of baseline body weight is recommended to improve glycemia and cardiovascular risk factors.
- Counseling and regular monitoring for adequate nutrition intake are advised for individuals pursuing intentional weight loss.
- Engage care team members to minimize use of weight-promoting medications when clinically appropriate.
- Individualized dose and dose titration for obesity pharmacotherapy should balance efficacy, benefits, and tolerability.
- Screening for overweight and obesity should incorporate additional body fat measurement (e.g., anthropometric assessment or direct measurement) where available and feasible to confirm excess adiposity.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Screening for overweight and obesity must include BMI and, where feasible, additional body fat measurement.
- Weight loss targets (5–7% of baseline body weight) are recommended to improve glycemia and cardiovascular risk factors.
- Intentional weight loss requires counseling and regular monitoring for adequate nutrition intake.
- Minimizing weight-promoting medications requires engagement of other care team members.
- Obesity pharmacotherapy dosing must balance efficacy, benefits, and tolerability.
- Alternative structured lifestyle programs must include clearly defined components and modes of delivery (e.g., in-person, telehealth, digital platforms) as per updated Recommendation 8.8b.

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Inclusion of GLP-1 RA-based therapy and/or metabolic surgery as treatment options for obesity in people with type 1 diabetes.
- Updated obesity pharmacotherapy costs as of 15 July 2025.
- Recommendation to consider alternative pharmacologic agents for treatment modification and intensification.
- Clarification of components of alternative structured lifestyle programs and modes of delivery.
- Recommendation for individualized obesity pharmacotherapy dosing and titration.
- Recommendation 8.21 now includes considering alternative pharmacologic agents for treatment modification and intensification in obesity management.
