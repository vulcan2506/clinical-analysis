# diabetes care journal
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Diabetes Care journal mission and scope

**Core Recommendations:**
- Promotes better patient care by serving the expanded needs of all health professionals committed to diabetes care
- Increases knowledge, stimulates research, and promotes better management of people with diabetes
- Publishes original research across multiple categories including Clinical Care, Epidemiology, Emerging Technologies, and Pathophysiology
- Disseminates ADA statements, consensus reports, review articles, and health news to clinically oriented professionals
- Targets physicians, researchers, epidemiologists, psychologists, diabetes educators, and other health professionals
- Publishes letters to the editor and points of view to foster dialogue among clinicians and researchers
- Provides a platform for ADA statements and consensus reports to disseminate official guidelines and recommendations

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Original research must focus on human studies
- Manuscripts must align with the journal's categories: Clinical Care/Education/Nutrition/Psychosocial Research, Epidemiology/Health Services Research, Emerging Technologies and Therapeutics, Pathophysiology/Complications, and Cardiovascular and Metabolic Risk

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Inclusion of Cardiovascular and Metabolic Risk as a distinct research category
