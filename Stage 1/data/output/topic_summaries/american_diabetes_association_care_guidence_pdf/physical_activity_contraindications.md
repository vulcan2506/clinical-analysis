# physical activity contraindications
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Vigorous exercise contraindications in diabetic retinopathy

**Core Recommendations:**
- Vigorous-intensity aerobic or resistance exercise may be contraindicated in patients with proliferative diabetic retinopathy or severe nonproliferative diabetic retinopathy due to risk of vitreous hemorrhage or retinal detachment.
- High-intensity exercise may also be contraindicated in patients with active diabetic macular edema due to risk of further retinal damage or hemorrhage.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Presence of proliferative diabetic retinopathy or severe nonproliferative diabetic retinopathy must be confirmed before applying contraindication.
- Retinal imaging (e.g., fluorescein angiography or optical coherence tomography) must confirm the absence of active neovascularization or macular edema before allowing moderate-intensity exercise in patients with diabetic retinopathy.

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Consultation with an ophthalmologist prior to initiating an intense exercise plan is recommended for patients with high-risk diabetic retinopathy.
- For patients with stable diabetic retinopathy without high-risk features, moderate-intensity exercise is generally safe and should be encouraged as part of diabetes management.
