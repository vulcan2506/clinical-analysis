# professional CGM for glycemic pattern analysis
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** professional CGM for glycemic pattern analysis

**Core Recommendations:**
- Professional CGM devices provide retrospective data (blinded or unblinded) to identify patterns of hypoglycemia and hyperglycemia.
- Useful for evaluating glucose levels when real-time CGM (rtCGM) or intermittently scanned CGM (isCGM) is unavailable or when patients prefer blinded analysis.
- Particularly beneficial for individuals using hypoglycemia-inducing agents to assess hypoglycemic periods and adjust medication doses.
- Can evaluate hyperglycemia patterns to guide treatment adjustments.
- Intermittent use of CGM (rtCGM or isCGM) in type 2 diabetes on noninsulin or basal insulin therapies may improve A1C levels.
- Professional CGM can be used to assess glycemic variability and time-in-range (TIR) metrics, which are critical for evaluating overall glycemic control beyond just hypoglycemia and hyperglycemia patterns.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Individuals using agents that can cause hypoglycemia (e.g., sulfonylureas, insulin).
- Patients with type 2 diabetes not on intensive insulin therapy for intermittent CGM use.
- Patients who prefer blinded analysis or shorter unblinded data exposure.
- Coupling CGM use with analysis, interpretation, and education to adjust medication and lifestyle behaviors.
- Patients with type 2 diabetes not on insulin at baseline for real-world professional CGM use.
- Professional CGM may be considered for patients with type 2 diabetes who are not achieving glycemic targets despite optimized lifestyle and oral therapy, to guide further treatment intensification.

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Use of professional CGM in type 2 diabetes not on insulin at baseline resulted in lower A1C at 6 months.
- Shift toward greater use of glucose-lowering medications with cardiometabolic benefits (e.g., SGLT2 inhibitors, GLP-1 receptor agonists) after professional CGM use.
- Intermittent CGM use in type 2 diabetes on noninsulin or basal insulin therapies may lead to early and late improvements in A1C levels.
- Professional CGM use in type 2 diabetes not on insulin at baseline may lead to earlier initiation of cardiometabolic-beneficial therapies (e.g., SGLT2 inhibitors, GLP-1 receptor agonists) due to identified glycemic patterns.
