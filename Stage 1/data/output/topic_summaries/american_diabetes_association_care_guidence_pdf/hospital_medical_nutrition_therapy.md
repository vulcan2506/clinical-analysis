# hospital medical nutrition therapy
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** hospital medical nutrition therapy

**Core Recommendations:**
- Provide adequate calories to meet metabolic demands
- Optimize glycemic outcomes
- Address personal food preferences
- Facilitate creation of a discharge plan
- Coordinate glucose checks, meal delivery, and nutritional insulin coverage (meal triad)
- Offer carbohydrate counting support for patients using insulin pumps or multiple daily injections
- Provide education on insulin stacking risks with 'meals on demand' systems

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Individualization based on treatment goals, physiological parameters, and medication use
- Controlled carbohydrate meal plans preferred for prandial insulin matching
- Carbohydrate counting available for patients who count carbohydrates at home
- Meal triad coordination to prevent hyperglycemia or hypoglycemia
- Hospital food service must support carbohydrate counting for implementation
- Real-time glucose monitoring required for patients using 'meals on demand' with insulin therapy

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Fixed meal plans or specified macronutrient percentages
- Uncoordinated glucose monitoring, meal delivery, and insulin dosing

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Meal triad coordination concept
- Meals on demand option (with caution for insulin stacking)
- Carbohydrate counting availability for insulin pump users
- Carbohydrate counting support for insulin pump users as a standard option
