# statin intolerance management
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Statin Intolerance Management

**Core Recommendations:**
- Switch to a different high-intensity statin if indicated
- Switch to moderate-intensity or low-intensity statin
- Lower the statin dose
- Use nondaily dosing with statins
- Add nonstatin treatments to maximum tolerated statin therapy
- Consider ezetimibe as a first-line nonstatin therapy in statin-intolerant patients

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Identify partial or complete statin intolerance (inability to tolerate sufficient dosage or any dose due to adverse effects)
- Confirm statin intolerance through a structured re-challenge protocol (e.g., trial of a different statin at lowest effective dose with gradual uptitration)

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Consider nonstatin treatments in combination with maximum tolerated statin therapy for improved LDL cholesterol goal achievement
- Evaluate for secondary causes of statin intolerance (e.g., drug interactions, hypothyroidism, vitamin D deficiency) before deeming a patient truly intolerant
