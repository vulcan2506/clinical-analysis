# HCV-related diabetes risk and treatment impact
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** HCV-related diabetes risk and treatment impact

**Core Recommendations:**
- HCV infection is associated with a higher prevalence of type 2 diabetes, present in up to one-third of individuals with chronic HCV.
- HCV may impair glucose metabolism directly via viral proteins and indirectly by altering proinflammatory cytokine levels.
- Newer direct-acting antiviral drugs produce a sustained virological response (cure) in nearly all cases.
- Successful HCV eradication with direct-acting antivirals improves glucose metabolism in individuals with diabetes.
- Meta-analysis shows mean reduction in A1C levels of 0.45% and reduced requirement for glucose-lowering medication post-HCV cure.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Chronic HCV infection diagnosis
- Documented type 2 diabetes or impaired glucose metabolism
- Eligibility for direct-acting antiviral therapy

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Use of direct-acting antivirals for HCV treatment to improve glucose metabolism in diabetic patients
- Monitoring A1C levels and glucose-lowering medication requirements post-HCV cure
