# vision rehabilitation care
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Vision rehabilitation care for diabetes-related vision loss

**Core Recommendations:**
- Counsel patients with diabetes-related vision loss on the availability and scope of vision rehabilitation care
- Provide or refer for a comprehensive evaluation of visual impairment by a practitioner experienced in vision rehabilitation
- Deliver educational materials and resources for eye care support alongside self-management education
- Evaluate patients to determine potential benefits from comprehensive vision restoration
- Address physical, psychological, behavioral, and social consequences of vision loss
- Coordinate with multidisciplinary teams (e.g., endocrinologists, occupational therapists, social workers) to deliver integrated vision rehabilitation care

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Diabetes-related vision loss must be confirmed
- Patient must be evaluated by a practitioner experienced in vision rehabilitation
- Educational materials and resources for eye care support must be provided
- Self-management education (e.g., glycemic management and hypoglycemia awareness) must be included
- Assessment of potential benefits from vision restoration must be conducted
- Vision loss must be assessed for its impact on activities of daily living (ADLs) and instrumental ADLs before initiating rehabilitation

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Comprehensive evaluation by a vision rehabilitation specialist
- Integration of educational materials and resources for eye care support
- Focus on functional independence and quality of life outcomes
- Holistic approach addressing psychological and social impacts of vision loss
- Evaluation for potential benefits from vision restoration therapies
- Evaluation of potential benefits from low-vision assistive technologies (e.g., magnifiers, screen readers, adaptive lighting)
