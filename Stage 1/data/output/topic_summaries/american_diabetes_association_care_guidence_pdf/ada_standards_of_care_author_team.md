# ADA Standards of Care author team
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** ADA Standards of Care author team composition and governance

**Core Recommendations:**
- Co-chaired by Mandeep Bajaj, MBBS, FRCP and Rozalina G. McCoy, MD, MS
- Includes multidisciplinary experts (endocrinologists, pharmacists, methodologists, and patient advocates)
- Follows CMSS principles for clinical practice guideline development
- Incorporates external peer review from academic and clinical specialists
- Acknowledges ADA Presidents and Presidents-elect for governance alignment
- Explicit acknowledgment of ADA Presidents and Presidents-elect (e.g., A. Enrique Caballero, Joshua J. Neumiller) for governance alignment
- Inclusion of specific external peer reviewers from diverse disciplines (e.g., cardiology, nephrology, endocrinology) to validate recommendations

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Adherence to CMSS code for interactions with companies to ensure transparency
- Use of evidence-based methodology led by Chief Methodologist (Raveendhara R. Bannuru, MD, PhD)
- Inclusion of patient-specific factors in guideline development (e.g., life expectancy, cognitive function)
- Regular updates to reflect new clinical evidence (annual revisions)
- External peer review by recognized experts in diabetes care and related fields

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Prior versions of guidelines without CMSS-aligned methodology
- Non-evidence-based recommendations from outdated consensus statements
- Guidelines not incorporating patient-centered outcomes

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Integration of CMSS principles for guideline development in 2026 Standards of Care
- Expanded multidisciplinary author team including methodologists and patient advocates
- Inclusion of new external peer reviewers for enhanced validity
- Updated governance structure with ADA Presidents and Presidents-elect alignment
- Annual review cycle to ensure timeliness and relevance of recommendations
