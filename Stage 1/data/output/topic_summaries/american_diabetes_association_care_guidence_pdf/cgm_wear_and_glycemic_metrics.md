# CGM wear and glycemic metrics
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** CGM wear and glycemic metrics

**Core Recommendations:**
- Use ≥14-day CGM wear time for pattern management
- Maintain ≥70% active CGM time out of 14 days
- Target TIR (70–180 mg/dL) ≥50% for older adults with complex/intermediate health
- Limit hypoglycemia (<70 mg/dL) to ≤1% (15 min/day) for older adults
- Deintensify therapy if hypoglycemia goals (<4% <70 mg/dL, <1% <54 mg/dL) are not met
- Engage in shared decision-making with patient/family to individualize glycemic goals and adjust therapy based on changing health status

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- CGM device must be worn for ≥14 days for valid pattern analysis
- Patient must be using insulin or sulfonylureas for hypoglycemia risk assessment
- Shared decision-making with patient/family to individualize glycemic goals
- Assess cognitive function, frailty, and comorbidities for older adults
- Prioritize hypoglycemia prevention in high-risk populations
- Assess patient's cognitive function, frailty, and comorbidities when setting glycemic goals for older adults
- Consider patient's health, function, capacity for self-management, and hypoglycemia risk when individualizing glycemic goals

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Fixed numeric A1C targets without individualized consideration
- Routine use of non-CGM-based glycemic monitoring for pattern management
- One-size-fits-all glycemic goals without accounting for hypoglycemia risk
- Ignoring patient-specific factors (e.g., life expectancy, self-management capacity)
- Overly aggressive glycemic targets in frail or high-hypoglycemia-risk individuals
- Routine use of fixed A1C targets without individualized consideration of patient-specific factors

**Updated Recommendations:**
- CGM wear time ≥14 days as standard for valid glycemic pattern analysis
- Active CGM time ≥70% as a minimum threshold for reliable data
- Hypoglycemia thresholds (<70 mg/dL <1%, <54 mg/dL <1%) as actionable CGM metrics
- Individualized TIR goals (e.g., ≥50% for older adults) based on health status
- Therapy deintensification if hypoglycemia metrics are exceeded
- Lower coefficient of variation (CV) target of <33% for additional hypoglycemia protection in insulin or sulfonylurea users
- Less stringent glycemic goals for individuals with significant cognitive/functional limitations, frailty, or severe comorbidities
