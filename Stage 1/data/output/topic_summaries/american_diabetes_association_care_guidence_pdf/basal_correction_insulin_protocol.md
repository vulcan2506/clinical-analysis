# basal-correction insulin protocol
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Basal-correction insulin protocol for type 1 diabetes

**Core Recommendations:**
- Correctional insulin alone is contraindicated for type 1 diabetes due to risk of hypoglycemia and hyperglycemia
- Insulin schedule must include basal and correction components for all hospitalized individuals with type 1 diabetes
- Basal insulin must not be held, even for those taking nothing by mouth or during care transitions
- Prandial insulin should be added when individuals are eating
- EHR policies and practice alerts must ensure basal insulin is administered
- Insulin pump or infusion basal insulin must be continued without interruption during procedures or imaging requiring contrast, with appropriate adjustments to prevent hypoglycemia or hyperglycemia

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patient must have type 1 diabetes
- Hospitalized individual status
- Basal insulin requirement must be accounted for
- Prescriber and nursing education must be ongoing
- EHR policies and practice alerts must be implemented
- Basal insulin dose must be individualized based on prior insulin requirements, glycemic targets, and clinical status (e.g., renal function, corticosteroid use)

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Correctional insulin alone as a standalone protocol
- Holding basal insulin during care transitions
- Relying solely on premeal glucose levels for insulin dosing
- Relying on sliding-scale insulin regimens as the primary insulin strategy for hospitalized individuals with type 1 diabetes

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Mandatory basal-correction insulin protocol for type 1 diabetes
- EHR policies to prevent basal insulin omission
- Continuous education for prescribers and nursing staff
- Prandial insulin addition when eating
- Basal insulin administration via subcutaneous, pump, or infusion
- Mandatory documentation of basal insulin administration timing and dose in the EHR to ensure accountability and traceability
