# Guideline Development Process
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Guideline Development Process

**Core Recommendations:**
- Defines the scope and intended audience for the guidelines
- Outlines the methodology and procedure for guideline creation
- Establishes ADA guidance categories and endorsement processes
- Includes a systematic review of evidence using GRADE methodology
- Involves multidisciplinary expert panel composition and conflict-of-interest management
- Requires external peer review by stakeholders before finalization
- Defines a transparent process for updating guidelines annually

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Requires evidence to meet predefined quality and relevance criteria before inclusion
- Mandates disclosure of potential conflicts of interest by all panel members
- Ensures guideline recommendations align with current regulatory standards and ethical considerations

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Integration of digital health tools and telemedicine recommendations in diabetes management
- Emphasis on social determinants of health in guideline recommendations
- Inclusion of continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) as a standard of care for certain patient populations
- Recommendations for personalized diabetes care plans based on patient preferences and comorbidities
- Updated pharmacologic treatment algorithms incorporating newer glucose-lowering medications
