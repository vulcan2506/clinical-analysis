# Standards of Care publication
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** ADA Standards of Care publication and advocacy framework

**Core Recommendations:**
- Annual updates to clinical practice recommendations by an interprofessional expert committee
- Provision of evidence-based, peer-reviewed statements on diabetes management and policy advocacy
- Implementation of advocacy-oriented statements to address discrimination, employment, driving, and insulin affordability
- Focus on reducing barriers to diabetes care, including cost and access issues
- Educational outreach to schools, employers, licensing agencies, and policymakers
- Publication of advocacy-oriented statements in peer-reviewed journals (e.g., Diabetes Care) alongside clinical recommendations

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Annual review and updates by the ADA Professional Practice Committee for Diabetes
- Evidence-based grading system for clinical practice recommendations
- Peer-reviewed publication of standards and advocacy statements
- Public comment period for Standards of Care updates
- Comprehensive list of advocacy statements available on ADA website
- Evidence-grading system for advocacy statements to ensure scientific rigor and policy relevance

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Inclusion of advocacy statements addressing insulin access and affordability as a strategic focus
- Emphasis on reducing discrimination and cost barriers in diabetes management
- Expansion of advocacy statements to include settings like schools, childcare programs, and detention facilities
- Inclusion of advocacy statements addressing diabetes management in detention facilities as a newly emphasized setting
