# population health collaboration framework
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Population health collaboration framework for diabetes care

**Core Recommendations:**
- Engagement and collaboration between people with diabetes, caregivers, interprofessional healthcare teams, health systems, community partners, payors, policymakers, and public health agencies
- Implementation of evidence-based diabetes care recommendations to improve individual and population health outcomes
- Addressing social determinants of health (e.g., food insecurity, housing insecurity, financial toxicity) to eliminate health disparities
- Integration of telemedicine, digital health interventions, and mHealth tools to enhance access and self-management support
- Use of community health workers, peer support, and culturally tailored interventions to improve diabetes outcomes
- Addressing health literacy and numeracy barriers to improve patient understanding and self-management
- Implementing food prescription programs and addressing food insecurity as part of diabetes care

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Existence of interprofessional healthcare teams trained in diabetes management
- Availability of community resources and partnerships to address social needs
- Access to telemedicine infrastructure and digital health tools for underserved populations
- Implementation of standardized screening protocols for social determinants of health
- Adoption of value-based care models and payment reforms to support population health initiatives
- Availability of validated screening tools for food insecurity and housing instability
- Presence of trained community health workers or community paramedics within the care network

**Previous Recommendations:**
- One-size-fits-all A1C targets without consideration of patient-specific factors
- Fragmented care models that do not integrate community and public health resources
- Reliance solely on traditional in-person care without leveraging telemedicine or digital tools
- Ignoring social determinants of health in diabetes management plans
- Lack of collaboration between healthcare systems, payors, and policymakers

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Integration of social determinants of health screening into routine diabetes care
- Adoption of telemedicine and digital health interventions to improve access and outcomes
- Implementation of community health worker programs to support diabetes self-management
- Use of value-based care models and alternative payment strategies to incentivize population health improvements
- Culturally tailored diabetes education and support programs to address health disparities
- Integration of food prescription programs into routine diabetes care plans
- Deployment of community paramedics for diabetes self-management education and support
