# weight management in diabetes care
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Weight Management in Diabetes Care

**Core Recommendations:**
- Individualized eating plan with energy deficit + enhanced physical activity for weight loss
- Behavior modification goals addressing physical activity, calorie restriction, and motivation
- Minimum 5% weight loss required for metabolic improvements; 7–10% for high-risk individuals
- Use of GLP-1 RAs or metabolic surgery for weight loss in type 1 diabetes with obesity
- Person-centered approaches with motivational interviewing for weight discussions
- Counseling on anticipating increased risk of hypoglycemia and reduction in insulin requirements when initiating GLP-1 RA therapy in type 1 diabetes
- Implementation of sick-day rules and ketone monitoring for patients on GLP-1 RA therapy in type 1 diabetes

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Intensive lifestyle intervention programs with frequent follow-up
- Screening for disordered eating behaviors during DSMES/MNT encounters
- Shared decision-making for GLP-1 RA therapy in type 1 diabetes
- Close monitoring of insulin requirements and ketone production with GLP-1 RA use
- Multidisciplinary evaluation for metabolic surgery in type 1 diabetes
- Detailed review of drug side effect profiles before initiating GLP-1 RA or dual GIP/GLP-1 RA therapy in type 1 diabetes
- Exclusion criteria assessment for preexisting gastroparesis, hypoglycemia unawareness, or recent episodes of diabetic/euglycemic ketoacidosis before starting GLP-1 RA therapy

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Rigid meal plans and strict tracking of food intake/body weight in high-risk individuals
- Extrapolating GLP-1 RA dose escalation protocols from type 2 to type 1 diabetes
- Assuming all patients with overweight/obesity want to discuss weight management

**Updated Recommendations:**
- GLP-1 RAs or dual GIP/GLP-1 RAs for weight loss in type 1 diabetes with obesity
- Minimum 5% weight loss threshold for glycemic/lipid/blood pressure benefits
- Culturally tailored interventions for populations with lower traditional intervention efficacy
- Long-term weight loss maintenance interventions with real-world clinic-based follow-up
- Modification of AID settings and mealtime insulin dosing with GLP-1 RA therapy
- Use of dual GIP and GLP-1 receptor agonists for weight loss in type 1 diabetes with obesity
- Consideration of comorbidities such as obstructive sleep apnea, MASH, or coronary artery disease to guide GLP-1 RA selection in type 1 diabetes
