# diabetic autonomic neuropathy
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Exercise safety in diabetic autonomic neuropathy

**Core Recommendations:**
- Cardiac investigation required before initiating physical activity more intense than accustomed
- Monitor for exercise-induced injury due to decreased cardiac responsiveness
- Assess postural hypotension risk before and during exercise
- Evaluate thermoregulation impairment during physical activity
- Screen for hypoglycemia susceptibility during and after exercise
- Assess impaired night vision due to impaired pupillary reaction during physical activity

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Diagnosis of diabetic autonomic neuropathy confirmed
- Cardiac autonomic neuropathy assessment completed
- Baseline cardiovascular risk stratification performed
- Individualized exercise prescription developed
- Continuous glucose monitoring recommended during activity
- Assessment of cardiovascular autonomic neuropathy (CAN) as an independent risk factor for silent myocardial ischemia

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Unsupervised high-intensity exercise programs
- Ignoring postural symptoms during physical activity
- Assuming normal cardiac response to exercise
- Routine exercise without prior autonomic screening
- Standardized exercise protocols without risk stratification

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Cardiac autonomic reflex tests (e.g., heart rate response to standing)
- Continuous glucose monitoring during exercise
- Gradual intensity progression with symptom monitoring
- Blood pressure monitoring before and after exercise
- Thermoregulation assessment in hot/cold environments
