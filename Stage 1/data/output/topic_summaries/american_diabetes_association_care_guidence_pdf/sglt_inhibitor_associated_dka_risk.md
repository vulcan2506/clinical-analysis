# SGLT inhibitor-associated DKA risk
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** SGLT inhibitor-associated DKA risk

**Core Recommendations:**
- SGLT inhibitors are not approved for use in people with type 1 diabetes due to increased DKA risk.
- Educate at-risk individuals about DKA signs, symptoms, mitigation, and management.
- Prescribe accurate tools for ketone measurement in at-risk patients.
- Individuals who have experienced DKA should not be treated with SGLT inhibition.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Risk assessment for DKA in individuals with type 1 or insulin-deficient type 2 diabetes before SGLT inhibitor therapy.
- Monitoring for very-low-carbohydrate eating patterns, prolonged fasting, dehydration, and excessive alcohol intake.
- Patient education on DKA symptoms and management strategies.
- Recognition that up to a third of SGLT inhibitor-associated DKA cases present with glucose levels <200 mg/dL, necessitating specific education on euglycemic presentation.

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Use of SGLT inhibitors in type 1 diabetes without strict risk mitigation protocols.

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Emphasis on ketone measurement tool prescription for at-risk patients.
- Guidance on DKA risk mitigation and management in section 6 of updated guidelines.
