# conflict of interest disclosures
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Conflict of Interest Disclosures in Guideline Development

**Core Recommendations:**
- Committee members disclosed financial or dualities of interest covering the 12 months prior to December 2025
- Disclosures include employment, research grants, speakers bureau, honoraria, ownership interest, consultant/advisory roles, and other dualities
- Research grants and consultant roles span multiple pharmaceutical and medical device companies (e.g., Novo Nordisk, Sanofi, Medtronic, Dexcom)
- Some members hold editorial or board positions in diabetes-related organizations or journals
- Disclosures are systematically documented and published alongside the guideline
- Disclosures explicitly include thresholds for financial conflicts (e.g., *≥$10,000 per year from company to individual, #grant or contract to university or other employer*)
- Some disclosures note editorial roles in journals (e.g., *Editor-in-Chief, BMI Open Diabetes Research and Care*)

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Disclosure of financial conflicts of interest is mandatory for all committee members
- Disclosures must cover the 12 months prior to guideline publication
- Disclosures must include specific categories (e.g., research grants, honoraria, consultancy)

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Inclusion of detailed conflict-of-interest disclosures for all committee members in the published guideline
