# systematic diabetes care improvement
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Systematic diabetes care improvement + team-based care + SDOH integration

**Core Recommendations:**
- Assess glycemic status and prior/current treatments at initial and follow-up visits
- Identify care partners and support systems at visits
- Screen for social determinants of health (food insecurity, housing insecurity, financial barriers, health insurance access, environmental factors, social capital)
- Use interprofessional team-based care models to improve A1C, blood pressure, LDL cholesterol, and ASCVD risk
- Implement quality improvement (QI) initiatives using patient registries, EHR tools, and benchmarking
- Screen patients for financial hardship of treatment, cost-related barriers to medication use, and rationing of essential services due to medical costs
- Assess and address gaps in diabetes care and health outcomes by stratifying clinical quality data by factors such as insurance status, race, ethnicity, preferred language, disability, and SDOH

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patient with type 1 or type 2 diabetes
- Access to primary or specialty care (endocrinology/diabetes care)
- EHR or patient registry for tracking metrics
- Interprofessional team including clinical champion, QI/data specialist, and patient representative
- Insurance coverage or financial support systems to minimize cost barriers
- Screening for financial barriers to care and cost-related medication nonadherence
- Access to community health workers or lay health coaches for self-management support

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Fixed A1C numeric targets without individualized consideration
- Routine use of high-deductible health plans without cost-sharing mitigation
- Exclusion of MASLD/MASH from treatment algorithm decisions
- Lack of SDOH screening during clinical encounters
- Visit-based billing models without value-based payment alignment

**Updated Recommendations:**
- GLP-1 RA with MASH/MASLD benefits for type 2 diabetes with overweight/obesity
- Osteoporosis drug therapy for older adults with T-score ≤-2.5 or -2.0 to -2.5 with fracture risk
- Vaccination recommendations extended to adolescents
- FIB-4 index >2.67 management by liver specialist
- Preventive drug lists with reduced cost-sharing for diabetes medications
- Preventive drug lists that reduce or eliminate out-of-pocket cost sharing for diabetes medications and related therapies
- Capped out-of-pocket payments for insulin at $35 per month for Medicare beneficiaries (Part D Senior Savings Model and Inflation Reduction Act)
