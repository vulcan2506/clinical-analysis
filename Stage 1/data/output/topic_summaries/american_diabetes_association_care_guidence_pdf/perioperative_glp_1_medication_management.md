# perioperative GLP-1 medication management
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Perioperative GLP-1 medication management

**Core Recommendations:**
- GLP-1 RAs may cause delayed gastric emptying, increasing residual gastric content despite fasting
- FDA added warnings about pulmonary aspiration risk during general anesthesia or deep sedation
- Personalized perioperative management is recommended based on drug characteristics, patient factors, and procedure type
- Preoperative gastric ultrasound may be considered to assess gastric contents
- Full stomach precautions and liquid nutrition protocols may be implemented for high-risk patients
- Increased residual gastric content persists despite prolonged fasting (up to 30 days) due to GLP-1 RA's long half-life and delayed gastric emptying effects

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Consider primary indication (diabetes or obesity), glycemic management, drug dose/duration, and gastrointestinal symptoms
- Evaluate type of surgery/procedure, urgency, and anesthesia type
- Assess patient risk for delayed gastric emptying (e.g., nausea, vomiting, dyspepsia)
- Implement alternative glycemic management (e.g., insulin) if GLP-1 RA is held and hyperglycemia is anticipated
- Use gastric ultrasound or other assessments to quantify gastric contents preoperatively
- Assess primary indication (diabetes vs. obesity) as it influences risk stratification and management decisions
- Evaluate urgency of surgery/procedure (elective vs. emergent) to determine appropriateness of personalized vs. conservative approaches

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Routine holding of GLP-1 RAs on the day of procedure for daily doses and 7 days prior for weekly doses
- Fixed withdrawal periods without considering individual patient factors
- One-size-fits-all fasting protocols without personalized risk assessment
- Withholding GLP-1 RAs for 7 days prior to weekly-dose procedures as a fixed protocol

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Personalized perioperative GLP-1 RA management based on comprehensive risk stratification
- Consideration of continuing GLP-1 RAs in low-risk elective surgery patients
- Implementation of liquid nutrition protocols 24 hours pre-procedure for high-risk patients
- Use of gastric ultrasound to quantify residual gastric content preoperatively
- Full stomach precautions for patients with symptoms of delayed gastric emptying
- Consider continuing GLP-1 RAs in low-risk elective surgery patients based on comprehensive risk assessment
