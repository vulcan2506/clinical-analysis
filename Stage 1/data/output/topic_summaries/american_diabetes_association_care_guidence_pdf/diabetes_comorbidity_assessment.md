# diabetes comorbidity assessment
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Comprehensive Diabetes Comorbidity Assessment

**Core Recommendations:**
- Assess risk of diabetes complications including ASCVD, heart failure, and chronic kidney disease
- Evaluate hypoglycemia risk and screen for retinopathy and neuropathy
- Assess for MASLD and MASH alongside standard diabetes complications
- Set individualized goals for A1C, blood glucose, time-in-range, lipids, blood pressure, weight, and physical activity
- Develop therapeutic treatment plans including lifestyle management, pharmacologic therapy, and specialist referrals
- Assess ASCVD and heart failure history as part of comorbidity evaluation

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patient must have a confirmed diagnosis of diabetes
- Clinician must perform evaluation at initial and all follow-up visits
- Patient characteristics must include assessment of age-matched comorbidity risks
- Diagnostic criteria for ASCVD, CKD, and liver disease must be applied per standard staging tables
- Prior treatment steps must include lifestyle management and pharmacologic therapy considerations
- Staging of chronic kidney disease must be performed using standard staging tables (e.g., Table 11.2)

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Inclusion of MASH (metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis) assessment in routine comorbidity screening
- Integration of MASLD (metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease) evaluation into standard care
- Explicit goal setting for time-in-range alongside traditional A1C and blood glucose targets
- Recommendation for weight management with pharmacotherapy or metabolic surgery as appropriate
- Structured assessment of cardiovascular and kidney disease risk factors as distinct therapeutic targets
