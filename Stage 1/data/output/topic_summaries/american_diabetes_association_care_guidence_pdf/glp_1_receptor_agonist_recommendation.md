# GLP-1 receptor agonist recommendation
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** GLP-1 receptor agonist recommendations across comorbidities

**Core Recommendations:**
- Recommend GLP-1 RA or dual GIP/GLP-1 RA for type 2 diabetes with symptomatic HFpEF to reduce heart failure events and symptoms
- Use GLP-1 RA for glycemic management in advanced CKD with demonstrated benefits
- Prefer GLP-1 RA with MASH benefits for type 2 diabetes with biopsy-proven MASH or high liver fibrosis risk
- Consider dual GIP/GLP-1 RA for MASLD or MASH with overweight/obesity for glycemic control
- Offer CGM at diabetes onset and anytime thereafter for adults on insulin or noninsulin therapies with hypoglycemia risk
- Recommend assessment for insulin therapy in individuals on immunotherapy who develop hyperglycemia to prevent diabetic ketoacidosis
- Consider metformin as first-line treatment for hyperglycemia due to mTOR inhibitors or PI3K inhibitors

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Type 2 diabetes diagnosis with symptomatic HFpEF for GLP-1 RA recommendation
- Advanced CKD for GLP-1 RA initiation and continuation
- Biopsy-proven MASH or high liver fibrosis risk for GLP-1 RA preference
- Overweight/obesity with MASLD or MASH for dual GIP/GLP-1 RA consideration
- Insulin therapy or noninsulin therapies with hypoglycemia risk for CGM recommendation
- Presence of symptomatic HFpEF for dual GIP/GLP-1 RA recommendation
- Biopsy-proven MASH or high risk for liver fibrosis for GLP-1 RA preference

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Dual GIP/GLP-1 RA for type 2 diabetes with symptomatic HFpEF
- GLP-1 RA for advanced CKD glycemic management
- GLP-1 RA with MASH benefits for biopsy-proven MASH or high-risk patients
- Dual GIP/GLP-1 RA for MASLD or MASH with overweight/obesity
- CGM at diabetes onset and anytime thereafter for adults on insulin or high-risk noninsulin therapies
