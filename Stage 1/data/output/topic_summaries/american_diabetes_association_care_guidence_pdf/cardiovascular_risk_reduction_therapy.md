# cardiovascular risk reduction therapy
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Cardiovascular risk reduction therapy

**Core Recommendations:**
- Use GLP-1 RA or SGLT2i irrespective of glycemic goal attainment in patients with HF, CKD, established CVD, or multiple risk factors
- Initiate insulin regardless of background therapy if symptoms of hyperglycemia are present or A1C >10% / blood glucose ≥300 mg/dL
- Continue glucose-lowering agents when initiating insulin therapy to maintain ongoing glycemic and metabolic benefits
- Avoid concurrent use of DPP-4 inhibitors with GLP-1 RA or dual GIP/GLP-1 RA due to lack of additional glucose-lowering benefit
- Reevaluate medication plans every 3–6 months to adjust for individualized goals and comorbidities
- Reassess the need for and/or dose of medications with higher hypoglycemia risk (e.g., sulfonylureas, meglitinides, insulin) when initiating a new glucose-lowering medication to minimize hypoglycemia and treatment burden

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patient must have type 2 diabetes with established ASCVD, HF, CKD, or indicators of high CVD risk
- Presence of symptoms of hyperglycemia or very high A1C (>10%) or blood glucose (≥300 mg/dL) for insulin initiation
- Biopsy-proven MASH or high risk for liver fibrosis for consideration of GLP-1 RA or pioglitazone
- Individualized assessment of hypoglycemia risk, treatment burden, and patient preferences
- Absence of contraindications or intolerance to specific glucose-lowering agents
- Presence of symptomatic heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF) for consideration of GLP-1 RA or dual GIP/GLP-1 RA therapy

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Fixed numeric A1C targets without individualization based on patient-specific factors
- Concurrent use of DPP-4 inhibitors with GLP-1 RA or dual GIP/GLP-1 RA
- Delaying treatment modification for adults not meeting individualized treatment goals
- Prioritizing weight and glucose management over mitigation of diabetes-related complications in algorithm design
- Use of sulfonylureas or DPP-4 inhibitors as preferred initial therapy over GLP-1 RA in moderate CVD risk

**Updated Recommendations:**
- GLP-1 RA and SGLT2i recommended independent of A1C attainment for patients with established or high risk of ASCVD, HF, or CKD
- GLP-1 RA preferred over insulin for initial or add-on therapy in adults without severe hyperglycemia or hyperglycemic crisis
- Combination therapy with GLP-1 RA and insulin recommended for greater glycemic effectiveness and reduced hypoglycemia risk
- GLP-1 RA and dual GIP/GLP-1 RA recommended for glycemic management and MASH treatment in patients with biopsy-proven MASH or high risk of liver fibrosis
- Integration of social determinants of health and shared decision-making tools in glucose-lowering management
