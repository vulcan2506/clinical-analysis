# health-promoting behaviors
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Health-promoting behaviors in diabetes self-management

**Core Recommendations:**
- Support glucose monitoring
- Encourage medication adherence
- Promote use of diabetes technologies
- Engage in regular physical activity
- Adopt healthy eating habits
- Facilitating opportunities for social support

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patient willingness to participate in self-management
- Access to diabetes education or certified diabetes care and education specialists (DCES)
- Availability of trained healthcare professionals or community health workers
- Appropriate tools for tracking or self-monitoring health behaviors
- Supportive social environment or opportunities for social support
- Prior training in evidence-based methods (e.g., motivational interviewing) for diabetes care professionals delivering behavior change strategies

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Rigid, one-size-fits-all behavioral interventions
- Overreliance on single-component behavior change strategies
- Exclusive use of financial incentives without evidence-based counseling
- Ignoring patient-specific barriers to behavior change
- Lack of tailoring interventions to individual or population needs

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Multicomponent interventions combining motivational interviewing, goal setting, and problem-solving
- Family-based or multisystem behavioral interventions for children and adolescents
- Digital health tools for delivering behavior change strategies
- Training for diabetes care professionals in evidence-based methods like motivational interviewing
- Use of behavioral economics approaches (e.g., social norms messaging) for short-term motivation
- Use of behavioral activation as an evidence-based behavioral strategy
