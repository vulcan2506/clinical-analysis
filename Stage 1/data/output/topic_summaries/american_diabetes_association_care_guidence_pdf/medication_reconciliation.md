# medication reconciliation
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** medication reconciliation at care transitions

**Core Recommendations:**
- Cross-check home and hospital medications to ensure safety of new and old prescriptions
- Verify no chronic medications are stopped during transitions
- Detect and resolve duplication of medications under different brand names
- Fill and review new or changed prescriptions with the patient and care partners at or before discharge

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patient must have an active medication list from home and hospital settings
- Access to patient's current medication history and discharge orders
- Presence of care partners or patient at discharge for review
- Patient's medication list must include dosage, frequency, route, and indication for each medication
