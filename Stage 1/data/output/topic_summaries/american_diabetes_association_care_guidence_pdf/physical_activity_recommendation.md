# Physical Activity Recommendation
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Physical Activity Recommendation

**Core Recommendations:**
- Encourage ≥150 min/week of moderate-intensity physical activity (uses large muscle groups, rhythmic in nature) OR ≥75 min/week vigorous-intensity activity
- Spread activity over ≥3 days/week with no more than 2 consecutive days of inactivity
- Supplement with 2–3 resistance, flexibility, and/or balance sessions weekly
- As little as 30 min/week of moderate-intensity activity improves metabolic profiles
- Include neuromotor exercises (e.g., tai chi, yoga) for older adults or those with balance impairments to reduce fall risk

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Assess patient readiness for physical activity using validated tools (e.g., PAR-Q+) before initiating recommendations

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Emphasize gradual progression of activity intensity and duration for sedentary individuals to minimize injury risk
