# DKA and HHS management
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** DKA and HHS management

**Core Recommendations:**
- Administer intravenous fluids, insulin, and electrolytes with close monitoring during treatment
- Ensure timely transition to maintenance subcutaneous insulin administration
- Identify and treat the precipitating cause of DKA or HHS
- Use clinical judgment for DKA resolution criteria (venous pH >7.3 or bicarbonate >18 mmol/L and plasma/capillary ketones <0.6 mmol/L)
- Use clinical judgment for HHS resolution criteria (serum osmolality <300 mOsm/kg, urine output >0.5 mL/kg/h, and glucose <250 mg/dL)
- Use calculated serum osmolality (2[Na+] + glucose/18 + BUN/2.8) for HHS resolution assessment

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Meet diagnostic criteria for DKA or HHS (Table 16.1)
- Confirm DKA diagnosis with either hyperglycemia or prior history of diabetes (including euglycemic DKA cases)
- Assess for precipitating factors such as reduced food intake, pregnancy, alcohol use, liver failure, or SGLT2 inhibitor therapy
- Monitor for mixed DKA-HHS presentations
- Evaluate patient-specific risk factors for hyperglycemic crises (type 1 or type 2 diabetes)
- Confirm DKA diagnosis with prior history of diabetes even in the absence of current hyperglycemia (euglycemic DKA)

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Withholding insulin until glucose stops dropping with fluid administration alone
- Withholding insulin until glucose stabilizes with fluid therapy alone (explicitly discouraged in favor of early insulin initiation)

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Inclusion of euglycemic DKA in diagnostic considerations (plasma glucose <200 mg/dL [<11.1 mmol/L])
- Emphasis on individualized discharge education for recognition, prevention, and management of DKA/HHS
- Use of clinical judgment over strict numeric thresholds for discharge criteria
- Recognition of rising hyperglycemic crisis rates in both type 1 and type 2 diabetes
- Include calculated serum osmolality in HHS resolution criteria alongside urine output and glucose thresholds
