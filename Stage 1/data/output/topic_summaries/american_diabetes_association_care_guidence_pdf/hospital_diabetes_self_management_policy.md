# hospital diabetes self-management policy
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Hospital diabetes self-management policy

**Core Recommendations:**
- Diabetes self-management in the hospital may be appropriate for select individuals who wish to continue self-care while acutely ill.
- Candidates must successfully perform diabetes self-management at home and have cognitive/physical skills not compromised by acute illness or delirium.
- Patients must have adequate oral intake and stable insulin requirements.
- Daily agreement between the person with diabetes and care team is required to continue self-management during hospitalization.
- Hospital policies may allow personal medication use (home or hospital-dispensed) under pharmacy verification and prescriber order, supervised by a registered nurse.
- Children with parental supervision may be considered for diabetes self-management in the hospital if they meet all other criteria.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Successful diabetes self-management at home.
- Cognitive and physical ability to self-administer insulin and perform glucose monitoring.
- Adequate oral intake.
- Stable insulin requirements.
- Daily reassessment by care team to confirm appropriateness of self-management.
- Consensus-based guidelines must be followed for insulin pump or CGM device use during hospitalization, including protocols for changing insulin infusion sites and CGM glucose sensors.

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Guidelines for wearing insulin pumps or CGM devices during hospitalization, including insulin infusion site and CGM sensor changes.
- Pharmacy exception process for personal medication use in the hospital.
- Requirement for prescriber order and registered nurse supervision for self-administration of medications.
- Policy must include a pharmacy exception process for personal medication use on a case-by-case basis, determined in consultation with the care team.
