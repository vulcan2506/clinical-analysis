# SGLT2 inhibitor therapy
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** SGLT2 inhibitor therapy

**Core Recommendations:**
- Administered orally, convenient for older adults with diabetes
- Demonstrates cardiovascular benefits in patients with established ASCVD
- Beneficial for people with heart failure and slows progression of chronic kidney disease
- Generally well tolerated among older adults, but requires thoughtful selection to avoid adverse effects
- May cause clinically significant volume depletion, higher risk in frail or orthostatic-prone older adults

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Established atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease (ASCVD) for cardiovascular benefits
- Presence of heart failure or chronic kidney disease for therapeutic indication
- Assessment of frailty or orthostatic risk before initiation in older adults
- Evaluation for recurrent or severe urinary tract infections before use
- Monitoring for genital mycotic infections and urinary incontinence symptoms

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Use of SGLT2 inhibitors without consideration of volume depletion risk in frail older adults
- Ignoring urinary incontinence symptoms before and after initiation
- Overlooking euglycemic diabetic ketoacidosis risk in multimorbid or PALTC residents

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Stratified analyses showing older adults may have similar or greater benefits than younger people
- Increased awareness of genital mycotic infections, especially in women
- Small increase in urinary tract infections requiring caution in susceptible individuals
- Euglycemic diabetic ketoacidosis as a rare but serious risk, particularly with infection triggers
- Oral administration convenience for older adults with diabetes
