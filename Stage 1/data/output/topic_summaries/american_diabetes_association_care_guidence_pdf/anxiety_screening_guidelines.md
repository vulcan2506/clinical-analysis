# anxiety screening guidelines
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Anxiety and fear of hypoglycemia screening in diabetes

**Core Recommendations:**
- Screen for anxiety symptoms at least annually in people with diabetes
- Screen individuals at high risk for hypoglycemia or with severe/frequent hypoglycemia for fear of hypoglycemia at least annually and when clinically appropriate
- Address anxiety symptoms within the scope of practice or refer to a qualified behavioral health professional if symptoms interfere with diabetes self-management or quality of life
- Conduct screening for both diabetes distress and anxiety at least annually in people with diabetes, followed by further clinical assessment to ensure accurate identification

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Anxiety symptoms may overlap with diabetes distress and/or hyperglycemia symptoms, requiring careful differentiation
- History of nocturnal hypoglycemia, co-occurring psychological concerns, and sleep disturbances contribute to fear of hypoglycemia
- Excessive diabetes self-management behaviors beyond prescribed needs may indicate underlying anxiety
- Prioritize screening for anxiety in individuals with a history of nocturnal hypoglycemia, co-occurring psychological concerns, or sleep disturbances
- Conduct further clinical assessment to differentiate anxiety from diabetes distress or hyperglycemia symptoms when overlapping symptoms are present

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Annual screening for fear of hypoglycemia in high-risk individuals or those with severe/frequent hypoglycemia
- Integration of anxiety and diabetes distress screening into routine diabetes care
- Refer individuals with fear of hypoglycemia to a trained healthcare professional for evidence-based intervention (e.g., cognitive behavioral therapy)
