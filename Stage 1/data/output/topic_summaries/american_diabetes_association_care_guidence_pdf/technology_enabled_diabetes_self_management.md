# technology-enabled diabetes self-management
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Technology-enabled diabetes self-management

**Core Recommendations:**
- Two-way communication between person with diabetes and healthcare team
- Individualized feedback tailored to person's needs
- Use of person-generated health data for self-management decisions
- Education integrated with technology use
- Exploration of virtual environments for DSMES with gamification principles
- Integration of person-generated health data into electronic health records (EHRs) for seamless care coordination
- Use of remote monitoring by healthcare teams to proactively adjust therapy based on real-time data

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Person with diabetes must be engaged in self-management decisions
- Healthcare team must provide individualized feedback
- Access to diabetes technologies (e.g., CGM, AID systems, connected glucose meters)
- Education on technology use and self-management
- Systematic approach for technology assessment and integration into care plan
- Healthcare team must have access to training on interpreting person-generated health data and providing individualized feedback
- Patient must demonstrate willingness and capability to engage with technology-enabled self-management tools

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Traditional DSMES without technology integration
- Non-personalized feedback approaches
- Rigid, non-adaptive diabetes care plans
- Routine reliance on self-reported glucose logs without technology integration for clinical decision-making

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Virtual DSMES environments with avatar-based interaction
- Reimbursement policies to increase DSMES access and use
- CGM combined with individualized DSMES or behavioral interventions
- DSMES plus intermittently scanned CGM (isCGM) for improved time in range
- Systematic technology assessment and integration into diabetes care plans
- Standardized protocols for remote monitoring and virtual DSMES delivery in clinical workflows
- Requirements for interoperability between diabetes technologies and EHR systems
