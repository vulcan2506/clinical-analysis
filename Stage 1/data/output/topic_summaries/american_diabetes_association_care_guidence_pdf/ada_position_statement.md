# ADA position statement
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** ADA position statement characteristics

**Core Recommendations:**
- ADA statements represent ADA's point of view or position on advocacy, policy, economic, or medical issues related to diabetes
- Statements undergo formal review including external peer review
- Review involves ADA Professional Practice Committee for Diabetes
- Review includes ADA clinical leadership and ADA scientific team
- Board of Directors review is conducted as warranted
