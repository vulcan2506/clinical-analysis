# hyperkalemia prevalence in CKD
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** hyperkalemia prevalence in CKD

**Clinical Actions:**
- Potassium abnormalities predispose to abnormal cardiac conduction and arrhythmias.
- Decreased GFR is generally associated with increased potassium concentration.
- Hyperkalemia is uncommon when eGFR >60 mL/min/1.73m².
- Hyperkalemia prevalence increases with lower GFR.
- Hyperkalemia is defined as serum potassium >5.0 mmol/L.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- eGFR ≤60 mL/min/1.73m² for significant hyperkalemia risk.
