# pediatric CKD progression risk calculator
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** pediatric CKD progression risk calculator

**Clinical Actions:**
- Utilizes a risk calculator derived from the CKiD group (2015) incorporating age, sex, glomerular versus nonglomerular disease, eGFR, hypertension, and laboratory parameters.
- Employs a combined risk calculator integrating CKiD data with the ESCAPE trial results, utilizing diagnosis, eGFR, and proteinuria.
- Validates the 4-value KFRE in the CKiD cohort, noting good discrimination but identifying incongruence between predicted and observed outcomes in higher predicted risk groups.
- Adopts a holistic approach for sex and gender considerations in risk equations, accounting for sex assigned at birth, sex hormone milieu, and gender identity.
- Integrates shared decision-making with the person with CKD when determining which sex/gender parameters to use in risk assessment.
- Provides online calculators for both CKiD-derived and ESCAPE-integrated risk models with direct access links (kidney.org or ckdprognosis.com)

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Availability of patient age, sex, and disease classification (glomerular vs nonglomerular).
- Measurement of eGFR, hypertension status, and relevant laboratory parameters.
- Access to specific online calculators (kidney.org or ckdprognosis.com).
- Clinical assessment of sex hormone milieu and gender identity.
- Engagement in shared decision-making with the patient.
- Access to proteinuria measurement (e.g., urine protein-to-creatinine ratio) for the ESCAPE-integrated calculator
