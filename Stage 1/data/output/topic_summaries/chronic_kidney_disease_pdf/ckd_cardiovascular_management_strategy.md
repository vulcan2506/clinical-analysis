# CKD cardiovascular management strategy
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** CKD cardiovascular management strategy for stable ischemic heart disease

**Clinical Actions:**
- Initial conservative approach with intensive medical therapy is suggested as an appropriate alternative to invasive strategy for stable stress-test confirmed ischemic heart disease in CKD
- Invasive strategy may be considered for patients with unacceptable angina symptoms, left ventricular systolic dysfunction attributable to ischemia, or left main disease
- Antianginal benefits of invasive strategy have not been demonstrated in CKD populations despite evidence in general populations
- Coronary revascularization remains important for angina relief in select CKD patients
- Intensive medical therapy should include full guideline-directed medical therapy for cardiovascular risk reduction

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Stable stress-test confirmed ischemic heart disease
- Exclusion of patients with severe angina symptoms, left ventricular dysfunction (EF <35%), or left main stem disease
- Consideration of CKD-specific factors (e.g., coronary calcification, microvascular disease) affecting revascularization feasibility
- Assessment of symptom control and patient preferences
- Implementation of strategies to reduce contrast-induced AKI risk if invasive strategy is pursued
- Exclusion of patients on dialysis or who have received a kidney transplant from the definitive conservative recommendation evidence base
- Requirement for satisfactory symptom control with intensive medical therapy to justify the initial conservative approach
