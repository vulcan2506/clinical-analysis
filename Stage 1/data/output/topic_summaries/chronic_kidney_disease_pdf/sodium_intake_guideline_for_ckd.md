# sodium intake guideline for CKD
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** sodium intake guideline for CKD

**Clinical Actions:**
- Recommend sodium restriction to people with CKD (excluding salt-wasting disease, malnutrition, or extreme heat exposure) to lower BP and albuminuria.
- Follow age-based Recommended Daily Intake for children with CKD and elevated BP (>90th percentile for age, sex, and height).
- Adjust WHO adult sodium intake (<2 g/d) downward for children based on energy requirements relative to adults.
- Supplement sodium intake in children with CKD and tubular conditions predisposing to sodium loss.
- Recommend sodium restriction in combination with pharmacological strategies to minimize risk of kidney disease progression and cardiovascular disease in CKD patients.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Exclude patients with salt-wasting kidney disease, malnutrition, or extreme climatic conditions from sodium restriction.
- Counsel children with CKD and elevated BP using age-based Recommended Daily Intake.
- Identify children born with low birth weight (<2.5 kg) as higher risk for hypertension and salt sensitivity.
- Individualize sodium restriction in patients with salt-wasting kidney disease, malnutrition, or extreme heat exposure to avoid exacerbating underlying conditions.
