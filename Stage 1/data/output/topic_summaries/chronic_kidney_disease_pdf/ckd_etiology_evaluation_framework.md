# CKD etiology evaluation framework
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** CKD etiology evaluation framework

**Clinical Actions:**
- Establish CKD cause using clinical context, personal/family history, social/environmental factors, medications, physical examination, laboratory measures, imaging, genetic and pathologic diagnosis
- Select diagnostic tests based on pretest probability of specific diagnosis informed by clinical presentation
- Tailor evaluation of cause based on available resources (e.g., urine protein reagent strip testing instead of ACR)
- Use genetic testing when high prevalence of monogenic subtypes, early CKD onset, syndromic features, consanguinity, or targeted treatment possibility exists
- Prefer kidney biopsy for exact diagnosis, treatment planning, and assessing disease activity/chronicity when clinically indicated
- Incorporate molecular diagnostics (e.g., gene panels, transcriptomics) in kidney biopsy when clinically indicated to enable precision nephrology and personalized treatment planning
- Recognize monoclonal gammopathy of renal significance (MGRS) in serologic and urine tests as a distinct entity requiring targeted evaluation even in the absence of multiple myeloma

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Pretest probability assessment of specific diagnosis based on clinical presentation
- Access to specialized kidney care services for advanced evaluations (e.g., biopsy, genetic testing)
- Educated workforce with expertise in kidney genetics and genomics for test interpretation
- Access to genetic counseling and medical genetics for psychosocial support and cost limitation
- Balanced benefits/harms consideration for invasive procedures like kidney biopsy
- Access to computational research infrastructure and bioinformatics support for interpretation of genetic and molecular diagnostic tests
- Availability of specialized kidney care services for advanced evaluations, including genetic testing and kidney biopsy, as part of tailored evaluation based on resource constraints
