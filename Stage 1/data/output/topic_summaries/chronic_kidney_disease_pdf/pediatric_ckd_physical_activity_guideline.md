# pediatric CKD physical activity guideline
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** pediatric CKD physical activity and sedentary behavior management

**Clinical Actions:**
- Encourage children with CKD to undertake physical activity aiming for WHO-advised levels (≥60 minutes daily for ages 5–17; 180 minutes daily for ages 1–5).
- Include aerobic activities as well as activities that strengthen muscle and bone in the physical activity regimen.
- Achieve a healthy weight through physical activity.
- Limit sedentary time, particularly screen time, to less than 2 hours per day on school days.
- Avoid restraining young children (ages 1–5) in a stroller or carrier for more than 60 minutes at a time.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patient must be a child with CKD.
- Age-specific targets apply: ages 5–17 require ≥60 minutes daily; ages 1–5 require 180 minutes daily.
