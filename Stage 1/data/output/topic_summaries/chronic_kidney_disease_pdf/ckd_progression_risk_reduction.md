# CKD progression risk reduction
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** SGLT2i for CKD progression risk reduction

**Clinical Actions:**
- SGLT2 inhibitors (SGLT2i) are recommended for reducing CKD progression risk in patients with diabetes and albuminuria, based on strong evidence from large trials and meta-analyses.
- Evidence for SGLT2i benefits in people without diabetes and with low albuminuria is limited but extrapolated from eGFR slope analyses and short-term trials, suggesting potential long-term benefits.
- SGLT2i are not currently recommended for pediatric CKD patients due to lack of clinical trial data, though pharmacokinetic/pharmacodynamic data in children with diabetes suggest similar profiles to adults.
- Theoretical concern that SGLT2i's glycosuric effect may lead to negative calorie balance, potentially interfering with optimal growth in small children with underlying growth retardation.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patients with diabetes and albuminuria should be considered for SGLT2i therapy to reduce CKD progression risk.
- For patients without diabetes, SGLT2i use is based on limited evidence and should be individualized with caution.
- Pediatric use of SGLT2i requires further clinical trials to establish safety and efficacy in CKD.
- Pediatric dosing of SGLT2i should be modeled based on pharmacokinetic/pharmacodynamic data, particularly for smaller children.
