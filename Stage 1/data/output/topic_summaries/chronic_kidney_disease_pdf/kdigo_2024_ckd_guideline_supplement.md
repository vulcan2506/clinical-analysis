# KDIGO 2024 CKD guideline supplement
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** KDIGO 2024 CKD Guideline Supplement

**Clinical Actions:**
- Provides comprehensive clinical practice guidelines for the evaluation and management of chronic kidney disease
- Updates recommendations based on the latest professional literature and clinical evidence
- Serves as a sponsored supplement to the main KDIGO guidelines with specific focus on CKD management

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Clinical decisions must reflect the opinions of the authors rather than necessarily reflecting the International Society of Nephrology
- Dosages and methods of use may reflect clinical experience or professional literature rather than standardized protocols
- In vitro and animal data do not necessarily correlate with clinical results in humans
