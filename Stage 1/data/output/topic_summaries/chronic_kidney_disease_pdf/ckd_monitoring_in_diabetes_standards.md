# CKD monitoring in diabetes standards
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** CKD monitoring frequency in diabetes

**Clinical Actions:**
- Monitoring frequency should increase with higher CKD stages (e.g., every 6 weeks for CKD G5, every 3 months for CKD G4).
- More frequent monitoring is indicated for patients with higher risk of disease progression.
- Monitoring should be adjusted based on changing clinical status, intercurrent events, and therapeutic interventions.
- Progression risk may vary by CKD etiology within the same GFR and albuminuria/proteinuria stage.
- Monitoring frequency should account for albuminuria trends, including changes in albuminuria/proteinuria levels as a key driver for adjusting monitoring intervals.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patients must have confirmed CKD diagnosis (G1–G5) with documented GFR and albuminuria/proteinuria levels.
- Baseline monitoring frequency should align with CKD stage (e.g., annual for G1–G2, every 6 months for G3).
- Documentation of CKD etiology is required to refine progression risk stratification within the same GFR and albuminuria/proteinuria stage.
