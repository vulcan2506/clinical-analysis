# blood pressure management guideline
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** blood pressure management in CKD

**Clinical Actions:**
- Individualized BP targets based on age, CVD, comorbidities, CKD progression risk, and treatment tolerance.
- Target systolic BP (SBP) <120 mmHg (when tolerated) using standardized office BP measurement (Recommendation 3.4.1).
- Use home-based or telemonitoring for practical BP monitoring and medication titration.
- In children with CKD, target 24-hour mean arterial pressure (MAP) ≤50th percentile for age/sex/height via ABPM (Recommendation 3.4.2).
- In children without ABPM, target manual auscultatory office SBP at 50th–75th percentile for age/sex/height (Practice Point 3.4.3).
- Weighing benefits of BP control against risks of postural hypotension, falls, and fractures in frail patients or those with limited life expectancy when setting SBP targets.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Standardized office BP measurement for SBP <120 mmHg target.
- Home-based or telemonitoring for practical BP management.
- ABPM for pediatric MAP targets; manual auscultatory office BP if ABPM unavailable.
- Consider frailty, life expectancy, and postural hypotension risks when setting BP targets.
- Exclusion of diabetes in SPRINT trial; cardiovascular benefits in diabetes supported by meta-analysis.
- Protocol-driven standardized setting for manual auscultatory office BP measurements in children when ABPM is unavailable.
