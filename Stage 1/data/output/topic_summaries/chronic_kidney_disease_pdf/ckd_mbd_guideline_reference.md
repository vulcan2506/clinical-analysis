# CKD-MBD guideline reference
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** CKD-MBD guideline reference and management

**Clinical Actions:**
- Treatments for CKD-MBD in CKD G3a–G5 should be based on serial assessments of phosphate, calcium, and PTH levels considered together.
- Higher serum phosphate concentrations are associated with mortality and vascular calcification.
- Low-phosphorus diets and phosphate binders are used to lower serum phosphate and reduce long-term CKD-MBD complications.
- Vitamin D replacement and calcimimetics are common strategies to control PTH and maintain calcium within normal range, though evidence suggests no benefit on clinical outcomes.
- Experimental data suggest serum phosphate concentration is directly related to bone disease, vascular calcification, and cardiovascular disease (CVD).
- Low-phosphorus diets and phosphate binders are used to lower serum phosphate, though more research is needed to fully understand their disease-modifying impact.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Serial assessments of phosphate, calcium, and PTH levels are required for treatment decisions in CKD G3a–G5.
- CKD-MBD management should consider abnormalities in serum calcium, phosphate, vitamin D metabolites, PTH, and FGF-23.
- CKD-MBD management should consider abnormalities in serum calcium, phosphate, vitamin D metabolites, PTH, and FGF-23, detectable early in CKD progression.
