# pediatric and adult BP guidance
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** pediatric and adult BP guidance in CKD

**Clinical Actions:**
- Adults with high BP and CKD should be treated with a target systolic blood pressure (SBP) of <120 mm Hg, when tolerated, using standardized office BP measurement.
- Consider less intensive BP-lowering therapy in people with frailty, high risk of falls and fractures, very limited life expectancy, or symptomatic postural hypotension.
- In children with CKD, 24-hour mean arterial pressure (MAP) by ambulatory blood pressure monitoring (ABPM) should be lowered to ≤50th percentile for age, sex, and height.
- Monitor BP once a year with ABPM and every 3–6 months with standardized auscultatory office BP in children with CKD.
- In children with CKD, when ABPM is not available, target manual auscultatory office SBP of 50th–75th percentile for age, sex, and height unless limited by signs or symptoms of hypotension.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Standardized office BP measurement for adults.
- 24-hour mean arterial pressure (MAP) by ambulatory blood pressure monitoring (ABPM) for children.
- Protocol-driven standardized setting for manual auscultatory office BP in children when ABPM is unavailable.
- Standardized office BP measurement must be used for adults with high BP and CKD.
- Manual auscultatory office SBP in children must be obtained in a protocol-driven standardized setting when ABPM is unavailable.
