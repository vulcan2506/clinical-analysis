# SGLT2 inhibitor therapy
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** SGLT2 inhibitor therapy in CKD with or without diabetes

**Clinical Actions:**
- SGLT2 inhibitors are recommended for adults with CKD and eGFR ≥20 ml/min/1.73 m², regardless of diabetes status.
- Initiation does not require alteration of CKD monitoring frequency.
- Reversible eGFR dip on initiation is not an indication to discontinue therapy.
- SGLT2 inhibitors reduce risk of kidney failure, AKI, cardiovascular death, and hospitalization for heart failure.
- SGLT2 inhibitors favorably reduce BP, uric acid, fluid overload, and serious hyperkalemia.
- SGLT2 inhibitors reduce the risk of hospitalization from any cause in people with CKD.
- SGLT2 inhibitors reduce the risk of myocardial infarction in people with CKD.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- eGFR ≥20 ml/min/1.73 m² with urine ACR ≥200 mg/g (≥20 mg/mmol), or
- heart failure irrespective of albuminuria level, or
- CKD with high risk of progression (e.g., albuminuria or reduced eGFR).
- No need for routine post-initiation blood test rechecks in adults with CKD.
- Exclusion of adults with polycystic kidney disease from large trial evidence.
- Consideration of initiating SGLT2i when eGFR falls below 20 ml/min per 1.73 m² and continuing until initiation of kidney replacement therapy (KRT), as demonstrated in large CKD population RCTs.
- No significant subgroup interaction by primary kidney diagnosis for kidney disease progression outcomes with SGLT2i use.
