# graded clinical recommendation framework
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** graded clinical recommendation framework

**Clinical Actions:**
- Strength of recommendation is indicated as Level 1 ('We recommend') or Level 2 ('We suggest')
- Certainty of supporting evidence is shown as A, B, C, or D
- Level 1 implies most patients would want the action and supports policy development
- Level 2 implies varying patient preferences requiring shared decision-making and stakeholder debate
- Practice points are consensus-based expert judgments not graded but used for implementation guidance
- Practice points are issued when a clinical question lacks a systematic review or for 'good practice statements' where the alternative is considered absurd

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Systematic review must be performed to generate a graded recommendation
- Clinical question must have sufficient evidence to assign certainty levels A-D
- Clinicians must consider patient values and preferences for Level 2 recommendations
- Practice points are used when no systematic review exists or for standard care frequency
- Policy implications differ based on whether the recommendation is Level 1 or Level 2
- Practice points must be considered as expert guidance and are not mandatory for implementation
