# CKD eGFR estimation guideline update
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** CKD eGFR estimation guideline update

**Clinical Actions:**
- Report eGFR using validated equations (CKD-EPI, EKFC, or region-specific modifications) with preference for creatinine-cystatin C combined equations (eGFRcr-cys) for improved accuracy.
- Avoid race-based coefficients in GFR estimation equations (e.g., 2021 CKD-EPI creatinine equation excludes Black race term).
- Use population-specific Q-values for creatinine-based EKFC equations in regional implementations.
- Average creatinine and cystatin C-based estimates (eGFRcr-cys) to enhance precision in GFR estimation.
- Prefer equations validated in the target population (e.g., EKFC for White Europeans, Black Europeans, or Black Africans).
- Use age-adjusted Q-values for creatinine-based EKFC equations to extend applicability to children.
- Average creatinine and cystatin C-based estimates (eGFRcr-cys) to obtain the most accurate GFR estimate, as recommended by EKFC.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Validation of GFR estimating equations against measured GFR (mGFR) in the target population.
- Use of high-performance metrics (e.g., P30 accuracy) to compare equations.
- Adherence to population-specific Q-values for creatinine-based EKFC equations.
- Consideration of local validation studies when selecting equations for regional use.
- Prioritize combined filtration marker equations (eGFRcr-cys) for critical decisions.
- Local regions must specify population-specific Q-values for creatinine-based EKFC equations before implementation in other regions.
- Validation studies comparing equations against measured GFR (mGFR) are required in the population of interest to support equation selection.
