# sodium intake counseling
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** sodium intake counseling in CKD

**Clinical Actions:**
- Dietary sodium restriction is usually not appropriate for patients with sodium-wasting nephropathy
- Follow age-based Recommended Daily Intake when counseling about sodium intake for children with CKD who have systolic and/or diastolic blood pressure >90th percentile for age, sex, and height

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Sodium restriction is contraindicated in patients with sodium-wasting nephropathy due to risk of volume depletion, hypotension, and accelerated kidney function decline
