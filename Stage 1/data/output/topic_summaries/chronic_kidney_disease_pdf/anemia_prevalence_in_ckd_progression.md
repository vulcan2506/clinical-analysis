# anemia prevalence in CKD progression
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** anemia prevalence in CKD progression

**Clinical Actions:**
- Mean hemoglobin is lower in adults with eGFR <60 mL/min/1.73m² compared to healthy adults, with progressive decline as GFR decreases.
- Prevalence of anemia (Hb <12 g/dL in men; <11 g/dL in women) increases with CKD stage: 14.9% (with diabetes) and 11.5% (without diabetes) in CKD G3, A1 vs. 60.7% and 57.4% in CKD G5, A3.
- Physiologic anemia in pregnancy may not require treatment, but treatment thresholds remain unclear and require clinical judgment.
- Anemia prevalence is higher in high-risk populations (e.g., those with diabetes) compared to the general population at equivalent CKD stages.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Anemia diagnosis requires hemoglobin thresholds: <12 g/dL in men and <11 g/dL in women.
- eGFR <60 mL/min/1.73m² is a prerequisite for anemia evaluation in CKD.
- Anemia evaluation in CKD requires confirmation of eGFR <60 mL/min/1.73m² prior to initiating diagnostic workup for anemia.
