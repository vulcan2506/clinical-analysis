# potassium-binding resin pharmacology
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** potassium-binding resin pharmacology

**Clinical Actions:**
- Sodium-potassium exchange resin (SPS) or calcium-potassium exchange resin (CPS) for potassium binding
- Calcium-potassium exchange polymer (Patiromer) for potassium binding
- Crystalline compound (SZC) traps K+ in exchange for hydrogen and sodium cations
- GI side effects are common with potassium-binding resins
- Potassium-binding resins are used for nonemergent hyperkalemia management
- Patiromer binds potassium in exchange for calcium ions, not sodium
- SZC’s potassium trapping occurs via a crystalline lattice structure with high selectivity for potassium

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Hyperkalemia diagnosis must be confirmed before initiating potassium-binding resins
- Assess patient's baseline potassium levels and renal function prior to use
- Monitor for GI adverse effects during therapy
- Consider drug interactions with other medications
- Evaluate patient's volume status and need for sodium or calcium exchange
- Assess patient’s baseline magnesium and calcium levels prior to initiating calcium-based potassium-binding resins (e.g., patiromer)
- Monitor for hypercalcemia in patients receiving calcium-based resins (patiromer) or calcium supplements
