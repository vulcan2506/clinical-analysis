# actionable genetic conditions
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** actionable genetic conditions in CKD

**Clinical Actions:**
- Identify glomerular disease due to mutations in Alport genes (COL4A3/4/5)
- Screen for conditions at risk for recurrence after kidney transplantation (e.g., aHUS, primary hyperoxaluria, APRT deficiency)
- Perform specific screening for extrarenal manifestations (e.g., intracranial aneurysms in ADPKD, renal cell carcinoma in FLCN)
- Incorporate genetic testing for reproductive counseling (e.g., prenatal/preimplantation diagnosis)
- Perform genetic testing for conditions with high recurrence risk post-transplant (e.g., CFH/CFI/C3 mutations in aHUS) to guide preemptive transplant strategies

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Genetic testing must be supported by evidence-based recommendations for clinical action
- Collaboration between nephrologists, geneticists, and genetic counselors for optimal implementation
- Standardized protocols for genetic testing and interpretation within a health system
- Shared decision-making with patients regarding the utility and risks of genetic testing
- Standardized operating protocols for kidney biopsy must include ultrasound guidance to minimize complications
