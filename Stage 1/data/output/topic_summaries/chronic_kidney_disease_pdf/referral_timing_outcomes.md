# referral timing outcomes
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** referral timing outcomes

**Clinical Actions:**
- Early referral significantly increases the likelihood of receiving permanent vascular access (RR: 3.22).
- Early referral increases the likelihood of initiating kidney replacement therapy with peritoneal dialysis (RR: 1.74).
- Early referral reduces three-month mortality (OR: 0.61).
- Early integration into nephrology services ensures optimal management of pediatric complications like growth restriction.
- Early referral promotes access to preemptive transplantation, which is the preferred kidney replacement therapy for children.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patient must have known or suspected CKD.
- Patient must be at risk of CKD as outlined in the guideline.
