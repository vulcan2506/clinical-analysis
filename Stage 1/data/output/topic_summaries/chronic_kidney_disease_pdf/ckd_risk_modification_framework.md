# CKD risk modification framework
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** CKD risk modification framework

**Clinical Actions:**
- Target underlying pathophysiology to reduce CKD progression risk and improve CKD manifestations and outcomes
- Provide safe and cost-effective therapies to achieve CKD treatment and risk modification
- Minimize access limitations for people with CKD to improve quality of life and healthcare system efficiency
- Identify at-risk individuals early in CKD course to maximize treatment benefits
- Adopt holistic management of CKD risks including BP, diabetes, lipids, anemia, and CKD-MBD

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Evidence-based guidelines for holistic management of CKD risks
- Previously published KDIGO guidelines for BP, diabetes, lipids, anemia, and CKD-MBD in CKD
- Laboratory monitoring of bicarbonate, potassium, and uric acid across CKD stages
