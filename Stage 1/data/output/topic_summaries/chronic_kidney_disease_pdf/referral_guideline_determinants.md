# referral guideline determinants
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** referral guideline determinants and implementation

**Clinical Actions:**
- Identify patient-related factors such as age, race, and comorbid illness to determine referral needs.
- Identify healthcare system-related factors including health insurance status, type of referring physician, and type of referring center.
- Implement local initiatives in conjunction with primary care providers to improve referral appropriateness and quality.
- Utilize a checklist for goal-directed care in CKD to support the referral process.
- Coordinate national policy and practice changes to improve outcomes regardless of resource levels.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Assessment of patient age and race.
- Evaluation of comorbid illness status.
- Verification of health insurance status.
- Identification of the type of referring physician.
- Identification of the type of referring center.
