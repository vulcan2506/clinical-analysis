# urine protein measurement unit conversion
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** urine protein measurement unit conversion

**Clinical Actions:**
- Conventional unit × conversion factor = SI unit
- Use provided conversion factors for accurate unit conversion between conventional and SI units
- Verify automated EHR conversions for unit consistency when interpreting proteinuria trends over time

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Apply conversion factors to albumin-to-creatinine ratio (ACR) for standardized reporting
- Convert calcium and creatinine measurements to SI units for consistency in clinical practice
- Ensure consistent unit reporting when monitoring proteinuria to avoid false interpretation of longitudinal trends
