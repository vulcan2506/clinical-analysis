# CKD risk stratification refinement
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** CKD risk stratification refinement using eGFRcr-cys

**Clinical Actions:**
- Replace creatinine-based eGFR (eGFRcr) with combined creatinine and cystatin C-based eGFR (eGFRcr-cys) for risk stratification to improve accuracy.
- Recognize that eGFRcr-cys demonstrates more linear associations with adverse outcomes compared to the J-shaped association observed with eGFRcr.
- Reclassify risk categories where eGFRcr-cys indicates higher risk than eGFRcr, such as moving patients with eGFR 45–59 mL/min/1.73m² and ACR <10 mg/g to higher risk groups.
- Utilize eGFRcr-cys to reduce misclassification of risk in patients with altered muscle mass or creatinine secretion issues.
- Apply multivariable-adjusted analyses including age, sex, smoking, blood pressure, lipids, BMI, and comorbidities to refine individual risk predictions.
- Use spline analyses to depict continuous risk relationships between eGFRcr-cys and complications, highlighting the linear associations compared to the J-shaped associations seen with eGFRcr.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Availability of cystatin C measurements in addition to serum creatinine.
- Use of validated equations for calculating eGFRcr-cys.
- Inclusion of comprehensive covariates (age, sex, smoking, BP, lipids, BMI, comorbidities) in risk modeling.
- Clinical judgment to interpret risk distributions based on specific outcome profiles.
- Access to updated risk prediction tools derived from the CKD Prognosis Consortium data.
- Inclusion of antihypertensive medication use as a covariate in multivariable-adjusted risk models.
