# NOAC efficacy in CKD
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** NOAC efficacy and safety in CKD for stroke prevention in atrial fibrillation

**Clinical Actions:**
- NOACs significantly reduce stroke or systemic embolic events by 19% vs warfarin (RR: 0.81; 95% CI: 0.73–0.91), particularly hemorrhagic strokes (RR: 0.49; 95% CI: 0.38–0.64)
- NOACs reduce intracranial hemorrhage by ~50% and all-cause mortality by 10% vs warfarin, with similar major bleeding risk overall
- Benefits of NOACs are consistent across CKD stages (including CrCl <50 mL/min) and subgroups (age, sex, diabetes, prior stroke, CHADS₂ score)
- NOACs offer simpler dosing and no INR monitoring, reducing patient and healthcare burden compared to warfarin
- NOACs are cost-effective or cost-saving for stroke prevention in atrial fibrillation in CKD

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- CKD does not importantly modify NOAC benefits down to G4, but dose adjustment for GFR is required
- Consider modifiable bleeding risk factors (e.g., alcohol intake) and use gastroprophylaxis (PPI) when combining with antiplatelet therapy
- Re-evaluate anticoagulation decisions at each consultation and at least every 6 months, especially for low-risk patients
- Consult product-specific summaries for latest dosing guidance (e.g., apixaban dose reduction criteria: age ≥80, weight ≤60 kg, or CrCl 15–29 mL/min)
- Discontinue NOACs before procedures based on bleeding risk, drug half-life, and GFR (e.g., ≥24h low risk, ≥48h high risk)
- Dose modification for NOACs must consider age, weight, and GFR (e.g., apixaban dose reduction criteria: age ≥80, weight ≤60 kg, or CrCl 15–29 mL/min) as per product-specific summaries
- Consider lower apixaban dose (2.5 mg b.i.d.) in CKD G5/G5D to reduce bleeding risk until safety data are available
