# RASi therapy guideline adaptation
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** RASi therapy guideline adaptation in CKD

**Clinical Actions:**
- RASi (ACEi or ARB) should be started for CKD patients with severely increased albuminuria (A3) regardless of blood pressure status.
- Administer RASi at the highest tolerated dose to achieve benefits demonstrated in trials.
- Monitor BP, serum creatinine, and potassium within 2–4 weeks of RASi initiation or dose increase.
- Consider dose reduction or discontinuation of RASi only in symptomatic hypotension, uncontrolled hyperkalemia, or uremic symptoms with eGFR <15 mL/min/1.73m².
- Reinitiate RASi after resolving adverse events (e.g., hyperkalemia) to avoid depriving patients of needed therapy.
- Restrict salt intake to maximize the efficacy of RASi therapy

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- CKD with severely increased albuminuria (A3) for initiating RASi (G1–G4, no diabetes required).
- Monitoring BP, serum creatinine, and potassium within 2–4 weeks of RASi initiation or dose adjustment.
- eGFR <15 mL/min/1.73m² for considering dose reduction or discontinuation due to uremic symptoms.
- ≥30% decrease in eGFR as a trigger to investigate for underlying conditions.
- Salt restriction to maximize RASi efficacy.
- eGFR <30 mL/min/1.73m² as a threshold for worse outcomes when RASi is stopped (observational studies and meta-analysis)
