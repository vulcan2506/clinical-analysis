# pediatric GFR estimation equations
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** pediatric GFR estimation equations

**Clinical Actions:**
- Use validated equations like CKiD U25 2021 eGFRcr, EKFC, or CKD-EPI40 for pediatric GFR estimation.
- Prefer CKiD U25 2021 eGFRcr equation for children under 25 years due to derivation in a multiracial cohort and external validation.
- EKFC equation is validated in European children and adults, allowing consistent GFR estimation across adolescence and young adulthood.
- Cystatin C–based equations are recommended for children with neurological disorders, muscle wasting, metabolic disorders, or very low–protein diets.
- Use CKD-EPI40 equation as an alternative validated option for pediatric GFR estimation when CKiD U25 or EKFC are not applicable or unavailable.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Equations must be validated in the target population (e.g., CKiD U25 for children with CKD).
- Alternative height/sex/age/creatinine-based equations are acceptable if proven to improve accuracy in the specific population.
- Alternative GFR estimation equations must demonstrate improved accuracy in the specific target population before being considered acceptable.
