# CKD guideline framework
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** CKD guideline framework

**Clinical Actions:**
- Adopt a patient-centered, multidisciplinary approach to CKD management
- Use KDIGO 2012 and subsequent updates as the primary framework for CKD evaluation and management
- Implement risk-based stratification for CKD progression and complications
- Emphasize early detection, regular monitoring, and individualized care plans
- Promote shared decision-making between patients and clinicians

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Baseline assessment including eGFR and albuminuria for all patients with suspected or confirmed CKD
- Regular follow-up with monitoring of kidney function, blood pressure, and cardiovascular risk
- Multidisciplinary care involving nephrologists, dietitians, pharmacists, and primary care providers
- Use of validated risk prediction tools (e.g., kidney failure risk equation) to guide management
- Adherence to evidence-based guidelines for CKD staging, treatment, and monitoring
- Incorporate standardized use of race-free GFR estimating equations (e.g., CKD-EPI 2021) in all clinical settings for CKD evaluation and management
