# CKD diagnostic and staging framework
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** CKD diagnostic and staging framework

**Clinical Actions:**
- Monitoring CKD through surveillance of albuminuria and GFR to update staging for prognosis and assess treatment effectiveness.
- No clear threshold defines a clinically relevant change in GFR or albuminuria; overinterpretation of small changes may lead to unnecessary management changes.
- The initial evaluation of an observed change in eGFR or ACR should be to repeat the test(s) to determine if the change is clinically significant or within biological and analytical variability.
- Monitoring of children in the peripubescent phase should be undertaken more frequently than the CKD stage-based recommended frequency due to high risk of progression.
- Frequency of monitoring in children should be individualized based on CKD severity, stage of puberty, and observed recent rate of progression.
- Consider the 95% CI of test reproducibility for both eGFR and ACR as a threshold for clinical evaluation of changes in kidney function or albuminuria.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Healthcare providers and patients must be educated about the variability of specific laboratory measurements in kidney disease.
- Observed changes in eGFR or ACR must be confirmed by repeating the test(s) before altering clinical management.
- Monitoring frequency in children must be informed by the severity of CKD, stage of puberty, and observed recent rate of progression.
- Pediatric monitoring frequency must account for the stage of puberty and observed recent rate of progression, not just CKD severity.
