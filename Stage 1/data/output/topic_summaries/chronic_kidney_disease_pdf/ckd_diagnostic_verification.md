# CKD diagnostic verification
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** CKD diagnostic verification and risk stratification

**Clinical Actions:**
- Verify unexpected results before diagnosing CKD in people without risk factors due to low pretest probability
- Perform subsequent testing to confirm diagnosis and complete evaluation in people with risk factors for CKD
- Determine timing of repeat samples based on clinical setting, risk factors, and concern for AKI/AKD
- Investigate persistent hematuria further as it may indicate glomerular disease, other kidney diseases, or urologic malignancy
- Recognize biological and analytical variability in SCr and urine albumin/protein not related to kidney disease properties
- Verify unexpected kidney function or albuminuria results in people without CKD risk factors due to low pretest probability and biological/analytical variability in SCr and urine albumin/protein
- Investigate persistent hematuria further to rule out glomerular disease, other kidney diseases, or urologic malignancy, including genitourinary malignancy

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Assessment of patient risk factors for CKD (e.g., hypertension, diabetes, cardiovascular disease, prior AKI/AKD)
- Evaluation of geographical prevalence of CKD (e.g., endemic CKDu, high APOL1 variants, environmental exposures)
- Identification of genitourinary disorders (e.g., structural urinary tract disease, recurrent kidney calculi)
- Differentiation between transient and persistent hematuria
- Consideration of clinical setting and concern for acute kidney injury or acute kidney disease
- Assess timing of repeat samples based on clinical setting, risk factors for CKD, and concern for AKI/AKD
- Evaluate hematuria for persistence to differentiate transient causes from pathological conditions
