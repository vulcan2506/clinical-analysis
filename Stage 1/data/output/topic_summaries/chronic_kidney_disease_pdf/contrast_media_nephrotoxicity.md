# contrast media nephrotoxicity
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** contrast media nephrotoxicity risk assessment and management in CKD

**Clinical Actions:**
- Consider indication for imaging studies based on general population indications and individual risk-benefit assessment in CKD patients.
- Use the term 'contrast-associated AKI (CA-AKI)' instead of 'contrast-induced AKI' due to weak causal links.
- Exercise caution in withholding contrast treatment in CKD G4–G5 patients solely based on GFR when evaluating potentially fatal conditions.
- Avoid delaying, not performing, or providing suboptimal imaging due to fear of contrast-associated complications in reduced GFR patients.
- Consider the indication for imaging studies in accordance with general population indications, with risks and benefits determined on an individual basis in the context of CKD.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Assess patient-associated risk factors: reduced GFR (eGFR <45 mL/min/1.73m² with other risk factors or eGFR <30 mL/min/1.73m²), diabetes mellitus, and reduced intravascular volume.
- Assess procedure-associated risk factors: high-osmolar contrast, large volume of contrast, and serial contrast procedures.
- Define reduced GFR as eGFR <45 mL/min/1.73m² with other risk factors or eGFR <30 mL/min/1.73m² for risk assessment.
