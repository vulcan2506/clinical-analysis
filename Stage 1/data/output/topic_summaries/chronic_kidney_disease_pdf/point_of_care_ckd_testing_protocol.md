# point-of-care CKD testing protocol
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** point-of-care CKD testing protocol

**Clinical Actions:**
- POCT may be used for creatinine and urine albumin measurement where access to a laboratory is limited or test at point-of-care facilitates the clinical pathway
- Ensure POCT devices meet preanalytical, analytical, and postanalytical quality criteria, including external quality assessment and result interpretation
- Generate eGFR estimates using POCT creatinine devices with equations consistent with regional standards
- POCT albuminuria devices should analyze creatinine and produce ACR, with capability to detect significant albuminuria (ACR ≥30 mg/g or ≥3 mg/mmol) in ≥85% of cases
- POCT can facilitate earlier diagnosis, monitoring, and treatment in rural/remote settings or where laboratory access is limited
- POCT devices should be standardized and calibrated with rigor comparable to laboratory tests
- POCT can be implemented in 'virtual ward' or hospital-at-home settings for remote monitoring

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- POCT devices must meet quality criteria for specimen collection, device performance, external quality assessment, and result interpretation
- POCT creatinine devices must generate eGFR using regionally consistent equations
- POCT albuminuria devices must produce ACR with ≥85% sensitivity for significant albuminuria (ACR ≥30 mg/g or ≥3 mg/mmol)
- Support from local laboratory services is required for purchase, evaluation, implementation, governance, and ongoing quality assurance of POCT
- POCT should be used based on specific clinical need or geographical/social circumstances where laboratory access is limited
- POCT devices must be standardized and calibrated with rigor comparable to laboratory tests
