# KDIGO guideline development acknowledgment
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** guideline development acknowledgment and public review process

**Clinical Actions:**
- Acknowledges Co-Chairs and immediate past Co-Chair for oversight of guideline development.
- Recognizes Work Group members for literature review, data extraction, and critical writing.
- Incorporates feedback from public external reviewers into the final publication.
- Lists specific individuals and organizations who provided feedback during the public review phase.
- Clarifies that participation in public review does not constitute endorsement of the guideline content.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Guideline must undergo rigorous assessment of available evidence by ERT members.
- Draft guideline must be assessed by public external reviewers before final publication.
- Work Group must consider all valuable comments from public reviewers.
- Suggested changes from reviewers must be incorporated where appropriate.
- Final publication must include a list of individuals who provided feedback.
