# GLP-1 receptor agonist recommendation
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** GLP-1 receptor agonist recommendation in T2D with CKD

**Clinical Actions:**
- Recommended as a long-acting GLP-1 RA for adults with T2D and CKD who have not achieved glycemic targets despite metformin and SGLT2 inhibitor use, or are unable to use those medications.
- Prioritize GLP-1 RAs with documented cardiovascular benefits.
- Preferred over insulin in people with T2D and CKD.
- Modestly reduces risk of hospitalization for heart failure (HR: 0.89) and all-cause mortality (HR: 0.88).
- GLP-1 RAs are recommended to be prioritized ahead of insulin in people with T2D and CKD, even when glycemic targets are not yet achieved with metformin and SGLT2 inhibitors.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Adults with type 2 diabetes (T2D) and chronic kidney disease (CKD).
- Failure to achieve individualized glycemic targets despite metformin and SGLT2 inhibitor treatment, or inability to use those medications.
- Documented cardiovascular benefits of the chosen GLP-1 RA.
- Documented prior cardiovascular disease (CVD) or high cardiovascular risk is a key consideration when selecting a GLP-1 RA with cardiovascular benefits.
