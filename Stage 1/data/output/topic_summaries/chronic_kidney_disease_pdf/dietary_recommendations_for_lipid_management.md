# dietary recommendations for lipid management
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** dietary recommendations for lipid management in CKD

**Clinical Actions:**
- Consider a plant-based 'Mediterranean-style' diet in addition to lipid-modifying therapy to reduce cardiovascular risk.
- Emphasize plant-based diets with higher consumption of fruit, vegetables, nuts, legumes, fish, olive oil, yogurt, and whole grains.
- Avoid diets high in red and processed meats, refined carbohydrates, and salt.
- Prefer vegetable sources of fats and polyunsaturated fatty acids (e.g., nuts, seeds, avocado, olive oil) over animal fats.
- Use a Mediterranean diet rich in extra virgin olive oil to reduce major cardiovascular events.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Adults aged ≥50 years with eGFR <60 mL/min/1.73m² (GFR categories G3a–G5) should receive statin or statin/ezetimibe combination therapy.
- Adults aged ≥50 years with CKD and eGFR ≥60 mL/min/1.73m² (GFR categories G1–G2) should receive statin therapy.
- Adults aged 18–49 years with CKD should receive statin therapy if they have known coronary disease, diabetes mellitus, prior ischemic stroke, or estimated 10-year coronary death or nonfatal myocardial infarction risk >10%.
- Estimate 10-year cardiovascular risk using a validated risk tool.
- Choose statin-based regimens to maximize absolute reduction in LDL cholesterol.
