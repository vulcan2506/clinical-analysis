# urine sample reporting standards
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** urine sample reporting standards for ACR

**Clinical Actions:**
- Implement laboratory reporting and handling standards to ensure accuracy and reliability of urine samples
- Report ACR in untimed urine samples in addition to urine albumin concentration rather than concentrations alone
- Store samples for albumin measurement fresh or at 4 °C for up to 7 days; avoid freezing at -20 °C
- Implement an external quality assessment scheme/program for urine albumin, creatinine, and ACR calculation

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Samples for albumin measurement must be analyzed fresh or stored at 4 °C for up to 7 days
- External quality assessment for urine albumin, creatinine, and ACR calculation is a preferred practice
- Implementation of an external quality assessment scheme/program for urine albumin and creatinine, including ACR calculation, is a preferred practice
