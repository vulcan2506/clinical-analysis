# Guideline Development Process
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Guideline Development Process - IOM Standards

**Clinical Actions:**
- Adherence to Institute of Medicine (IOM) standards for developing trustworthy clinical practice guidelines
- Use of a systematic review process to evaluate evidence
- Incorporation of a multidisciplinary guideline development group
- Implementation of a transparent process for formulating recommendations
- Regular updates based on new evidence and feedback
- Use of a structured process for managing conflicts of interest among guideline development group members
- Involvement of patient and public representatives in the guideline development process

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Compliance with IOM standards for guideline development
- Systematic review of available evidence
- Multidisciplinary expert panel involvement
- Transparent methodology for evidence evaluation and recommendation formulation
- Mechanism for periodic guideline review and updates
- Formal management of intellectual and financial conflicts of interest with disclosure and mitigation strategies
- Inclusion of patient and public perspectives in the development process
