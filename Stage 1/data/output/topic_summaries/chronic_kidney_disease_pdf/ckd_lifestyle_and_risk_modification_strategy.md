# CKD lifestyle and risk modification strategy
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** CKD lifestyle and risk modification strategy

**Clinical Actions:**
- Encourage physical activity compatible with cardiovascular health, tolerance, and frailty
- Achieve optimal body mass index (BMI)
- Avoid tobacco products
- Undertake moderate-intensity physical activity for ≥150 minutes/week or as tolerated
- Provide tailored advice on physical activity intensity and type for those at higher fall risk
- Advise people with CKD to undertake moderate-intensity physical activity for a cumulative duration of at least 150 minutes per week (1D evidence)
- Provide advice on physical activity intensity (low, moderate, or vigorous) and type (aerobic vs. resistance, or both) for people at higher risk of falls

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Referral to providers/programs (e.g., psychologists, renal dietitians, pharmacists, physical/occupational therapy, smoking cessation) where indicated and available
- Consider BMI in conjunction with ethnicity, diet, comorbidity, physical activity, fall risk, and lab values for weight advice
- Consider BMI in conjunction with ethnicity, diet, comorbidity, physical activity levels, risk of falls, and laboratory values when providing weight advice
