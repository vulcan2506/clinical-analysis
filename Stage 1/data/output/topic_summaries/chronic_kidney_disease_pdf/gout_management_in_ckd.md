# gout management in CKD
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** gout management in CKD

**Clinical Actions:**
- Initiate uric acid–lowering therapy after first episode of gout in CKD, especially if serum uric acid >9 mg/dl (535 μmol/l) or no avoidable precipitant.
- Prescribe xanthine oxidase inhibitors (e.g., allopurinol) as first-line for symptomatic hyperuricemia in CKD.
- Use low-dose colchicine or intra-articular/oral glucocorticoids for acute gout in CKD; avoid NSAIDs.
- Limit alcohol, meats, and high-fructose corn syrup intake as nonpharmacological gout prevention.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Serum uric acid >9 mg/dl (535 μmol/l) or absence of avoidable precipitant for initiating uric acid–lowering therapy.
- Symptomatic hyperuricemia present for prescribing xanthine oxidase inhibitors.
- Serum uric acid concentration >9 mg/dl (535 μmol/l) or absence of avoidable precipitant for considering initiation of uric acid–lowering therapy after first episode of gout in CKD.
