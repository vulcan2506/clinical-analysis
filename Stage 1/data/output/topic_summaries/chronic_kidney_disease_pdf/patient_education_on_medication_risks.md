# patient education on medication risks
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** patient education on medication risks and drug stewardship

**Clinical Actions:**
- Educate patients regarding expected benefits and possible risks of medications to enable identification and reporting of adverse events.
- Encourage patients to inform non-nephrology prescribers of their CKD diagnosis to facilitate dose and side effect considerations.
- Utilize interactive electronic health applications to improve patient knowledge application and medication management.
- Print recent eGFR estimation results for patients to bring to future consultations to alert providers of medication risks.
- Write down a list of ongoing medications to alert other healthcare providers of potential medication risks and benefits.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Education materials must be inclusive of the patient's literacy level and language.
- A diagnosis of CKD must be reflected in medical records to alert physicians on medication adjustments.
- Collaborative relationships with pharmacists or use of clinical decision support systems should be established for drug stewardship.
- Structured medication reviews should be conducted by clinical pharmacists or knowledgeable healthcare providers.
- Clinical decision support systems should be integrated into EMRs to provide alerts on dose adjustments and drug-drug interactions.
