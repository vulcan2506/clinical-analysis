# anticoagulant dosing by renal function
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** anticoagulant dosing by renal function in CKD

**Clinical Actions:**
- Warfarin dosing is adjusted to maintain INR 2–3 across all eCrCl ranges.
- Apixaban is dosed at 5 mg b.i.d. for eCrCl >25 mL/min, with 2.5 mg b.i.d. considered for eCrCl 15–30 mL/min.
- Dabigatran is dosed at 150 mg b.i.d. for eCrCl >30 mL/min, with 110 mg b.i.d. or 75 mg b.i.d. considered for lower eCrCl.
- Edoxaban is dosed at 60 mg QD for eCrCl >50 mL/min, reduced to 30 mg QD for eCrCl 15–50 mL/min.
- Rivaroxaban is dosed at 20 mg QD for eCrCl >50 mL/min, reduced to 15 mg QD for eCrCl 15–50 mL/min.
- Warfarin dosing is adjusted to maintain INR 2–3 for eCrCl 15–30 mL/min, with consideration for dose adjustment rather than mandatory reduction.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Dosing adjustments are based on estimated creatinine clearance (eCrCl) thresholds.
- Warfarin requires INR monitoring for dose adjustment regardless of renal function.
- Apixaban dosing requires consideration of eCrCl cut-off at 25 mL/min for dose reduction.
- Dabigatran dosing may require reduction to 110 mg b.i.d. or 75 mg b.i.d. in moderate to severe renal impairment.
- Edoxaban and Rivaroxaban dosing reductions are mandatory for eCrCl ≤50 mL/min.
- Dabigatran dosing at 110 mg b.i.d. is explicitly considered for eCrCl 31–50 mL/min as an alternative to 150 mg b.i.d.
