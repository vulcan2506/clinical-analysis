# clinical practice guideline framework
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Guideline Framework and Application

**Clinical Actions:**
- Assists decision-making without defining a standard of care
- Acknowledges variations in practice based on patient needs, resources, and institutional limitations
- Encourages clinicians to adapt recommendations to their own clinical practice
- Explicitly states that the guideline is designed to assist (not dictate) decision-making
