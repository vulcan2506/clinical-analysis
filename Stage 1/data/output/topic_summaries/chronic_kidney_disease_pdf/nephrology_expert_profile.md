# nephrology expert profile
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** nephrology expert profile in KDIGO CKD guideline development

**Clinical Actions:**
- Leads multidisciplinary research units to produce evidence for high-quality, equitable patient care aligned with evolving healthcare guidelines
- Develops and implements analytic strategies using large-scale global data (e.g., CKD-PC consortium with 30M+ participants) to answer clinically meaningful kidney disease questions
- Integrates multimodal omics data into kidney disease research and clinical decision-making
- Serves as Co-Chair of KDIGO, guiding guideline development and implementation
- Practices as a nephrologist while maintaining PhD-level expertise in epidemiology and population health
- Serves as associate editor for major nephrology journals (e.g., Nephrology Dialysis Transplantation)
- Actively participates in international nephrology organizations (e.g., ERA Council, ASN/AHA Kidney Council)

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- MD or equivalent medical degree with nephrology fellowship training
- Leadership roles in major nephrology research consortia (e.g., CKD-PC)
- Publication record in major nephrology journals (e.g., >350 scientific papers)
- Active involvement in guideline development bodies (e.g., KDIGO Co-Chair)
- Expertise in integrating clinical, epidemiological, and omics-based evidence
- Fellowship training in nephrology with internal medicine background
- Leadership roles in national data systems (e.g., US Renal Data System, Scientific Registry of Transplant Recipients)
