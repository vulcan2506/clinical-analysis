# CKD risk stratification heatmap
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** CKD risk stratification heatmap + individual risk prediction

**Clinical Actions:**
- Use both eGFR and ACR for assessing CKD severity and prognosis, as emphasized by heatmap color-coding of relative risks (RRs) for adverse outcomes.
- Apply individual-level risk prediction equations (e.g., KFRE) to estimate absolute risk for specific adverse events, beyond population-level RRs.
- Integrate risk prediction tools into clinical workflows (EMRs, lab systems, or mobile apps) to personalize CKD management and guide treatment decisions.
- Prioritize externally validated risk equations with high discrimination (C-statistics >0.8) and routine data availability for global implementation.
- Use risk prediction to inform discussions on goals of care, modality education, and earlier initiation of disease-modifying therapies.
- Use risk prediction to identify individuals for earlier use of disease-modifying therapy based on absolute risk benefits.
- Prioritize risk equations that use routinely collected data to ensure feasibility in low-resource settings and global health equity.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- CKD G3–G5 classification with available eGFR and ACR data for risk equation application.
- Externally validated risk prediction models (e.g., KFRE, KPNW, Z6 Score) with required variables (e.g., age, sex, eGFR, ACR, comorbidities).
- Local calibration of risk equations when possible to optimize accuracy in specific populations.
- Standardized reporting of risk thresholds in laboratory reports and EMRs for clinical decision-making.
- Avoid use in AKI/AKD or younger individuals with CKD G1–G2 where proximal outcomes (e.g., 40% eGFR decline) may be more appropriate.
- Availability of routinely collected data variables (e.g., eGFR, ACR, demographics, comorbidities) for model implementation.
- Standardized reporting of risk results in laboratory reports and EMRs with appropriate guidance on risk thresholds.
