# kidney biopsy indications
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** kidney biopsy indications and pediatric considerations

**Clinical Actions:**
- Kidney biopsies should be considered when they can provide information to identify cause, facilitate prognostication, and inform treatment strategies.
- Biopsy is often deferred due to potential harm or lack of recognition of utility, but evidence suggests low risk of harm.
- In children and young people with kidney failure, genetic testing may be pursued first to potentially obviate the need for kidney biopsy.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Suspected or diagnosed CKD in adults and children.
- Clinical judgment that biopsy can identify cause, facilitate prognostication, or inform treatment strategies.
- Biopsy should be considered when it can identify the cause of CKD, facilitate prognostication, or inform treatment strategies in both adults and children.
