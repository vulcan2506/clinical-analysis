# GFR estimation methodology
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** GFR estimation methodology in CKD

**Clinical Actions:**
- Use creatinine-based eGFR (eGFRcr) for adults at risk for CKD
- Use combined creatinine and cystatin C-based eGFR (eGFRcr-cys) if cystatin C is available for more accurate GFR estimation
- Do not assume chronicity based on a single abnormal eGFR or ACR; repeat measurements are required to confirm CKD
- Use validated GFR estimating equations within geographical regions to ensure consistency and reduce clinical confusion
- Standardize GFR thresholds for disease definition and staging using equations optimized for the local population
- Use eGFRcr-cys as the preferred method for GFR estimation when cystatin C is available due to superior accuracy in risk stratification and reclassification of GFR stages

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Cystatin C availability for eGFRcr-cys estimation
- Repeat GFR and ACR measurements over at least 3 months to confirm chronicity
- Use of GFR estimating equations validated in the population of interest
- Review of past GFR measurements, albuminuria/proteinuria, imaging findings, kidney pathology, and medical history
- Geographical standardization of GFR estimating equations within regions
- Mechanism for regional review and selection of validated GFR estimating equations by national kidney societies or regional laboratory groups (e.g., via national kidney society collaboration with laboratory physician organizations)
- Use of GFR estimating equations developed with assays traceable to reference materials and rigorous mGFR methods for validation
