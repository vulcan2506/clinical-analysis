# CKD referral criteria
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** CKD referral criteria for adults and pediatric patients

**Clinical Actions:**
- Refer adults with CKD to specialist kidney care services based on specific circumstances outlined in Figure 48.
- Refer children and adolescents with CKD to specialist kidney care services if they meet any of the specified pediatric criteria.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- For pediatric patients, confirm ACR ≥30 mg/g (3 mg/mmol) or PCR ≥200 mg/g (20 mg/mmol) on a repeat first morning void sample when well and not during menstruation.
- Persistent hematuria in pediatric patients requires referral.
- Any sustained decrease in eGFR in pediatric patients necessitates referral.
- Hypertension in pediatric patients is a referral criterion.
- Kidney outflow obstruction or anomalies of the kidney and urinary tract in pediatric patients warrant referral.
- Confirmation of proteinuria (ACR ≥30 mg/g or PCR ≥200 mg/g) must be done on a repeat first morning void sample when the pediatric patient is well and not during menstruation.
