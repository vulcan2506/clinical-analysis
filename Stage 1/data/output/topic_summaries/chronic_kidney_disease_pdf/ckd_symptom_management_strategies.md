# CKD symptom management strategies
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** CKD symptom management strategies

**Clinical Actions:**
- Assist patients to live better with kidney disease regardless of life expectancy within a supportive care framework
- Address symptom clusters (e.g., CKD-associated pruritus and emotional/psychological distress) where treating one symptom may alleviate others
- Develop individualized treatment strategies accounting for CKD complexities across different populations
- Prioritize symptom management strategies that target shared pathophysiological mechanisms (e.g., chronic inflammation or uremic toxin accumulation) to address symptom clusters holistically

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Symptom management should be integrated into a supportive care framework
- Strategies must consider the variation in evidence levels for managing different CKD symptoms
- Integrate symptom management into a broader supportive care framework that includes patient education, advance care planning, and caregiver support
