# KDIGO guideline update publication
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** KDIGO guideline update publication process

**Clinical Actions:**
- Guidelines are developed through a rigorous, multi-year process involving clinicians, researchers, and patients
- Evidence Review Team conducts systematic literature reviews with GRADE methodology for evidence certainty
- Guideline statements reflect clinical questions addressed by evidence reviews
- Practice points provide guidance on clinical questions not amenable to evidence review
- Public review phases engage stakeholders to ensure transparency and objectivity

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Scope of Work undergoes open public review to engage all stakeholders
- Request for Proposal used to enlist an independent Evidence Review Team
- Screening of Guideline Co-Chairs and Work Group members for conflicts of interest
- Evidence Review Team extracts studies with appropriate design and outcomes deemed important by patients and clinicians
- Penultimate guideline version undergoes public review to capture additional perspectives
