# finerenone initiation criteria
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** finerenone initiation criteria in T2D with CKD

**Clinical Actions:**
- Suggest nonsteroidal mineralocorticoid receptor antagonist (finerenone) for adults with T2D, eGFR >25 mL/min/1.73m², normal serum potassium, and albuminuria (>30 mg/g) despite maximum tolerated RAS inhibitor.
- Monitor serum potassium at 1 month after initiation, then every 4 months.
- Initiate finerenone at 10 mg daily if eGFR 25–59 mL/min/1.73m²; 20 mg daily if eGFR ≥60 mL/min/1.73m².
- Increase dose to 20 mg daily if on 10 mg daily and tolerated.
- Hold finerenone if hyperkalemia occurs; restart at 10 mg daily if K+ ≤5.0 mmol/l.
- Monitor serum creatinine/eGFR concurrently with serum potassium during finerenone therapy
- Consider continuing MRAs (steroidal or nonsteroidal) in patients with potassium 5.5–6.0 mmol/l if clinically appropriate, despite conservative guideline thresholds

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Adults with type 2 diabetes (T2D)
- eGFR >25 mL/min/1.73m²
- Normal serum potassium concentration
- Albuminuria (>30 mg/g) despite maximum tolerated RAS inhibitor
- High risk of CKD progression and cardiovascular events
- Persistent albuminuria (>30 mg/g [>3 mg/mmol]) despite maximum tolerated dose of RAS inhibitor (RASi)
