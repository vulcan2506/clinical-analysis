# CKD drug stewardship principles
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** CKD drug stewardship principles

**Clinical Actions:**
- Prescribe medications responsibly, considering indication, benefit-risk profile, and potential nephrotoxicity in CKD
- Monitor efficacy, safety, and therapeutic levels for medications with narrow therapeutic windows or nephrotoxicity
- Discontinue medications when they no longer serve their intended purpose or pose harm
- Collaborate with pharmacists in multidisciplinary teams for optimized medication management
- Inquire about and discourage indiscriminate use of OTC medications, herbal remedies, and dietary supplements with nephrotoxic potential
- Encourage judicious use of NSAIDs under nephrologist supervision when alternatives are unsuitable, balancing risks of nephrotoxicity against risks of opioid-related adverse events
- Provide patient education on the risks of falsified/substandard medications and advise against purchasing medications from illicit internet sources

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Consider altered pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics in CKD when prescribing medications
- Assess patient-specific factors (e.g., comorbidities, age, hypoalbuminemia) affecting drug sensitivity
- Balance accessibility, affordability, and patient preferences in medication selection
- Monitor eGFR, electrolytes, and therapeutic drug levels as indicated for high-risk medications
- Consult pharmacists for comprehensive medication review and care plan development
- Assess and address barriers to medication access (e.g., affordability, availability) in low-resource settings to ensure equitable CKD care
- Evaluate patient-specific risks of herbal remedy and dietary supplement toxicity, including interactions with prescribed medications and volume depletion states
