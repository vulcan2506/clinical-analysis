# GFR assessment standardization
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** GFR assessment standardization using creatinine and cystatin C

**Clinical Actions:**
- Report eGFR in addition to serum concentrations of filtration markers using validated equations.
- Report eGFR rounded to the nearest whole number and relative to a body surface area (BSA) of 1.73m² in adults.
- Flag eGFR levels <60 ml/min per 1.73m² as low in adults.
- Use enzymatic methods for creatinine assays where possible.
- Measure filtration markers with assays having CV <2.3% for creatinine and <2.0% for cystatin C, with calibration traceable to international standards.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Separate serum/plasma from red blood cells by centrifugation within 12 hours of venipuncture.
- When cystatin C is measured, measure creatinine on the same sample to enable eGFRcr-cys calculation.
- For infants/small children, ensure quality control includes the lowest end of the expected range of values.
- Use enzymatic creatinine assays in children due to higher non-creatinine chromogen interference with Jaffe assays.
- Flag eGFRcr <90 ml/min per 1.73m² as low in children/adolescents over 2 years.
- Report serum creatinine (SCr) concentration rounded to the nearest whole number when expressed in standard international units (µmol/L) and to the nearest 100th when expressed in conventional units (mg/dL).
- Report serum cystatin C concentration rounded to the nearest 100th when expressed in conventional units (mg/L).
