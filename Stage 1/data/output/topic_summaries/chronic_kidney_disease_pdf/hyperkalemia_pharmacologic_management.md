# hyperkalemia pharmacologic management
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** hyperkalemia pharmacologic management in CKD

**Clinical Actions:**
- Use newer potassium exchange agents with improved tolerability and long-term safety for nonemergent hyperkalemia management
- Select therapy based on clinical nuances, local availability, and formulary restrictions
- Facilitate continued use of RASi/MRA with newer potassium exchange agents
- Consider differing mechanisms of action, onsets of effect, and interactions when choosing agents
- Consider patient-specific factors such as renal function, comorbid conditions, and concurrent medications when selecting potassium exchange agents
- Monitor serum potassium levels closely after initiating or adjusting potassium exchange therapy

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Nonemergent hyperkalemia presentation
- Awareness of local availability or formulary restrictions for agent selection
- Stable renal function or non-dialysis-dependent CKD for nonemergent hyperkalemia management
- No contraindications to potassium exchange agents (e.g., bowel obstruction for oral agents)
