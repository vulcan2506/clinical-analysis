# pediatric CKD risk factors
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** pediatric CKD risk factors

**Clinical Actions:**
- People born preterm, especially if also small for gestational age, are at increased risk for CKD and kidney failure.
- Additional insults after birth such as neonatal AKI and childhood obesity can further increase the risk of CKD.
- Decreased nephron number is largely related to the increased risk in preterm infants.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patient must have a history of being born preterm.
- Patient may have been small for gestational age.
- Patient may have experienced neonatal AKI.
- Patient may have childhood obesity.
