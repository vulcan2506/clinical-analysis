# PROM tool selection criteria
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** PROM tool selection criteria for CKD

**Clinical Actions:**
- SF-36 version 2 is widely used and well validated but requires a license fee
- RAND-36 is an older version of SF-36, does not require a license fee, and is only available in English and Arabic
- PROMIS and PROMIS-29 are based on extensive item banks, available in paper and electronic versions, and well validated in general and CKD populations

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Screening for malnutrition in CKD G4–G5, aged >65, poor growth (pediatrics), or symptoms like involuntary weight loss, frailty, or poor appetite should be done twice annually using a validated assessment tool
- Screening for malnutrition in CKD G4–G5, aged >65, poor growth (pediatrics), or symptoms such as involuntary weight loss, frailty, or poor appetite should be done twice annually using a validated assessment tool.
