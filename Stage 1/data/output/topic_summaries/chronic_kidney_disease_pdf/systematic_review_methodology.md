# systematic review methodology
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** systematic review methodology in CKD guideline development

**Clinical Actions:**
- Conducted by multidisciplinary teams including epidemiologists, nephrologists, and research coordinators
- Led by experienced Evidence-based Practice Center (EPC) with over 20 years of systematic review expertise
- Incorporates Cochrane and GRADE methodologies for evidence synthesis and grading
- Uses comprehensive literature search strategies across multiple databases
- Includes meta-analyses, qualitative synthesis, and evidence profile development

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Evidence Review Team must include methodologists with Cochrane and GRADE experience
- Systematic reviews must follow standardized protocols (e.g., PRISMA guidelines)
- Requires statistical expertise for meta-analyses and data evaluation
- Must address conflicts of interest declarations for all team members
- Incorporates patient-reported outcomes and person-centered practices
