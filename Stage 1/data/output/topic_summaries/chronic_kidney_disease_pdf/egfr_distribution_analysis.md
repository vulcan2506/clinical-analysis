# eGFR distribution analysis
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** eGFR distribution analysis and potassium physiology in CKD

**Clinical Actions:**
- eGFR distribution analysis is used to understand potassium physiology and its impacting factors in CKD patient care.
- Hyperkalemia is less prevalent in people with preserved GFR.
- Acute hyperkalemia is defined as a potassium result above the upper limit of normal not known to be chronic, with no consensus on chronicity criteria.
- Decreased eGFR, higher ACR, prior diabetes, hyperglycemia, constipation, RASi, and MRA are risk factors for hyperkalemia.
- SGLT2i do not appear to increase serum potassium values.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Understanding potassium physiology and its impacting factors is important for effective patient care in CKD.
- Risk factors for hyperkalemia must be assessed in CKD patients.
- eGFR distribution analysis must account for population density to assess hyperkalemia risk probabilistically rather than relying on binary thresholds.
