# glycemic management guidelines
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** GLP-1 RA use in T2D with CKD after metformin/SGLT2 failure

**Clinical Actions:**
- Use long-acting GLP-1 RA in adults with T2D and CKD who fail to meet individualized glycemic targets despite metformin and SGLT2 inhibitor therapy
- Select GLP-1 RA agents with documented cardiovascular benefits
- Consider patient-specific factors (e.g., eGFR, risk of hypoglycemia, gastrointestinal tolerability) when selecting a long-acting GLP-1 RA in CKD patients with T2D

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Adults with type 2 diabetes (T2D) and chronic kidney disease (CKD)
- Failure to achieve individualized glycemic targets despite metformin and SGLT2 inhibitor use
- Inability to use metformin or SGLT2 inhibitors
- Documented failure to achieve individualized glycemic targets despite optimized metformin and SGLT2 inhibitor therapy (or contraindications to these agents)
- Assessment of cardiovascular risk profile to guide GLP-1 RA selection with documented cardiovascular benefits
