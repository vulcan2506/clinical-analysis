# RASi initiation recommendation
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** RASi initiation recommendation in CKD

**Clinical Actions:**
- Start RASi (ACEi or ARB) for CKD with severely increased albuminuria (G1–G4, A3) without diabetes.
- Administer RASi using the highest tolerated dose to achieve benefits.
- Monitor BP, serum creatinine, and potassium within 2–4 weeks of initiation or dose increase.
- Manage hyperkalemia with measures to reduce serum potassium rather than reducing or stopping RASi.
- Continue ACEi or ARB unless serum creatinine rises >30% within 4 weeks or in cases of symptomatic hypotension, uncontrolled hyperkalemia, or uremic symptoms.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- CKD with severely increased albuminuria (G1–G4, A3) without diabetes for RASi initiation.
- Tolerated dose of RASi to achieve benefits.
- Monitoring BP, serum creatinine, and potassium within 2–4 weeks of initiation or dose increase.
- eGFR <15 mL/min/1.73m² for dose reduction or discontinuation in symptomatic hypotension or uncontrolled hyperkalemia.
- Normal to mildly increased albuminuria (A1) with specific indications (e.g., hypertension or heart failure) for RASi consideration.
- CKD with severely increased albuminuria (G1–G4, A3) without diabetes is the primary indication for RASi initiation (1B recommendation strength).
