# renal dosing and GFR assessment
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** renal dosing and GFR assessment in CKD

**Clinical Actions:**
- Failure to account for GFR when designing drug-dosing regimens can lead to treatment failure or adverse events.
- Assessment of GFR is critical for guiding medication choice and dosing decisions.
- There is inconsistency between dosing guidance and package inserts or pharmacopeias.
- Regulatory agencies now accept any contemporary, widely accepted, and clinically applicable eGFR equation for pharmacokinetic studies.
- Inconsistencies exist between manufacturer-provided dosing guidance in pharmacopeias, textbooks, or online references and clinical practice needs for accurate GFR-based drug dosing.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Use validated eGFR equations (eGFRcr-cys or mGFR) for accurate GFR assessment.
- Consider GFR when designing drug-dosing regimens to avoid treatment failure or adverse events.
- Pharmacokinetic studies for medication approval are not universally required to include abnormal kidney function data, despite the importance of GFR in dosing decisions.
