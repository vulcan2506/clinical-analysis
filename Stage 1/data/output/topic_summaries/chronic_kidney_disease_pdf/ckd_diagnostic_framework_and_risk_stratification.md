# CKD diagnostic framework and risk stratification
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** CKD diagnostic framework and risk stratification using CGA classification

**Clinical Actions:**
- CKD is defined as abnormalities of kidney structure or function present for ≥3 months with implications for health
- Classification uses Cause, GFR category (G1–G5), and Albuminuria category (A1–A3) abbreviated as CGA
- Risk stratification is color-coded: Green (low risk), Yellow (moderately increased risk), Orange (high risk), Red (very high risk)

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Abnormalities must be present for a minimum of 3 months
- Classification requires assessment of Cause, GFR, and Albuminuria
