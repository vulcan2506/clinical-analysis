# SGLT2i therapeutic recommendation
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** SGLT2i therapeutic recommendation in CKD

**Clinical Actions:**
- SGLT2 inhibitors are recommended for adults with CKD and eGFR ≥20 ml/min/1.73 m² (1A strength)
- SGLT2 inhibitors are recommended for adults with CKD and urine ACR ≥200 mg/g or heart failure regardless of albuminuria (1A strength)
- SGLT2 inhibitors may be continued even if eGFR falls below 20 ml/min/1.73 m² unless not tolerated or KRT is initiated
- SGLT2 inhibitors should be withheld during prolonged fasting, surgery, or critical illness to reduce ketosis risk
- Reversible eGFR decrease on SGLT2i initiation is not an indication for discontinuation
- SGLT2i initiation or use does not necessitate alteration of frequency of CKD monitoring

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Adults with CKD and eGFR ≥20 ml/min/1.73 m²
- Urine ACR ≥200 mg/g (≥20 mg/mmol) OR presence of heart failure
- For eGFR 20–45 ml/min/1.73 m² with urine ACR <200 mg/g (<20 mg/mmol), SGLT2i use is suggested (2B strength)
- Type 2 diabetes status is not required for CKD treatment recommendation
- CKD monitoring frequency does not need alteration due to SGLT2i initiation
