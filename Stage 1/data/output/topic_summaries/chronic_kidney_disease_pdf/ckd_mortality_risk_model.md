# CKD mortality risk model
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** CKD mortality risk model

**Clinical Actions:**
- Models predict risk of death, nonfatal CVD events, or kidney failure in adults with CKD G4+ at 2 and 4 years using multinational data.
- A 5-year mortality model exists for older adults with CKD G3 from the United States (Cardiovascular Health Study).
- Models have modest discrimination (C-statistics approximately 0.70).
- Models are more appropriate for identifying high-risk groups to facilitate earlier discussions about conservative care or alternative goals of care.
- Models should not be used to determine futility of initiating kidney replacement therapy (KRT).

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Applicable to adults with CKD G4+ for 2- and 4-year risk predictions.
- Applicable to older adults with CKD G3 for 5-year risk predictions.
