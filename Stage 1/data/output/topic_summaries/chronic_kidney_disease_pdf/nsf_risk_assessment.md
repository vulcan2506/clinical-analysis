# NSF risk assessment
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** NSF risk assessment and gadolinium contrast selection

**Clinical Actions:**
- Identify patients at greatest risk for NSF including those with AKI, undergoing KRT, and those with CKD G4–G5.
- Recognize that most unconfounded cases are associated with American College of Radiology group I gadolinium-based contrast media.
- Acknowledge that there is additional risk with repeated doses of gadolinium-based contrast media.
- Note that incidence of NSF has not been reported later than 2012, raising questions about the true current risk.
- Prefer the use of newer linear and macrocyclic gadolinium-based contrast media in people with GFR <30 ml/min per 1.73 m².

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Assessment of GFR to identify patients with GFR <30 ml/min per 1.73 m².
- Identification of specific high-risk conditions: AKI, KRT, or CKD G4–G5.
- Evaluation of prior exposure to gadolinium-based contrast media to assess repeated dose risk.
