# pediatric blood sampling method
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** pediatric blood sampling method

**Clinical Actions:**
- Use small sample volumes for pediatric testing
- Prefer fingerprick sampling over venipuncture in children when feasible

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Applicability limited to pediatric populations
- Consider research recommendations in Chapter 6 for detailed guidance
- Use of fingerprick sampling is conditional on the child's age, developmental stage, and ability to cooperate with the procedure
