# GFR estimation equation refinement
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** GFR estimation equation refinement (removal of race coefficient)

**Clinical Actions:**
- Historical GFR estimating equations (e.g., CKD-EPI 2009) incorporated age, sex, and race variables to minimize systematic errors in subgroups.
- The 2021 CKD-EPI equation removed the Black race coefficient to reduce systematic differences between groups.
- Newer equations (e.g., CKD-EPI 2021) use only age and sex variables for GFR estimation in adults.
- Pediatric equations (e.g., CKiD U25 2021) incorporate age, sex, and height for GFR estimation.
- Historical GFR estimating equations (e.g., CKD-EPI 2009) reported not using the Black race coefficient despite being developed with race as a variable, indicating potential inconsistency in application.
- The CKD-EPI 2009 equation included modifications for pediatric use (CKD-EPI 40) despite being designed for adults.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- GFR estimation should use validated equations (e.g., CKD-EPI 2021) that exclude race coefficients for consistency across populations.
- Age and sex variables remain essential for accurate GFR estimation in adults.
- GFR estimating equations must be validated in diverse populations, including representation of Black, non-Black, Asian, and Hispanic/Native American individuals, to ensure generalizability and reduce bias.
- Use of population-specific Q-values (e.g., in EKFC 2021) is recommended to account for regional and ethnic variations in creatinine kinetics when available.
