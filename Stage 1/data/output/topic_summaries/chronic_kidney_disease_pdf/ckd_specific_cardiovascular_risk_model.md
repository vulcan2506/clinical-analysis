# CKD-specific cardiovascular risk model
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** CKD-specific cardiovascular risk model

**Clinical Actions:**
- Use CKD-specific risk prediction tools (e.g., QRISK3, ckdpc.org) for adults with CKD to avoid underestimation of cardiovascular risk.
- Modify existing CVD models (PCE/SCORE) by including eGFR and albuminuria to improve risk prediction accuracy.
- Apply the CKD patch to PCE for improved ASCVD risk calibration and the eGFR patch to SCORE for better CVD mortality prediction.
- Consider the PREVENT equations for individuals with CKD, as they include eGFR in the primary model and ACR in an add-on model.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Risk prediction must account for CKD-specific factors (eGFR, albuminuria) to avoid underestimation of cardiovascular risk.
- Use of CKD-specific or modified models is recommended over general population tools for accurate risk stratification.
