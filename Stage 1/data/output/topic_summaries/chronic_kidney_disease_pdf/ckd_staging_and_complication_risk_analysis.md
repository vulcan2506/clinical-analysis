# CKD staging and complication risk analysis
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** CKD staging and complication risk analysis

**Clinical Actions:**
- Use eGFRcr-cys and ACR categories for CKD staging and risk stratification
- Apply multivariable-adjusted analyses to assess risks for 10 common complications by age
- Transition from eGFR-based to risk-based approach for CKD care
- Monitor GFR and albuminuria frequency based on CKD stage and risk
- Use risk equations (e.g., CKD-PC) to predict kidney failure and eGFR decline
- Evaluate CKD etiology using specific diagnostic algorithms (Figure 8)
- Identify actionable genetic variants for hereditary kidney diseases (Figure 9)

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- eGFRcr-cys and ACR measurements for accurate CKD staging
- Multivariable-adjusted risk models for complication prediction
- Continuous eGFR and albuminuria monitoring per CKD stage
- Use of validated risk equations (e.g., CKD-PC) for outcome prediction
- Special considerations for CKD care across the lifespan
- Use of validated risk equations available at specific URLs (e.g., ckdpc.org) for 5-year progression prediction
- Implementation of the 'sick day rule' for medication management during acute illness
