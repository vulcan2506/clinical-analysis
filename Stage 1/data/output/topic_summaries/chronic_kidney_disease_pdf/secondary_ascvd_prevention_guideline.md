# Secondary ASCVD Prevention Guideline
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Aspirin for Secondary ASCVD Prevention in CKD

**Clinical Actions:**
- Recommend oral low-dose aspirin (75–100 mg) for secondary prevention in people with CKD and established ischemic cardiovascular disease (1C recommendation).
- Consider gastroprotection (e.g., proton-pump inhibitors) when prescribing antiplatelet therapy to mitigate bleeding risk.
- Use P2Y12 inhibitors (e.g., clopidogrel, ticagrelor) as noninferior alternatives for aspirin-intolerant patients.
- Apply dual antiplatelet therapy strategies post-acute coronary syndrome or percutaneous coronary intervention, with CKD not modifying benefits of ticagrelor.
- Avoid coadministration of clopidogrel and omeprazole due to potential reduced clopidogrel effectiveness.
- Consider gastroprotection (e.g., proton-pump inhibitors) when prescribing antiplatelet therapy or antithrombotic therapy, particularly when such therapies are combined, due to increased bleeding risk.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Established ischemic cardiovascular disease (e.g., prior myocardial infarction, ischemic stroke, or peripheral arterial disease) must be confirmed for secondary prevention eligibility.
- Assess bleeding risk and provide patient counseling on net benefits before initiating aspirin therapy.
- Assess patient preferences and quality of life priorities (e.g., minimizing risk of worsening ischemic heart disease or recurrent stroke-related disability) when considering aspirin therapy in CKD.
