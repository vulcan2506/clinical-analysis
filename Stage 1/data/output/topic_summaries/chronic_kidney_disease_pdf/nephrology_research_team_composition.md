# nephrology research team composition
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** nephrology research team composition

**Clinical Actions:**
- Multidisciplinary team composition including PhD, ScM, MS, MD, PhD, BS, BA, MSPH, and PharmD, MSc professionals
- Inclusion of academic ranks (Professor, Assistant Professor) and research roles (Research Associate, Research Assistant)
- Diverse educational backgrounds (e.g., Medicine, Pharmacy, Public Health, Epidemiology)
- Collaborative structure with senior and junior members (e.g., Professor alongside Research Assistants)
- Team size of 8 members with varied expertise in nephrology research
- Inclusion of international members (e.g., MBBS from non-US background)
- Team includes members with dual roles (e.g., MD, PhD holding both clinical and research positions)

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Mandatory inclusion of at least one member with clinical nephrology training (e.g., MD, MBBS)
- Requirement for team members to have prior peer-reviewed publication experience in nephrology
