# oncology GFR assessment
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** oncology GFR assessment for anticancer therapy

**Clinical Actions:**
- GFR is critical for anticancer therapy selection, dosing, and trial eligibility.
- Cockcroft-Gault remains commonly used despite inaccuracy compared to validated eGFR equations.
- eGFRcr (CKD-EPI) and eGFRcr-cys (CKD-EPI) predict mGFR more accurately than Cockcroft-Gault in oncology.
- BSA-adjusted eGFR may be indicated for specific situations like carboplatin dosing.
- mGFR may be preferred for initial dosing of select drugs (e.g., carboplatin, cisplatin, methotrexate).
- Non-GFR determinants of creatinine and cystatin C (e.g., inflammation, muscle wasting) are amplified in people with cancer, reducing the reliability of all estimating equations.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Adopt the same GFR evaluation approach as described in Section 1.2 for oncology practice and clinical trials.
- Consider non-GFR determinants of creatinine and cystatin C in people with cancer.
- Regularly assess risk, benefit, and value of medications in pregnancy due to physiological changes affecting GFR.
- For neonates, specific guidance on drug dosing should be followed due to lower GFR compared to other pediatric populations.
