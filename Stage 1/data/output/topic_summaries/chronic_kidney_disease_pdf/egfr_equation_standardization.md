# eGFR equation standardization
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** eGFR equation standardization and race-neutral computation

**Clinical Actions:**
- Use the same eGFR equation within geographical regions (as large as possible, e.g., continent, country, or region)
- Avoid using race in the computation of eGFR
- Use validated equations for children developed or validated in comparable populations

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Geographical regions should be defined locally and as large as possible
- Equations may differ for adults and children within regions
- Geographical regions should be defined locally by healthcare systems or national bodies to ensure practical applicability
