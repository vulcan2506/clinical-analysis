# KDIGO 2024 CKD management guideline
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** KDIGO 2024 CKD guideline update and scope

**Clinical Actions:**
- Provides actionable recommendations based on rigorous formal evidence review and GRADE approach
- Includes practice points for clinical activities where systematic reviews were not conducted
- Targets broad audience of healthcare providers and patients with CKD while considering policy implications
- Integrates new therapies with lifestyle management and existing medications using patient-engaging approaches
- Presents relative and absolute risks from CKD Prognosis Consortium (CKD-PC) for specific outcomes

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Established diagnosis of CKD (persistent reduction in GFR and/or markers of kidney damage)
- Excludes people receiving dialysis and kidney transplant recipients from the primary focus
- Screening efforts should include assessments of GFR and albuminuria in high-risk populations
- Care should be holistic, addressing patient circumstances and psychological challenges
- Recommendations must consider individual patient preferences and individual risks and benefits
- Established diagnosis of CKD requiring persistence of GFR reduction and/or markers of kidney damage for >3 months
- Exclusion of people receiving dialysis and kidney transplant recipients from the primary focus of the guideline
