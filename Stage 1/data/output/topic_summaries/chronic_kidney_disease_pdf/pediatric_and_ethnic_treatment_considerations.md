# pediatric and ethnic treatment considerations
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** pediatric and ethnic treatment considerations in CKD

**Clinical Actions:**
- No uric acid-lowering trials conducted in children.
- Asian ethnicities may be at higher risk of serious skin cutaneous reactions if carrying the HLA-B*5801 allele.
- HLA-B*5801 allele screening may be considered before allopurinol treatment in Asian populations.
- Screening for HLA-B*5801 may not be cost-effective despite potential risk reduction.
- Most well-informed people with CKD prefer optimizing therapies with proven benefit for CKD progression.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- HLA-B*5801 screening should be considered in Asian patients prior to allopurinol therapy.
