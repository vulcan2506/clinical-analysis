# pregnancy counseling and risks
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** pregnancy counseling and risks in CKD

**Clinical Actions:**
- Review teratogenicity potential of medications for people with CKD of child-bearing potential
- Provide regular reproductive and contraceptive counseling aligned with patient values and preferences
- Discontinue teratogenic CKD medications (e.g., ACEi/ARBs, mTOR inhibitors) before conception or at conception
- Continue CKD-specific medications during pregnancy/lactation (e.g., hydroxychloroquine, tacrolimus, cyclosporin, eculizumab, prednisone, azathioprine, colchicine, IVIG)
- Conduct multidisciplinary preconception, pregnancy, and lactation care with obstetrics and subspecialties
- Conduct sex-specific medication dosing adjustments for RAAS-blocking medications in CKD, considering lower doses in women due to potential improved outcomes and lower RAAS activity

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Medication chart review to replace teratogenic medications before conception or establish strict monitoring plan
- Strict monitoring and cessation of teratogenic medications at conception
- Consideration of contraceptive methods with less clear impact on BP (e.g., nonoral hormonal contraceptives)
- Multidisciplinary care coordination before conception and throughout pregnancy and lactation
- Sex-specific medication dosing adjustments due to differences in body weight, composition, and drug metabolism
- Ensure a strict monitoring plan with cessation of potentially teratogenic medications at conception if replacement before conception is not possible
