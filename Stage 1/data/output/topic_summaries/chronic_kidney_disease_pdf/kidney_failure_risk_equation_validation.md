# kidney failure risk equation validation
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** kidney failure risk equation validation

**Clinical Actions:**
- C-statistics are used to evaluate the discrimination of kidney failure risk equations for predicting progression.
- Brier scores are utilized to assess the overall accuracy of kidney failure risk equations.
- R² statistics are calculated to determine the variance explained by kidney failure risk equations.
- Sensitivity and specificity metrics are applied to evaluate the ability to start kidney replacement therapy (KRT).
- Validation is performed across adults, children, and young people with CKD stages G1–G5.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patient population must include adults, children, and young people.
- CKD staging must range from G1 to G5.
- The equation must be validated for predicting progression to kidney failure.
- The equation must be validated for predicting the need to start kidney replacement therapy (KRT).
