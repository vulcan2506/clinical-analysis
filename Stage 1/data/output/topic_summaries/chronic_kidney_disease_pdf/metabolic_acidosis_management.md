# metabolic acidosis management
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** metabolic acidosis management in CKD

**Clinical Actions:**
- Consider pharmacological treatment with or without dietary intervention to prevent acidosis in adults with CKD when serum bicarbonate <18 mmol/l.
- Monitor treatment to ensure serum bicarbonate does not exceed the upper limit of normal.
- Monitor treatment to avoid adverse effects on blood pressure control, serum potassium, or fluid status.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Serum bicarbonate <18 mmol/l in adults with CKD for initiating pharmacological treatment.
