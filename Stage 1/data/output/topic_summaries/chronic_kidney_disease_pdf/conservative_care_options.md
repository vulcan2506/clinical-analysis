# conservative care options
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** conservative care options in CKD

**Clinical Actions:**
- Inform people with CKD about options for KRT and comprehensive conservative care
- Support comprehensive conservative management for those who choose not to pursue KRT
- Provide access to resources for advanced care planning in end-of-life care
- Integrate supportive care focused on improving HRQoL at any CKD stage
- Conduct active symptom assessment and management in CKD G5
- Integrate palliative care discussions early in the CKD G5 disease course, not just at end-of-life
- Recognize three distinct groups receiving conservative care: those who choose it, those with resource constraints preventing KRT, and those with unrecognized CKD G5

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Person-centered care with shared decision-making
- Detailed communication including prognosis estimation and advance care planning
- Psychological, social, family, cultural, and spiritual support
- Interventions to delay progression and minimize risks (excluding dialysis)
- Culturally appropriate shared decision-making with consideration of health literacy
- Prognosis estimation must incorporate unbiased survival data and person-centered outcomes (QoL, symptom burden, family support) for shared decision-making in CKD G5
- Culturally appropriate care structures may involve families or religious organizations in delivering sensitive end-of-life care
