# albuminuria testing protocol
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** albuminuria testing protocol in CKD

**Clinical Actions:**
- Use first void in the morning midstream sample for initial testing of albuminuria in adults and children.
- Prioritize urine ACR over other methods for initial albuminuria testing.
- Confirm positive reagent strip albuminuria/proteinuria with quantitative laboratory measurement (ACR or PCR).
- Confirm ACR ≥30 mg/g (≥3 mg/mmol) on a random untimed urine with a subsequent first morning void sample.
- In children, obtain first morning urine sample and prefer both urine PCR and ACR for initial testing.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Initial testing must use a first void in the morning midstream sample where possible.
- Quantitative confirmation (ACR or PCR) is required if initial semiquantitative tests are positive.
- Confirmatory testing must express results as a ratio to urine creatinine.
- Follow-up testing for ACR ≥30 mg/g must include a subsequent first morning void sample.
- Consider biological factors (e.g., hematuria, menstruation) that may affect interpretation.
- Confirm ACR ≥30 mg/g (≥3 mg/mmol) on a random untimed urine with a subsequent first morning void midstream urine sample for confirmatory testing.
