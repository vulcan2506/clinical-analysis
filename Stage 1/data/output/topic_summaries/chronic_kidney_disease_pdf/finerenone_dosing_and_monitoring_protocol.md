# finerenone dosing and monitoring protocol
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** finerenone dosing and monitoring protocol

**Clinical Actions:**
- Initiate finerenone at 10 mg daily if eGFR 25–59 ml/min/1.73 m², or 20 mg daily if eGFR ≥60 ml/min/1.73 m²
- Monitor serum potassium (K+) at 1 month after initiation, then every 4 months
- Increase dose to 20 mg daily if on 10 mg daily and K+ remains stable
- Restart 10 mg daily if previously held for hyperkalemia and K+ now ≤5.0 mmol/l
- Hold finerenone for hyperkalemia, adjust diet/concomitant medications, and recheck K+
- Consider reinitiation of finerenone if previously held for hyperkalemia when serum potassium (K+) ≤5.0 mmol/l, with adjustments to diet or concomitant medications to mitigate recurrence

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- eGFR >25 ml/min/1.73 m²
- Normal serum potassium concentration at initiation
- Albuminuria >30 mg/g (>3 mg/mmol) despite maximum tolerated dose of RAS inhibitor
- Consistent normal serum potassium monitoring after initiation
- Prioritize agents with documented kidney or cardiovascular benefits
- eGFR ≥25 ml/min/1.73 m² for initiation
