# CKD pain management strategies
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** CKD pain and symptom management strategies

**Clinical Actions:**
- Use an adapted WHO Analgesic Ladder for pain management in CKD, considering altered pharmacokinetics of analgesics.
- Assess risk of substance abuse and obtain informed consent before starting opioids, including discussion of goals, expectations, risks, and alternatives.
- Use topical analgesics cautiously due to risk of systemic absorption and adverse events.
- Address contributing factors for sleep disorders, restless leg syndrome, depression, poor appetite, nausea, and vomiting (e.g., pain, pruritus, anemia, mood disorders).
- Refer patients at risk of aberrant behaviors or adverse outcomes to specialist pain, palliative, or supportive care clinics.
- Use massage therapy for musculoskeletal pain management

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Assessment of pain etiology and severity before initiating management.
- Adjustment of pharmacological treatments due to altered pharmacokinetics in CKD.
- Nutritional assessment and intervention for patients with frailty, age >65 years, weight loss, poor growth (pediatrics), poor appetite, or CKD G4–G5 using validated tools.
- Correction of contributing factors such as hyperphosphatemia and iron deficiency/anemia for restless leg syndrome.
- Dietary assessment by a renal dietitian for patients with poor appetite, nausea, or vomiting.
- Nutritional assessment and intervention using validated tools (e.g., 7-Point SGA, Malnutrition-Inflammation Score, Mini Nutrition Assessment) for patients with frailty, age >65 years, weight loss, poor growth (pediatrics), poor appetite, or CKD G4–G5
