# pediatric CKD management
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** pediatric CKD management during acute illness

**Clinical Actions:**
- Temporary discontinuation of diuretics and RASi should be considered during acute dehydrating illnesses to prevent hypotension and AKI
- A clear plan for restarting discontinued medications must be communicated to patients and documented in the medical record

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Patient must have underlying tubular disorders
- Patient must be experiencing an acute dehydrating illness
