# CKD progression management guideline
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** CKD progression management guideline methodology

**Clinical Actions:**
- Systematic screening of 30,861 citations with dual independent review at title/abstract and full-text stages
- Use of Cochrane Risk of Bias 2 tool for RCTs and QUADAS-2 for diagnostic studies
- Evidence synthesis using random-effects models (DerSimonian and Laird) for dichotomous and continuous outcomes
- Meta-analysis conducted only for studies with sufficient similarity in population, duration, and comparisons
- Resolution of disagreements through consensus or third-reviewer consultation
- Use of PRISMA diagrams (Supplementary Figures S1–S12) for each systematic review to document study flow and screening process
- Inclusion of nonrandomized studies (232) alongside RCTs (145) in the evidence review for CKD progression management

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Dual independent screening and data extraction by ERT members
- Use of validated risk-of-bias tools (Cochrane RoB 2, QUADAS-2, ROBIS)
- Minimum of 2 studies required for meta-analysis
- Stratification by intervention type if substantial heterogeneity (I² >50%)
- Random-effects models for pooled estimates
