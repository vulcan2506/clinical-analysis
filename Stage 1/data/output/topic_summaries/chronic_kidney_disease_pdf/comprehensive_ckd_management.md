# comprehensive CKD management
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** comprehensive CKD management

**Clinical Actions:**
- Treat people with CKD with a comprehensive treatment strategy to reduce risks of progression of CKD and its associated complications.
- Encourage people with CKD to undertake physical activity compatible with cardiovascular health, tolerance, and level of frailty; achieve an optimal body mass index (BMI); and not to use tobacco products.
- Advise people with CKD to undertake moderate-intensity physical activity for a cumulative duration of at least 150 minutes per week, or to a level compatible with their cardiovascular and physical tolerance.
- Advise people with CKD to avoid sedentary behavior.
- Encourage children with CKD to undertake physical activity aiming for WHO-advised levels (≥60 minutes daily) and to achieve a healthy weight.
- Advise people with CKD to lose weight if they are obese, considering individual risk factors and comorbidities.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Referral to providers and programs (e.g., psychologists, renal dietitians, pharmacists, physical and occupational therapy, and smoking cessation programs) should be offered where indicated and available.
- Early identification and referral to specialist kidney care services to reverse, delay, or prevent progression of disease.
- Use validated risk equations (e.g., KFRE) to ascertain those at high probability of kidney failure within 2 years to guide referral decisions.
- Timely referral (minimum 3 months before KRT initiation) for assessment, education, preparation for KRT, and creation of access.
- Application of risk prediction tools to identify those at risk of progression and determine action thresholds for multidisciplinary care.
- Consider advising/encouraging weight loss for people with obesity and CKD, tailored to age, ethnic background, comorbidities, and resource access.
