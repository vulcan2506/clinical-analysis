# CKD monitoring protocol
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** CKD monitoring protocol for progression and risk stratification

**Clinical Actions:**
- Assess albuminuria (or albuminuria/proteinuria in children) and GFR at least annually in people with CKD.
- Increase monitoring frequency for individuals at higher risk of CKD progression when measurements impact therapeutic decisions.
- Evaluate a change in eGFR of >20% on subsequent testing as it exceeds expected variability.
- Evaluate GFR reductions of >30% in people initiating hemodynamically active therapies as it exceeds expected variability.
- Evaluate a doubling of ACR on subsequent testing as it exceeds laboratory variability.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Use externally validated risk equations to estimate absolute risk of kidney failure in people with CKD G3–G5.
- Use disease-specific, externally validated prediction equations for IgAN and ADPKD.
- Use externally validated models for cardiovascular risk prediction that incorporate eGFR and albuminuria.
- Use externally validated models for mortality risk prediction developed specifically in CKD populations.
- Use externally validated models for cardiovascular risk prediction that incorporate eGFR and albuminuria for guiding preventive therapies in people with CKD.
