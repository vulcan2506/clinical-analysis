# KDIGO conflict of interest policy
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** KDIGO conflict of interest policy

**Clinical Actions:**
- KDIGO requires all Work Group members to complete, sign, and submit an annual disclosure and attestation form for all relationships that might be perceived as or are actual conflicts of interest.
- All reported conflict of interest information is published in full in the Work Group members' Disclosure section and maintained on file at KDIGO.
- KDIGO makes every effort to avoid actual or reasonably perceived conflicts of interest arising from outside relationships or personal, professional, or business interests.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Work Group members must annually update and submit disclosure forms to KDIGO.
- Disclosure information must be published in its entirety in the Work Group members' Disclosure section.
