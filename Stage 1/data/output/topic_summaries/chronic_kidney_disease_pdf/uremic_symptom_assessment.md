# uremic symptom assessment
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** uremic symptom assessment in CKD

**Clinical Actions:**
- Ask people with progressive CKD about uremic symptoms (e.g., reduced appetite, nausea, and level of fatigue/lethargy) at each consultation
- Use a standardized validated assessment tool for uremic symptoms
- Assess patient-reported outcomes (PROs) including HRQoL, fatigue, pain, physical function, depression, and daily activity
- Implement evidence-informed management strategies to address identified symptoms
- Facilitate shared decision-making and 2-way communication between healthcare providers and patients
- Include sexual dysfunction as a key symptom target in advanced CKD (CKD G4–G5) during uremic symptom assessment

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Use of validated assessment tools for uremic symptoms
- Regular screening at each consultation for progressive CKD
- Consideration of patient-reported outcomes (PROs) as part of routine care
- Access to multidisciplinary care teams including dietary counseling and psychological support
- Use of standardized outcome measures such as ICHOM-recommended tools
- Use of ICHOM-recommended standardized outcome measures (e.g., PROMIS) for patient-reported outcomes in CKD G3–G5
- Screen for malnutrition twice annually in CKD G4–G5 patients aged >65 or with symptoms such as involuntary weight loss, frailty, or poor appetite using a validated tool
