# CKD dietary management strategy
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** CKD dietary management strategy

**Clinical Actions:**
- Advise people with CKD to adopt healthy and diverse diets with higher plant-based food consumption and lower ultraprocessed foods.
- Use renal dietitians or accredited nutrition providers for tailored dietary education on sodium, phosphorus, potassium, and protein intake.
- Avoid high protein intake (>1.3 g/kg/d) in adults with CKD at risk of progression.
- Consider very low–protein diets (0.3–0.4 g/kg/d) with supplementation in metabolically stable adults with CKD at risk of kidney failure.
- Do not restrict protein intake in children with CKD; target upper-normal protein and energy intake for growth.

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Tailor dietary adaptations to individual needs, CKD severity, and comorbid conditions.
- Prescribe very low–protein diets only under close supervision in metabolically stable adults.
- Use renal dietitians or accredited nutrition providers for education.
- Avoid protein restriction in metabolically unstable people with CKD.
- Avoid protein restriction in children with CKD due to growth impairment risk.
- Prescribe very low–protein diets (0.3–0.4 g/kg/d) with essential amino acids or ketoacid analogs only in adults with CKD who are willing and able to adhere to the regimen.
