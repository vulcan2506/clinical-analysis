# Contrast Imaging and Risk Stratification
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Contrast Imaging and Risk Stratification in CKD

**Clinical Actions:**
- Determine risks and benefits of imaging studies on an individual basis in the context of CKD
- Assess AKI risk in CKD patients receiving intra-arterial contrast using validated tools
- Manage intravenous radiocontrast media in accordance with radiology society consensus statements for patients with AKI or GFR <60 ml/min/1.73 m²
- Preferentially offer American College of Radiology group II and III gadolinium-based contrast agents for patients with GFR <30 ml/min/1.73 m² requiring gadolinium
- Consider alternative imaging modalities (e.g., ultrasound, MRI without gadolinium) when contrast imaging risks outweigh benefits in CKD patients

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- CKD diagnosis must be established prior to contrast imaging
- Validated AKI risk assessment tools must be used for intra-arterial contrast procedures
- GFR <60 ml/min/1.73 m² requires adherence to radiology society consensus statements for intravenous radiocontrast
- GFR <30 ml/min/1.73 m² necessitates use of specific gadolinium-based contrast agents
- Ensure hydration status and volume optimization prior to contrast administration in patients with AKI or GFR <60 ml/min/1.73 m² undergoing intravenous contrast procedures
