# Lipid Measurement Guideline Update
*Source: 2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA 
 AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on
the Management of Dyslipidemia.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Lipid Measurement Guideline Update: LDL-C Estimation + ApoB/Lp(a) Assessment + CAC Imaging

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Use Martin/Hopkins or Sampson/NIH equations over Friedewald for LDL-C estimation in adults and children with standard lipid profiles.
- Prefer nonfasting lipid profiles for most adults due to minimal prognostic differences vs fasting, except in TG ≥400 mg/dL or genetic dyslipidemia.
- Measure apoB in adults on lipid-lowering therapy (LLT) with ASCVD, CKM syndrome, type 2 diabetes, or elevated TG to guide intensification after LDL-C/non-HDL-C goals are met.
- Measure Lp(a) at least once in all adults for ASCVD risk assessment.
- Use CAC score >0 AU to initiate LLT in intermediate-risk adults; ≥100 AU or ≥75th percentile to intensify LLT.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Fasting lipid profile required for TG ≥400 mg/dL, suspected TG metabolism disorders, or family history of premature ASCVD/genetic dyslipidemia.
- Standard lipid profile includes TC, HDL-C, TG (direct measurement), with LDL-C and non-HDL-C calculated.
- CAC scoring recommended for adults at intermediate or borderline risk to refine LLT intensity decisions.
- Fasting lipid profile required for TG ≥200 mg/dL in individuals with known or suspected disorders in triglyceride metabolism or if further evaluation of hypertriglyceridemia is warranted.

**Significance Level:** MODERATE

**Clinical Recommendation:** Lipid Panel Measurement Protocol + Serial Sampling & Additional Testing

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- Three serial samples 3 weeks apart are recommended for Total Cholesterol, Triglycerides, HDL & LDL Cholesterol to account for physiological and analytical variations.

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Physiological and analytical variations necessitate serial sampling for accurate lipid measurement.
- Fasting for at least 9-12 hours prior to lipid panel measurement to minimize dietary influences on triglycerides and other lipid fractions.

# Lipid & Cardiovascular Risk Management

These profiles outline standardized clinical protocols for lipid and cardiovascular risk management, emphasizing evidence-based thresholds, precise measurement methodologies, and risk stratification to guide treatment decisions.

**Key Features:**
- Lipid Test Result Thresholds: Risk-based LDL-C classification (<70 mg/dL 'High', <100 mg/dL 'Moderate', <130 mg/dL 'Low')
- Lipid Panel Measurement Protocol: Serial sampling (three samples 3 weeks apart) for accurate lipid assessment
- Fasting Glucose Measurement: Hexokinase method for baseline glucose quantification with strict 8-hour fasting requirement

**Notable Details:**
- Extreme/Very High Risk Groups require LDL-C goals of <50 mg/dL (optional ≤30 mg/dL) and non-HDL-C goals of <80 mg/dL (optional ≤60 mg/dL)
- Serial lipid sampling accounts for physiological and analytical variations in lipid measurements
- Non-HDL-C goals are aligned with LDL-C targets for high-risk groups
- The Hexokinase method is a gold-standard enzymatic assay for glucose quantification
- Fasting duration must be strictly adhered to avoid interference from recent food intake
- Risk categories are defined by LDL-C thresholds: <70 mg/dL (High), <100 mg/dL (Moderate), and <130 mg/dL (Low)
- Accurate fasting glucose measurement is essential for diagnosing diabetes or prediabetes

### Lipid and Cardiovascular Risk Management

This collection of guidelines and recommendations focuses on evidence-based management of lipid disorders and cardiovascular risk across diverse populations, emphasizing individualized care, advanced therapies, and integration of novel biomarkers and genetic testing.

**Key Features:**
- 2026 Dyslipidemia Guideline Update: Intensified LDL-C and non-HDL-C targets (<55 mg/dL and <85 mg/dL) for severe hypercholesterolemia with ASCVD using ezetimibe, PCSK9 mAb, or bempedoic acid
- Dyslipidemia Treatment Guideline: Shift from statin monotherapy to combination therapy targeting multiple lipid pathways with shared decision-making
- Lp(a) Integration in Cardiovascular Risk Assessment: Elevated Lp(a) ≥50 mg/dL triggers statin intensification or PCSK9 inhibitor consideration in high-risk patients
- Lipid Management in Pregnancy: Discontinue statins during gestation except hydrophilic statins (e.g., pravastatin) after individualized risk-benefit discussion
- Obesity-Related Lipid Management: Non-HDL-C and apoB prioritized over LDL-C; Lp(a) screening recommended at least once in adulthood
- Familial Hypercholesterolemia Treatment: Genetic sequencing and validated risk scores (e.g., Montreal-FH-SCORE) guide advanced therapy selection
- Pediatric Lipid Management: Lifestyle intervention first; statins for children ≥8 years with LDL-C ≥160 mg/dL unresponsive to lifestyle
- Hypertriglyceridemia Management: Lifestyle modification (diet, exercise, weight loss) as first-line for TG ≥150 mg/dL after secondary cause evaluation
- Genetic Testing for FH: Panel-based testing for LDLR, APOB, PCSK9 improves detection and enables cascade screening
- Coronary Artery Disease Screening: Routine screening in asymptomatic individuals not recommended; targeted testing for symptomatic or high-risk patients
- Dietary Recommendations: Plant-based Mediterranean-style diet emphasized for lipid reduction, especially in CKD
- Physical Activity Recommendations: ≥150 minutes/week moderate-to-vigorous aerobic exercise plus resistance training 2 days/week
- Primary Prevention Guideline: Health behavior counseling and risk-based lipid-lowering therapy discussions for adults aged 30–59

**Notable Details:**
- PCSK9 monoclonal antibodies are recommended for severe hypercholesterolemia with ASCVD to achieve LDL-C <55 mg/dL
- Lp(a) ≥50 mg/dL is a threshold for intensifying statin therapy or considering PCSK9 inhibitors in high-risk patients
- Statin therapy should be discontinued during pregnancy except for hydrophilic statins (e.g., pravastatin) in select cases
- Genetic sequencing for FH diagnosis improves detection beyond limited-variant screening and enables cascade screening
- Non-HDL-C and apoB are preferred lipid markers over LDL-C in obesity due to better reflection of atherogenic risk

# point-of-care kidney function testing
*Source: Chronic Kidney Disease.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** point-of-care kidney function testing for creatinine and albumin

**Clinical Actions:**
- POCT may be used for creatinine and urine albumin measurement where access to a laboratory is limited or to facilitate the clinical pathway
- Ensure pre-analytical, analytical, and post-analytical quality criteria are met for POCT devices
- Generate an estimate of GFR using the equation consistent with the region when using POCT for creatinine
- POCT ACR devices should produce a positive result in 85% of people with significant albuminuria (ACR ≥30 mg/g or ≥3 mg/mmol)
- POCT devices for albuminuria testing should also analyze creatinine to produce an ACR when feasible

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Access to a laboratory is limited or point-of-care testing facilitates the clinical pathway
- Pre-analytical, analytical, and post-analytical quality criteria must be met
- Use region-consistent GFR estimation equation with POCT creatinine
- POCT ACR devices must meet performance criteria for detecting significant albuminuria
- External quality assessment must be included in the quality criteria for POCT devices

# hypertension and kidney function management
*Source: American Diabetes Association Care Guidence.pdf*

**Clinical Recommendation:** Hypertension and kidney function management in diabetes

**Core Recommendations:**
- Frequency of kidney function assessment clarified
- Systolic blood pressure goals emphasized
- Monitoring for medication intolerance in nonpregnant individuals with diabetes and hypertension
- Monitoring frequency for eGFR drops, serum potassium changes, and hypokalemia with ACE inhibitors, ARBs, or MRAs
- Potassium level monitoring after initiation of nonsteroidal mineralocorticoid receptor antagonists (nsMRAs)

**Patient Eligibility Criteria:**
- Urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio ≥100 mg/g
- eGFR 30–90 mL/1.73 m²
- On a renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system (RAS) inhibitor
- Type 2 diabetes diagnosis
- Non-dialysis status for SGLT2 inhibitor use

**Previous Recommendations:**
- Fixed numeric A1C targets for older adults (implied by individualized targets in broader guidelines)

**Updated Recommendations:**
- Simultaneous initiation of SGLT2 inhibitor and nsMRA can be considered in eligible patients
- SGLT2 inhibitor use in non-dialysis individuals to reduce CKD progression and cardiovascular risk
- Guidance on GLP-1-based therapy in individuals on dialysis to reduce cardiovascular risk
- Reordered RAS inhibitor dose placement in kidney outcome trials figure
- Clarified guidance on kidney-protective medications in pregnant or sexually active individuals of childbearing potential
- Guidance on initiation or continuation of GLP-1-based therapy in individuals on dialysis to reduce cardiovascular risk (Recommendation 11.11b)
- Clarified guidance on kidney-protective medications that are potentially harmful in pregnant and sexually active individuals of childbearing potential (Recommendation 11.10)
