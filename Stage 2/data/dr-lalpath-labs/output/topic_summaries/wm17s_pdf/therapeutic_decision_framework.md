# Therapeutic Decision Framework
*Source: WM17S.pdf*

**Therapeutic Decision Framework**

**Significance Level:** LOW

**Clinical Recommendation:** Therapeutic Decision Framework for Low-Risk Patients

**Key Clinical Actions:**
- In low-risk patients, consider therapy only after an initial non-pharmacological intervention for at least 3 months
  *Guideline context: Clinical guidelines suggest that therapeutic decisions should incorporate cost/value methodology and patient-centered outcomes, ensuring that interventions are justified based on both clinical and economic evidence (2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PLA-PCNA Guideline on the Management of Dyslipidemia).*
- *Guideline context: Individualized clinician-patient discussions must precede dyslipidemia medication initiation, addressing benefits, risks, side effects, and patient preferences (2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on the Management of Dyslipidemia).*
- For patients at borderline or intermediate risk (3% to <10% 10-year ASCVD risk), consider risk enhancers to personalize decisions about lipid-lowering therapy initiation and intensity (2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on the Management of Dyslipidemia).
- Assess for potential drug–drug interactions before initiating statin therapy, particularly with CYP3A4-metabolized statins (e.g., atorvastatin, lovastatin, simvastatin) (2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on the Management of Dyslipidemia).

**Additional Key Clinical Actions (guideline-derived):**
- If therapy is indicated, statin intensity should align with baseline risk: moderate-intensity (≥30% LDL-C lowering) for intermediate risk (5% to <10%) and high-intensity (≥50% LDL-C reduction) for high risk (≥10%) (2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PCNA Guideline on the Management of Dyslipidemia).

**Patient Preparation / Diagnostic Criteria:**
- Patient must be classified as low risk per guideline criteria
  *Guideline context: Adherence to updated cost/value assessment frameworks and standardized health economic models is required when applying guideline recommendations to ensure transparency and rigor (2026 ACC-AHA-AACVPR-ABC-ACPM-ADA AGS-APhA-ASPC-NLA-PLA-PCNA Guideline on the Management of Dyslipidemia).*
  *Guideline context: The guideline framework is designed to assist—not dictate—clinical decision-making, acknowledging variations in practice based on patient needs and institutional resources (Chronic Kidney Disease guideline).*
